How Do I Find Parking Near a Concert Venue?

Parking near major arenas and stadiums fills up fast, especially on event nights. Most venues offer official on-site lots that can be pre-purchased online, often at a lower price than day-of. Local garages and surface lots within walking distance are usually cheaper. For large events, arriving 60 to 90 minutes early and knowing your exit route in advance can save 30+ minutes after the show.
