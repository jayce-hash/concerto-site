The Essentials

Eight tools covering everything you actually need — before, during, and after concerts, games, festivals, and nights out.

City Guides →

Curated dining, bars, and hotels anchored to your venue’s neighborhood. No generic lists — only spots worth your time before or after the show.

Events Near Me →

Open the app and instantly see every major concert, sports game, comedy show, and festival happening near you tonight.

Tour Info →

Full routing schedules, direct ticket links, and venue-specific details for every major tour currently on the road.

Setlists →

See what songs an artist is playing on this tour, track local start times, and link every track directly to Apple Music or Spotify.

1-Tap Rideshare →

Open Uber or Lyft with your venue’s exact drop-off and pickup zones already filled in — no fumbling with addresses after the show.

Bag Policies →

Look up the exact bag rules for your venue before you leave the house — dimensions, clear-bag requirements, and prohibited items for 300+ venues.

Parking →

Official lots, nearby alternatives, pricing, and insider tips on how to get out fast when 50,000 people are trying to leave at the same time.

Concessions →

Know exactly what food, drinks, and vendors are inside your venue before you arrive — so you can head straight to the right spot.
