Questions

Common questions.

How much does it cost?

$99 a month, or $999 a year paid upfront. No commissions, no booking fees, and you can cancel anytime.

How fast does my card go live?

Within about five minutes of paying. Setup takes about two minutes.

Where will fans see my restaurant?

First in the Nearby Restaurants row in the Concerto app, on your venue’s page and in Your Night for fans who saved a show there. Partner listings don’t appear on concertocity.com.

Which venues can I choose?

Any of Concerto’s 600+ venues worldwide, as long as your restaurant is within 5 miles.

What is a Perk?

An optional offer, like a free dessert on show nights. It adds a gold star to your card, and signed-in Concerto members tap the star to see it. Fans use it as you describe, usually by showing it in the Concerto app. You can change or end it anytime by email.

How do I cancel?

Use the link in your Stripe receipt. Your card stays up through the period you’ve paid for.
