How Perks work

Fans show a live code.
Your staff scan it.

A Perk is an offer just for concert fans. You choose the offer and how fans redeem it, and it shows in gold on your card.

To use it, a fan taps Use Perk in the Concerto app and shows a live code. Your staff scan it with any phone camera and enter your 4-digit staff code. There’s no equipment, app or setup.

The code changes every few seconds, so screenshots don’t work. Each fan can use your Perk once a day, and every redemption is counted in your monthly report. Concerto doesn’t handle payment or take a cut, and you can change or end your Perk anytime.

Questions

Common questions.

How much does it cost?

$299 a month, or $2,990 a year paid upfront. No commissions, no booking fees, and you can cancel anytime.

How fast does my card go live?

Within about five minutes of paying. Setup takes about two minutes.

Where will fans see my hotel?

On your venue’s page in the Concerto app and on concertocity.com, labeled Concerto Partner, and in Your Night for fans who saved a show there.

Which venues can I choose?

Any of Concerto’s 600+ venues worldwide, as long as your hotel is within 5 miles.

Do I need a Perk?

No. A Perk is optional, but it’s what makes your card stand out. You can add one when you sign up.

How do fans redeem a Perk?

They tap Use Perk in the Concerto app and show a live code. Your staff scan it with any phone camera and enter your 4-digit staff code.

Can someone use a screenshot?

No. The code changes every few seconds and can be used once per fan, per day.

How do I cancel?

Use the link in your Stripe receipt. Your card stays up through the period you’ve paid for.
