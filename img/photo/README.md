# Editorial photography slots

Drop a file here and rerun the build (the push script does this). Until a file
exists, the slot renders nothing. Use .jpg or .webp, 2400px wide, under 400 KB.

| File | Where it appears | Shot |
|---|---|---|
| crowd.jpg | Homepage, after the three-phone product row | Crowd from behind, facing a lit stage. Arena scale. No readable artist or logos. |
| city-night.jpg | Homepage, before the closing CTA | City street or skyline at night, lights, motion. Reads "after the show". |
| dinner.jpg | Concerto+ page, above Your Night | Two to four people at a restaurant table, evening, warm light. Faces not required. |
| venue-exterior.jpg | Partners hub, above the four partner types | Arena or theater exterior at dusk, fans walking in. Avoid signage you do not have rights to. |
| stage-lights.jpg | Investors, above the thesis | Wide stage with lights and audience silhouettes. Abstract enough to be any show. |
| arrival.jpg | About, above the Concerto standard | Gates, entry line, or fans holding phones at the door. Bag and phone visible is a plus. |

Sources that are free for commercial use with no attribution: unsplash.com and
pexels.com. Search terms that work: "concert crowd stage lights", "arena
exterior night", "city night lights street", "friends dinner restaurant
evening", "concert entrance line". Reject anything with a readable artist name,
brand logo, recognizable performer face, or an obviously identifiable venue you
have no relationship with. Prefer navy, gold, and warm tones; avoid neon green
and magenta EDM palettes that fight the brand.
