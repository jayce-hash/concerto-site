# Concerto 2.5 Product Law

## The product
Concerto is the library for the concert and the night around it.
Saving a show turns that library into one cohesive experience for that exact night.

Core loop:
Discover -> Save -> Prepare -> Go -> Get Home -> Remember

## The saved-show page
The countdown card is the primary entrance.
The page is the destination, not a second Home and not a menu of shortcuts.
Useful information lives on the page:
- show details
- verified venue essentials
- weather when reliable
- getting there
- setlist
- restaurants, hotels, things to do around the venue
- getting home

The same page changes emphasis as show day approaches. It does not become a separate mode.

## Concerto+ boundary
Free Concerto tells the fan everything they need to know.
Concerto+ tells the fan what to do with it.

Do not paywall basic accurate venue information, discovery, setlists, weather, parking, rideshare guidance, or nearby browsing merely to make Premium look stronger.
Concerto+ is the orchestration / concierge layer: personalized planning, timing, contextual alerts, and eventually partner benefits.

Facts come from data and official sources. AI interprets facts; AI does not invent them.

## Platform law
Concerto should look like the same company everywhere, but not force every platform to look identical.

Same across platforms:
- account state
- saved shows
- plans and entitlement
- venue facts and verification
- product language
- navy / gold brand system
- typography roles
- trust treatment

Platform-specific excellence is encouraged:
- iOS: widgets, notifications, deep links, haptics, native share sheets, Live Activities when stable, App Intents / Spotlight when justified
- web app: same core product state, adapted to browser/desktop interaction
- public web: SEO, discovery, media, investor, creator and partner storytelling

## Brand law
Gold is earned. Use it for verification, Concerto+, selection and primary action, not decoration.
Playfair carries emotion and editorial hierarchy. DM Sans / system utility type carries information and controls.
Verification is a product feature and must be visible without being loud.

## Company law
Every surface should feel owned by a real company rather than assembled feature by feature.
Avoid duplicate flows. If Concerto already knows the show, artist, venue, date or location, do not ask the user for it again.
