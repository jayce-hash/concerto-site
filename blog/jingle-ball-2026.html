Jingle Ball 2026: every stop, every lineup, and what to know

By Concerto · November 3, 2026

The 2026 iHeartRadio Jingle Ball Tour plays nine arenas between December 1 and December 17, starting at Ariat Arena in Fort Worth and ending at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville. Every stop has its own lineup, and every arena has its own bag rule, from 22 x 14 x 9 inches at Madison Square Garden to 4 x 6 x 1.5 inches at TD Garden.

When and where is Jingle Ball 2026?

Here is every stop, with the start time and lineup iHeartMedia announced. Times are local. Each arena links to its Concerto venue guide.

Who plays the most Jingle Ball stops?

The Beaches play all nine. OneRepublic plays six: Los Angeles, Chicago, Detroit, Washington, D.C., Philadelphia and Nashville. Ludacris plays five, and SOMBR plays four on the East Coast run from New York to Philadelphia. Olivia Rodrigo plays only Los Angeles, and the Jonas Brothers play only Washington, D.C.

What bag can you bring to Jingle Ball?

This is where Jingle Ball catches people out. The tour is the same, but the bag rule is set by each arena, and they are very different. A bag that is fine at Madison Square Garden will not get into TD Garden two nights later.

ArenaBag ruleChecked
Ariat ArenaBags up to 12 x 6 x 12 in. No backpacks, duffels, laptop bags or oversized totes.Oct 2026
Intuit DomeClutches or clear bags only, up to 10 x 6 x 2 in, and every bag is X-rayed.Oct 2026

Capital One ArenaNo bags. Wallet-size clutches up to 5 x 7 in, plus medical and parenting bags.Aug 2026

The smallest limits are at Little Caesars Arena and TD Garden, where anything bigger than a phone wallet is too big. Capital One Arena allows no bags at all beyond a small clutch. If you are not sure, bring the smallest bag you own, or none.

Can you leave Jingle Ball and come back in?

Usually not. Ariat Arena, Intuit Dome, Little Caesars Arena, Madison Square Garden, TD Garden and Xfinity Mobile Arena all say there is no re-entry once your ticket is scanned. At Capital One Arena, leaving during a concert means buying a new ticket to get back in. Bridgestone Arena says re-entry depends on the event, and Allstate Arena has not published a rule, so treat leaving as final there too.

How do you get home after Jingle Ball?

Every arena empties at once after the last act. Know your pickup spot before the show ends, not after.

  • Ariat Arena: Pickup is on Harley Avenue on the north end of the building.
  • Intuit Dome: The Mobility Hub on the first level of the East Garage is the only rideshare spot.
  • Allstate Arena: No published zone. Walk a block or two from the main exit before you request.
  • Little Caesars Arena: Westbound Fisher Service Drive, next to the UWM Garage.
  • Madison Square Garden: No zone. Get picked up on 7th or 8th Avenue between 31st and 34th Streets.
  • TD Garden: The rideshare zone on Merrimac Street.
  • Capital One Arena: 6th and E, 7th and E, 5th and G, or 8th and G Streets.
  • Xfinity Mobile Arena: Lyft pickup is in Lot D, at the southeast corner of the arena.
  • Bridgestone Arena: Drop-off is the eastbound curb lane of Broadway between 5th and 6th Avenues.

At Madison Square Garden, Concerto’s House Map also shows every entrance, food stand and restroom by section.

Is Jingle Ball on TV?

Yes. ABC airs an iHeartRadio Jingle Ball 2026 special in December, and it streams the next day on Hulu, Disney+ and on demand. iHeartMedia has not announced the exact air date yet.

Frequently asked questions

When does Jingle Ball 2026 start?

The tour opens Tuesday, December 1 at Ariat Arena in Fort Worth and ends Thursday, December 17 at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville.

Where do you buy Jingle Ball tickets?

iHeartMedia sells Jingle Ball tickets through Ticketmaster. General on-sale started Friday, October 2.

Can you bring a bag to Jingle Ball?

It depends on the arena. Madison Square Garden allows bags up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches, while TD Garden and Little Caesars Arena allow nothing larger than 4 x 6 x 1.5 inches. Check your arena’s rule above.

Does Jingle Ball support a charity?

Yes. Eight stops support the Ryan Seacrest Foundation, and the Fort Worth show supports Kidd’s Kids. A portion of ticket sales goes to each.

Can the Jingle Ball lineup change?

Yes. iHeartMedia says artists and events can change or be canceled without notice, so check the lineup close to your show.
