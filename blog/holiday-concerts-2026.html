Holiday concerts 2026: the biggest Christmas tours this winter

By Concerto · November 3, 2026

Four big holiday tours play North America this season. Trans-Siberian Orchestra brings The Ghosts of Christmas Eve to more than 50 cities from November 19 to December 30. Pentatonix plays arenas from November 5 to December 22. The iHeartRadio Jingle Ball visits nine arenas from December 1 to 17, and Lady A plays eight shows from December 10 to 21.

When is Pentatonix on tour?

The Christmas in the City Tour opens November 5 at the Tacoma Dome and closes December 22 at the Kia Forum in Los Angeles. In between it plays arenas from British Columbia and Ontario to Texas, Georgia and Arizona. The full list of cities is on the Pentatonix tour page.

What should you know before a holiday arena show?

The tour is the same in every city, but the rules are set by each venue. Bag limits run from a phone-size clutch at some arenas to 22 x 14 x 9 inches at Madison Square Garden, and many arenas do not allow re-entry once your ticket is scanned. Holiday shows also bring big winter coats, so check whether your venue offers coat check or lets you carry one in.

Open your venue’s Concerto guide before you leave. It has the bag rule, entrances, parking, rideshare pickup and re-entry, each checked against the venue’s own sources and dated.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Trans-Siberian Orchestra 2026 tour start?

November 19, with shows in Green Bay, Wisconsin and Council Bluffs, Iowa. It ends December 30 in Buffalo and St. Louis.

Why does Trans-Siberian Orchestra play two cities on the same day?

Two touring groups perform The Ghosts of Christmas Eve at the same time in different parts of North America.

Where does the Pentatonix Christmas tour start and end?

It opens November 5 at the Tacoma Dome in Tacoma, Washington and closes December 22 at the Kia Forum in Los Angeles.

Where is Lady A’s last show of the year?

The Ryman Auditorium in Nashville on December 21, with a matinee and an evening show.

Can I bring a bag to a holiday arena show?

It depends on the venue, not the tour. Check your venue’s Concerto guide for its bag rule before you leave.
