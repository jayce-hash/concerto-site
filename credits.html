Sources And Credits.

Where Concerto’s information comes from, and the notices that go with it.

Venue Guides

Bag policies, entry, parking, accessibility and other venue details are researched from each venue’s own official pages. Every guide shows the date it was last checked.

Rules can change, and a venue’s staff make the final call at the door. If something looks wrong, use Report Wrong Info on that page and it will be checked.

Tour Dates And Tickets

Event dates, times and ticket links are provided by Ticketmaster. The time shown is the show time unless a page says otherwise.

Buying a ticket happens on the seller’s own site, under the seller’s terms. Concerto does not sell tickets.

Setlists

Each setlist shows the show and date it was reported from, or says that it is an official tour playlist. Setlists in Medley’s Community are added and confirmed by fans who were there.

Artists change songs and their order from night to night, so a setlist is a guide to what a tour has been playing, not a promise for your show.

Places And Photos

Restaurants, hotels and things to do near a venue, and many venue photos, are powered by Google Maps. Walking and driving times are estimates.

In some cities, nearby places come from OpenStreetMap: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.

Places marked as partners have added their own details.

Hotel Links

The See Hotels link on a venue page opens a hotel search through Stay22. If you book, Concerto may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It is always labeled as a partner link.

Music

Songs open in Apple Music or Spotify, whichever you choose. Saving a setlist as a playlist works with Apple Music and needs an Apple Music subscription. Concerto, Medley and Reprise are not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple or Spotify.

Independence

Concerto LLC is independent from artists, venues, teams, promoters and ticket sellers. Names of artists, tours and venues are used only to identify them.
