The standard

Every partnership has to work on three fronts.

We are not interested in generic ad inventory. A strong Concerto partnership creates measurable value for the partner, meaningful value for the fan, and sustainable value for Concerto.

Concerto gets

Value worth building around.

Paid sponsorship, valuable inventory or access, official information, content rights, or another clearly defined contribution.

Fans get

A reason to care.

A useful offer, better access, a show-night benefit, trusted information, or an experience that makes the night better.

Partners get

High-intent distribution.

Relevant app, website, and social placement with a clear action path and campaign-level reporting.

Distribution beyond the follower count
