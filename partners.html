Why the timing matters

Intent is more valuable when it has a place and a date.

Concerto is not trying to sell a banner to somebody scrolling a feed. A saved show tells us a fan is planning around a specific venue on a specific night. That makes the nearby restaurant, hotel, transportation, and venue experience genuinely relevant.

The goal is simple: create value for the fan first, then make the value measurable for the partner.
