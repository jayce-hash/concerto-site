Reach concert fans
near your venue.

What you get

First in the row
for your venue.

  1. 01

    First in Nearby Restaurants or Nearby Hotels.

    Your place leads the row for your venue in the Concerto app, on the venue’s page and in Your Night for every show there. With more than one partner at a venue, the order rotates daily.

  2. 02

    First on the website and in answers.

    Your place appears near the top of your venue’s page on concertocity.com, and Concerto AI and Plan My Night suggest it first when fans ask where to eat or stay.

  3. 03

    The same card as every place around you.

    Your photo from your Google listing, your price, and your distance from the venue. Tapping it opens directions.

  4. 04

    An optional Perk.

    Add an offer, like a free dessert on show nights. A gold star appears on your card, and signed-in fans tap it to see the offer.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. 01

    Pick your venue.

    Search for your restaurant or hotel near any of Concerto’s 600+ venues worldwide. Your address, location, and photo fill in from Google.

  2. 02

    Add an offer if you want one.

    Optional. Add an offer and your reservation or booking link. Fans who tap your star see the offer and can go straight to your link.

  3. 03

    Pay, and you’re live.

    Restaurants are $99 a month and hotels $299 a month, with savings on annual plans and no commissions or booking fees. You’re live within minutes, and each month you see how many fans viewed and tapped your card.

Where it shows

In the app, on your venue’s page online, and in Concerto AI and Plan My Night.
Always labeled Concerto Partner.
