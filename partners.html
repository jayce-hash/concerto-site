Why it works

Fans come for the venue.
Then they plan the night.

Fans find Concerto when they look up their venue before a show: the bag policy, parking, when doors open. Those searches already bring Concerto about 14,000 Google search appearances a day, ten times as many as in July.

Once a fan knows what they can bring, the next thing they decide is where to eat and where to stay. The same page answers it, and Concerto Partners come first.

What you get

First in the row
for your venue.

  1. 01

    First in Nearby Restaurants or Nearby Hotels.

    Your place leads the row for your venue in the Concerto app, on the venue’s page and in Your Night for every show there. With more than one partner at a venue, the order rotates daily.

  2. 02

    First on the website and in answers.

    Your place also leads its Nearby row on your venue’s page on concertocity.com, with its photo, and Concerto AI and Plan it for me suggest it first when fans ask where to eat or stay.

  3. 03

    The same card as every place around you.

    Your photo from your Google listing, your price, and your distance from the venue. Tapping it opens directions.

  4. 04

    An optional Perk.

    Add an offer, like a free dessert on show nights. A gold star appears on your card, and signed-in fans tap it to see the offer.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. 01

    Pick your venue.

    Search for your restaurant or hotel near any of Concerto’s 1,000+ venues worldwide. Your address, location, and photo fill in from Google.

  2. 02

    Add an offer if you want one.

    Optional. Add an offer and your reservation or booking link. Fans who tap your star see the offer and can go straight to your link.

  3. 03

    Pay, and you’re live.

    Restaurants are $99 a month and hotels $299 a month, with savings on annual plans and no commissions or booking fees. You’re live within minutes, and each month you see how many fans viewed and tapped your card.

Where it shows

In the app, on your venue’s page online, and in Concerto AI and fans’ planned nights.
Always labeled Concerto Partner.
