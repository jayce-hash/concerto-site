Where Perks live

In Account, and around the show you saved.

The full Perks view is in the app under Account. A Perk only appears on Home, in Your Night, or on a venue page when it is tied to the show you are actually going to.

Before the show

Dinner, stays, arrival.

Restaurant offers, reservation windows, concert hotel rates, late checkout, and parking placed where you plan them.

At the show

Venue and artist benefits.

Official venue offers, merch or concession benefits, presale and VIP access, and fan experiences when they exist.

After the show

Keep the night moving.

Late-night dining, hotel benefits, and the way home, tied to the venue and the time the encore ends.

The rules

No fake inventory. No hidden sponsors.

  • A Perk appears only when a real partner has created a real fan benefit.
  • Paid placements are always labeled Partner.
  • Concerto Picks are editorial and are never for sale. Perks and Picks never mix.
  • A Perk is tied to a show, venue, or city. It is not a feed.
  • Until a real Perk exists near your show, the space stays empty rather than filled with filler.
