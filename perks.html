Built for intent

Before. At. After.

The point is not another deals page. A Perk should make a real concert night better and give the partner a measurable action in return.

Before the show

Dinner, stays, arrival.

Restaurant offers, reservation benefits, hotel rates, late checkout, parking, or other useful pre-show value.

At the show

Venue and artist benefits.

Official venue offers, merch or concession benefits, ticket or VIP access, and fan experiences when available.

After the show

Keep the night moving.

Late-night dining, hotel benefits, transportation, and post-show experiences tied to the venue and timing.

Context, not ads

The benefit appears where the decision happens.

Perks can surface inside Your Night, venue context, nearby discovery, tour experiences, the website, and selected Concerto social campaigns. Paid relationships are labeled; editorial Concerto Picks remain separate.

The rule

No fake inventory.

Perks only appear when a real partner creates a real fan benefit. Until then, Concerto does not manufacture offers just to make the surface look busy.

In the app, the full Perks view is available from Account. Relevant Perks can also surface inside Your Night when they are tied to a saved show.

Built with partners

Something useful has to happen for all three sides.

The fan gets a meaningful benefit. The partner gets high-intent action. Concerto gets a sustainable partnership worth building around.
