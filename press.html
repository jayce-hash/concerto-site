Concerto, From the Concert to the City®

Concerto helps fans plan the concert and the night around it. The platform combines verified venue logistics, live tour and setlist information, nearby restaurants, hotels and things to do, plus Concerto+ personalized planning.

  • Founded and operated by Jayce Wells
  • Available on iPhone and the web
  • 300+ researched venue guides
  • Built for concerts and live-event nights
What makes it different

The product begins where ticketing usually ends.

Trust

Verified venue intelligence

Important venue details are researched against official sources and surfaced with verification dates when available.

Context

One night, connected

Save a show and Concerto connects the venue, weather, setlist, nearby places, transportation, and getting-home information around that exact event.

Personalization

Concerto+

The membership turns the information into a personalized plan for when to go, where to stop, what to check, and how to end the night.

Founder

Built from the fan problem outward.

Jayce Wells founded Concerto after seeing how fragmented concert-night planning remained even after tickets were purchased. He has built and operated the product, website, social channels, and venue-data operation as a solo founder, with a four-month public-relations partnership supporting media outreach.

The goal is simple: make the night around the concert feel as considered as the concert itself.
