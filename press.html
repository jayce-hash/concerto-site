Concerto, From the Concert to the City®

Concerto helps fans plan the concert and the night around it. The iPhone app and website combine verified venue logistics for 346 venues, live tour and setlist information, nearby restaurants, hotels, and things to do, and Concerto+ personalized planning. Concerto is independent from artists, venues, teams, and promoters. concertocity.com

  • Founded and operated by Jayce Wells, Dallas-Fort Worth
  • Available on iPhone; public guides on the web
  • 346 researched venue guides, 158 tour guides, 56 live setlists

What makes it different

The product begins where ticketing usually ends.

Trust

Verified venue intelligence.

Bag policy, parking, rideshare, concessions, accessibility, re-entry, ticket pickup, and gates, researched against official sources and dated.

Context

One night, connected.

Save a show and Concerto connects the venue, weather, setlist, nearby places, transportation, and getting-home information around that exact event.

Personalization

Concerto+.

The membership turns the information into a personalized plan for when to go, where to stop, what to check, and how to end the night.

Founder

Built from the fan problem outward.

Jayce Wells founded Concerto after seeing how fragmented concert-night planning remained even after tickets were purchased. The goal is simple: make the night around the concert feel as considered as the concert itself.
