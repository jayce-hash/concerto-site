The Foundation

The Essentials.

Everything you need for the perfect night out. Concerto's core suite of features is available entirely for free.

01

Venue Intel

The backbone of Concerto. We actively cover over 300 venues around the world, removing the guesswork from your arrival. No more Googling. No more cross-referencing five different pages. Everything you need, in one place, every time.

  • Bag Policy: Exactly what’s allowed and what isn’t, verified by our team.
  • Parking: Official lots, alternatives, and how to avoid the post-show trap.
  • Concessions: What’s inside and what to expect.
  • 1-Tap Rideshare: Direct links to Uber and Lyft with the venue drop-off pre-inputted.
02

Setlists & Tour Info

Know what you’re walking into before you walk in. See exactly what songs an artist has been playing on the current tour, which deep cuts they’ve been pulling out, and what the closer has been. You’re not guessing; you’re prepared.

Every track on the setlist links directly to Apple Music and Spotify so you can prep your playlist before the lights go down.

03

City Guides

For when the show takes you somewhere new. Concerto doesn’t leave you stranded with just a hotel and a venue address. Each City Guide gives you the neighborhood context.

We skip the generic Yelp results and provide curated, handpicked dining and hotel options physically anchored to your venue. Built for the show, not just the destination.

04

Events Near Me

Your starting point. Open the app and instantly see what’s happening around you tonight—concerts, comedy, tours, and festivals. No setup, no friction. Just what’s on in your city right now.
