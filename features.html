How to Use Concerto

Find a show, save it, and build your night on one page. Here’s one night, start to finish: Olivia Rodrigo at Intuit Dome.

  1. 1

    Find Your Show

    Search an artist, a venue or a city, or ask Concerto AI. Your next show sits at the top of Home.

  2. 2

    Save It

    Tap the bookmark on your date and sign in. Concerto builds Your Night for that show.

3

Build Your Night

Everything for the show, on one page. The bar at the top takes you to any part of it.

  1. Your plan at a glance

    Where you’re eating and staying, how you’re getting there and home, your seat and the show times, on one card. Tap + to add anything.

  2. Your seat

    Section, row and seat up front. At four arenas, House Map shows the nearest doors, food and restrooms.

  3. Getting there

    Directions, Uber and Lyft, and the venue’s own rideshare zone. Driving? Pick your garage.

  4. Eat, stay and do

    Restaurants, hotels and things to do near the venue. Add one and it becomes part of your plan.

  5. The venue’s rules

    The bag rule in plain words, then doors, re-entry, food inside and accessibility, from official sources.

  6. The setlist

    The songs the tour has been playing, ready to play in Apple Music.

  7. Getting home

    The pickup spot, Uber and Lyft, and your Exit Plan for the way out.

Make it yours

Add to My Night

Pick where you’re eating, where you’re staying and what else you’re doing. Each one lands in your plan with directions from the venue.
