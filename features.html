How to Use Concerto

Find a show, save it, and plan your night. Here’s one night, start to finish: Olivia Rodrigo at Intuit Dome.

  1. 1

    Find Your Show

    Search an artist, a venue or a city, or ask Concerto AI. Your next show sits at the top of Home.

  2. 2

    Save It

    Tap your show, or the bookmark on your date, and sign in. Your Night is ready for that show.

3

Fill In Your Night

Nothing to answer up front. Change anything, anytime.

  1. Laid Out When You Save

    Save a show and your night is already in order: doors, the show and the way home.

  2. Fill the Open Spots

    Getting there, dinner, your bag and your ticket. One tap each, or skip. Concerto+ fills them in for you.

  3. Your Night, in Order

    Dinner, doors, your seat, the show and the ride home. Tap any stop for its details.

Concerto+

Concerto+ Runs Your Night

Your Night is free, and you can fill it in yourself. With Concerto+, Concerto does it for you, and on show day Leave Time tells you when to leave. A Show Pass turns it on for one show, through the morning after, for $4.99. Concerto+ covers every show for $29.99 a year, with 30 Concerto AI answers a day.

  • Concerto+ Plan it for me

    Before: Concerto picks how to get there and where to eat, with a reason for each pick.

  • Concerto+ Bag Check

    At the door: take a photo of your bag and know before you leave if the venue allows it.

  • Concerto+ Exit Plan

    The end: an alert before the last song, with your way out and where to meet your ride.
