# Concerto 2.5 Universal Product Language

This file is the copy source of truth for product, web, support, press, partner, creator, investor, App Store, and social-facing descriptions.

## Brand

**Concerto**
Everything you need for the concert and the night around it.

**Registered brand promise**
From the Concert to the City®

Use the registered mark in the official lockup, first prominent brand use on corporate/marketing pages, and legal/brand contexts. Do not repeat ® in every sentence.

## Core product model

**Discover**
Find concerts, venues, tours, and events around you or in a city you are planning to visit.

**Save a Show**
Saving a show tells Concerto which night matters to the user.

**Your Night**
The complete show-specific experience opened from a saved-show countdown. It contains the useful information itself, not a menu of shortcuts: show details, verified venue essentials, weather, getting there, setlist, nearby restaurants/hotels/things to do, and getting home.

Do not call this "Show Mode." Do not introduce a second branded destination unless the product genuinely requires one.

**Near Me**
Discovery for concerts, venues, and events around the user or a selected city. Near Me is not the same as the venue-specific nearby content inside Your Night.

**Verified**
Factual information checked against an official source. When available, show the date it was checked. Facts come from data and official sources; AI may interpret facts but never invent them.

## Free Concerto

Free Concerto gives fans the trustworthy information they need to make the night work. Core venue facts, setlists, weather, getting-there guidance, nearby browsing, and discovery should not be weakened simply to create a paywall.

Preferred description:
"Free Concerto gives you everything you need to know for the show and the night around it."

## Concerto+

**Concerto+ is the personalized concierge layer.**

Preferred description:
"Concerto+ turns everything Concerto knows about your show into a personalized plan for the night."

Short version:
"Your whole night, planned around you."

Product boundary:
- Free tells the fan what is true and what is nearby.
- Concerto+ tells the fan what to do with it.

Concerto+ can include personalized planning, timing, contextual alerts, AI Bag Check, weather-aware recommendations, getting-home planning, and future partner benefits.

Do not define Concerto+ as only "three features." Individual benefits can evolve without changing what the membership means.

## Plan My Night

Plan My Night is an action inside Concerto+, not the identity of the membership.

Preferred description:
"Build a personalized timeline around your saved show, including where to go, when to leave, when to arrive, and how to get home."

## AI Bag Check

Preferred description:
"Photograph the bag you are bringing and Concerto checks it against the venue's posted bag policy before you leave."

Never claim the feature guarantees venue entry. The venue has final authority.

## Concerto Rewind

Concerto Rewind is the history/year-in-review identity layer built from attended shows. Do not call it Passport.

## Partner language

Concerto partners should create genuine value for fans around a real show, not generic ad inventory.

Preferred framing:
"Reach fans who already know where they will be and when they will be there."

Examples include pre-show offers, ticket-holder perks, late-night offers, hotel packages, upgrades, giveaways, and useful venue-specific experiences.

Always label paid/featured partner placements clearly.

## Creator language

Use **Creators** or **Collaborate with Concerto**, not "influencers" as the primary public label.

Preferred framing:
"Create something fans can actually use."

Examples include city picks, venue guides, concert-night recommendations, giveaways, social collaborations, and Rewind campaigns.

## Company language

Concerto is a consumer platform at the intersection of live events, local discovery, travel, and fan experience. Avoid presenting it as only a concert utility or only a ticket-adjacent app.

Founder story may be used as proof of execution, but the company must not read as a personal side project.

## Platform law

Concerto should look like the same company everywhere, but not force every platform to look identical.

Same company: brand, vocabulary, trust treatment, account state, saved shows, entitlement, facts.
Best platform: native iOS capabilities on iPhone, desktop/editorial breadth on web, discovery/SEO on public pages.
