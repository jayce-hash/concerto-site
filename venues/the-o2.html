Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

The O2 enforces a bag policy for safety, allowing only A4 size bags or smaller into the arena. Large bags must be stored at a facility outside the main entrance.

Allowed
  • A4 size bags
  • standard handbags
Not Allowed
  • backpacks
  • travel cases
  • laptop bags
  • camera bags
  • large tote bags
Extra Notes

American Express Cardholders receive complimentary bag drop.

Inside the Venue

Concessions

The O2 Arena offers a diverse range of food and drink options, from burgers and pizzas to burritos and stir fry. Special highlights include the Lanson Champagne Bar and the Mixology Bar with unique cocktails. Exclusive areas like Sky Backstage and O2 Blueroom provide additional experiences for Sky and O2 customers.
