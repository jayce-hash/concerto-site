Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

Starlight Theatre enforces a clear bag policy to enhance safety and expedite entry. Only clear bags up to 12″ x 6″ x 12″, one-gallon clear zip-seal bags, and small clutches or fanny packs up to 4.5″ x 6.5″ are allowed.

Allowed
  • Clear bags up to 12″ x 6″ x 12″
  • One gallon clear zip-seal bags
  • Small clutch bags or fanny packs up to 4.5″ x 6.5″
Not Allowed
  • Tinted or colored bags
  • Bags exceeding size limits
Extra Notes

Exceptions are made for medically necessary items and diaper bags after inspection.

Getting There

Parking

General parking at Starlight is free and available on a first-come, first-served basis. Premium parking costs $35 and must be purchased in advance. Season ticket holders receive complimentary premium parking.

Inside the Venue

Concessions

Starlight Theatre offers a variety of food and drink options, including BBQ plates, burgers, chicken sandwiches, and vegetarian options like the Beyond Burger. Highlights include the Star Bar with frozen margaritas and daiquiris, and the new Brick House Coffee & Creamery for cold treats.

Notable Stands
  • Star Bar
  • Luna & Co. Pizzeria
  • Brick House Coffee & Creamery
