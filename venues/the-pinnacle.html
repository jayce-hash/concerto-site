Bag Policy

Small purses and fanny packs up to 12” x 12” x 6” are allowed. Backpacks of any kind or size are prohibited.

Getting There

Parking

There is ample paid parking available at Nashville Yards in multiple garages, including Nashville Yards, Pinnacle Tower, and Amazon Towers. The garages are gateless for easy entry/exit, and event parking rates vary by event.

Key Lots
  • Nashville Yards
  • Pinnacle Tower
  • Amazon Towers
