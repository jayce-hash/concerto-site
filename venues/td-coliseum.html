Getting There

Parking

There is no on-site parking at TD Coliseum, but upgraded parking can be purchased for the York Boulevard Parkade through the TD Coliseum Mobile App. Several nearby lots and garages are within walking distance, and arriving early is recommended.

Key Lots
  • York Boulevard Parkade
  • Bay Street Parkade
  • Hunter Street Parkade
  • Jackson Square Garage

Inside the Venue

Concessions

TD Coliseum offers a variety of food and drink options ranging from quick-service concessions to luxury dining experiences. Highlights include local favorites and arena classics, premium dining with bespoke bars, and themed lounges like the TD Lounge and Vinyl Room. Special offerings feature Ontario cheese boards, Hamilton Brewery drafts, and Niagara wines.

Notable Stands
  • TD Lounge
  • Vinyl Room
  • Event Level Club
  • Rogers Club
  • House of Peroni
  • Iron Cow Public House
  • Sugar and Salt
