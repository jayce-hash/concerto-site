Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

Travel light as oversized bags are discouraged. Approved bags include small personal bags and clear bags.

Allowed
  • 6”x8”x2” or smaller bags
  • clear bags up to 12”x12”x6”
  • one (1) gallon plastic resealable storage bag

Getting There

Parking

Street parking is limited along Mount Royal Avenue and nearby streets. The preferred parking option is the Fitzgerald Garage, located at 80 West Oliver Street, which offers ample parking without the need for prepayment or reservation.

Key Lots
  • Fitzgerald Garage

Inside the Venue

Concessions

The Lyric in Baltimore offers a variety of standard concession items including fresh made popcorn, soft pretzels, assorted theater box candy, and a selection of beverages such as soda, bottled water, beer, wine, seltzers, and spirits. All food is allowed inside the theater, and bars are located on the first and second floors.
