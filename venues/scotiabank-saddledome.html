Venue Info

Scotiabank Saddledome does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Policies may change or vary by event. Review the linked official guidance shortly before arrival.

Parking is limited due to construction, and fans without parking permits are encouraged to use public transit or off-site parking. Parking is managed by Calgary Parking and can be paid via the MyParking app or on-site pay machines.

The Scotiabank Saddledome offers a diverse range of food and beverage options, including classic concessions like popcorn and hot dogs, specialty items such as the Shorty short rib sandwich, and dietary options like vegan meals. Notable highlights include the Fireball Whisky Bar, Tacos and Tequila, and the Coca-Cola Test Kitchen with unique monthly culinary concepts.
