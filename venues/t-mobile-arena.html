Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

T-Mobile Arena prohibits bags or backpacks except for small personal bags measuring up to 9″ x 5″ x 2″. Fanny packs, crossbody bags, and clear stadium bags are allowed if they meet size requirements.

Allowed
  • small personal bags (9″ x 5″ x 2″ max)
  • fanny packs
  • crossbody bags
  • clear stadium bags
Not Allowed
  • bags larger than 9″ x 5″ x 2″
  • backpacks

Accessibility

ADA & Accessible Services

Fully ADA accessible. Accessible seating throughout. Elevators, ramps, accessible restrooms on every level. ADA drop-off at East Suite Entrance via NY-NY parking garage.

Entry & Exit

Re-Entry Policy

Strict no re-entry for all events.

Ticket Pickup

Will Call

Contact: 702-692-1414 for details.

Getting In

Gates & Entrances

East and West entrances. ADA drop-off at East Suite Entrance. Rideshare pickup post-event at New York-New York or Park MGM.
