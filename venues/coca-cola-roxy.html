Venue Info

Bags must not exceed 12” x 6” x 12”. Small clutch bags up to 6"x9" are allowed. Backpacks and multi-compartment bags are prohibited.

Allowed
  • Bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Small clutch bags up to 6"x9"
Not allowed
  • Backpacks
  • Multi-compartment bags
  • Laptop cases

There are various parking decks available around The Battery, with the Orange and Delta decks being the closest to the venue. On Braves game days, parking availability may vary.

  • Orange deck
  • Delta deck

Coca-Cola Roxy offers a variety of late-night bites including classic options like fries, hot dogs, and grilled cheese, as well as specialty items such as a Smash Burger and Crispy Chicken Po'Boy. The EP Lounge features exclusive dishes like Braised Short Rib Hoagie and Jack and Coke Glazed Pork Belly. The kitchen closes at 9:00 PM.
