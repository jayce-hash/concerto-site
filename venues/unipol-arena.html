Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

Backpacks larger than an A4 sheet are prohibited and must be checked at the cloakroom. Small bags like fanny packs, purses, and clutches are allowed.

Getting There

Parking

Unipol Arena offers parking for 5000 cars and has reserved spaces for disabled people. Accredited disabled users can access free parking via a separate entrance.

View Official Parking Guide →
Inside the Venue

Concessions

Food and drinks are heavily restricted inside the arena. Only plastic bottles up to 0.5 liters without caps are allowed, and bringing in any kind of food is prohibited unless exceptions are made by contacting the venue.
