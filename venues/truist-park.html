Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

Truist Park enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Only specific types and sizes of bags are allowed.

Allowed
  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag 1 Gallon
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets up to 6.5” x 4.5”
  • Seat cushions without pockets up to 18” wide
Not Allowed
  • Backpacks
  • Bags and wallets exceeding 6.5" x 4.5"
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags
  • Briefcases
  • Fanny packs
  • Cinch bags
Extra Notes

Diaper bags and medical bags are exceptions.

Getting There

Parking

Truist Park sits in The Battery Atlanta in Cobb County with color-coded decks and lots (Green, Gold, Orange, Red) around the complex, sold prepaid by event. Buying in advance is strongly recommended as drive-up is limited and pricey; there is no rail service, so plan for traffic.

Key Lots
  • Green Deck
  • Gold Deck
  • Orange Deck
  • Red Deck

Inside the Venue

Concessions

Truist Park offers a variety of food and beverage options, including classic comfort foods with bold flavors and modern twists. Highlights include the Beer Garden for local brews, Bridges Grille's signature Garlic Fries, and Cadillac Diner's indulgent Chili Cheese Hot Dog. New offerings include a Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry.

Notable Stands
  • Beer Garden
  • Bridges Grille
  • Cadillac Diner
  • Coop & Kennel Pub
  • Golden Spike Chophouse
