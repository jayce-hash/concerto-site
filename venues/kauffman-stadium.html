Know Before You Go

Venue Info

Kauffman Stadium enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Allowed
  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag (1 Gallon)
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”
  • Seat cushions without pockets, zippers or concealable areas up to 18” wide
Not allowed
  • Backpacks
  • Bags and wallets exceeding 6.5" x 4.5"
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage, computer bags, camera bags, briefcases, fanny packs, cinch bags

Medical bags and diaper bags are exceptions.

View full policy →

Kauffman Stadium is part of the Truman Sports Complex east of Kansas City, with large on-site surface lots accessed off I-70. General parking is sold prepaid or at the gate (cashless) and prices rise for premium games. Tailgating is permitted in designated areas.

  • General Lots
  • Premium/Preferred Lots

Kauffman Stadium offers a variety of food and beverage options including classic comfort foods with a modern twist, elevated ballpark classics, and hearty eats. Highlights include specialty items like Bacon Wrapped Hot Dogs, Pastrami Fry, and a variety of local and popular beers.
