Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

TD Garden discourages bringing bags to events to ensure safety and quick entry. Small bags, clutches, crossbodies, and wristlets are allowed if they do not exceed 4in x 6in x 1.5in. Oversized bags can be stored on-site for a fee.

Allowed
  • Small bags
  • Clutches
  • Crossbodies
  • Wristlets
Extra Notes

Exceptions are made for diaper bags, breast pump bags, and bags needed for medical purposes, which are subject to visual or x-ray screening.

Getting There

Parking

The North Station Garage is conveniently located directly underneath TD Garden and is open daily from 5:00 AM to 1:00 AM. Visitors can also book parking with SpotHero for additional options.

Inside the Venue

Concessions

TD Garden offers a diverse range of food and beverage options including pizza, burgers, vegan and gluten-free choices, sweets, and cocktails. Highlights include a $5 value menu and arena-exclusive snacks. Various bars and markets provide craft beers, cocktails, and family-friendly options.

Accessibility

ADA & Accessible Services

Fully ADA accessible. Wheelchair seating on all levels. Assistive listening devices, request 14 days prior. ADA line: 617-624-1750 or accessibleneeds@tdgarden.com.

Entry & Exit

Re-Entry Policy

No re-entry under any circumstances.

Ticket Pickup

Will Call

Box Office on Level 1, east side of North Station. Opens 2 hrs before event. Photo ID + credit card used at purchase required.

Getting In

Gates & Entrances

5 general admission gates. Doors open 60–90 mins before showtime.
