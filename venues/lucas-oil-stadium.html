Know Before You Go

Venue Info

All bags brought into Lucas Oil Stadium must comply with the NFL's clear bag policy. Bags must be clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC and not exceed 12"x 6"x 12".

Allowed
  • clear plastic bags
  • vinyl bags
  • PVC bags
  • bags not exceeding 12"x 6"x 12"
Not allowed
  • outside food & beverages

Medical exceptions are permitted, and bags containing medical devices and related supplies, including breast pumps, are not subject to the clear bag policy but will be screened.

View full policy →

Accessible parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis. Additional parking options and pricing details are not specified in the provided content.

Official parking guide →

Lucas Oil Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, ranging from classic football favorites to new culinary experiences. All concession stands are accessible and accept major credit cards. Sodexo Live! is the exclusive food and beverage provider.

Accessibility services and seating arrangements at Lucas Oil Stadium may vary by event. Contact the venue before purchasing tickets if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Lucas Oil Stadium does not publish one re-entry rule that applies to every event. Confirm with venue staff before exiting because re-entry may be prohibited.

Ticket delivery, box-office hours, and will-call procedures can vary by event and ticketing partner. Review the instructions attached to your official order and contact the venue before event day if pickup is required.

Entrance assignments and door times at Lucas Oil Stadium vary by event. Check the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.
