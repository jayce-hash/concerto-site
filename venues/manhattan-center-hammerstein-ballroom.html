Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

All bags are subject to search, and backpacks of any size are prohibited. Bags over 22″ by 14″ by 9″ are not allowed, with see-through/transparent bags recommended.

Allowed
  • See through/transparent bags
Not Allowed
  • Backpacks of any size
  • Bags over 22″ by 14″ by 9″
  • Outside food and beverages
  • Weapons
  • Professional photography equipment
  • Noisemaking devices
  • Drones
  • Skateboards
  • Aerosol cans
  • Tools
  • Toys and/or projectiles
  • Cans, bottles, glass or any other beverage containers
  • Chains, studded bracelets or oversized studded rings
  • Large and non-folding umbrellas
  • Illegal drugs or paraphernalia
Extra Notes

Prescription drugs must be in the original labeled container with conforming government ID (respiratory inhalers excepted).
