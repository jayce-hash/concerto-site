Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

Backpacks are not allowed in the facility. All bags will be checked upon entry.

Not Allowed
  • backpacks
  • pocket knives
  • weapons of any kind
  • laser pointers
  • outside food or beverage
  • coolers
  • tablet devices larger than 7 inches
  • spurs
  • artificial noisemakers

Getting There

Parking

The T-Mobile Center offers convenient on-site parking in the Magenta Lot, available for advance purchase on a first-come, first-served basis. Additional parking is available at the Red Lot Parking Garage and the Yellow Lot Parking Garage. Parking links for concerts can be found on individual event pages.

Key Lots
  • Magenta Lot
  • Red Lot Parking Garage
  • Yellow Lot Parking Garage
