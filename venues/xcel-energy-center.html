Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

For safety and speed of entry, bags are discouraged at events. Purses up to 12” x 12” x 6” are allowed at designated X-ray entry points. Wristlets, clutches, or wallets 4” x 6” x 1.5” or smaller are permitted at all entrances.

Allowed
  • Purses no larger than 12” x 12” x 6”
  • Wristlet, clutch or wallets 4” x 6” x 1.5” or smaller
Not Allowed
  • Backpacks
  • Coolers
  • Large totes
Extra Notes

Medical and parenting bags are permitted and subject to X-ray screening.

Getting There

Parking

Parking is available at RiverCentre and Kellogg Parking Ramps, both connected to the arena. Event day parking is first come, first served, and ParkWhiz reservations are not available for these ramps.

Key Lots
  • RiverCentre Parking Ramp
  • Kellogg Parking Ramp

Inside the Venue

Concessions

Xcel Energy Center offers a variety of food and drink options including traditional sausages, craft beers, pizza, burgers, and more. Special offerings include the Wild $6 Happy Hour and the Wild Market with Amazon’s Just Walk Out technology. Alcohol sales end during the third period of Minnesota Wild games or one hour before the end of other events.
