Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

All bags over 12″x12″x6″ are prohibited and subject to search upon entry. Guests can refuse a bag search, but the venue may deny entry.

Allowed
  • Bags up to 12″x12″x6″
Not Allowed
  • Bags larger than 12”x6”x12”
  • Weapons of Any Kind
  • Outside Alcohol
  • Illegal Drugs/Substances
  • Glass Containers
  • Cans
  • Food or drinks (except items on allowed list)
  • Hard sided/large coolers
  • Large or oversized bags/back packs
  • Laser pointers
  • Animals (except service animals)
  • Fireworks
  • Selfie Sticks
  • Signs larger than 8.5×11 inches
  • Professional Cameras
  • Wallet Chains
  • Laptops/Computers/iPads
  • Ski Masks
Extra Notes

Oversized items can be checked at Coat Check.

Getting There

Parking

Limited parking spaces are available on a first-come, first-served basis in designated lots. Lot A opens 1 hour before doors, Lot C and Lot D open at 6:00 PM on weeknights and 4:30 PM on weekends. Valet parking is also available and opens one hour before doors.

Key Lots
  • LOT A
  • LOT C
  • LOT D
  • VALET

Inside the Venue

Concessions

The venue prohibits outside food or drinks, except for water in a factory sealed or empty bottle. Concession stands are available on all accessible seating levels, offering a variety of food and drink options.
