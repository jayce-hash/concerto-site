Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

Bags no larger than 5” x 9” are permitted, along with diaper and medical bags, which are subject to search upon entry. Strollers are not allowed inside Target Center.

Allowed
  • bags no larger than 5” x 9”
  • diaper bags
  • medical bags
Not Allowed
  • strollers
Extra Notes

Diaper bags and medical bags are allowed but subject to search.

Getting There

Parking

Parking is available at Ramps A, B, and C, with reservations recommended through SpotHero for discounts up to 50% off. Be aware of ongoing construction affecting I-94 and I-394.

Inside the Venue

Concessions

Target Center offers a diverse range of food options, including new stands and traditional favorites. Highlights include chef-driven dishes, local ingredients, and unique offerings like the French Onion Dog, S’more Nachos, and a variety of international and comfort foods.
