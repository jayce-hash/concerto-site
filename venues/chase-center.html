Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

Chase Center has a strict bag policy to ensure the safety and enjoyment of all guests. Bags must be clear and no larger than 14" x 14" x 6".

Allowed
  • Clear bags no larger than 14" x 14" x 6"
Not Allowed
  • Backpacks
  • Purses larger than a clutch
  • Non-clear bags
Extra Notes

Exceptions are made for medical bags and diaper bags, which will be subject to inspection.

Parking

Chase Center offers multiple parking options, including official lots and nearby garages. Pricing varies depending on the event and location, and it is recommended to book in advance. Public transportation and rideshare services are also encouraged due to limited parking availability.

Accessibility

ADA & Accessible Services

ADA drop-off: 16th St & Terry Francois Blvd, East Entrance. Wheelchairs from Thrive City or Main Lobby to seats. ADA parking in Mercedes-Benz & Warriors Way Garages, pre-purchase required. Contact: guestexperiences@warriors.com.

Entry & Exit

Re-Entry Policy

Re-entry policy varies by event, check your event page.

Ticket Pickup

Will Call

West Entrance Box Office. Mon–Fri 10am–4pm; event days only on weekends. No printed tickets, mobile only.

Getting In

Gates & Entrances

Portal system, ticket shows your portal. Use any entrance to reach your portal. ADA entry at East Entrance.
