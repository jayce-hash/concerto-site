Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

FedExForum enforces a clear bag policy, allowing only specific clear bags and wallets inside the venue. Bags must be clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC and no larger than 6x8x2 inches.

Allowed
  • Clear bags made of plastic, vinyl or PVC no larger than 6x8x2 inches
  • One quart or smaller clear plastic storage/freezer bag
  • Wallets no larger than 5x3.5 inches
Not Allowed
  • Bags with all-over print or decoration
  • Metal or chain straps
Extra Notes

Exceptions are made for medically necessary items after inspection.

Getting There

Parking

FedExForum offers parking in the Gossett Motors Parking Garage with 1,500 spaces, available on a season basis for basketball games and first-come, first-served for other events. Rates vary by event type, and accessible parking is available with appropriate tags.
