Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

All bags entering Simmons Bank Arena will be inspected, and bags larger than 14" x 14" x 6" are not allowed. Backpacks, large bags, suitcases, and luggage are prohibited.

Not Allowed
  • Bags larger than 14" x 14" x 6"
  • Backpacks
  • Large bags
  • Suitcases
  • Luggage

Inside the Venue

Concessions

Alcoholic beverages can be purchased inside the arena, with a limit of two drinks per person at a time. No outside food or beverages are allowed.
