Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

Frost Bank Center allows bags 12" x 12" x 6" or smaller, with magnetometer screening at entry. Bag policies may vary by event.

Allowed
  • Bags 12" x 12" x 6" or smaller
Not Allowed
  • Backpacks
  • Oversized totes or purses
  • Duffel bags
  • Luggage
  • Camera bags
  • Laptop bags
  • Drawstring bags that exceed size limits
Extra Notes

Diaper bags and medical bags are allowed and will be screened and tagged by Security.

Inside the Venue

Concessions

Frost Bank Center offers a variety of food and drink options, including casual dining at The Agave Club and upscale public concessions at the Frost Club. Highlights include creative shareable appetizers at the ULTRA Club, brisket tacos at Davila's BBQ, and smashburgers at Roadhouse. The venue is cashless and features a wide selection of beverages, including premium cocktails and souvenir draft beers.
