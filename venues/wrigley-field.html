Getting There

Parking

Wrigley Field sits in a dense residential neighborhood with very limited parking; most streets are permit-only. The Cubs offer a prepaid remote lot at Gallagher Way/DeVry with a shuttle, plus scattered private lots and garages that fill fast. The CTA Red Line Addison stop is the easiest way in.

Key Lots
  • Remote lot (Gallagher Way / DeVry)
  • Private neighborhood lots

Inside the Venue

Concessions

Wrigley Field offers a variety of food and beverage options including classic comfort foods with modern twists, such as Chicken Tenders & Fries, Cheeseburgers, and specialty hot dogs. Highlights include the Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry. Fans can also enjoy a selection of local and popular beers at the Beer Garden.

Notable Stands
  • Beer Garden
  • Bridges Grille
  • Cadillac Diner
  • Coop & Kennel Pub
  • Golden Spike Chophouse
