Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

Soldier Field enforces the NFL Clear Bag Policy, allowing clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small non-clear bags up to 4.5” x 6.5”. All other bags are prohibited.

Allowed
  • Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags
  • Small non-clear bags up to 4.5” x 6.5”
Not Allowed
  • Coolers
  • Briefcases
  • Diaper bags
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage
  • Computer bags
  • Any non-clear bag larger than 4.5” x 6.5”
Extra Notes

Medical bags are allowed and will be searched. Diapers must be in a clear bag. Each family member can carry an approved clear bag and a clutch purse.

Getting There

Parking

Parking is available at several lots including North Garage, Waldron Deck, and South Lot. Rates vary by event, and parking can be reserved in advance. Free shuttles are available from McCormick Place Lot B on Bears game days.

Key Lots
  • North Garage
  • Waldron Deck
  • South Lot
  • Adler Lot
  • East Museum Lot

Concessions
