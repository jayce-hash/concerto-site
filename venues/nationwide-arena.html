Know Before You Go

Venue Info

Nationwide Arena enforces a no bag policy, allowing only specific exceptions. All bags are subject to x-ray screening.

Allowed
  • medical bags
  • diaper bags
  • clutches no larger than 8”x 5”x 1”

Medical bags and diaper bags are exceptions to the no bag policy.

View full policy →

There are over 15,000 parking spaces within a 10-minute walk of Nationwide Arena, with prices varying by location. Parking can be purchased in advance or on the event day. Accessible parking is available but may be limited during heavily attended events.

  • McConnell garage
  • Front Street garage
Official parking guide →

The Nationwide Arena offers a variety of food and drink options, with alcohol sales strictly regulated according to Ohio law. Delaware North Sportservice is the primary vendor, ensuring compliance with ID checks for alcoholic beverages.

Concessions guide →

ALL pick-ups and drop-offs take place on West Street. Vehicles MUST be attended at all times.

Accessibility services and seating arrangements at Nationwide Arena may vary by event. Contact the venue before purchasing tickets if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Nationwide Arena does not publish one re-entry rule that applies to every event. Confirm with venue staff before exiting because re-entry may be prohibited.

Ticket delivery, box-office hours, and will-call procedures can vary by event and ticketing partner. Review the instructions attached to your official order and contact the venue before event day if pickup is required.

Entrance assignments and door times at Nationwide Arena vary by event. Check the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.
