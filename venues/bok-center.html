Venue Info

Fans can bring one clear bag per person not exceeding 12″ x 6″ x 12″ or a one-gallon clear plastic bag. Small purses or clutches no larger than 5″ x 8″ are also allowed.

Parking is available for purchase prior to events through ParkWhiz. Consider arriving early and using sustainable transportation options like the city bus or carpooling.

BOK Center offers a variety of food and drink options, including fast and casual chicken from Big Chicken, traditional arena fare, and unique offerings like boozy slushies from Slush Rush. The venue is cashless, accepting only debit/credit cards for payment.

Accessibility services and seating arrangements at BOK Center may vary by event. Contact the venue before purchasing tickets if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

BOK Center does not publish one re-entry rule that applies to every event. Confirm with venue staff before exiting because re-entry may be prohibited.

Ticket delivery, box-office hours, and will-call procedures can vary by event and ticketing partner. Review the instructions attached to your official order and contact the venue before event day if pickup is required.

Entrance assignments and door times at BOK Center vary by event. Check the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.
