Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

Guests attending public ticketed events at UW – Panther Arena are allowed to bring a clear stadium bag up to 12″ x 6″ x 12″, a gallon-sized clear zip bag, a wristlet up to 4″x6″x1″, or a diaper or medical bag. Bags will be searched upon entry.

Allowed
  • 12″ x 6″ x 12″ clear stadium bag
  • gallon-sized clear zip bag
  • 4″x6″x1″ wristlet
  • diaper bag
  • medical bag
Extra Notes

Diaper bags and medical bags are exceptions and will be searched.

Inside the Venue

Concessions

UW-Milwaukee Panther Arena offers a diverse range of food and beverage options through its partner, Levy Restaurants. The menu includes quality concessions, backstage catering, and elegant banquet options, with dietary accommodations noted for gluten-free, nut, seafood, vegan, and vegetarian preferences.
