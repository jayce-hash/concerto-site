Getting There

Parking

There is no public parking available at or around Tottenham Hotspur Stadium. Visitors are advised not to drive to events due to parking restrictions and the operation of tow trucks. Cycling is encouraged with ample cycle parking available.

Inside the Venue

Concessions

Tottenham Hotspur Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, including hot food, salads, barista coffee, and bakery goods at the M Café, which is open daily except on event days. Special event menus and exclusive items like loaded nachos and doughnuts are available during specific events.
