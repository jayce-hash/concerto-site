Getting There

Parking

Parking is available at Thunderbird Parkade, the closest to the venue, and Health Sciences Parkade, which offers quicker exit times. Event rates may vary, and payment can be made via the HonkMobile app on the day of the event.

Inside the Venue

Concessions

The Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre offers a variety of food choices, including healthy options, at its concession stands. Beverages are also available for every event, ensuring guests can quickly return to their seats.
