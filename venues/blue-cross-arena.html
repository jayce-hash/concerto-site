Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

The Blue Cross Arena will implement a Clear Bag Policy effective January 5, 2026, allowing only clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags no larger than 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutch purses. Metal detector screening will be conducted at entrances.

Allowed
  • clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags no larger than 12” x 6” x 12”
  • one-gallon clear plastic freezer bag
  • small clutch purse no larger than 4.5" x 6.5"
Not Allowed
  • metal or hard plastic beverage containers
  • laptops
  • iPads
  • tablets
  • similar electronic devices
Extra Notes

Diaper bags are allowed if the child is present. Exceptions for credentialed staff, approved media, and medically necessary items after inspection.
