Know Before You Go

Bag Policy

All bags, purses, or personal items are subject to search and must be within the size limit of 13” x 15” x 6”. Exceptions are made for diaper and medical bags, which may exceed the size limit but will be searched.

Allowed
  • Bags within 13” x 15” x 6”
Not Allowed
  • Over-sized bags/backpacks/duffel bags
Extra Notes

Diaper and medical bags may exceed the size limit but will be searched.
