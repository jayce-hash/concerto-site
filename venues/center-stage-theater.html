Getting There

Parking

Parking is available at the Center Stage Garage for $10-$25 and the Midtown Plaza Deck for $17.50. Both accept credit, debit, and ParkMobile, but no cash. Limited complimentary parking is available for box office visits.

Inside the Venue

Concessions

Nearby dining options include Aveline Bar & Lounge, Hartley Kitchen & Cocktails, and Nan Thai Fine Dining, offering a variety of cuisines and valet parking services. Patrons spending $50 at Hartley or Aveline receive complimentary valet parking.
