Parking

Self parking is complimentary for all guests and visitors at Circus Circus Las Vegas. Oversized vehicles can park in the RV area for an additional $20.00 per day, subject to availability.

Inside the Venue

Concessions

The venue offers a variety of quick-serve food and drink options, including gourmet coffees and pastries at Starbucks, fresh doughnuts at Krispy Kreme, and Italian gelato at Cocolini. Guests can also enjoy classic fast-food offerings from Popeyes and Dairy Queen, as well as Asian cuisine from Pick Up Stix.
