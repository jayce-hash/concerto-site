Concerto+

More of Your Night.

Concerto+ adds the parts of the night that take the most planning: the way out, your bag, and the evening around the show. It lives inside Your Night, marked with a gold +, right where you need it.

What is included

Five things that make the night easier.

  1. 01

    Exit Plan

    The fastest way out of the venue and the best place to get picked up, so the night doesn’t end in a crowd at the curb.

  2. 02

    AI Bag Check

    Take a photo of your bag and Concerto checks it against the venue’s published policy. It shows the rule it used, and the venue still makes the final call.

  3. 03

    Plan My Night

    Dinner nearby, when to leave, and the show, planned around your seat and how you’re getting there.

  4. 04

    More Concerto AI

    30 answers a day instead of 3, for every question about your show.

  5. 05

    More alerts

    Before You Go and Heading Out on show night, plus onsale alerts for the artists you follow. The show-day alert and the memory card reminder stay free.

Where it lives

Inside Your Night,
not another screen.

Each Concerto+ feature sits in the tab where you need it. Exit Plan is in After. AI Bag Check is in Arrive. Plan My Night is in Before.

Before you join, each one shows as a gold row that tells you what it does. Once you’re a member, the same row shows your answer.
