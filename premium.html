Concerto Premium.

Take the friction out of the live music experience. Premium features built for the people who treat going to a show as an event.

Bag Check

Don't just read the policy—test it. Instead of guessing if your bag meets the venue's rules, hold it up to your camera. Concerto’s AI cross-references your bag's dimensions and structure against the specific venue's database.

Get an instant Pass, Warn, or Fail verdict, complete with the exact policy rules cited. No surprises at the door.

✨

Live Mode

Once you arrive at the venue, Concerto switches into a real-time companion. We provide an editorial onboarding that sets the tone for the night.

When the encore is over, you receive a shareable Story Card capturing your night. It's a beautifully designed, full-screen canvas documenting the show—think of it as a Strava post for your concert.

🗺️

Concerto+

You tell us the show, the city, and your vibe. Concerto+ builds the rest. We generate a full night-out timeline, including dinner reservation timing, precise arrival windows, and where to grab drinks after the show.

These aren't generic suggestions. It's an actual, mathematical plan tailored to the venue, the artist, and your unique style.

🎪

Festivals

Festivals aren’t just shows—they’re multi-day logistical puzzles. Concerto Premium unlocks everything you need to navigate them seamlessly.

Access full lineups, schedules, set times, stage maps, and survival intel that makes the difference between a chaotic weekend and an unforgettable one.
