Questions

Before you subscribe.

Do I need Concerto+ to use the app?

No. Venue guides, tours, setlists, Near Me, saved shows, countdowns, and nearby places are free. Concerto+ adds the personalized plan.

How is Concerto+ billed?

Through your Apple ID at $7.99 a month or $69.99 a year. Cancel any time in iPhone Settings. Access continues to the end of the period.

Does Concerto+ guarantee entry or timing?

No. The plan is built from published venue information and live conditions, and the venue always makes the final call at the door. Concerto+ helps you prepare; it does not replace the venue's rules.
