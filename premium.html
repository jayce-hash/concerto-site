Concerto Premium

The night, without the friction.

Everything in Concerto is free. Premium removes the last three problems standing between you and the show.

AI Bag Check

Photograph the bag you are planning to bring. Concerto estimates its dimensions and structure, checks it against that venue’s own posted policy, and gives you a verdict before you leave the house — so the security line is a walk‑through, not a turn‑around.

Concerto+

Concerto+ is the AI night planner. Tell it your show, your vibe and your pace, and it builds the whole evening around doors time: where to eat, when to leave, where to park, and the quiet corner to get your ride home from.

Passport

Passport is your live music history, collected. Every show you attend earns that venue’s stamp, and the collection grows with every night out — a record of where you have been and who you saw.

What it costs

$7.99 per month, or $69.99 per year. Subscriptions run through your Apple ID and renew until cancelled; you can cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings. Premium unlocks on every device you sign in with.

What stays free

The core of Concerto costs nothing and always will:

  • Verified bag policies, parking and rideshare pickups for 300+ venues around the world
  • Events near you, night by night
  • Tour guides and setlists for 70+ active tours
  • Saved shows, countdowns and doors alerts

Premium lives in the app — Bag Check needs your camera, and the planner and Passport are built for the hours you are out.
