Your Night.

Your Night is free, and you can fill it in yourself. With Concerto+, Concerto does it for you: it plans the evening, tells you when to leave for it, checks your bag against the venue’s rule, and gets you out at the end. A Show Pass turns it on for one show.

Free or Concerto+

Your Night is the same page either way.

  1. 01

    Free

    You fill in your night: how you’re getting there, dinner, your bag and your ticket. Concerto lays it out in order, alerts you on show day, and tells you if the show changes.

  2. 02

    Concerto+

    Concerto picks how you get there and where you eat, with a reason for each pick. On show day it tells you when to leave, from how long the trip is right then. It checks your bag from a photo, and alerts you before the last song with your way out.

What is included

Concerto handles four moments of the night.

  1. 01

    Before the show: Plan it for me

    Concerto picks how to get there and where to eat near the venue, gives a reason for each pick, and lays out the night in order around the venue’s doors. Swap any pick.

  2. 02

    Leaving: Leave Time

    On show day, Concerto checks how long the trip is from where you are to your dinner stop or the venue, and tells you when to leave. It checks again while Your Night is open, and alerts you ten minutes before. It needs your location, which is used for the trip and not saved.

  3. 03

    At the door: Bag Check

    Take a photo of your bag and Concerto checks it against the venue’s published policy. It shows the rule it used, and the venue still makes the final call.

  4. 04

    The end of the night: Exit Plan

    An alert about ten minutes before the last song, with your way out and where to meet your ride or find your car. Where a venue publishes its pickup spot, exits and lots, Concerto uses them.

  5. 05

    Anytime: More Concerto AI

    30 answers a day instead of 3, for every question about your show.

  6. 06

    Anytime: More alerts

    New dates and presales for the artists you follow. The show-day alert, Time to Head Out, the Reprise reminder, and This Weekend stay free.
