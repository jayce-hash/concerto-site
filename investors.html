From the founder

Where help goes furthest

Four things, named plainly.

  1. 01

    Getting in front of fans.

    Concerto is found by people who already have a ticket. Growth experience in consumer apps is what we would use fastest.

  2. 02

    The rooms.

    Introductions to venues, promoters, and the teams who run buildings. That is how a guide becomes a partnership.

  3. 03

    The brands around the night.

    Restaurants, hotels, and rideshare are already part of a concert night. We want the people who know how those deals get done.

  4. 04

    Operating in live music.

    Anyone who has run a tour, a venue, or a ticketing product sees problems a fan-side app cannot.

Concerto is founder-led, and the product is built. These are the areas where another set of hands or one good introduction moves the most.
