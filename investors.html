The concert creates intent. Concerto connects what comes next.

A fan with a saved show is about to decide on transportation, food, lodging, timing, and what to do around a specific venue on a specific date. Concerto is built to meet that intent before it fragments across a dozen unrelated products.

The thesis

The ticket is only the beginning of the spend.

Ticketing ends the moment the purchase clears. Everything after it, the venue rules, the parking, the dinner, the hotel, the setlist, the way home, is still scattered across venue sites, maps, review apps, and group chats. Concerto is the product for that gap.

Trust

Verified venue intelligence.

346 structured venue guides, eight verified sections each, researched from official sources and dated. The reason Concerto gets opened when the stakes of the night become real.

Intent

Saved-show context.

Once a show is saved, the product knows the venue, city, date, and artist the fan is planning around. That context is the targeting layer.

Monetization

Subscription plus partners.

Concerto+ monetizes orchestration at $7.99 a month. Partner campaigns monetize useful local and event-driven value without turning the product into a banner network.

Demand signals

Attention already exists. The next stage is capture.

Concerto has demonstrated discovery through social and search before spending on either. These are reach and attention metrics, not users or revenue, and they are the reason the product is worth building distribution on.

What is built

A real product, a shared data engine, and two presentation systems.

  • Native iOS app: saved shows, Your Night, venue intelligence, Near Me, Concerto+, AI Bag Check, Perks, alerts

  • 346 venue guides, 158 tour guides, 56 live setlists, 101 tours tracked for setlists
  • Concerto+ subscription live through the App Store
  • Concerto Partners program with restaurant, hotel, venue, and artist tracks
  • Independent from artists, venues, teams, and promoters
