01 · The problem

The concert starts long before the first song.

Concerto began with the questions that hit every fan before a show. Will my bag get me turned away? Where should I park? Where can we eat nearby? What did the artist play last night?

Each question feels small. Together they shape the entire night for millions of people, and the answers online are too often old, scattered, or wrong.

So I taught myself to build the product I could not find.

Concerto is live on the App Store today. I built the product, researched the venues, verified the tours, and created the operating system behind it, all as a solo founder.

02 · The signal

The content people stop for is the product itself.

Live market proof

A short video covering the Jonas Brothers’ newly announced tour setlist outperformed every previous Concerto post by a real margin.

It was the second breakout this year driven specifically by verified setlist information. That is not an adjacent content strategy. It is evidence that Concerto’s core advantage, timely and checkable concert information, is what earns attention.

Verified information→Organic attention
