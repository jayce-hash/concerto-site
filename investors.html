The company today

Built around one fan experience.

Venue guidance, tour information, nearby discovery, and planning, around one saved show, with Concerto AI to answer fans’ questions about it. The app and website are built, and two sister apps share its account: Reprise for photos after the show, and Medley for setlists.

Concerto makes money two ways. Concerto+ is $4.99 for one show, or $29.99 a year, and adds Plan it for me, Leave Time, Bag Check, and Exit Plan. The same membership covers Reprise+. Concerto Partners lets restaurants and hotels near a venue come first in its Nearby rows, at $99 and $299 a month.

The founder

Built by one person.

Jayce Wells started designing Concerto at 21 and founded the company at 22. His background is in marketing, not software. In 2026 he took the product back from an outside developer, rebuilt it himself, and then launched two more apps on the same account.

Concerto, Reprise, Medley and a website with a guide to each of 1,000+ venues were built, and are run, by one person, with no agency and no engineering payroll. Help goes into growth, not into finishing the product.

The demand

Fans are already
asking these questions.

Before a show, fans search for the bag policy, parking, door times and the setlist. Google is sending those searches to Concerto’s venue and tour guides, and Concerto is still new, so it is climbing in the results every month.

The fan reading a venue’s bag policy is planning their night, and the next thing they decide is where to eat and stay. That is where Concerto Partners appear.

From November 20 to 22, 2026, Google sent more than 1,000 visits to those guides in one weekend.

Where help goes furthest

Four things, named plainly.

  1. 01

    Getting in front of fans.

    Concerto is found by people who already have a ticket. Growth experience in consumer apps is what we would use fastest.

  2. 02

    The rooms.

    Introductions to venues, promoters, and the teams who run buildings. That is how a guide becomes a partnership.

  3. 03

    The brands around the night.

    Restaurants, hotels, and rideshare are already part of a concert night. We want the people who know how those deals get done.

  4. 04

    Operating in live music.

    Anyone who has run a tour, a venue, or a ticketing product sees problems a fan-side app cannot.

Concerto is founder-led, and the product is built. These are the areas where another set of hands or one good introduction moves the most.
