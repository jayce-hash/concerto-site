Why I built this

Concerto exists because I got tired of the moment right before a show, standing outside a venue, not knowing if my bag would get me turned away, not knowing where to park, not knowing what they'd even played the night before. That's a small problem on its own. It happens to millions of people every week, and nobody had actually solved it with real, dated, verified information.

I taught myself to build the entire technical stack to fix it. Concerto is live on the App Store today, and I've built every part of it myself, including the parts most people would have hired out.

What's actually working right now

This week

I posted a short-form video about the Jonas Brothers' new tour setlist. It's outperformed everything else I've ever posted, by a real margin. This is the second time this has happened this year with verified setlist content specifically, not a one-off. The thing I built the company around, real, checkable information, is the thing that actually gets people to stop scrolling.

Why I want relationships, not just money

Concerto's second real revenue line, alongside the subscription, is featuring a restaurant or hotel near a venue, free to start, paid once there's real traffic behind it. That grows through actual introductions, not ad spend. If you already know people who run restaurant groups, or hotels near major venues, or the venues themselves, you can shorten that from a cold email to a real conversation. That's worth more to me right now than the same dollar amount from someone I'd have to introduce to the industry myself.
