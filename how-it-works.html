How It Works

Everything for your night out, in one place.

Concerto is the app you open after you buy the ticket. Here is the whole thing, from picking a show to walking out after the encore.

1

Find your venue

Search any of 300+ venues, or let Events Near Me show what is coming up around you.

VenuesEvery arena, stadium, theater, and festival Concerto covers, each with its own guide.
Events Near MeShows happening close to you, sorted by date.
2

Plan the meal and the stay

Your City Guide is a short, curated list of where to eat, drink, and stay near the venue, picked for people going to the show, not pulled from a generic review site.

Before the showSit-down spots that feed you and get you out before doors.
Quick biteFast and close when you are racing the clock.
After the encoreWhere the night keeps going once the lights come up.
Stay the nightWalkable rooms if you are making a trip of it.
3

Sort the logistics

The stuff that usually trips people up, answered for your exact venue before you leave the house.

Bag policyThe exact rules, plus AI Bag Check on Premium: hold your bag to the camera for a Pass, Warn, or Fail.
ParkingWhere to park and what it runs.
RideshareThe right Uber and Lyft drop and pickup zones.
ConcessionsWhat food and drink is inside.
4

Know the show

Walk in knowing what you are about to hear and where to buy safely.

SetlistsWhat the artist has been playing on this tour.
ToursEvery stop on a tour, with official ticket links. Concerto never sells tickets itself.
5

Let Concerto run the night

Premium ties it together: tell Concerto+ your show and your vibe and it builds the evening, with dinner timing and arrival window mapped to your venue. Live Mode turns the app into your in-show companion and hands you a shareable Story Card at the end.
