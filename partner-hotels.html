What this should accomplish

A partnership only works if all three sides win.

  • Fans: A concert-night rate, late checkout, parking, upgrade, shuttle, welcome perk or package
  • You: High-intent event-night travelers
  • Concerto: Paid sponsorship, room benefit, room-night inventory, hosted value or another meaningful contribution
The three-front standard

Know what everyone is bringing to the table.

What fans get

A reason to care.

  • A concert-night rate, late checkout, parking, upgrade, shuttle, welcome perk or package
  • A stay that makes geographic sense for the show
  • A benefit that is genuinely better than ordinary public booking
What you get

A useful commercial outcome.

  • High-intent event-night travelers
  • Booking and offer engagement tied to venue/show context
  • A differentiated reason for fans to choose the property
What Concerto looks for

A real partnership contribution.

  • Paid sponsorship, room benefit, room-night inventory, hosted value or another meaningful contribution
  • Permission to activate the offer across agreed surfaces
  • A direct booking path or trackable campaign action when possible
