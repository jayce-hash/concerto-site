The three-front standard

Every partnership has to work for the fan, for you, and for Concerto.

What fans get

A reason to care.

  • A concert rate, late checkout, parking credit, or a welcome perk
  • A hotel chosen for the venue and the night, not a generic listing
  • Clear disclosure when a placement is paid

What you get

A useful commercial outcome.

  • High-intent bookings tied to a specific event date
  • A booking action inside Your Night and the venue page
  • Visibility across tour-date demand you cannot reach through search alone

What Concerto looks for

A real contribution.

  • A real fan rate or benefit, an inventory block, or paid sponsorship
  • Permission to promote the partnership across app, web, and social
  • A trackable booking link
