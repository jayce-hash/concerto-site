What Bags Are Allowed at Concerts and Stadiums?

Most major venues and stadiums in the US enforce a clear bag policy. Typically, you can bring a clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bag no larger than 12″×6″×12″, or a small clutch no larger than 4.5″×6.5″. Backpacks, non-clear bags, and hard-shell cases are almost universally prohibited. Rules vary by venue and event — search below to find the exact policy for your specific show.
