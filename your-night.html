Your Night

Your whole show,
on one page.

Save a show and tell Concerto three things: how you’re getting there, what bag you’re bringing, and where you’re sitting. Your Night answers everything else for that show, from the setlist to the ride home.

Set it up once

Three questions, answered once.

  1. 01

    How are you getting there?

    Driving, rideshare, transit, or walking. Directions open the right way in Apple Maps, and parking or Uber and Lyft show up when they matter.

  2. 02

    What bag are you bringing?

    Clear bag, small bag, backpack, or none. Your Night checks it against the venue’s published rule and tells you plainly if it works.

  3. 03

    Where are you sitting?

    Section, row, and seat, or the floor. They sit on a ticket at the top of Arrive, one tap from the ticket itself.

Four tabs

Every part of the night, in the order you need it.

Before

Get ready.

  • The setlist the tour has been playing, ready to play
  • Show-night weather
  • Add to Calendar, with an alert before the show
  • Plan My Night: dinner, when to leave, and the show (Concerto+)

Arrive

Get in.

  • Your ticket, one tap away
  • Directions the way you’re traveling
  • Parking, or Uber and Lyft
  • Your bag, checked against the rule
  • Doors, gates, and re-entry
  • AI Bag Check from a photo of your bag (Concerto+)

Show

Enjoy it.

  • Tap when the headliner comes on
  • Food, drink, and accessibility inside
  • The full setlist

After

Get home.

  • Uber or Lyft, with pickup at the venue
  • The venue’s rideshare pickup spot
  • Exit Plan: the fastest way out and where to get picked up (Concerto+)
  • Your memory card

The page looks and works the same every time you open it. Parts marked + are Concerto+.

After the show

Memory card and Passport.

After the show, Concerto finds the photos you took there and puts them on a card with the artist, the date, the venue, and the setlist. Save it or share it.

Every show you go to gets a stamp in your Passport, with your shows, venues, artists, and cities for the year, ready to share.
