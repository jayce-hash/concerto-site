Your night, laid out

A few open spots, one tap each.

  1. 01

    Your ticket

    A screenshot fills in your seat. Or type it.

  2. 02

    Getting there

    Driving, rideshare, transit, or walking, with the venue’s own pickup spot or garages.

  3. 03

    Dinner, your bag, the way home

    Pick a place nearby or add your own, with a time if you have a reservation. Check your bag against the rule, and say how you’re heading home. Skip anything.

Your Night

The night, in order.

  1. 01

    5:45 PM · Dinner nearby

    When to leave and the walk to the venue. Tap for directions.

  2. 02

    6:30 PM · Doors open

    Your bag against the rule, and the entrances.

  3. 03

    Your seat

    Section, row, and seat. One tap opens your ticket.

  4. 04

    7:30 PM · The show

    The setlist the tour has been playing.

  5. 05

    After · The way home

    The venue’s pickup spot, Uber and Lyft, and with Concerto+, an Exit Plan timed to the last song.

Tap any stop to change it. Your plan is saved with the show.

After the show

Reprise.

Every show you go to is kept in Reprise. Concerto finds the photos you took there and puts them on a card with the artist, the date, the venue, and the setlist. Save it or share it.

Reprise also keeps your totals: your shows, venues, artists, and cities.

Concerto AI

Ask anything about your show.

Every Your Night page has its own question bar. Concerto AI already knows the venue and the artist, so “Can I bring a portable charger?” gets an answer for your show, from the venue’s own rules.
