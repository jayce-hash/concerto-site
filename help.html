Getting Started

Getting Started

What is Concerto for?+

Everything between buying a ticket and walking into the venue. Bag policy, parking, where to eat nearby, when doors open, and what the artist has been playing on tour. If it’s a question you’d ask on the way to a show, it belongs here.

Do I need an account?+

No. You can browse every venue guide, every tour, and every setlist without signing in, and you can save shows too. An account keeps your saved shows, favorites, and history if you switch phones or reinstall. Without one, that data lives only on this device.

How do I search?+

Tap the search bar at the top of Home in the app. It searches venues, artists, and cities together. On the website, use Search in the main navigation to find public venue and tour guides.

Saving Shows

Saving Shows

How do I save a show?+

Tap the bookmark icon next to any show on a tour page, venue page, or Near Me. Your next saved show moves to the top of Home with a live countdown.

What happens after I save one?+

Home gives you the countdown. Tap it to open Your Night, where venue essentials, weather, setlist, restaurants, hotels, things to do, getting there, and getting home live together. Concerto+ turns those pieces into a personalized night plan.

Where do my past shows go?+

A saved show stays as your next show through the night, then moves to History on your Account page about six hours after it starts.

Can I add a show I went to before I had Concerto?+

Not yet. History currently fills from shows you saved in the app.

Venue Guides

Venue Guides

What does the shield icon mean?+

That venue’s bag policy and parking information were checked against the venue’s own official sources. The date shown is when that check happened, so you can see how recently the information was verified.

What is in a venue guide?+

Bag policy, entrances and doors, parking, rideshare pickup, concessions, accessibility, upcoming shows, and nearby restaurants, hotels, and things to do. Directions, Uber, and Lyft are one tap from the top in the app.

How do I find a venue?+

The Venues tab searches 346 venue guides by name, city, or state. You can also reach a venue from a tour date or Near Me.

Tours and Setlists

Tours and Setlists

How do I follow an artist?+

Open a tour page in the app and tap Follow. Followed artists appear on your Account page, and Concerto+ members can receive alerts when new dates become available.

What’s the difference between following an artist and favoriting a tour?+

Following an artist lasts across tours. Favoriting a tour is specific to that run of shows.

Where do setlists come from?+

Concerto publishes setlist information for tracked tours and shows the date it was last updated. If a tour has not started or a setlist is not available yet, we say Setlist Coming Soon rather than guess.

Does Concerto sell tickets?+

No. Ticket links open Ticketmaster, where the actual ticket sale happens. Concerto is built around what comes after the ticket.

Near Me

Near Me

What does Near Me show?+

Shows, games, and events around you, grouped by night. You can filter by location, date range, and category.

Can I browse a city I am not in?+

Yes. Change the location filter to another city and browse it the same way, which is useful when planning a trip around a show.

Your Night

Your Night

What is Your Night?+

Your Night is the complete page for one saved show. Tap the countdown on Home to see venue essentials, weather, getting-there guidance, the setlist, nearby restaurants, hotels and things to do, plus getting-home information in one place.

Where did nearby restaurants, hotels, and things to do go?+

They live inside Your Night because they are specific to the venue you are actually going to. Near Me remains the broader discovery experience around you or another city.

What is the difference between a Concerto Partner and a nearby place?+

A Concerto Partner is a restaurant, hotel, venue, or artist Concerto has a real relationship with, always labeled Partner. A Concerto Perk is the fan benefit that Partner gives. Everything else you see nearby is simply a place near the venue, with no label and no offer.

Concerto Perks

Concerto Perks

What are Concerto Perks?+

Concerto Perks are benefits created with Concerto Partners and surfaced inside Your Night at the moment they matter: before the show, at the venue, your stay, and the ride home. They launch with the first partnerships. Every partner placement is labeled Concerto Partner, and a place without the label is simply a nearby place.

Concerto+

Concerto+

What do I actually get?+

Concerto+ is the personalized concierge layer. It turns everything Concerto knows about your saved show into a plan around you: where to go, when to leave, when to arrive, weather-aware timing, getting-home guidance, show-day and onsale alerts, and AI Bag Check. Free Concerto tells you what you need to know; Concerto+ helps decide what to do with it.

What does it cost?+

$7.99 a month or $69.99 a year. Subscriptions renew until canceled and are managed through your Apple ID.

I already paid but the app says I am not a member.+

Go to Account and tap Restore Purchases. That re-checks with Apple and restores your membership after a reinstall or phone change too.

How do I cancel?+

In iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Concerto. Canceling stops the renewal and you keep access through the end of the paid period.

Alerts and Notifications

Alerts and Notifications

What alerts will I get?+

Concerto+ alerts are tied to the shows you save. Show Day at 9am with the forecast. Before You Go at 5pm to check policies and keep ride-home links handy. Heading Out at 9pm with rideshare links. A Recap at 11am the next day. Follow an artist and Onsale Alerts tell you about new tour dates and presale timing for saved shows. Each alert type has its own switch in Settings.

I am not getting any alerts.+

Check that you have Concerto+, that iOS notification permission is enabled for Concerto, and that the relevant show is actually saved.

Can I turn some off but not others?+

Yes. Open Account, then Settings, then Notifications. Each alert type has its own switch.

If Something Looks Wrong

If Something Looks Wrong

A venue or tour is missing.+

Tour dates come live from Ticketmaster, so a tour with no upcoming dates listed there may not appear. Venue coverage grows as guides are researched and verified. Email support@concertocity.com if something you expect is missing.

The information looks out of date.+

Bag-policy and parking guides show when they were last confirmed. If a verification date looks old, trust the venue’s own site over us and let us know so we can re-check it.

The app is showing old data.+

Concerto caches data so it works quickly and offline. Pull down on a list to force a refresh.

Something is broken.+

Email support@concertocity.com with what you were doing when it happened. Specific steps and screenshots are especially useful.

Account and Privacy

Account and Privacy

How do I sign in?+

Open Account, then enter your email and password. If you have not made an account yet, the same screen can create one.

What data does Concerto collect?+

Saved shows, favorites, and usage data used to understand which features are used. Usage analytics can be controlled in Settings under Privacy. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?+

Open Account, then Settings, then Delete Account. That removes your saved shows, favorites, and history.
