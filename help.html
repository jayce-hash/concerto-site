How Concerto Works

  1. Find your showThe search bar on Home, or Venues, Tours, and Near Me.
  2. Save itTap your show, or its bookmark, and sign in.
  3. Your Night Is Laid OutSave a show and the night is already in order: doors, the show, and the way home. Fill the open spots in one tap each (getting there, dinner, your bag, your ticket), or skip them. With Concerto+, Plan it for me does it for you.
  4. After the showEvery show you go to is kept in Reprise, with a card of your photos, the setlist and your seat to share.

Getting Started

Getting Started

What is Concerto?+

Finding and saving shows, venue guides, setlists, places nearby, planning your night yourself, and the show-day alert are free. Concerto+ adds Plan it for me, Leave Time, Bag Check, Exit Plan, and more Concerto AI answers.

What is free, and what is Concerto+?+

To save a show, yes. Browsing every venue guide, tour, setlist, and Near Me is open to everyone. Saving a show starts Your Night, your past shows, and show-day alerts, so it needs an account: Sign in with Apple or Google takes one tap, or use your email. Your shows then stay with you on every device.

Do I need an account?+

Tap the circle at the top right of Home, Venues, Tours, or Near Me. Your account, Reprise, favorites, notifications, appearance, and privacy are all on that one sheet.

Find a Show

Find a Show

How do I find my show?+

Type the artist or the venue in the search bar on Home and tap your show to save it. You can also browse the Venues, Tours, and Near Me tabs; every show there has a bookmark.

What is in a venue guide?+

The bag rule, entrances and doors, parking, rideshare, re-entry, tickets and will call, food and drink, and accessibility, each with the date it was checked against the venue’s own sources. Plus upcoming shows there, nearby restaurants, hotels, and things to do, and one-tap Directions, Uber, and Lyft.

How do I hear a song from a setlist?+

Tap the song. The first time, choose Apple Music or Spotify, and every song opens there from then on. You can change it in Settings. When you are signed in, the same choice is used in Medley. This works on a tour page and in Your Night.

Can I save a setlist to Apple Music?+

On iPhone, yes. Under the setlist on a tour page or in Your Night, tap Save to Apple Music. Concerto finds each song and adds the whole setlist, in order, to your library as a playlist. It needs an Apple Music subscription and iOS 16 or later. Songs that are not on Apple Music are left out.

What is on a tour page?+

Every upcoming date with a bookmark to save it, the setlist the tour has been playing, and Follow, which saves the artist. With Concerto+, Follow also alerts you when they add dates or presales.

What is House Map?+

A map of the venue’s concourses with every food stand, bar, restroom, entrance, and guest service pinned by section, from the venue’s own maps and food pages. Open it from the venue’s page. If you added your seat, what’s closest to you comes first. Free, at Madison Square Garden, Crypto.com Arena, American Airlines Center, and Scotiabank Arena so far, with more arenas coming.

What does Near Me show?+

Shows around you, grouped by night. Change the location to browse another city, and filter by date.

What can I ask Concerto AI?+

Anything about a venue or show, like "Can I bring a backpack to MSG?" It answers from Concerto’s own venue guides and says so when it doesn’t know. 3 answers a day are free; Concerto+ gets 30.

Save a Show

Save a Show

How do I save a show?+

Tap your show in search, or the bookmark next to any show on a venue page, a tour page, or Near Me. If you’re not signed in yet, Concerto asks you to sign in first, then saves the show and opens Your Night.

What happens after I save one?+

Your Night opens with the night already laid out. A few spots are open: how you are getting there, dinner, your bag, and your ticket. Answer each in one tap, or skip it. A screenshot of your ticket fills in your seat. The show also sits at the top of Home and in the Your Night tab.

Where do I see all my saved shows?+

Tap My shows at the top of the Your Night tab, or My Shows in your account. Every upcoming show is there; tap one to open it. Past shows are in Reprise.

How do I remove a saved show?+

In My Shows, tap Edit, then Remove. You can also tap Remove this show at the bottom of its Your Night page.

Your Night

Your Night

What is Your Night?+

Your plan for one saved show, as a timeline: dinner, getting there, doors, your seat, the show, and getting home. Tap a stop for its details: your seat opens your ticket, doors opens the bag rule, the show opens the setlist. Below it are places nearby, the venue guide, and the setlist.

How do I add a restaurant or hotel to my night?+

Tap Add a stop under your plan, or Add to my night under Eat, Stay & Do. Pick a place, or type in your own (the table you booked, the hotel you are staying at), and choose before or after the show. Set a time if you have one, like a reservation, and your night is timed around it. Tap the three dots on a stop to change its time or take it off.

What if my show changes?+

Concerto checks your saved shows with Ticketmaster. If the time or date changes, or the show is postponed or cancelled, Your Night says so at the top and your alerts move with it.

Where are the Concerto+ tools?+

On your show. In Your Night, Plan it for me sits above your plan, Leave Time shows at the top on show day, Bag Check is in the Venue section, and Exit Plan opens from the end of your night. The card on Home has Bag Check and Exit Plan for that show too.

How does "Open my ticket" work?+

It opens where your ticket lives. Ticketmaster, AXS, SeatGeek, and StubHub tickets only work in their own app or Apple Wallet, so Concerto opens that app. For one tap straight to the ticket, add it to Apple Wallet from the ticket app and choose Apple Wallet in Your Night. Paper or PDF tickets show full screen.

How do I change my seat, bag, or travel?+

Tap the three dots on that line of your night: getting there, doors (for your bag), your seat, or the last song.

What does the lock mean?+

That tool is part of Concerto+. Tap it to see what it does and unlock it for one show or every show.

After the Show

After the Show

What is Reprise?+

Every show you’ve been to, in one place, each with its photo card, plus your totals. Open it from the Reprise card on Home or from your account. A show moves there about six hours after it starts. Reprise is also its own app: it turns the photos from a show or a trip into a full album, and it uses the same account.

How do I share a show from Reprise?+

Open Reprise and tap the show. Concerto finds the photos you took at the show and puts them on a card with the artist, venue, your seat, and the setlist, sized for Instagram Stories. Share it or save it to Photos.

What is Concerto Wrapped?+

A look back at your year in concerts. It appears in Reprise each December with your shows, artists, venues and cities, your most-seen artist, home venue and busiest month, and a card to share. Concerto lets you know when it is ready.

Can I add a show I went to before I had Concerto?+

Not yet. Reprise fills in from shows you saved.

Reprise and Medley

Reprise and Medley

What are Reprise and Medley?+

Two more apps from the creator of Concerto. Reprise turns the photos from a show or a trip into an album and a card to share. Medley is every setlist in one place, with fans streaming shows.

Do they use my Concerto account?+

Yes. One account covers all three. Sign in to one and you are signed in to the others on the same iPhone, and the shows you save here appear in both.

How do I open them from Concerto?+

When Reprise is on your iPhone, a past show has Make An Album In Reprise, and the alert you get the morning after a show opens that night in Reprise. When Medley is on your iPhone, a setlist has Open This Setlist In Medley. Both are also listed under Your Apps in your account.

Does Concerto+ cover Reprise?+

Yes. Concerto+ includes Reprise+, and a Show Pass for a show also unlocks that show’s album in Reprise. Medley is free.

Concerto+

Concerto+

What do I get?+

Plan it for me (Concerto picks how to get there and where to eat, with a reason for each, from the venue guide and the places nearby, and you can swap any pick), Leave Time (on show day, when to leave from where you are, with an alert ten minutes before), Bag Check (a photo of your bag checked against the venue rule), Exit Plan (when to move, your way out, and the official pickup, timed to the set), 30 Concerto AI answers a day instead of 3, and more alerts. Everything else in Concerto stays free.

What does it cost?+

Open your account (the circle at the top right of Home) and tap Restore Purchases. Do the same after reinstalling or switching phones.

I paid but the app says I’m not a member.+

In iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Concerto. You keep Concerto+ through the end of the year you paid for.

Alerts

Alerts

How does Leave Time work?+

On show day, in the last four hours before you need to be there, Your Night shows when to leave. Concerto checks how long the trip is right then, from where you are to your dinner stop or the venue, by the way you said you are getting there. It checks again every few minutes while Your Night is open, and your Time to Head Out alert moves to ten minutes before. It needs location allowed for Concerto. Your location is used for the trip and not saved.

What alerts will I get?+

Free for every saved show: Show Day at 9am with the doors and show time (with Directions and Your Night buttons), Time to Head Out two hours before the show, and a reminder the next morning to add your photos in Reprise. If a saved show changes time, is postponed or is cancelled, Your Night says so. Free every Friday at 5pm: the concerts near you that weekend, only when there are some. Concerto Wrapped arrives December 10. Concerto+ (or a Show Pass for that show) adds your Exit Plan near the end of the night if you made one, and new dates and presales for artists you follow.

How do I turn alerts on or off?+

Open your account (top right of Home) and scroll to Notifications. Each alert has its own switch.

I am not getting any alerts.+

Check that notifications are on for Concerto in iOS Settings and that the show is saved. Exit Plan and new date and presale alerts need Concerto+ or a Show Pass. This Weekend needs a city set in your account.

If Something Looks Wrong

If Something Looks Wrong

A venue or tour is missing.+

Tour dates come from Ticketmaster, so a tour with no dates listed there won’t appear. Venues are added as they’re researched. Email us and we’ll look.

The information looks out of date.+

Every venue section shows the date it was checked. If it looks old, trust the venue’s own site and tap Report wrong info so we can re-check.

Something is broken.+

Email support@concertocity.com with what you were doing. It goes to a real person.

Account and Privacy

Account and Privacy

How do I sign in?+

Tap the circle at the top right of Home, then Sign in with Apple (iPhone), Continue with Google, or use your email.

What data does Concerto collect?+

Your saved shows and favorites, and anonymous counts of which features get used (never what you search or ask). Your Your Night answers, photos, and notes stay on your phone. Turn usage data off under Privacy in your account. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?+

Open your account, scroll to More, and tap Delete Account. Cancel Concerto+ first in iOS Settings.
