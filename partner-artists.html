What we can do together

Three ways to work
with Concerto.

  1. 01

    Keep your tour page accurate.

    Send confirmed dates, set times and setlist details, and we’ll keep your tour page current in the app and on concertocity.com.

  2. 02

    Reach fans going to your shows.

    Share tour news, merch or VIP information with fans who saved one of your shows.

  3. 03

    Promote a fan offer.

    Feature a merch discount, presale or fan experience for Concerto members on your tour page.

How it works

A conversation first.

Send us a note with what you have in mind. We’ll reply by email to talk through the details, including pricing if it applies. There’s no commitment until we both agree.
