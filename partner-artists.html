Artist & Tour Partnerships

Extend the fan experience beyond the stage.

Concerto can become the utility layer around a tour: the show, venue, city, setlist, fan benefits and everything that happens before and after the performance. The goal is not another promo placement; it is a tour companion fans can actually use.

What this should accomplish

A partnership only works if all three sides win.

  • Fans: Ticket or VIP opportunities, exclusive access, tour-specific Perks, better venue context and a richer show-night experience
  • You: Deeper fan engagement around each show
  • Concerto: Paid campaign budget, tickets/VIP inventory, official information, approved assets, access or another meaningful contribution
The three-front standard

Know what everyone is bringing to the table.

What fans get

A reason to care.

  • Ticket or VIP opportunities, exclusive access, tour-specific Perks, better venue context and a richer show-night experience
  • Useful information and benefits connected to the artist they already care about
  • Clear separation between official collaboration and editorial coverage
What you get

A useful commercial outcome.

  • Deeper fan engagement around each show
  • A useful destination for tour, venue, setlist and city context
  • More ways to activate tickets, VIP, merch, experiences and social content
What Concerto looks for

A real partnership contribution.

  • Paid campaign budget, tickets/VIP inventory, official information, approved assets, access or another meaningful contribution
  • A clear contact for tour information and approvals
  • Permission for agreed product, web and social activations
