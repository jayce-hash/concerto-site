# Concerto Product Presentation Standard

Public product presentation is iOS-first and uses consistent iPhone Pro-style hardware framing.

Marketing captures shown on the public website are normalized to:
- 12:00 time
- full cellular signal
- full battery
- no location-services indicator
- no recording indicator
- no notification clutter
- consistent hardware framing and Dynamic Island treatment

The website may present the same Concerto product data differently from the native app. One content system, two presentation systems.
