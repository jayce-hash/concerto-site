Shaky Knees Music Festival

Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Small clutches and fanny packs up to 6 by 9 inches may be non-clear and have no more than one pocket. Larger bags must be clear and smaller than 12 by 6 by 12 inches; qualifying empty hydration packs are allowed.

Allowed

  • One-pocket clutch or fanny pack up to 6 by 9 inches
  • Clear bag smaller than 12 by 6 by 12 inches
  • Empty hydration pack with limited compartments

Not allowed

  • Non-clear bags larger than 6 by 9 inches
  • Clear bags at or above 12 by 6 by 12 inches
  • Clutches and fanny packs with multiple pockets
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Shaky Knees Music Festival. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The festival is in Piedmont Park. No general on-site parking program is published; use public transportation or the current festival travel guidance. ADA parking and entry instructions are published separately for credentialed guests.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Request rideshare service to Piedmont Park and follow in-app directions. After the festival, exit the park and use the nearest pickup point shown by the app.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Shaky Knees publishes dedicated guidance for ADA parking and entry, accessible entrances, viewing areas, restrooms, mobility devices, ASL, hearing accommodations, service animals, and medical dietary needs. Review the accessibility center before arrival.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food trucks serve local and regional options, including Southern barbecue and vegetarian-friendly choices. Consult the current festival map for vendor locations.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests may leave and re-enter until 7:00 p.m. local time. Wristbands are scanned on exit, and all returning guests must pass through security again.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The box office is beside the Piedmont Park Community Center at 12th Street and Piedmont Avenue. It handles will call, wristband assistance, upgrades, and eligible exchange-ticket support; government-issued ID is required.

Official source
