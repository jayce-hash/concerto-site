Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Depending on the organiser’s security rules, security may hold backpacks and bags larger than DIN A4 at the entrance; larger luggage cannot be checked at the venue.

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Foyer access typically begins around one hour before an event; the organiser may change the opening time.

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Märzparkgarage and Stadthallengarage together provide over 1,400 parking spaces; weekday street parking restrictions apply from 9 a.m. to 10 p.m.
