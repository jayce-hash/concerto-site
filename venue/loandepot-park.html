Concessions

Concessions

LoanDepot Park offers a variety of food and drink options, including classic ballpark eats and local Miami cuisine. Highlights include the All-Star Steals menu with affordable pricing and the 3o5 Menu available during home games. Fans can also enjoy mobile food ordering for convenience.

Verified 2026-08-20

Accessibility

Accessibility

Accessible parking is available in all four (4) parking garages and two (2) surface parking lots (East Lot 2 & East Lot 3) on a first-come, first-served basis. Wheelchair accessible and semi-ambulatory seats with operable armrests are located in various sections on all ballpark levels.

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in LoanDepot Park's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.
