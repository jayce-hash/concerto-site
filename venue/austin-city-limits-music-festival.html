Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

No backpacks or large bags over 11”x17” are allowed. Diaper and medical bags are permitted.

Allowed

  • Diaper bags
  • Medical bags

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Large bags over 11”x17”

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Austin City Limits Music Festival. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There is no official parking garage affiliated with the venue, but nearby options are available. Limited valet parking is offered at the W Hotel for ACL Live ticketholders on a first-come, first-served basis. Metered parking starts at $2 per hour and varies by time and day.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Let Lyft be your ride to the action! Use code ACLLIVELYFT for 50% off your first 2 rides (up to $10/ride).

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Austin City Limits Music Festival, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The venue is cashless, and all concessions, bars, and merchandise stands accept credit/debit cards and mobile payments only. No specific food or drink options are detailed on the page.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Austin City Limits Music Festival's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Austin City Limits Music Festival. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
