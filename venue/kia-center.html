Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Kia Center enforces a strict no bag policy to ensure guest safety. Exceptions are made for small clutch bags and medically necessary items.

Allowed

  • Small clutch bags no larger than 6.5" x 4.5"
  • Medical and parent bags (max. 14" x 14" x 6")
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

In general, doors open to the public (1) hour before the printed event time on your ticket.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at the ThreatLocker Garage and 520 W Pine St. Garage, with prices ranging from $10-$20. Reserved parking can be purchased via Ticketmaster or the Kia Center Box Office. Accessible parking is available with direct elevator access.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests are encouraged to be dropped off and picked up at the corner of Hughey and Pine Street.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Kia Center, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Kia Center has expanded its food and beverage offerings to include eight local favorites, featuring a new 4(or)7 menu with items priced at $4 or $7. Highlights include Nauti Lobstah, Black Rooster Taqueria, Domi Italian Ice, and Kelly’s Homemade Ice Cream.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Kia Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

You may also purchase tickets in-person by visiting the Kia Center box office on Church Street.

Official source
