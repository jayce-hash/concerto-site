Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Bill Graham Civic Auditorium allows factory sealed water bottles, empty refillable water bottles, personal food items, and empty Camelbaks. Totems less than 6ft tall and at 1/2in thick are also permitted if made of light materials.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Bill Graham Civic Auditorium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There is limited parking around the Bill Graham Civic Auditorium, so plan accordingly. The Civic Center Garage nearby charges a flat rate during events. Carpooling and public transportation are encouraged.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Bill Graham Civic Auditorium, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Bill Graham Civic Auditorium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Box Office opens 30 minutes prior to door time on the night of the show and is located at the front of the venue at 99 Grove Street (closer to Larkin Street). Bill Graham Civic Auditorium do not have regular box office hours at Bill Graham Civic Auditorium but we are open for sales 2 hours before show time on all show nights.
