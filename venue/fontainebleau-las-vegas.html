Bag policy

Fontainebleau Las Vegas does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Fontainebleau Las Vegas. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fontainebleau Las Vegas offers complimentary self-parking for Nevada residents and Fontainebleau Rewards members until May 31, 2026. Hotel guests enjoy complimentary self-parking for the duration of their stay. Self-parking costs $20 for up to 24 hours after complimentary eligibility ends, with special event pricing applicable during certain times.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Fontainebleau Las Vegas's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Fontainebleau Las Vegas, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fontainebleau Las Vegas offers 24-hour in-room dining with a variety of global-inspired menu options. Guests can enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner, and late-night dining, with specific hours for each meal period. Orders can be placed via phone or online.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Fontainebleau Las Vegas's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026
