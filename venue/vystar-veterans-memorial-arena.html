Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches are permitted after search; backpacks are not. Medical-device carriers larger than that limit can be admitted following inspection.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Screened medical-device carrier

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Oversized ordinary bags
Official source

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena’s FAQ and parking page do not set out a dedicated rideshare pickup area for all shows.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena publishes a separate accessibility guide and provides guest service locations on its concourses for assistance.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concession and merchandise stands are cashless. Outside food and beverages are prohibited except medically needed food and drinks.

Allowed

  • Medical-related food or drink

Not allowed

  • Outside food or drink otherwise
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests cannot leave and use the same ticket to return, except in medical or emergency situations.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena has an Ascension St. Vincent’s Box Office and Will Call; refer to its box-office page or the concert ticket confirmation for show-day pickup details.

Official source
