Bag Policy

Bag Policy

MetLife Stadium allows clear bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" and small non-clear purses up to 4.5" x 6.5". All bags are subject to inspection before entry.

Parking

Parking

Parking for tours is free and available in Lot F or G. Visitors should enter through the MetLife Gate located between the Moody's and Verizon Gates.

Concessions

Concessions

MetLife Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, with alcohol sales managed by Delaware North. Guests are limited to purchasing two alcoholic beverages at a time, and sales end at the beginning of the third quarter for football games.
