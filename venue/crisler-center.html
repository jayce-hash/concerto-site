Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

All bags, including purses and fanny packs, are prohibited. One appropriately sized bag is allowed for a guest with medical or childcare needs after it is inspected and tagged at an entrance solutions table.

Allowed

  • One inspected medical- or childcare-necessity bag

Not allowed

  • Purses
  • Fanny packs
  • All other bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Gates open 60 minutes before tipoff. The northeast entrance serves general admission, accessible entry, public will call, and designated pass lists; the northwest entrance is student-only.

Official source

The A–Z guide links separate parking instructions for men’s basketball and other Crisler Center events; lots and permit requirements vary by event.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The northeast-entrance elevator provides access between arena areas for guests with accessibility needs. Accessible seating exchanges and designated parking are available under the venue guide.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Full-service concession stands operate around the concourse, and transactions are cashless and contactless.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The A–Z guide lists exit and re-entry rules for Crisler Center; standard event entry does not permit unrestricted re-entry.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Public will call is at the Northeast Entrance. Guests parking inside the stadium concourse can follow the accessible south-side path to that entrance.
