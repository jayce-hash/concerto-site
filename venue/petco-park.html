Parking

Parking

Petco Park is in downtown San Diego's East Village, served by the Padres Parkade, Tailgate Park, and many nearby garages and lots. Prepaid parking through the team or SpotHero is cheaper than drive-up. The MTS Trolley stops at the Gaslamp/Convention Center stations a short walk away.

Concessions

Concessions

Petco Park offers a variety of food and beverage options including classic comfort foods with modern twists, such as crispy chicken tenders, cheeseburgers, and specialty hot dogs. Highlights include the Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry, with a selection of local and popular beers available at the Beer Garden.
