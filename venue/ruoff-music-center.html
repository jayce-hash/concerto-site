Ruoff Music Center

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Clear plastic or vinyl totes up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches, nonclear clutches up to 6 × 9 inches, and inspected medical bags are permitted.

Allowed

  • Clear tote up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Clutch up to 6 × 9 inches
  • Inspected medical bag

Not allowed

  • Other ordinary bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office is at the west gates. Gate and lot hours vary by concert; check the event listing, and expect security and bag screening.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

One parking pass is required per vehicle; general parking starts at $20 online and costs $25 on show day. Lots usually open one hour before gates; upgraded parking is available.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Rideshare drop-off and pickup use Gate 2A, north of Boden Road and 146th Street. For pickups, drivers should arrive 45 minutes before the show ends.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue provides accessibility arrangements and a separate accessibility guide; contact staff or the box office about specific needs.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drinks are sold in the venue plazas. Guests may bring food in a clear one-gallon bag per person; the venue is cashless.

Allowed

  • Food in one clear one-gallon bag
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Re-entry is prohibited; leaving requires a new ticket to return.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call tickets are collected from the box office beside the west main gate with photo ID matching the purchaser. The box office opens at noon on show days.

Official source
