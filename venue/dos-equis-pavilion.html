Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Only small clutches, wristlets, or fanny packs no larger than 6" x 9" and clear plastic bags no larger than 12" x 12" x 6" are allowed. All bags are subject to search.

Allowed

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Dos Equis Pavilion. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking at Dos Equis Pavilion requires a purchased pass per vehicle, with general parking starting at $20 online or $25 on-site. Upgraded options like Easy Out, Ultra VIP, VIP, and Reserved Parking are available for closer access and faster entry.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Dos Equis Pavilion's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Dos Equis Pavilion, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Dos Equis Pavilion offers a variety of food and drink options, including Mosh Burger's hormone-free beef burgers, Rebel Hen's crispy chicken tenders, and Ziggy's Magic Pizza Shop's NY-inspired pizza. Koko's Bavarian provides a taste of German culture with sausages and pretzels, while general concessions offer classic snacks like popcorn and nachos with a selection of beverages.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Dos Equis Pavilion's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Dos Equis Pavilion. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
