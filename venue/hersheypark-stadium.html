Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Hersheypark Stadium offers a wide variety of food and drink options, including traditional concessions like hot dogs and hamburgers, as well as unique offerings such as Cuban sandwiches and smoked barbecue. Beverage options include a selection of beers, wines, cocktails, and fresh squeezed lemonades.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Hersheypark Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Will Call is located at Hersheypark Stadium Box Office and opens at 9 a.m. on the day of the concert. For guests who may not have credit or debit cards, they can convert cash to prepaid debit cards at the Cash-to-Card kiosks located in the Box Office lobby and on the concourse outside of the Hershey Sports store.
