Parking

Parking

Yankee Stadium parking is in several garages and lots around the Bronx ballpark operated by third parties, with prepaid rates (via SpotHero/Ticketmaster) cheaper than drive-up. Many fans use transit: the 4, B and D trains stop at 161st St-Yankee Stadium and Metro-North serves the Yankees-E 153rd St station. Lots open a couple hours before first pitch.

Concessions

Concessions

Yankee Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, including classic comfort foods with bold flavors and modern twists. Highlights include specialty items like Bacon Wrapped Hot Dogs, Pastrami Fries, and indulgent treats like Soft Serve and Milkshakes. The venue features a lively Beer Garden and several concession stands with unique offerings.
