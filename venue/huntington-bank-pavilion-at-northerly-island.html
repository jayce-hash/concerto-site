Huntington Bank Pavilion at Northerly Island

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC tote bags no larger than 12 × 6 × 12 inches and nonclear clutch bags no larger than 6 × 9 inches are allowed. Larger medical bags undergo additional screening.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Clutch up to 6 × 9 inches

Not allowed

  • Other bags
  • Oversized bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Gates usually open 60–120 minutes before showtime, but the exact time varies by show. Check the show’s event page; parking opens one hour before the gate time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Parking is not included in the concert ticket, and the venue recommends reserving in advance. Lots open one hour before the scheduled gate time.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Normal drop-off is near Adler Planetarium and pickup is on McFetridge Drive in front of the Field Museum. For expanded-lawn shows, both use the Field Museum.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue allows larger screened medical bags, including a separate bag for breast-pumping equipment. Ask guest services about seating or other accommodations.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and beverages are sold on site; guests may also bring food in a clear one-gallon bag and up to one gallon of water in a factory-sealed or empty plastic reusable bottle.

Allowed

  • Food in clear one-gallon bag
  • Water up to one gallon in a factory-sealed or empty plastic bottle

Not allowed

  • Metal water bottles
  • Hydration packs
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

No re-entry after leaving, except when a severe-weather evacuation is cleared; keep the ticket for re-scanning after such an evacuation.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue visit FAQ does not publish a universal concert will-call collection point or time.

Official source
