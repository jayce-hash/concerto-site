Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All bags are subject to search at Ryman Auditorium. Oversized bags and backpacks are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Weapons
  • Knives
  • Pepper spray/mace
  • Oversized bags
  • Inflatable items
  • Posters/signs
  • Backpacks
  • Outside food and beverage
  • Cans
  • Bottles
  • Alcohol
  • Drugs
  • Laser pens
  • Markers
  • Spray paint
  • Face paint
  • Laptops/tablets
  • Musical instruments
  • Noise making devices
  • Cameras with detach lenses
  • Video cameras
  • Monopods
  • Tripods
  • Audio recording devices
  • Selfie sticks
  • Cameras with telephoto or zoom lenses

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Ryman Auditorium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Self-parking is available directly across the street at Fifth + Broadway. Parking can be purchased upon entering the garage. The Ryman does not have a public parking lot.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Let Lyft be your ride to the action! Use code RYMANAUD for 50% off your first 2 rides (up to $10/ride).

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Ryman Auditorium, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Ryman Auditorium offers a variety of concessions including popcorn, candy, and pretzels available in the lobby on each floor. Fully stocked bars are also available for drinks. Nearby, Ole Red and Category 10 provide additional dining and entertainment options with live music and unique experiences.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Ryman Auditorium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Ryman Auditorium. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
