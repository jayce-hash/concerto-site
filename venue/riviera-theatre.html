Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Only bags smaller than 12 × 12 inches may enter; they do not need to be clear but are searched. Backpacks are prohibited and oversize bags cannot be held at will call.

Allowed

  • Bag smaller than 12 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags 12 × 12 inches or larger
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors usually open 90 minutes before showtime, but the exact time is posted on each event page. Small bags are searched at entry.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theatre does not operate public parking. Its FAQ lists nearby surface lots at Lawrence and Broadway, Lawrence and Winthrop, and Truman College on Wilson.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The operator’s Riviera FAQ does not designate a rideshare pickup or drop-off point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Call the theatre at 773-275-6800 to arrange ADA accommodations.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue is cashless and accepts debit and credit cards; outside food and drinks are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Riviera Theatre does not allow re-entry.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Use the AXS app to display your mobile ticket; paper tickets are not required. The Riviera box office sells any remaining tickets on show nights and opens when doors open.

Official source
