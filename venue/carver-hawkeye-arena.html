Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Carver-Hawkeye Arena enforces a clear-bag policy and subjects all people and items to security screening. Small wristlets or clutches may be carried through the walk-through detector.

Allowed

  • Policy-compliant clear bags
  • Small wristlets or clutches

Not allowed

  • Bags that do not meet the clear-bag policy
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The north, west, east, and south entrances open 90 minutes before tipoff.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Free public parking begins 90 minutes before tipoff in Lots 43NW, 43W, and 65. Free ADA parking is first come, first served in Lot 40 with a valid state-issued placard or plate.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published specific rideshare guidance on its official venue or guest-information pages.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Accessible seating is available through the Athletics Ticket Office, and free ADA parking is provided in Lot 40. Accessible sidewalks connect Lot 40 to the south entrance.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Concession stands are cashless and accept credit or debit cards; stands open 90 minutes before competition. Beer, wine, and hard seltzer are sold, but outside alcohol is prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside alcohol
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Patrons may not exit and re-enter Carver-Hawkeye Arena.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Will call is inside the north entrance lobby, and pickup requires photo ID. Media credential pickup is at the south entrance.

Official source
