Bag policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Small clutch purses and fanny packs 6 x 9 inches or smaller do not need to be clear and may have no more than one pocket. Other bags larger than 6 x 9 inches must be clear and smaller than 12 x 6 x 12 inches. Empty hydration packs are allowed within the festival policy.

Allowed

  • small clutch/fanny packs up to 6 x 9 inches
  • clear bags smaller than 12 x 6 x 12 inches
  • empty hydration packs within policy

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Lollapalooza, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Chicago Park District offers a variety of concessionaires providing unique dining and recreational opportunities. These include sit-down meals, quick snacks, and rental services. Concessions typically operate from Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day.
