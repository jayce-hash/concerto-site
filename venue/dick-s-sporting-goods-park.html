Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Soft-sided bags no larger than 14 × 14 × 6 inches are permitted after search. Medical and diaper bags of the same size are screened; there is no bag check, and concert restrictions may vary.

Allowed

  • Soft-sided bag up to 14 × 14 × 6 inches
  • Screened medical or diaper bag up to 14 × 14 × 6 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized backpacks or bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bag and guest searches occur at stadium entry. Concert gate arrangements and any changes to the general guest policy are determined by the individual event.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The stadium has nearly 5,000 parking spaces, with general lots A, AA, B, BB, C, D, E, F, G and H. More than 100 accessible spaces are distributed among lots, first come first served with valid credentials.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Rideshare, taxi and passenger pickup and drop-off use Lot H on the west side of the stadium, near Gate G and accessed along 60th Avenue.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible parking has over 100 spaces, primarily in Lots A, B, C and F, with additional spots in G and H. Display a valid disability plate or placard and request assistance from Guest Relations on the west concourse near Section 129.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drinks are sold in the stadium; outside food and beverages are generally prohibited. One sealed clear plastic water bottle or an empty clear plastic bottle of 32 ounces or less is allowed under the general policy, but concert rules may vary.

Allowed

  • One factory-sealed clear plastic water bottle
  • Empty clear plastic bottle up to 32 ounces

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages otherwise
  • Outside alcohol
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests who exit the park for any reason cannot re-enter with the same ticket.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will Call is at the Main Box Office on the west side; bring your own photo ID. The guide says the box office opens 90 minutes before an event, while ordinary ticket purchases are online and delivered electronically.

Official source
