Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Rate Field enforces a clear bag policy to ensure guest safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Allowed

  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag (1 Gallon)
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”
  • Seat cushions without pockets, zippers or concealable areas up to 18” wide

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags and wallets exceeding 6.5" x 4.5"
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags
  • Briefcases
  • Fanny packs
  • Cinch bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Rate Field. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Rate Field (the White Sox ballpark on Chicago's South Side) has large on-site lots labeled around the park off the Dan Ryan, sold prepaid or at the gate. Lots open a few hours before first pitch and tailgating is allowed in designated areas. The CTA Red Line Sox-35th stop is adjacent.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fans arriving to the ballpark via a rideshare service will be dropped off in Lot A and can proceed to Gate 5 to enter.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Should you have questions or suggestions regarding accessible accommodations of our facility please contact Guest Relations at 312-674-5225, (TDD) 312-674-5188 or by visiting whitesox.com/fanfeedback. To access one of the sensory locations, please contact any Guest Service Representative, Security Guard, or go directly to the Guest Relations Booth located behind home on your level.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Rate Field offers a variety of food and beverage options, including classic comfort foods with bold flavors and modern twists. Highlights include specialty items like the Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry, as well as indulgent treats like Soft Serve and Milkshakes. Fans can enjoy a selection of local and popular beers at the Beer Garden.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Rate Field's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Rate Field. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
