Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Gallagher Square at Petco Park enforces a clear bag policy to ensure guest safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutch bags no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Parking

Parking

Gallagher Square is the park-within-the-park at Petco Park in downtown San Diego, so use Petco Park parking: the Padres Parkade, Tailgate Park, and nearby East Village garages and lots. Prepaid parking is cheaper than drive-up, and the MTS Trolley stops a short walk away.
