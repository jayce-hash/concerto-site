Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Only small purses and fanny packs up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches may enter; backpacks and larger bags are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Small purse or fanny pack up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Larger bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The door time printed on the ticket marks the start of entry; concerts can begin 30–75 minutes later. The venue advises against arriving more than one hour before doors.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theater recommends the two public garages on Lafayette within one block on either side of Fourth Street. They often charge a flat fee after 5 p.m.; the theater has no valet.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ does not designate a rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Contact the box office ahead to discuss accessible accommodations. The balcony is accessible only by stairs, while the main floor is available for shows with general admission.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theater has a full-service bar and does not serve food. Outside food or drink is prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drink
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Re-entry is not allowed at any event once the ticket has been scanned.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ links to separate box office information but does not publish a universal will-call pickup procedure.

Official source
