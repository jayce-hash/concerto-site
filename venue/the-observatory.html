    Bag policy

    Checked Oct 11, 2026

    Bags and backpacks up to 12" x 6" x 12" are allowed, along with clutches up to 4.5" x 6.5". Larger bags are not allowed.

    Allowed

    • Bags and backpacks up to 12" x 6" x 12"
    • Clear backpacks within the size limit
    • Small clutches up to 4.5" x 6.5"

    Not allowed

    • Bags or backpacks larger than 12" x 6" x 12"
    • Luggage
    • Duffel bags
    • Camping-size backpacks
    Official source

    Entrances and doors

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    The indoor Observatory entrance is at the front of the building; an accessible ramp leads into the main room. Check the individual show for its door time.

    Official source

    Parking

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    Parking is signed near the venue at West Lake Center Drive and South Harbor Boulevard. Pre-purchase is encouraged; remaining spaces are very limited and filled first come, first served.

    Official source

    Rideshare

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    The indoor Observatory visit page does not publish a designated rideshare pickup or drop-off area.

    Official source

    Accessibility

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    The main room has an accessible entry ramp, an ADA section and accessible restrooms near the stage. Request a sign-language interpreter at least two weeks before the show.

    Official source

    Food and drink

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    The indoor venue offers food and drinks on its patio and optional food-and-beverage packages in the balcony. The Fast Lane patio opens one hour before doors for select shows; general admission guests can use it once doors open.

    Official source

    Re-entry

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    Staff can assist a guest with a service animal to leave and re-enter for an animal relief break. The indoor venue does not publish a general re-entry rule for other guests.

    Official source

    Tickets and will call

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    The venue says its box office opens on show days about one hour before doors, but does not publish a separate will-call pickup procedure.

    Official source
