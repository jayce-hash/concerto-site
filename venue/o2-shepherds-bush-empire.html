Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Each guest may bring one small bag no larger than A4 size (29.7 by 21 by 15 centimetres). Large bags and rucksacks are not permitted, and all bags may be searched.

Allowed

  • One small bag no larger than A4 size (29.7 by 21 by 15 centimetres)

Not allowed

  • Large bags
  • Rucksacks
  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at O2 Shepherds Bush Empire. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The venue is well served by public transport. A limited blue-badge bay is behind the venue on Pennard Road, and additional blue-badge parking is available at Westfield London on Ariel Way; confirm current restrictions before travelling.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in O2 Shepherds Bush Empire's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

If you're coming to a show in the main auditorium or O2 Academy2 Birmingham, there's an accessible toilet on the balcony level (first floor), it’s controlled by a RADAR key and our staff will also provide access to it if needed and also in stalls.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bars operate inside the venue, with selection and opening points varying by event. Outside food and beverages are not permitted unless required for medical reasons and agreed with the venue.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in O2 Shepherds Bush Empire's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

You can book with Ticketmaster or come to the venue in person, our box office opening hours are on our Contact us page.
