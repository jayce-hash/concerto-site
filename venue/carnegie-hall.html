Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The hall FAQ says unchecked bags should be no larger than an ordinary backpack, approximately 18 × 12 × 8 inches; larger and oversized items are not permitted.

Allowed

  • Bag up to approximately 18 × 12 × 8 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags and items

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The hall’s official guidance lists accessible entrances and elevators for its concert halls, plus free assistive listening devices and large-print programs. The Stern Auditorium balcony has no elevator service.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Weill Café at Carnegie Hall offers daytime coffee and meals and pre-concert dining; availability for a particular performance should be checked beforehand.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The hall publishes box-office contact and ticket-purchase information but no universal concert will-call pickup time in the cited visitor guidance.
