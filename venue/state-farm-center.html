Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

State Farm Center enforces a clear-bag and prohibited-items policy. Guests should bring only a policy-compliant clear bag or approved small personal item.

Allowed

  • Policy-compliant clear bags

Not allowed

  • Items listed as prohibited by the arena

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The arena uses upper-concourse ramps and east and west lower entrances; door assignments and opening times vary by event.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Parking surrounds the arena in multiple lot quadrants. Guests should use the official parking map and event instructions for the assigned area.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Accessible parking is available in each parking-lot quadrant around the arena. The venue provides accessible seating and may arrange sign-language interpretation with advance notice.
