    Bag policy

    Checked Oct 11, 2026

    Clear totes up to 12" x 6" x 12" and clutches up to 4.5" x 7" are allowed. Other bags larger than a clutch are not allowed.

    Allowed

    • Clutches up to 4.5" x 7"
    • Clear totes up to 12" x 6" x 12"
    • Clear drawstring bags up to 12" x 12"
    • 1-gallon clear plastic storage bags
    • Diaper bags (with child)
    • Items related to a medical condition

    Not allowed

    • Bags larger than 4.5" x 7", including purses, backpacks and binocular cases
    • Containers
    • Boxes, gift bags or wrapped packages
    Official source

    Entrances and doors

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    The arena uses upper-concourse ramps and east and west lower entrances; door assignments and opening times vary by event.

    Official source

    Parking

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    Parking surrounds the arena in multiple lot quadrants. Guests should use the official parking map and event instructions for the assigned area.

    Official source

    Rideshare

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    The venue has not published specific rideshare guidance on its official venue or guest-information pages.

    Official source

    Accessibility

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    Accessible parking is available in each parking-lot quadrant around the arena. The venue provides accessible seating and may arrange sign-language interpretation with advance notice.

    Official source

    Food and drink

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    All State Farm Center concession transactions are cashless.

    Official source

    Re-entry

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    State Farm Center has a no-re-entry policy.
