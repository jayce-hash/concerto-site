Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For safety and security, only small clutches, belt bags, fanny-packs, or clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” are allowed. Backpacks are not permitted.

Allowed

  • small clutches
  • belt bags
  • fanny-packs
  • clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”

Not allowed

  • backpacks

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at History. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking can be added to your ticket purchase for a guaranteed space at 1141 Eastern Avenue, located behind the venue. It's recommended to purchase parking in advance to ensure availability.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For pickups and drop-offs, the designated Lyft zone is located at Kishigo Lane Rideshare, PUDO: 43.666392, -79.314892.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Accessible Seating Area is located on the Mezzanine level. Both levels have accessible bathrooms, and a designated area for those with accessibility issues.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

History offers custom menus, premium beers, and handcrafted cocktails to cater to various appetites and event needs. The venue collaborates with culinary partners to provide personalized food and beverage services.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in History's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lost and forgotten items can be picked up on show days at our Box Office, which opens 1 hour before the scheduled Doors time. The box office operates only on show days and opens one hour prior to the advertised door time, until 10pm.

Official source
