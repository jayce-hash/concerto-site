Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Festhalle does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Festhalle. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking options include Park + Ride spaces, with a recommendation to use public transport due to included RMV access with event tickets. Arriving early is advised due to potential security checks.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Festhalle's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Festhalle, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Visitors are not allowed to bring food or drinks into the Festhalle. There are various food stands available inside the venue offering a wide range of drinks and snacks.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Festhalle's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Festhalle. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
