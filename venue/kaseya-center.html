Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags exceeding 10” x 6” x 2” are not permitted inside the Kaseya Center. Guests should adhere to the size restrictions for a smooth entry.

Parking

Parking

Due to ongoing development, fewer parking options are available near Kaseya Center. Patrons are advised to consider alternative parking options with flat rates within walking distance. Public transportation is recommended.

Rideshare

Gates & Entrances

Accessibility

Accessibility

Both lounges are located at center court on the event level, beneath the bowl seating, and are accessible via private entrances from the P2 Garage and from street level Premium Entrances (Gate 4).

Verified 2026-08-20

Concessions

Concessions

Kaseya Center offers a diverse culinary experience with options ranging from chef-driven tacos and smashburgers to vegan street eats and Caribbean bowls. Special offerings include Game Day Value Menu and Family Meal Deals, providing affordable dining options for fans.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Guests will not be permitted to leave an event/game and re-enter the same event.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Reverse ATMs will also be located on-site (next to Box Office and in sections 109, 121, 318, Inside Gate 1A: Next to Miami HEAT Store) to convert your cash into a prepaid debit card. The Kaseya Center Ticket Office is located on NE 8th Street, between Gates 3 and 4.
