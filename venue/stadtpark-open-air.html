Stadtpark Open Air

Know before you go

What matters at Stadtpark Open Air.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Only bags whose largest side is no larger than a DIN A4 sheet are permitted, such as belt bags, gym bags, and tote bags. Larger bags, backpacks, and helmets may have to be placed in paid storage.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Parking is available at the 'Überseering' in the City Nord, which is about a 5-10 minute walk from the venue. No specific pricing information is provided. Consider using public transportation as the event ticket includes an HVV ticket.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Stadtpark Open Air's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →

Concessions

Food and beverage availability at Stadtpark Open Air varies by event and operating setup. Review the linked official guest information before arrival.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Stadtpark Open Air, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Stadtpark Open Air's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Stadtpark Open Air. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Entry begins no later than one hour before the event; for sold-out concerts the venue says entry can begin about 90 minutes before the show.
