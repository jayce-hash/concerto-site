Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Clear bags or small wristlets (4x6) are strongly encouraged for all events. Baggage such as bags, briefcases, backpacks, and luggage are strictly prohibited.

Allowed

  • Clear bags
  • Small wristlets (4x6)

Not allowed

  • Drugs
  • Weapons of any kind
  • Baggage
  • Vaping devices
  • Noisemakers
  • DSLR Cameras
  • Laser pointers
  • Outside food or beverage
  • Bags
  • Briefcases
  • Backpacks
  • Luggage
  • Cameras with professional lenses, detachable lenses or detachable flashes

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Amica Mutual Pavilion. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available 24/7 in two garages adjacent to the Amica Mutual Pavilion. Event parking is $25 plus tax, with hourly rates starting at $8. Monthly plans are also available. EV charging stations can be reserved through Parkwhiz.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Amica Mutual Pavilion's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The AMP & RI Convention Center’s North and South Garages ( ADA accessible) are conveniently located adjacent to the AMP. Guests are advised to contact the ADA/Guest Services Info line at 401.331.6700 at the time of ticket purchase or at least 14 days prior to the date of the scheduled event to arrange for interpretive services.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Amica Mutual Pavilion offers a variety of food options including pizza, chicken tenders, hamburgers, pretzels, nachos, popcorn, hot dogs, and specialty items like doughboys. Alcoholic beverages such as craft beer, domestic beers, hard seltzers, and specialty cocktails are available. A new taco stand offers made-to-order tacos, Margaritas, and Coronas.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Amica Mutual Pavilion's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Will Call window, located at the AMP Box Office, is for Ticketmaster purchases, team, and internal reservations marked for will call. Will Call tickets are available on the day of the event only.

Official source
