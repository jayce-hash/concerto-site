Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

One clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches or a one-gallon clear bag is permitted; also allowed are clear fanny packs up to 13 × 6 × 3 inches and clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches. Large bags and backpacks are prohibited; paid lockers are available.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear bag
  • Clear fanny pack up to 13 × 6 × 3 inches
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Large bags
  • Large backpacks

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible seating is in sections 101, 102, 103, 202 and 204 and on the lawn. Select the accessible seating option on the event ticket page.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Outside food and drinks are prohibited except a factory-sealed water bottle of 24 ounces or less, an empty plastic bottle, and small factory-sealed allergy-related food. Alcohol purchases are limited to two drinks per person per transaction.

Allowed

  • Sealed water bottle up to 24 ounces
  • Empty plastic bottle
  • Small sealed allergy-related food

Not allowed

  • Outside food or drinks otherwise
  • Outside alcohol
