Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

At Stade de France, large bags and suitcases are prohibited, while standard backpacks up to 15 liters are allowed. There is a free left-luggage service available at the venue.

Allowed

  • Standard backpacks (maximum 15 liters)

Not allowed

  • Suitcases
  • Backpacks larger than 15 liters
  • Professional cameras
  • Video cameras
  • GoPros
  • Monopods
  • Tripods
  • Selfie sticks
  • Audio recording devices
  • Cameras with removable lenses or lenses longer than 15 cm
  • Banners
  • Insignia
  • Badges
  • Leaflets
  • Animals (except guide dogs)
  • Weapons
  • Tools
  • Glassware
  • Helmets
  • Foghorns
  • Rigid and large diameter flag poles
  • Bars
  • Misters
  • Metal water bottles
  • Mini fans
  • Bottles of water with caps larger than 50cl
  • Flares and fireworks
  • Laser pointers
  • Stroller
  • Pram
  • Cosy
  • Computers
  • Tablets
  • Any alcoholic beverage

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Stade de France. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking at Stade de France requires advance purchase of tickets, as no parking tickets are sold on event day. Car parks open from 8 am to 1 am for evening events, and from 8 am to 9 pm for matinees. Access is restricted to authorized vehicles with parking tickets.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Stade de France's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Stade de France, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A wide selection of drinks and food is available throughout events at Stade de France. The venue features a food truck village on the forecourt and various refreshment stands inside offering sandwiches and drinks.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Stade de France's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Stade de France. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
