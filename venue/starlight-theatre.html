Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Starlight Theatre enforces a clear bag policy to enhance safety and expedite entry. Only clear bags up to 12″ x 6″ x 12″, one-gallon clear zip-seal bags, and small clutches or fanny packs up to 4.5″ x 6.5″ are allowed.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12″ x 6″ x 12″
  • One gallon clear zip-seal bags
  • Small clutch bags or fanny packs up to 4.5″ x 6.5″

Not allowed

  • Tinted or colored bags
  • Bags exceeding size limits

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Gates open at 6:30 p.m., the seating bowl opens at 7 p.m., and the show begins promptly at 8 p.m.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

General parking at Starlight is free and available on a first-come, first-served basis. Premium parking costs $35 and must be purchased in advance. Season ticket holders receive complimentary premium parking.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Rideshare drivers should enter via 63rd Street, with pick-up and drop-off on Starlight Road near Gate 4.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

ADA-accessible seating with wheelchair spaces and movable-arm seats is available.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Starlight Theatre offers a variety of food and drink options, including BBQ plates, burgers, chicken sandwiches, and vegetarian options like the Beyond Burger. Highlights include the Star Bar with frozen margaritas and daiquiris, and the new Brick House Coffee & Creamery for cold treats.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Starlight Theatre's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Starlight Theatre. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
