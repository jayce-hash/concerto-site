Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Each guest may bring one bag no bigger than A4; rucksacks and larger bags are prohibited and all bags are checked. The on-site cloakroom is for permitted small items, not large bags.

Allowed

  • One bag up to A4 size

Not allowed

  • Rucksacks
  • Bags larger than A4
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Check the event page and ticket for door and curfew times; security checks bags in the arrival queue.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

There are two accessible bays outside the front of the venue on Stockwell Road. The operator’s travel guide does not promise regular show parking.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The academy’s FAQ and venue arrival pages do not designate a rideshare pickup site.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Two accessible parking bays are on Stockwell Road; accessible tickets can be booked online, and the venue publishes detailed access advice.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Cashless bars sell drinks and snack food, with free drinking water. Outside food, drinks and chewing gum are prohibited; contact the venue beforehand about health needs.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
  • Chewing gum
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ covers a designated outdoor smoking area but does not publish a universal rule for leaving and re-entering the site.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Tickets are held in the O2 Academy Venues app and each group member needs their own ticket. If a ticket cannot be found, ask the ticket-resolution team in the queue; the box office is accessible only once inside.

Official source
