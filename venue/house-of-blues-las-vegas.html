Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" are allowed. Every bag is searched, and non-clear bags receive additional screening.

Concessions

Concessions

The Music Hall sells local craft beer, light bites, and full-bar service during shows. The venue is cashless for concessions, bar, and in-venue ticket sales; a separate Restaurant & Bar is also on site.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Will Call

The box office opens at noon on show days. Day-of-show will call requires a valid photo ID and the credit card used for purchase; call 702-632-7600 for advance pickup questions.
