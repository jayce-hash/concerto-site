Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches are permitted after search; screening is required at entry.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Gates open at a show-specific time shown on the event detail page; patrons pass through a magnetometer and may request alternative hand screening.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

General parking costs $25 in advance or $30 on show day and is first come first served in surrounding lots 1–4. The venue also sells North and South Lot upgrades.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ does not designate a specific rideshare loading point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible parking and arrival information is available from the venue; use the event page to find the applicable gate and request staff assistance.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Outside food and drink are generally prohibited, with a special picnic exception on designated Picnic Series concerts. The venue is cashless and can convert cash to a card at Guest Services.

Allowed

  • Picnic items at designated Picnic Series shows

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks at ordinary concerts
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Re-entry is prohibited at regular events. Picnic Nights are an exception allowing guests to return picnic supplies to their vehicles.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will Call is at the venue Ticket Office; bring photo ID and the purchasing card. On concert days the office opens at 2 p.m. and closes at 9 p.m.

Official source
