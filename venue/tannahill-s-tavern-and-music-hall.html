Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches and small nonclear clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches are allowed; all bags may be inspected. Large bags and backpacks are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Nonclear clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags and backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Music Hall doors usually open about 60 minutes before showtime, though the individual event page controls. Metal detection or pat-down and bag inspection may occur at entry.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Valet parking near the Exchange Street entrance is listed at $20, with additional paid lots a short walk away. The venue recommends arranging another way to travel if possible.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue recommends rideshare because parking in the Stockyards is limited; its FAQ does not specify a dedicated pickup curb.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible tickets are sold online and there are limited seats for last-minute needs. For assisted listening, call at least two days ahead; for an interpreter, at least four weeks ahead.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Five full-service bars sell drinks inside; the tavern serves food before shows. Outside food or drinks are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests who leave cannot return without a new ticket.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Collect will-call tickets at the venue box office with photo ID matching the purchaser’s name. Tour and guest-list tickets usually arrive on the event day close to door time.

Official source
