Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lenovo Center does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Lenovo Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is accessible via Edwards Mill Road from I-40 or Wade Avenue. The main entrance to the Lenovo Center parking lots is on the left. For detailed parking options and pricing, refer to the Parking Guide.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Lenovo Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Lenovo Center, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lenovo Center offers a variety of food and drink options including gourmet hot dogs, fresh-breaded chicken, and signature mac & cheese bowls. Special offerings include Sup Crush branded vodka drinks and Cheerwine Slushies.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Exit / Re-entry into Lenovo Center is NOT permitted, including for smoking.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

To purchase tickets, call 919-865-1510, or visit the NC State Ticket Office.

Official source
