Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lumen Field enforces a clear bag policy to enhance public safety. Only clear bags, small clutches, and clear backpacks within specified dimensions are allowed.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags not exceeding 12" x 6" x 12"
  • One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags
  • Clear backpacks with no more than 2 pockets
  • Small clutches/purses/wallets not exceeding 4.5" x 6.5"

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
  • Bags with pockets, zippered compartments, metal frames, or extra straps

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Lumen Field. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lumen Field offers on-site parking at the Lumen Field Parking Garage and North Lot, both open daily. Parking rates may vary on event days, and pre-purchase is recommended due to limited availability. Oversized vehicles are only accommodated in the North Lot.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Lumen Field's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Additional ADA parking is available at the Union Station Garage on a first come, first served basis.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lumen Field offers a diverse range of food and drink options inspired by Northwest flavors. Highlights include specialty pizzas, burgers, poke nachos, and a variety of beverages including local craft beers and cocktails. Some stands feature Amazon's Just Walk Out Technology for a seamless experience.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Lumen Field's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Lumen Field. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
