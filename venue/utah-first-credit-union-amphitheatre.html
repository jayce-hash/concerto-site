Know before you go

What matters at Utah First Credit Union Amphitheatre.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Only small clutches, wristlets, or fanny packs no bigger than 6”x 9” and clear plastic bags no bigger than 12"x12"x6” are allowed. All bags are subject to search upon entry.

Verified 2026-08-20

Parking

Parking

General admission parking is included with your ticket. Upgraded options such as Premier Parking, Ultra Premier Parking, and Reserved Parking are available, with Reserved Parking ensuring a quick exit after the event. All parking lots are conveniently located on the east side of the venue.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

Rideshare drop off and pick up is available at 5150 Upper Ridge Rd., West Valley City, UT 84118.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Utah First Credit Union Amphitheatre offers a variety of food and drink options, including Mosh Burger's hormone-free beef burgers, Rebel Hen's crispy chicken tenders, and Ziggy's Magic Pizza Shop's NY-inspired pizzas. Koko's Bavarian provides German-inspired sausages and pretzels, while general concessions offer popcorn, nachos, and a selection of beverages. CVT Premium Soft Serve Ice Cream is also available in a convenient pouch.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

The accessible entrance to our lawn accessibility section is located on the south & north sides of the front of the lawn, behind the 300s sections. The accessible entrance is a paved ramp that leads to the pit and is located on both the south & north side of the 200s sections.

Verified 2026-08-20
