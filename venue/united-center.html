Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

100% ADA compliant. Wheelchair seating on all levels. Elevators near Gates 3 & 7. ADA parking in Lot G off Damen Ave. Sensory Room at Gate 2½. Sensory bags at Guest Relations Gates 2 & 6. Contact: 312-455-4500.
