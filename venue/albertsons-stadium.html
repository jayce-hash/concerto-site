Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Fans may bring one clear plastic or vinyl bag up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches, a one-gallon clear freezer bag, or a small purse/clutch up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches. Large purses, backpacks, luggage, computer bags, and bags over the limit are prohibited.

Gates & Entrances

All Albertsons Stadium entries open two hours before kickoff for Boise State football. Most tickets use an any-ticket, any-gate policy, with exceptions for Stueckle Sky Center, North End Zone, and student tickets; check the ticket for the suggested entry.
