Bag policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Fans may bring one clear plastic or vinyl bag up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches, a one-gallon clear freezer bag, or a small purse/clutch up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches. Large purses, backpacks, luggage, computer bags, and bags over the limit are prohibited.

Allowed

  • clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches
  • one-gallon clear freezer bags
  • small purse/clutch up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • large purses
  • backpacks
  • luggage
  • computer bags
  • bags larger than permitted size
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 4, 2026

All Albertsons Stadium entries open two hours before kickoff for Boise State football. Most tickets use an any-ticket, any-gate policy, with exceptions for Stueckle Sky Center, North End Zone, and student tickets; check the ticket for the suggested entry.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Game-day parking is available across Boise State campus and off campus. The current stadium fan guide lists reserved and day-of options; availability and event pricing vary, so use the current interactive map before arrival.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 4, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in the current Albertsons Stadium fan guide. Use the current event map and signed drop-off/pickup areas on event day.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Accessible parking and seating are available. The current fan guide lists accessible game-day parking and wheelchair-accessible shuttle service, while the Boise State Ticket Office can assist with accessible seating.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Albertsons Stadium concessions are cashless; credit/debit cards, mobile payments, and prepaid debit cards are accepted. Food and beverage offerings can change by event.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 4, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Albertsons Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 4, 2026

For ticket assistance, use the current Boise State Athletics Ticket Office information. Mobile-ticket or game-day issues can be handled through the main box office, subject to event instructions.
