Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Scotiabank Arena enforces a restricted bag policy allowing only small purses, clutches, or fanny packs smaller than 16.5cm x 11.5cm. Bags not meeting this requirement may be subject to x-ray inspection.

Allowed

  • small purses
  • clutch
  • fanny pack type bags smaller than 16.5cm x 11.5cm
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Main entrance on Bay Street. Connected to Union Station via underground PATH walkway.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Scotiabank Arena offers two levels of parking for private suite holders and office tenants, with a flat fee of $50. Accessible parking is limited and requires advance booking. Public parking is available nearby at 15 York Street with indoor access to the arena.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Scotiabank Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fully ADA accessible. Wheelchair seating, elevators, and accessible services throughout.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Scotiabank Arena offers a diverse range of food and drink options, including gourmet hot dogs, sushi, poutine, and classic burgers. Specialty bars serve premium spirits and cocktails, enhancing the game or concert experience.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

No re-entry policy.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Box Office on Bay Street. Government-issued photo ID required.
