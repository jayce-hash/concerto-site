Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All guests entering U.S. Bank Stadium will have their bags searched and scanned. Permissible bags will be allowed after inspection.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at U.S. Bank Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There are over 20,000 parking spaces available within a 20-minute walk of U.S. Bank Stadium. Parking lots typically open six hours before events and close two hours after. Accessible parking is available in all lots and ramps.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in U.S. Bank Stadium's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bank Stadium is designed with accessible parking, accessible vehicle drop off areas, power-assisted doors, restrooms, concessions, water fountains, ATMs, seating areas, assistive listening devices.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

U.S. Bank Stadium offers a diverse range of food and beverage options, including local Minnesota flavors. Highlights include bratwurst, cheeseburgers, cheese curds, and a variety of snacks like popcorn and nachos. Beverage options feature craft beers and cocktails.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in U.S. Bank Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bank Stadium Ticket Office is located on the Northwest corner of the stadium between the Legacy and Polaris Gates.

Official source
