Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All bags are subject to inspection. Only personal handbags and bags containing baby items are permitted.

Allowed

  • personal handbags
  • bags containing baby items

Not allowed

  • bags larger than 14’’ x 12’’ x 6’’
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Videotron Centre. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is accessible via Soumande St. and Avenue du Colisée. Customers have free access to the P2B parking lot via Soumande St., except on event days when parking fees apply.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Videotron Centre's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Videotron Centre, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Videotron Centre offers a variety of high-quality food concessions with menus to suit all tastes and budgets, including gourmet options in premium areas. Club Bud lounge bar provides a wide selection of Labatt beers in a festive atmosphere.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Videotron Centre's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Videotron Centre. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
