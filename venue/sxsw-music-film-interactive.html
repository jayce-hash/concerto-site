Bag policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

SXSW bag policies vary by venue. Attendees are encouraged to travel light; luggage and large cases are not permitted, and larger bags may be inspected. Major Film & TV and Music Festival venues publish their own size/clear-bag limits.

Not allowed

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at SXSW (Music, Film & Interactive). Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Driving and parking in downtown Austin during SXSW can be expensive and time-consuming due to street closures and increased traffic. It is recommended to use alternative transportation options like walking, shuttles, or rideshares.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in SXSW (Music, Film & Interactive)'s current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by SXSW (Music, Film & Interactive), but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Food and beverage availability varies across SXSW venues and activations. Check the individual venue or event listing rather than relying on one festival-wide concessions menu.

Re-entry

Checked Sep 4, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in SXSW (Music, Film & Interactive)'s current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at SXSW (Music, Film & Interactive). Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
