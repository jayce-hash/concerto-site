Know before you go

What matters at SXSW (Music, Film & Interactive).

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

SXSW bag policies vary by venue. Attendees are encouraged to travel light; luggage and large cases are not permitted, and larger bags may be inspected. Major Film & TV and Music Festival venues publish their own size/clear-bag limits.

Parking

Parking

Driving and parking in downtown Austin during SXSW can be expensive and time-consuming due to street closures and increased traffic. It is recommended to use alternative transportation options like walking, shuttles, or rideshares.

Rideshare
