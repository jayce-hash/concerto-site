Bag policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

For U.S. Rolling Loud festivals, bags must generally be clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC and no larger than 12 x 6 x 12 inches. One-gallon clear freezer bags, small clutches up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches, and empty clear hydration packs are also allowed.

Allowed

  • clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches
  • one-gallon clear freezer bags
  • small clutches up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches
  • empty clear hydration packs

Not allowed

  • non-clear backpacks, purses, or bags outside the listed exceptions
  • tinted or opalescent bags
