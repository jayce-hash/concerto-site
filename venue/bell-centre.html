Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Bell Centre prioritizes security by conducting electronic searches at entrances. Smoking and vaping are prohibited inside the venue.

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Bell Centre. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Bell Centre is centrally located and easily accessible, with various parking options nearby. Pricing and specific lot details are not provided on the website.

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Bell Centre's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Bell Centre, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Bell Centre offers a variety of quick serve counters providing a range of food and beverage options for visitors. Guests can enjoy a quick meal on the go with diverse choices available throughout the venue.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Bell Centre's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Bell Centre. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
