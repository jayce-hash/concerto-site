Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches may enter after inspection. Nonclear bags receive an extra search.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Mobile tickets are scanned at entry. The Music Hall is primarily standing room; check the event for door time and allow time for bag search.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue lists nearby parking arrangements and links its ParkWhiz booking option; the rate depends on the concert.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ does not designate a rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Contact the box office at 216-523-2583 about an accessible entrance or parking; the Music Hall is mainly standing room, with some reserved VIP seating.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The adjacent House of Blues Restaurant & Bar serves meals; a same-day restaurant purchase of at least $25 per person can qualify for priority entry at most shows. Outside food and drinks are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests can re-enter when they retain both a proper hand stamp and ticket stub.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office opens at 4 p.m. on show days; most event tickets use mobile entry, so consult the ticket confirmation for any will-call arrangement.

Official source
