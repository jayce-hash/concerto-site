Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Ohio Stadium does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Parking

Parking

Parking at Ohio Stadium is available through season permits or day-of-game sales. Season permits are reserved for Buckeye Club members, while day-of-game parking ranges from $30 to $60 depending on proximity. Arrive early to secure your spot.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Ohio Stadium's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

Ohio Stadium offers a wide variety of food and drink options, including fan favorites like hot dogs, bratwurst, and nachos, as well as local specialties such as Hot Chicken Takeover and Barrio. Mobile ordering is available at all permanent stands, allowing fans to skip the line and enjoy their meals faster.

Accessibility

Accessibility

ADA Shuttle: A wheelchair accessible shuttle will transport guests in need (and their companions) from the disability parking lots to Ohio Stadium. Accessible restrooms are located near sections 8A, 9A, 10A, 11A, 17A, 18A, 19A, 28A, 33A, 34A, 37A, 38A, 3B, 12B, 28B, 7C, 10C, 11C, 13C, 14C, 15C, 19C, 20C, 23C, 28C, 29C, 17D, 19D, 25D.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Effective September 2008: The Ohio State Ticket Office will no longer accept outside will call. Will call located at the end of the 1st baseline at Bill Davis Baseball Stadium and is open 1 hours prior to game time on home game days.

Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Guests entering with medical or child care needs must enter through the Steinbrenner Band Center entrance (between Gate 10 & 12) or at the Press Entrance (between the Huntington Club and Gate 23) to have their items inspected.
