Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For safety and efficiency, only small clutches, belt bags, fanny-packs, or clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” are allowed. Backpacks are not permitted.

Allowed

  • small clutches
  • belt bags
  • fanny-packs
  • clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”

Not allowed

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at The Danforth Music Hall. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Danforth Music Hall does not have its own parking. There is paid public parking available on Danforth Avenue and the surrounding streets. Additionally, there is a Green P lot located behind Shoppers Drug Mart across the street from the venue.

Official source
