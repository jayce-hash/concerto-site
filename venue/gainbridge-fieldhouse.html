Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The bag policy at Gainbridge Fieldhouse requires guests to travel light and subjects them to visual inspections. Bags larger than 6"x9"x1.5” are not allowed.

Not allowed

  • Alcohol, drugs, illegal substances, or any paraphernalia associated with drug use
  • Audio/Video recorders, professional cameras, tripods
  • Backpacks
  • Bags larger than 6"x9"x1.5”
  • Beach balls or any projectiles or missiles
  • Cans, bottles, coolers, or other similar containers
  • Clothing that displays obscene/offensive language and/or images
  • Drones
  • Fireworks
  • Hard-sided bags
  • Laser pens/pointers of any type
  • Military or Tactical style clothing/gear
  • Masks or Full Facial Covering
  • Noisemakers such as air horns
  • Objects that obstruct another guest’s view
  • Outside food or drinks
  • Pepper spray/Mace
  • Poles or sticks
  • Signs, flags, banners larger 11″X17″ or affixed to any pole
  • Signs that display obscene/offensive language and/or images
  • Skateboards/roller blades
  • Tripods, mono pods, or selfie sticks
  • Weapons of any type, including chains, knives, firearms, and spiked jewelry
  • Any other item deemed unacceptable by event/building management

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Gainbridge Fieldhouse. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There are several parking options within walking distance to Gainbridge Fieldhouse, including the Virginia Avenue Parking Garage, which is connected via a sky bridge. Parking passes can be purchased in advance through ParkWhiz.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Gainbridge Fieldhouse's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Gainbridge Fieldhouse, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The KeyBank Guest Chef Program at Gainbridge Fieldhouse features local chefs showcasing their signature dishes during Pacers games and select events. Highlights include a variety of cuisines such as classic American, Indian, and soul food, providing fans with a taste of Indy's diverse culinary scene.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Gainbridge Fieldhouse's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Box Office hours are typically Monday-Friday from 12:00 p.m. The Box Office is located in Gainbridge Fieldhouse in the Ascension St.
