Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Fillmore Detroit does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at The Fillmore Detroit. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Fillmore Detroit has partnered with ParkWhiz for easy and reliable parking options. Accessible parking is available on Elizabeth Street and Columbia Street, with paved terrain and street crossings to the venue.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in The Fillmore Detroit's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Limited storage is available for wheelchairs, canes, walkers, and other mobility devices. The accessible restroom is located on the main level off to the left of the main floor.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Fillmore Detroit offers a Fast Lane and State Bar Pass for quick entry and access to drinks at The State Bar, which is attached to the venue. There are no specific details about food options available on the venue's website.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in The Fillmore Detroit's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at The Fillmore Detroit. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
