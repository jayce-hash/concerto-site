Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Bags up to 14 × 14 × 6 inches are permitted and searched. Backpacks, including drawstring and fanny packs, are prohibited; medical and parenting bags are exceptions.

Allowed

  • Bags up to 14 × 14 × 6 inches
  • Medical bags
  • Parenting bags

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Drawstring bags
  • Fanny packs
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Doors typically open 60 to 90 minutes before the event; the precise time varies by artist or promoter. Guests undergo security screening at entry.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The arena has a PARC-operated multilevel garage beneath it and says more than 21,000 spaces are within five blocks. Advance reservations are available through its preferred parking partners.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published a specific rideshare pickup or drop-off location in its A-Z guide.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

An accessible drop-off area is on Main Street between Second and Third Streets, with ramp access at Pepsi Plaza. The arena offers accessible seating and sensory bags at the Fan Assistance booth on the main concourse.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The arena has more than 170 fixed and portable concessions points. Concessions are cashless and accept major cards and mobile payments.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Re-entry is prohibited during events.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Will-call tickets may be collected at any open ticket-office window starting 60 minutes before the event; bring a valid picture ID. Certain VIP and promoter pickups may have different times.
