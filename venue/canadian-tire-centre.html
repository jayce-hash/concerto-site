What matters at Canadian Tire Centre.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags are restricted to clutch or wallet style bags smaller than 4″x6″x1.5″ and will undergo contactless security screening. Hard-sided bags and backpacks are not permitted.

Parking

Parking

Parking options at the Canadian Tire Centre include Slap Shot Lots and General Parking, with prices ranging from $21 to $40 depending on the event and lot. Payment for Slap Shot Lots is made via the Indigo Neo Parking App, while other lots can be pre-purchased online through Ticketmaster.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Canadian Tire Centre's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

Canadian Tire Centre offers a variety of dining options including Bert's Bar with stone-fired pizzas and burgers, Legacy with Brady's Burger, and Club CIBC with a small plates menu and extensive wine selection. Brookstreet Lounge provides a buffet for VIP members, while Ice Level Bar offers public beverage service.

Accessibility

To request documentation in an accessible format or with communication support please email accessibility@ottawasenators.ca or contact SSE’s Director, Security and Guest Services by phone at – 613-599-0122. Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Box Office is located on the east side of the building at the main entrance, Gate 1. The box office opens two hours prior to hockey games, two hours prior to concert show time and is closed on non-event days.

Gates & Entrances
