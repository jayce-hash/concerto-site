Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags are restricted to clutch or wallet style bags smaller than 4″x6″x1.5″ and will undergo contactless security screening. Hard-sided bags and backpacks are not permitted.

Allowed

  • clutch or wallet style bags smaller than 4″x6″x1.5″

Not allowed

  • hard-sided bags
  • backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Unless otherwise stated, doors open 60 minutes before non-Senators events. Doors open for Ottawa Senators games two hours before game start time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking options at the Canadian Tire Centre include Slap Shot Lots and General Parking, with prices ranging from $21 to $40 depending on the event and lot. Payment for Slap Shot Lots is made via the Indigo Neo Parking App, while other lots can be pre-purchased online through Ticketmaster.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Canadian Tire Centre's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

To request documentation in an accessible format or with communication support please email accessibility@ottawasenators.ca or contact SSE’s Director, Security and Guest Services by phone at – 613-599-0122. To obtain further information relating to Canadian Tire Centre’s service animal and support person policies, please contact accessibility@ottawasenators.ca or SSE’s Director, Security and Guest Services at – 613-599-0122.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Canadian Tire Centre offers a variety of dining options including Bert's Bar with stone-fired pizzas and burgers, Legacy with Brady's Burger, and Club CIBC with a small plates menu and extensive wine selection. Brookstreet Lounge provides a buffet for VIP members, while Ice Level Bar offers public beverage service.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Canadian Tire Centre's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Box Office is located on the east side of the building at the main entrance, Gate 1. The box office opens two hours prior to hockey games, two hours prior to concert show time and is closed on non-event days.
