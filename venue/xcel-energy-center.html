Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For safety and speed of entry, bags are discouraged at events. Purses up to 12” x 12” x 6” are allowed at designated X-ray entry points. Wristlets, clutches, or wallets 4” x 6” x 1.5” or smaller are permitted at all entrances.

Allowed

  • Purses no larger than 12” x 12” x 6”
  • Wristlet, clutch or wallets 4” x 6” x 1.5” or smaller

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Coolers
  • Large totes

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Xcel Energy Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at RiverCentre and Kellogg Parking Ramps, both connected to the arena. Event day parking is first come, first served, and ParkWhiz reservations are not available for these ramps.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Gate 4 on 5th Street is the official pick-up and drop-off location for ride share services.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Xcel Energy Center, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Xcel Energy Center offers a variety of food and drink options including traditional sausages, craft beers, pizza, burgers, and more. Special offerings include the Wild $6 Happy Hour and the Wild Market with Amazon’s Just Walk Out technology. Alcohol sales end during the third period of Minnesota Wild games or one hour before the end of other events.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Xcel Energy Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Xcel Energy Center. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
