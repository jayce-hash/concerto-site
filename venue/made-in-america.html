Made in America does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Made in America. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

No current festival transportation or parking plan is published. Do not use unrelated museum directions as festival guidance; consult the official event site if a new edition is announced.

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Made in America's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

After using ADA Entry, please visit the Accessibility Services Hub so that you can use ADA Entry for the rest of the event.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The National Constitution Center offers a variety of food and beverage options including Delegates' Cafe and Starbucks. Delegates' Cafe features salads, sandwiches, and daily specials like Philly cheesesteaks, while Starbucks provides handcrafted beverages and premium teas. Catering and boxed lunch options are available for groups.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Made in America's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lost and Found will be open from 11am-4pm on Monday, September 5th at the box office, located at Aviator Park (outside the venue’s main entrance). Will call is located at Aviator Park.
