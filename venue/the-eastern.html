Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Small purses and bags are allowed after search, without a clear-bag requirement. Large bags and backpacks, including laptop bags, are prohibited; no maximum dimensions are published.

Allowed

  • Small purse or bag

Not allowed

  • Large bags
  • Backpacks
  • Laptop bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The main entrance is at 777 Memorial Drive, with another accessible entrance at 800 Old Flat Shoals Road. The interval from doors to showtime is typically an hour; check the event listing.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Parking is available in the RT Dairies deck and a second deck on Old Flat Shoals Road. The deck gives the first 30 minutes free; rates are posted by the parking operator.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Eastern’s venue FAQ names its entrances and parking decks but does not designate a rideshare pickup or drop-off curb.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Both the 777 Memorial Drive main entrance and the 800 Old Flat Shoals Road lower entrance are accessible. ADA viewing is on the floor and upper mezzanine; request an interpreter two weeks ahead.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drinks are available on the rooftop during shows, opening at door time. Outside food and beverages and bottles are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
  • Bottles
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue’s published FAQ and prohibited-item rules do not state a general re-entry policy.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call tickets can be collected from the venue box office on show night starting two hours before doors. The box office faces the Dairies Courtyard beside the main entrance.

Official source
