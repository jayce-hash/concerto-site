Know before you go

What matters at Raymond James Stadium.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Raymond James Stadium enforces a No Bag policy for all events, allowing only one clutch purse no larger than 4.5” by 6.5”. Bags larger than this size are not permitted, even if clear.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Parking at Raymond James Stadium is cashless and contactless, with prices varying by event. Parking is first-come, first-served, and advance purchase is recommended. RV and camper parking is available in designated lots.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

The rideshare drop-off and pick-up area is located at Steinbrenner Field. For events with higher volumes, check the website for updates.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Raymond James Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, including local specialties and chef-driven creations. Highlights include $6 domestic beer cans, a $20 gameday combo, and the Tampa Chef Series featuring local culinary offerings. The venue emphasizes locally sourced and sustainable ingredients.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Raymond James Stadium, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Re-entry is not permitted unless in the event of a medical emergency.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Raymond James Stadium Ticket Office is only open on event days; it does not have normal operating hours. For lost or stolen tickets on event day, visit the Ticket Office located in the South Plaza between Gates C and D.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Raymond James Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.
