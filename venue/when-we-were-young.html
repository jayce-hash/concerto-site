When We Were Young

Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

When We Were Young does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at When We Were Young. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The festival is taking 2026 off and has announced a return to Las Vegas in October 2027. Review the official site for the returning edition's transportation and parking plan.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Uber and Lyft ride-share services are available, with pick-up and drop-off near the main entrance.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by When We Were Young, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The venue offers a variety of quick-serve food and drink options, including gourmet coffees and pastries at Starbucks, fresh doughnuts at Krispy Kreme, and Italian gelato at Cocolini. Guests can also enjoy classic fast-food offerings from Popeyes and Dairy Queen, as well as Asian cuisine from Pick Up Stix.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in When We Were Young's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at When We Were Young. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
