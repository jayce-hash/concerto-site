Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Oracle Park enforces a clear bag policy to enhance safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Parking

Parking

Oracle Park's main lot is Pier 48/Lot A across McCovey Cove, with additional private garages and lots throughout San Francisco's South Beach and Mission Bay. Prepaid parking is recommended as drive-up is limited and expensive. Muni Metro and Caltrain (4th & King) both stop a short walk away.

Concessions

Concessions

Oracle Park offers a variety of food and beverage options including classic comfort foods with a modern twist, elevated ballpark classics, and hearty eats. Highlights include specialty items like Bacon Wrapped Hot Dogs, Pastrami Fries, and a selection of local and popular beers.

Accessibility

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Open on gamedays only, Will Call is located at the Box Office on King Street. The Box Office opens 2.5 hours before gametime.

Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Serving as the main entrance to the ballpark, the Willie Mays Gates are located at the corner of Third and King Streets. Exceptions for medical or ADA needs will be allowed, subject to re-screening by security through any of the entry gates.
