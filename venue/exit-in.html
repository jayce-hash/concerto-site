Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Backpacks of any kind are prohibited. The Exit/In FAQ does not list a maximum dimension or clear requirement for other bags.

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue is at 2208 Elliston Place. Concert door times appear on the venue calendar, and the box office opens at doors.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Nearby paid options include a garage across from the venue and a lot behind it on Louise Avenue; street spaces on Louise and Elliston Place are limited.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue recommends rideshare because parking is limited, but its FAQ does not designate a specific pickup or drop-off curb.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue is mainly standing room, with a limited number of seats available first come first served. Contact the venue before the show for ADA accommodations.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Outside food and beverages are prohibited. An outdoor smoking area is available to the left of the room.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Exit/In does not allow re-entry.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office sells tickets when doors open and the ticketing partner is TicketWeb; no general will-call pickup procedure is stated in its FAQ.

Official source
