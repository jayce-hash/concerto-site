What matters at Enterprise Center.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Enterprise Center does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Parking

Parking

Parking is available at the garage attached to Enterprise Center and other nearby facilities. Visitors can access parking from various streets including Clark Avenue and 18th Street. The City Traffic Department advises against using the 14th Street exit from I-64/U.S. 40 for better traffic flow.

Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Enterprise Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

Enterprise Center has concession stands and portable carts across the Plaza and Mezzanine levels with stadium staples, local/specialty foods, soft drinks, beer, wine, and mixed drinks. Offerings and locations can vary by event.

Accessibility

Accessibility

To purchase accessible tickets for an upcoming event, please call our Box Office ADA Hotline at 314-622-5420 or click here. Spaces featuring accessibility for oversized vans are available on the first level of the garage.

Re-Entry Policy

Gates & Entrances
