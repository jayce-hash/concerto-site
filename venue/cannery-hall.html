Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Purses and other bags must be smaller than 12 × 12 inches and are searched. Backpacks, large bags and tote bags are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Bag smaller than 12 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Large bags
  • Tote bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors open at the time printed on the concert ticket. The entrance is at 1 Cannery Row, and guests may line up at the front on show day.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue reports paid parking close to the building but does not publish a specific lot name or price on its FAQ.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Cannery Hall FAQ does not publish a designated rideshare pickup or drop-off location.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office handles ADA arrangements. Guests seeking an accommodation should contact the venue and arrive 10–15 minutes before doors.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue has bars that remain open as long as practical during shows. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests aged 21 or older may re-enter except at all-ages shows; those shows have no re-entry. Bags are searched again on return.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office at 1 Cannery Row opens one hour before scheduled performances; the FAQ does not give a separate will-call procedure.

Official source
