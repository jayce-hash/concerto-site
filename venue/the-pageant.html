Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags, purses and backpacks larger than 12 × 6 × 12 inches are prohibited; smaller bags are subject to the event security policy.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Larger bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Halo Bar offers early admission before the main doors for concert ticket holders, subject to capacity. Check the show listing for the main door time.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Pageant uses ramps to reach its tiers and provides an elevator; contact staff about an accessible viewing arrangement.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Outside beverages and beverage containers, including bottles, cups and flasks, are prohibited. Halo Bar provides a preshow bar and early entry when space permits.

Not allowed

  • Outside beverages
  • Outside beverage containers
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests over 21 may re-enter with a valid hand stamp or wristband. Guests under 21 cannot re-enter after leaving.

Official source
