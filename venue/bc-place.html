Bag Policy

Bag Policy

BC Place permits small opaque bags no larger than 6.5 by 8.5 inches and clear plastic bags no larger than 12 by 12 by 6 inches. All bags are searched.

Parking

Parking

Parking near BC Place ranges from $10 to $30 during events. Limited accessible parking is available, with a dedicated drop-off area for mobility devices at Gate F on Pacific Boulevard.

Gates & Entrances

Accessibility

Accessibility

BC Place's accessible drop-off and pick-up area is located at section 147, just off Pacific Blvd across from Plaza of Nations. Sensory bags containing fidget tools and noise-cancelling headphones are available, until supplies last, for checkout at no cost by leaving an ID at our any Guest Services Desk locations (Section 234 and Section 446) in the Stadium.
