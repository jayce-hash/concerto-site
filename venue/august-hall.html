Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Small to medium personal bags, purses and backpacks are permitted after a search. The FAQ does not give numeric size limits and there is no coat check.

Allowed

  • Small to medium personal bag
  • Small to medium backpack
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The show-night box office opens one hour before the published door time. Check the event listing for the actual door time and age requirement.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue names the Ellis O’Farrell Garage at 123 O’Farrell Street, Union Square Garage at 333 Post Street and California Parking Garage at 665 Sutter Street.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ gives its 420 Mason Street address and transit stops but does not designate a rideshare pickup area.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue is accessible; email the box office with the guest name and show for seating accommodation. ADA seating is limited to one companion per guest.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The FAQ says food is not sold at August Hall, though drinks are available; outside food and beverages are prohibited, except an empty water bottle.

Allowed

  • Empty water bottle

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests age 21 or older with valid ID may step outside to smoke and re-enter; guests under 21 cannot re-enter.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Tickets can be bought online or at the 420 Mason Street box office, which opens one hour before posted doors on show nights. The FAQ does not state a separate will-call process.

Official source
