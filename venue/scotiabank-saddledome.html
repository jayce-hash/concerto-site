Scotiabank Saddledome

Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Scotiabank Saddledome does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Driving: The Scotiabank Saddledome main entrance is located on the north side of the Saddledome off 14th Avenue and 5th Street S.E.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is limited due to construction, and fans without parking permits are encouraged to use public transit or off-site parking. Parking is managed by Calgary Parking and can be paid via the MyParking app or on-site pay machines.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Pick up and drop off locations can be found at the West Plaza or on 17th Ave Extensions.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Scotiabank Saddledome, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Scotiabank Saddledome offers a diverse range of food and beverage options, including classic concessions like popcorn and hot dogs, specialty items such as the Shorty short rib sandwich, and dietary options like vegan meals. Notable highlights include the Fireball Whisky Bar, Tacos and Tequila, and the Coca-Cola Test Kitchen with unique monthly culinary concepts.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

In keeping with our commitment to safety and security and as a result of a thorough review of best practices in the arena industry, effective July 1, 2018, exiting and re-entering the building during events (sporting and non-sporting) will not be permitted at the Scotiabank Saddledome.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Calgary Flames and Ticketmaster Ticket Office is located on the north side of the Saddledome, located off 14th Avenue and 5th Street S.E. The Will Call is located at the main ticket office at the Northeast corner of the Scotiabank Saddledome on the day of the event.

Official source
