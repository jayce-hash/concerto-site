Know before you go

What matters at Addition Financial Arena.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Addition Financial Arena enforces a clear bag policy, prohibiting backpacks and bags larger than 12'' x 6'' x 12''. Small purses no larger than 5.5'' x 8.5'' are allowed.

Parking

Parking

Event parking is available in Garages F, D, H, and lots D1 and D2, with advanced purchase options on Ticketmaster or at the Venue Box Office. Parking is free for UCF students and staff with a valid UCF ID during University events.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

Drop off locations for patrons are located at the corners of East Plaza Drive and Gemini Blvd and West Plaza Drive and Gemini Blvd.

Concessions

Concessions

Addition Financial Arena offers a variety of food and drink options, including Cuban sandwiches, chicken tenders, and artisan pizzas. The venue is cashless, accepting major credit cards and Apple Pay. Special offerings include exclusive student discounts at Knightmare Snacks during UCF basketball games.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Accessible seating is available on the main concourse, with elevators on the west side of the arena lobby. Accessible parking is offered in Garages D and F. Call 407-823-3070 in advance for interpreting, wheelchair, or other accommodation requests.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Once a guest leaves during an event, re-entry is not permitted unless another ticket is purchased.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Will Call

The box office is in front of the arena on East Plaza Drive. Will-call tickets may be collected only by the named account holder with valid government-issued ID. Regular hours are Monday–Friday, 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.; event-day hours vary.

Entrances & Gates

Entrance & Doors

Public doors generally open one hour before the scheduled event, although times may vary. Arrive early for security screening and use the entrance designated in event communications or on-site signage.
