Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Hard Rock Stadium allows clear plastic bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" and small bags or fanny packs up to 4.5" x 6.5". Bag searches will be conducted using X-Ray technology or by hand.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic bags that do not exceed 12" x 6" x 12"
  • Small bags or fanny packs that do not exceed 4.5" x 6.5"
  • Diaper bags

Not allowed

  • All purses or bags that exceed 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Non-clear bags that exceed 4.5” x 6.5”

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Hard Rock Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking at the stadium is sold out, but Park & Ride passes are still available. ADA parking is available in both inner and outer lots, with mobility assistance offered in outer lots. Wheelchair escorts are available from stadium gates to seating locations.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Hard Rock Stadium's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Hard Rock Stadium, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Hard Rock Stadium offers a diverse range of food options including American, Tex-Mex, Japanese, and more. Highlights include mobile ordering at select locations and a cashless payment system. Unique offerings feature upscale dining and international cuisines.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Hard Rock Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Hard Rock Stadium. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
