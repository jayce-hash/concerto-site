Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The ground-level entrance has a lift to the main general-admission floor, where staff can set up an accessible viewing area and chair at house right. The balcony is not accessible.

Official source

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call tickets can be picked up on show night after doors open. Phone barcodes are accepted when visible; the venue has no physical box office for advance purchases.
