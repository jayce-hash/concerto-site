Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Petersen Events Center enforces a clear bag policy allowing clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags not exceeding 12" x 6" x 12" and small clutch purses. Diaper bags and medically necessary items are exceptions but will be inspected.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags not exceeding 12" x 6" x 12"
  • One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags
  • Small clutch purses approximately 4.5" x 6.5"
  • Poster tubes (must be empty)

Not allowed

  • Coolers
  • Backpacks
  • Sealed packages
  • Food or Beverage (except for special medical needs)
  • Cans, bottles, glass or other similar containers
  • Video, audio, professional cameras, recording devices, tripods and bipods
  • Air horn

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Petersen Events Center offers a variety of food and beverage options through its premier service provider, Chartwells. Guests can enjoy menus from Baseline Grill, Fastbreak, Coaches Corner, and Hardwood Cafe, with additional premium options available in suites.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Exiting and re-entering the building with the same ticket is not permitted.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Will Call pick up is available on the day of the event when the box office opens. On site ticket sales are only available when the box office is open.
