Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

One clear plastic or vinyl bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches, including a clear backpack, or one nonclear clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches is permitted. Medical and child-accompanied diaper bags are inspected; no bag check is provided.

Allowed

  • Clear bag or backpack up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches
  • Inspected medical bag

Not allowed

  • Other bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Tickets are usually mobile entry and screenshots are invalid. Consult the show page for opening times and allow time for bag inspection.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Over 6,000 on-site spaces serve the complex; parking prices depend on the event, and payment is cashless. Lots open at least one hour before showtime.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Greensboro Complex parking page does not specify one official rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Marked spaces near the Coliseum main entrance need a state-issued placard or plate and are subject to the regular parking fee. Accessible tickets are sold by the venue ticket office.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concession stands sell food and drinks and accept card payment only. Outside food and drinks are prohibited unless a special dietary need is arranged in advance.

Allowed

  • Prearranged dietary exception

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drink otherwise
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Tickets generally allow only one entry; exiting prevents re-entry unless a specific event announces an exception.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will Call opens two hours before ticketed events; show government ID and the purchasing credit card, with the same name on both. The Coliseum ticket office opens at noon on arena event days.

Official source
