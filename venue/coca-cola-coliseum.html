Coca-Cola Coliseum

Know before you go

What matters at Coca-Cola Coliseum.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Coca-Cola Coliseum enforces a restricted bag policy to minimize contact and prevent prohibited items. Only small purse type bags smaller than 16.5cm x 11.5cm are allowed.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

There are over 5000 parking spots available adjacent to Coca-Cola Coliseum, operated by Exhibition Place. Rates are subject to change, and special event rates may apply. Accessible parking is available in a surface lot directly outside the facility.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Coca-Cola Coliseum's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Coca-Cola Coliseum offers a variety of friendly, fast, and fresh food and beverage options for visitors to enjoy during games and shows. The venue features a range of concessions located throughout the concourse, ensuring guests have access to delicious snacks and drinks.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Accessible parking near Coca-Cola Coliseum is available on a first-come, first-served basis in Exhibition Place Lot 3, located just southwest of the building, between the Enercare Centre (formerly the Direct Energy Centre) and BMO Field.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Coca-Cola Coliseum is a non-smoking facility.* Fans wishing to smoke must exit the building however you will not be allowed to re-enter the facility, per the Coca-Cola Coliseum re-entry policy. As a security measure, no re-entry is permitted after you have exited the Coca-Cola Coliseum gates.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Tickets may also be available in person at the Scotiabank Arena Box Office or the Coca-Cola Coliseum Ticket Office, however, this office is open only when there is a ticketed event in the building. Coca-Cola Coliseum Will Call is located at the Ticket Office inside the main doors of the building.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Coca-Cola Coliseum. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Verified 2026-08-20
