Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Portland’5 uses touchless screening that allows guests to carry a bag without emptying it. Its general attending guide does not specify a bag dimension or require clear bags; some individual events publish additional restrictions.

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests pass through a touchless security system with bags in hand; medical-device users may ask for alternate screening. Arrival and seating times depend on the event.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Keller offers accessibility information including seating, guest services and accessible parking. Some historic seating areas require steps, so confirm the accessible seating location when booking.

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Lobby concessions sell snacks, drinks and alcohol and accept cards; free cash-to-card machines are available. Outside food and drinks are prohibited, and lidded drinks are usually allowed into the auditorium.
