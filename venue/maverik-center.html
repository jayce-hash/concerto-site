Rideshare

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Maverik Center, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Maverik Center offers a variety of food and drink options including burgers, fries, nachos, and ice cream. Highlights include the Bacon Cheeseburger, Chicken Tinga Tacos, and a selection of flavored milkshakes. Special offerings feature a range of desserts and premium items.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Maverik Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.
