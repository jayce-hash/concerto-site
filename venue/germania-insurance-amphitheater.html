Germania Insurance Amphitheater

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The amphitheater has a specific 2026 bag policy: a clear tote up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches or a one-gallon clear bag; small nonclear clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 × 2 inches are permitted. Bags are searched at entry.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches
  • One-gallon clear bag
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 × 2 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
  • Backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests enter through Grand Plaza gates after metal-detector and bag screening. Parking opens an hour before gates, while actual gate time is given on each event listing.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Standard parking in Lots E, F and H is included with the concert ticket. Upgrades are typically in Lots C and D, and parking opens one hour before gates unless the event says otherwise.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The official concert rideshare and taxi drop-off and pickup area is Lot E; other lots are not approved for passenger pickup.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible seats are available in sections 102–106 and 202–206 and on the GA lawn. Complimentary accessible parking is in Lots E, F and H with a valid placard, and wheelchair escorts can be requested at Guest Services.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Outside food and beverages are prohibited. An empty, reusable collapsible water bottle or cup up to 20 ounces is allowed.

Allowed

  • Empty collapsible bottle or cup up to 20 ounces

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

No re-entry is allowed after leaving the gates.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Tickets are delivered digitally as mobile QR codes through Ticketmaster and presented on a phone at the gate. The Grand Plaza Box Office opens two hours before gates for event-day accessible-ticket sales, subject to availability.

Official source
