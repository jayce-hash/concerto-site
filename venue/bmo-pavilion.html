Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All bags and personal items are subject to inspection at the entrance. Security screening is mandatory for all attendees.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at BMO Pavilion. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is on a first-come, first-served basis with no tailgating allowed. Bicycle parking is available outside the South Gate. Consider using SpotHero or Parkwhiz to reserve parking in advance.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Rideshare pickups and drop-offs are directed to North Jackson Street near the South Gate.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by BMO Pavilion, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food and beverage availability at BMO Pavilion varies by event and operating setup. Review the linked official guest information before arrival.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in BMO Pavilion's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Will Call tickets can be picked up at the South Gate Box Office on the day of the show only. During Summrfest, the Box Office is open from 10:00 am - 9:00 pm on festival days.

Official source
