Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Approved bags are clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches, one-gallon clear resealable bags, or clutches up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches. Clear diaper bags that meet the size rule are permitted.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear resealable bags
  • Clutches up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches
  • Compliant clear diaper bags

Not allowed

  • Non-compliant bags

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The northwest entrance serves player-guest, recruit, and high-school-coach will call; other public entrance assignments and opening times vary by event.

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026
