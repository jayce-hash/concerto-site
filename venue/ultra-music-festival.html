What matters at Ultra Music Festival.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Permitted bags include clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags no larger than 13 by 17 inches, one-gallon clear zip bags, small hand-sized clutches, qualifying hydration packs, and waist packs. Medical exceptions are inspected individually.

Parking

Parking

There is no parking at the Bayfront Park festival site. Ultra recommends Brightline, Tri-Rail, Metrorail, or Metromover; downtown garages and rideshare services are available away from the entrance.
