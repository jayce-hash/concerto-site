Bag policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Only small backpacks or bags up to 25 liters in capacity are permitted.

Allowed

  • small backpacks or bags up to 25L

Not allowed

  • larger backpacks or bags
  • tablets and iPads
  • motorcycle helmets

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Movistar Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Movistar Arena offers a diverse range of food and drink options, including hot dogs from Oscar Mayer, pizzas from Papa Johns, and iberico products from Navidul. Vegan options are available at Better Balance, and mobile service is provided by BEBERAPID for convenience.

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Movistar Arena. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
