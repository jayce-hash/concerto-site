Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Target Field enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Allowed

  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag (1 Gallon)
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”
  • Seat cushions without pockets, zippers or concealable areas up to 18” wide

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags and wallets exceeding 6.5" x 4.5"
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags
  • Briefcases
  • Fanny packs
  • Cinch bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Target Field. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Target Field is in downtown Minneapolis with numerous ramps and lots within a few blocks (ABC Ramps, Hawthorne, and others); prepaid parking is recommended. The METRO light rail and Northstar commuter rail both stop at Target Field Station next to the ballpark.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Target Field's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Premier Suite Level and all individual suites at Target Field are fully wheelchair accessible. A guest drop-off area is located outside of Gate 29 and Gate 14 for guests with mobility issues.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Target Field offers a variety of food and drink options, including classic comfort foods with bold flavors and modern twists. Highlights include specialty items like Bacon Wrapped Hot Dogs, Pastrami Fries, and indulgent treats like Soft Serve and Milkshakes. Fans can enjoy a selection of local and popular beers at the Beer Garden.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Target Field's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

On non-game days during the season, the Target Field Box Office is open from 10:00am – 4:00pm, Monday through Friday. The main Target Field Box Office is located on Target Plaza.
