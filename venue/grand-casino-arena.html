Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For safety and speed of entry, guests are discouraged from bringing bags. Purses up to 12” x 12” x 6” are allowed at designated X-ray points, while smaller wristlets, clutches, or wallets are allowed at all entrances.

Allowed

  • Purses no larger than 12” x 12” x 6”
  • Wristlet, clutch or wallets 4” x 6” x 1.5” or smaller

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Coolers
  • Large totes

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Grand Casino Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Grand Casino Arena offers parking at RiverCentre and Kellogg Parking Ramps, both connected to the venue. Parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis for most events. ParkWhiz reservations are not available for these ramps.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Gate 4 on 5th Street is the official pick-up and drop-off location for ride share services.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Grand Casino Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Grand Casino Arena offers a variety of food and drink options including local craft beers, traditional sausages, and unique burgers. Highlights include the Wild Market with Amazon's Just Walk Out technology and the Wild $6 Happy Hour featuring discounted drinks and snacks.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Grand Casino Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Grand Casino Arena Box Office is located in the main lobby at the southeast entrance of Grand Casino Arena (Gate 1). Box Office Summer Hours (May 18 through Labor Day)
