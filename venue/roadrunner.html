Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Small bags are searched; bags larger than 14 × 14 inches, all backpacks, briefcases, luggage and duffels are prohibited. Coat check will not hold bags.

Allowed

  • Small searched bag up to 14 × 14 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Briefcases
  • Luggage
  • Duffel bags
  • Bags larger than 14 × 14 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

All guests pass through walk-through metal detectors and bags are inspected; there is no storage for restricted items. The listed event time is generally the door time, with music often beginning 30–60 minutes later.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

There is no dedicated lot; public paid garages are available at 71, 40 and 80 Guest Street. These garages have accessible spaces.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue guide does not designate an app-based rideshare pickup or drop-off zone.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

All entries are accessible, with ADA viewing in front of stage right and at the back of the balcony. Email the venue after buying a ticket to request accessible seating or ASL interpretation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue has bars and a Guest Room private bar; it accepts cash, cards and contactless payment. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue does not allow re-entry after leaving during the concert.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will Call at the Roadrunner box office opens when doors open on event days. Fee-free tickets may also be bought at that box office on show nights.

Official source
