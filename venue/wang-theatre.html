Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Boch Center entry policy describes guest screening but does not publish a fixed Wang Theatre bag size or clear-bag rule.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theatre screens guests at entry and can deny admission to a guest who declines screening; check the specific show for its doors and any added security rule.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Nearby options include Tufts Medical Center Garage adjacent to the Wang and other Theatre District garages. Check the venue directions page for booking and access.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Lyft is the Boch Center’s official rideshare partner; the FAQ does not specify a separate loading location at the Wang Theatre.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible seating is available in portions of the orchestra and orchestra boxes. The Wang has a Grand Lobby elevator to all floors and a chair lift to its Lower Lobby.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concessions are sold in the theatre lobby. Whether purchased food and drinks may go into the auditorium depends on the show.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ does not state a general re-entry policy; some forced-will-call events require immediate admission without readmittance.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will Call is typically available one hour before curtain with ID and either confirmation number or purchasing card. The box office also opens Tuesday–Saturday noon–6 p.m.

Official source
