Barclays Arena

Hamburg · DE

Know before you go

What matters at Barclays Arena.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags larger than DIN A4 size (21.0cm x 29.7cm) are not allowed inside the arena. Prohibited items include suitcases, boxes, motorcycle helmets, and strollers.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Parking costs EUR 10 per vehicle, with special rates for RVs. VIP parking is available for certain ticket holders. Parking lots are located near the arena, but access may be restricted during events at the nearby Volksparkstadion.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Barclays Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

The Barclays Arena offers a premium hospitality experience with areas designed for comfort and enjoyment. Visitors can relax in exclusive settings such as the Captain's Skyloft, Platinum, Premium Club, and Premium Lounge before or after events.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Barclays Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Barclays Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Barclays Arena. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Barclays Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.
