Rideshare

Rideshare

Guests who are using ride share services should be dropped off at the west entrance of the arena, in the drop off lane at Dickies Way. Guests can be picked up for ride share on Harley Avenue, which is located on the north end of the building.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Dickies Arena has a Sensory Needs Room, which is located on the northwest corner of the 200 level. If you require accessible seating and you have tickets in a non-accessible seating location, please contact the Dickies Arena Box Office at 817-402-9801 as soon as possible for accommodations.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Dickies Arena Box Office is located at 1911 Montgomery Street, on the southwest side of the venue and across from the Chevrolet Parking Garage. To purchase tickets for an upcoming event, please visit Ticketmaster.com or go to the Dickies Arena Box Office at 1911 Montgomery Street.
