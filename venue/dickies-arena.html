Bag policy

Checked Sep 30, 2026

Bags can be no larger than 12 x 6 x 12 in. Backpacks, luggage, duffel bags and messenger or laptop bags are not allowed, and clear bags are encouraged for faster entry.

Allowed

  • Bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 in
  • Clear bags (encouraged)

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Luggage
  • Duffel bags
  • Messenger and laptop bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Dickies Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 30, 2026

Park in the 2,200-space Chevrolet Garage at 3464 Trail Drive or the Yellow Lots across Trail Drive. Prices depend on the event, lots take cash or card, and they open 3.5 hours before showtime. Prepaid parking is available.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Pickup is on Harley Avenue on the north side of the arena, and drop-off is at the west entrance on Dickies Way.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Dickies Arena has a Sensory Needs Room, which is located on the northwest corner of the 200 level. If you require accessible seating and you have tickets in a non-accessible seating location, please contact the Dickies Arena Box Office at 817-402-9801 as soon as possible for accommodations.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Dickies Arena offers a variety of food and beverage options for event attendees, including traditional concession stands and specialty vendors. Guests can enjoy a range of snacks, meals, and drinks to enhance their event experience.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 30, 2026

Re-entry is not permitted at Dickies Arena.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Dickies Arena Box Office is located at 1911 Montgomery Street, on the southwest side of the venue and across from the Chevrolet Parking Garage. To purchase tickets for an upcoming event, please visit Ticketmaster.com or go to the Dickies Arena Box Office at 1911 Montgomery Street.

Official source
