Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Heritage Bank Center does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Heritage Bank Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at the East Garage for pre-paid parking pass holders and the Central Riverfront Garage. Both garages are conveniently located near the venue. No specific pricing information is provided.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Heritage Bank Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Heritage Bank Center, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Heritage Bank Center offers food and drink options through its partners, including All Access Bar & Grill located on the 5th floor. Nearby, guests can also enjoy specials at Holy Grail and Tin Roof, which are within walking distance.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Heritage Bank Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Heritage Bank Center. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
