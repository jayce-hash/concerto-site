Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Small bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" are allowed, but all bags will be searched prior to entry. No backpacks are permitted.

Allowed

  • Small bags up to 12" x 6" x 12"

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Outside food, drinks, snacks, or candy
  • Professional recording equipment
  • GoPros
  • Smart glasses
  • Tablets
  • Gimbals
  • Steadycams
  • Tripods
  • Selfie sticks
  • Glass
  • Chairs
  • Blankets

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at The Fillmore Silver Spring. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There are two public parking garages nearby: Spring Cameron Garage is one block away and Town Square Garage is two blocks away. For rates and hours, visit the county website. The Fillmore Silver Spring has partnered with ParkWhiz for easy parking options.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in The Fillmore Silver Spring's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by The Fillmore Silver Spring, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Fillmore Silver Spring offers a selection of beer, wine, spirits, and non-alcoholic beverages along with food and snacks. The venue is fully cashless, accepting major credit cards for all purchases. Guests can also enjoy pre-show bites in the Vertigo Lounge.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in The Fillmore Silver Spring's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at The Fillmore Silver Spring. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
