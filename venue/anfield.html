Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Only A5-size bags or official show merchandise in the provided clear carrier bag may enter. Admitted bags are searched and tagged; large bags and suitcases are prohibited.

Allowed

  • A5-size bag
  • Show merchandise in official clear carrier

Not allowed

  • Large bags
  • Suitcases
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Check the concert ticket and stadium map for the assigned entrance; concert entrance routes can differ from match days. Arriving before doors or camping near the stadium is prohibited.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The official concert travel guide advises against residential-street parking and describes the concert bus from Sandhills Station.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The designated post-show pickup and drop-off area is Utting Avenue Car Park; arrange to meet your driver there.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Wheelchair places must be arranged through the disability ticketing team; pitch standing is not wheelchair accessible. Accessible toilets and two Changing Places facilities are available.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drink vendors operate inside the stadium, with accessible serving counters in most areas. Outside drinks in plastic bottles over 500 ml and glass containers are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Plastic drink bottles over 500 ml
  • Glass bottles
Official source
