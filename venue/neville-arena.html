Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Auburn’s basketball guide prohibits backpacks and bags larger than 12 × 12 × 12 inches and warns that promoters may impose additional rules for special events. Check the concert announcement for its own limits.

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags larger than 12 × 12 × 12 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The August 2026 Ella Langley concert guide set doors at 6 p.m. for a 7:10 p.m. first performance. Door times for other concerts depend on their event guidance.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Parking for the August 2026 Ella Langley concerts included a free Stadium Deck and $50 card-only day-of parking in the Campus Safety and Athletics Complex lots. Those locations and prices were specific to those shows; consult the next concert’s event page.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For the August 2026 Ella Langley concerts, rideshare pickup and drop-off were assigned to bus stops on War Eagle Way north of the arena and Heisman Drive east of Jordan-Hare Stadium. The location may change for another show.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Elevators inside Neville Arena are available to guests with disabilities or special needs; accessible parking for basketball is at Wire Road and Extension Loop.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The arena basketball guide lists multiple stands and says concessions and merchandise transactions are cashless, using credit or debit cards and Tiger Cards.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The arena basketball guide says EMS may authorize a medical re-entry wristband at its discretion. It does not publish a general concert re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The August 2026 concert guide states that tickets for those shows were digital and guests could download them to a mobile wallet. It gives no will-call pickup procedure.

Official source
