Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bridgestone Arena discourages bringing bags to events for safety and speed of entry. Bags larger than 12”x12”x6” are prohibited, while smaller bags are subject to screening.

Allowed

  • Bags smaller than 6”x4”x1.5”
  • Bags between 6”x4”x1.5” and 12”x12”x6” (subject to screening)
  • Diaper bags
  • Bags for medical reasons

Not allowed

  • Backpacks of any size
  • Bags larger than 12”x12”x6”

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Located at 501 Broadway, Nashville. Doors open 90 mins to 2 hrs before showtime.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at multiple locations around Bridgestone Arena, including Fifth + Broadway Garage, 222 2nd Ave Garage, and Nashville Yards Garages. Prices vary, with some options available for purchase online or upon entry.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Bridgestone Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

ADA accessible venue. Wheelchair seating, elevators, and ramps throughout. Contact box office for accessible seating.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bridgestone Arena offers a variety of dining options including the BetMGM Sports Lounge, which features Smashburger, Crispy Salmon, and hand-crafted cocktails. Pete & Terry's Tavern provides craft beer and a pet-friendly atmosphere. Both venues offer a lively environment with sports programming.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Box Office open event days from 1:00 PM. Will call requires photo ID, available 2 hrs before event. Cashless venue.
