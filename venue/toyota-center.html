Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Toyota Center encourages fans to travel light, allowing only small bags no larger than 10" x 6" x 2", diaper bags, and medical bags. All bags are subject to search and must use designated entrances.

Allowed

  • bags no larger than 10" x 6" x 2"
  • diaper bags
  • medical bags

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Multiple gates around the arena. Located at 1510 Polk St, Houston.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Toyota Center offers a dedicated 2,500-space parking garage called the Toyota Tundra Garage, which is cashless and has a private sky bridge for premium guests. Garage parking is not available for non-passholders on Houston Rockets game days. Surface parking lots and street parking are also available nearby.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Head West towards Austin St, on the other side of Root Memorial Square Park to pick up your Lyft.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fully ADA compliant. Wheelchair seating on all levels. Accessible parking and drop-off. Elevators and ramps throughout.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food and alcoholic beverages are sold inside Toyota Center. Outside alcohol is prohibited, and all points of sale are cashless; event-specific service restrictions can apply.
