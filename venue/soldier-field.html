Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Soldier Field enforces the NFL Clear Bag Policy, allowing clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small non-clear bags up to 4.5” x 6.5”. All other bags are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags
  • Small non-clear bags up to 4.5” x 6.5”

Not allowed

  • Coolers
  • Briefcases
  • Diaper bags
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage
  • Computer bags
  • Any non-clear bag larger than 4.5” x 6.5”

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Soldier Field's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

If Ticketmaster has exhausted its allocation of wheelchair-accessible seating, but conventional seating is still available, the disabled patron is invited to proceed with the purchase of conventional seating near their desired location and then reach out to the respective team (Chicago Bears at 847.295.6600 or Chicago Fire FC at 888-MLS-FIRE).

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Alcoholic beverages are available for purchase at designated concession stands within Soldier Field. Alcohol policies are clearly posted at each stand. No specific food options or special offerings are detailed in the available information.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fans are not permitted to exit and re-enter the stadium. Gate 8 also serves as the exit and re-entry gate should a Service Animal need a relief area.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

PNC ATMs are located in the following areas throughout the stadium: Dr Pepper Patio located in the South Courtyard near Gate 0, Sections 128, 350, North Garage near the Bears Pro Shop, outside Gate 10 next to Will Call Booth.
