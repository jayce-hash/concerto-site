Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The official basketball A–Z guide says the SEC clear-bag policy applies at all events in Humphrey Coliseum; medically necessary nonclear bags are screened at any entrance. Consult the linked clear-bag details and any concert announcement before bringing a bag.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The guide states basketball gates open 90 minutes before tip-off, with security screening at each entrance. It does not give a universal concert opening time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The basketball guide lists limited $5 public parking east of Dudy Noble Field off Lakeview Drive and free lots at Zacharias Village, Barnes & Noble, Bost Extension Center, Memorial Hall and Garner Hall. These are basketball arrangements; concert parking may differ.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The guide identifies a vehicle drop-off lane on Lakeview Drive on the east side of the Coliseum, but does not designate a rideshare pickup zone.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The guide lists accessible seating in Sections 101, 103, 107, 109, 111, 113, 116 and 119; elevators are behind Section 111 and in Mize Pavilion. Contact the ticket office in advance for accommodations.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The basketball A–Z guide prohibits outside food and drink but does not publish specific concession offerings for concerts.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drink
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Coliseum guide says there are no pass-outs or re-entry without purchasing a new ticket.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The athletics ticket office and will call are in the east lobby and open 90 minutes before basketball tip-off; a concert pickup time is not listed.

Official source
