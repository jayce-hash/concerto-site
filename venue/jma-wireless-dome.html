Bag Policy

Bag Policy

The JMA Wireless Dome enforces a clear bag policy and uses walkthrough metal detectors to enhance public safety and expedite entry. Each fan is allowed one clear bag and one small clutch or purse.

Parking

Parking at the JMA Wireless Dome is cashless, accepting all major credit and debit cards. Key parking areas include Skytop, John A. Lally Athletics Complex, and Stadium West, with specific directions provided for each.
