Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The JMA Wireless Dome enforces a clear bag policy and uses walkthrough metal detectors to enhance public safety and expedite entry. Each fan is allowed one clear bag and one small clutch or purse.

Allowed

  • 12” x 6” x 12” Clear Tote
  • One-Gallon Clear Plastic Storage Bag
  • 4.5” x 6.5” Clutch or Small Purse
  • Diaper Bag

Not allowed

  • Backpack
  • Binocular case
  • Briefcase
  • Camera case
  • Cinch bag
  • Computer bag
  • Fanny pack
  • Large purses
  • Mesh bag
  • Oversize tote
  • Printed pattern plastic bags
  • Tinted plastic bags

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking at the JMA Wireless Dome is cashless, accepting all major credit and debit cards. Key parking areas include Skytop, John A. Lally Athletics Complex, and Stadium West, with specific directions provided for each.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in JMA Wireless Dome's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Football and Men’s Basketball single game parking is available at the Skytop accessible lot.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The JMA Wireless Dome offers a variety of food and drink options including beer, candy, caramel corn, coffee, cotton candy, hamburgers, hot dogs, ice cream, nachos, pizza, popcorn, pretzels, sausage, soda, water, and wine. A special 'Brew and BBQ' stand features BBQ sandwiches and a selection of bottled beers.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in JMA Wireless Dome's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at JMA Wireless Dome. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
