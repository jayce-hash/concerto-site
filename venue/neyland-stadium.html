Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For the Morgan Wallen concerts, the university allowed one clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches or a one-gallon clear bag, plus a clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches. No other bags were permitted except small medical bags; check guidance for the next concert.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear bag
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches
  • Small medical bag

Not allowed

  • Other bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For the September 2024 Morgan Wallen concerts, gates opened at 4:30 p.m. for 6:30 p.m. shows. Future concert gate times must be checked on that concert’s event page.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For the 2024 Morgan Wallen shows, campus lots with concert passes opened at 2 p.m. and university buses switched to event service then. Those times are specific to those shows.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published a specific rideshare pickup point in its Morgan Wallen concert guidance.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Garth Brooks concert guide designated accessible elevators and entry at Gates 15 and 21 for that show. Confirm the entry arrangement for the next concert.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The official Neyland concert guide for Garth Brooks says concession stands are throughout the concourses. It does not publish offerings or prices for other concerts.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The official Garth Brooks concert guide stated that guests could not leave Neyland Stadium and be readmitted. Check the next concert’s event-specific policy.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The university’s Morgan Wallen concert guidance said general-bowl tickets used the Ticketmaster app and premium tickets used the Tennessee Athletics app. It did not provide a will-call pickup procedure.

Official source
