Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For the October 2026 Usher and Chris Brown show, the official event page allows clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches, one-gallon clear bags and clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches. Medical items may be inspected; backpacks, fanny packs and diaper bags are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear freezer bag
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches
  • Medically necessary items

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Fanny packs
  • Diaper bags
  • Large purses
