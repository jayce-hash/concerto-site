What matters at SoFi Stadium.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

At SoFi Stadium, permitted bags must be clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC and not exceed 12" x 6" x 12". Small non-clear bags up to 4.5" x 6.5" are also allowed.

Verified 2026-08-20

Parking

Parking

Parking passes must be purchased in advance to avoid higher fees on event day. Onsite parking is limited for NFL games, with alternative options like Park and Ride available. For concerts and non-NFL games, parking lots open 3 to 4 hours before the event.

Verified 2026-08-20

Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in SoFi Stadium's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

SoFi Stadium offers a diverse range of food and drink options, featuring local flavors and popular favorites. Visitors can enjoy gourmet burgers, craft beers, and a variety of international cuisines. Special offerings include vegan and vegetarian options to cater to different dietary preferences.

Accessibility

ADA &amp; Accessible Services

All 12 entries ADA accessible. Wheelchair podiums inside Entries 4, 8 & 10. ADA drop-off: Kareem Court, 3178 Pincay Dr, Inglewood. ADA parking in every zone with valid placard + pass. Contact: 844-55-SOFI.
