Know before you go

What matters at Red Rocks Amphitheatre.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Red Rocks Amphitheatre allows single-pocket bags and clear bags no larger than 13” x 15” x 8”. Small purses, fanny packs, and hydration packs up to 2L are also permitted.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Parking is free at Red Rocks Amphitheatre, with lots opening 2 hours before event door time. Arriving early is recommended to avoid traffic, and electric vehicle charging is available at the Trading Post parking lot.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

Passenger pickup for private vehicles, taxis, Uber/Lyft is located in the Jurassic Lot.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Red Rocks Amphitheatre offers a variety of food and drink options including BBQ, NY-style pizza, chicken sandwiches, gyros, tacos, smoothies, and specialty coffee. The Ship Rock Grille provides a unique dining experience with stunning views, though reservations are recommended for concert nights.
