Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Only bags smaller than 12 × 12 inches are allowed; clear material is not required. Backpacks and oversize bags are prohibited, and small bags are searched.

Allowed

  • Bag smaller than 12 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags 12 × 12 inches or larger
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors usually open one hour before showtime; confirm the exact door time on that event’s listing.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Vic has no own parking; its FAQ points to nearby street parking and a garage across the street at 3130 Sheffield Avenue.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Vic FAQ does not publish a dedicated rideshare pickup or drop-off point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Call the venue directly at 773-472-0449 about ADA accommodations.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Outside food and drinks are prohibited. The venue is cashless and accepts debit and credit cards.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Vic Theatre does not allow re-entry.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Tickets are displayed through AXS Mobile ID on a phone; no paper ticket is required. The Vic box office at 3145 North Sheffield Avenue sells advance tickets, with hours listed in the FAQ.

Official source
