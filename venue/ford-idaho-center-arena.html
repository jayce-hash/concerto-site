Parking

Parking

The Ford Idaho Center offers 4,000 on-site parking spaces with a fee of $10 per vehicle. Prepaid parking is available via ParkMobile, and reserving in advance is recommended to guarantee a spot. RV parking is available for $40, but overnight parking is not permitted.
