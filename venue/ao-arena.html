Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Children sized backpacks are allowed, but bags larger than an A3 piece of paper are not permitted. Exceptions can be made for infant changing items.

Allowed

  • Children sized backpacks

Not allowed

  • Bags larger than an A3 piece of paper

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at AO Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Official parking is available at the on-site CitiPark with 958 spaces, including 40 Blue Badge bays. Parking can be pre-booked, but Blue Badge bays are on a first come, first served basis.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in AO Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A member of the Accessibility Team will contact you during our office hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm. If you require a wheelchair to get to the venue but have not booked or don’t need wheelchair accessibility seating, we will happily look after this for you.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The AO Arena in Manchester offers a variety of food and drink options for visitors. While specific details about the offerings are not provided in the accessibility guide, visitors can expect typical arena concessions such as snacks, beverages, and possibly some local specialties.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in AO Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

This box office is open on show days from 1 hour before doors and will close 15 minutes after the main act has gone on stage. AO Arena's box office is situated in the Trinity Way tunnel located next to the CitiPark AO Arena Car Park entrance.

Official source
