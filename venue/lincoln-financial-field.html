Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fans can bring one clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bag not exceeding 12″ x 6″ x 12”, a one-gallon clear plastic freezer bag, or a small clutch bag no larger than 4.5″ x 6.5″. Exceptions are made for medically necessary items after inspection.

Allowed

  • Bags that are clear plastic, vinyl or PVC and do not exceed 12″ x 6″ x 12”
  • One-gallon clear plastic freezer bag (Ziploc bag or similar)
  • Small clutch bags approximately the size of a hand but not larger than 4.5″ x 6.5″

Not allowed

  • Purses larger than a clutch bag
  • Coolers
  • Briefcases
  • Backpacks
  • Fanny packs
  • Cinch bags
  • Luggage
  • Seat cushions
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags
  • Binocular cases
  • Any bag larger than the permissible size

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Lincoln Financial Field. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lincoln Financial Field offers various parking options with different rates for Eagles games, concerts, and other events. Regular vehicles are charged $50 for Eagles games, $40 for concerts, and $30 for other events, while oversized vehicles are charged $100, $80, and $60 respectively. Traffic patterns are directed by the Philadelphia Police to ensure efficient exit from the Sports Complex.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Lincoln Financial Field's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Lincoln Financial Field, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lincoln Financial Field offers a diverse range of food and drink options, including exclusive club level offerings like Jerk Chicken Sandwiches and Philly Pressed Italiano Sandwiches. Local favorites feature iconic Philadelphia flavors such as cheesesteaks and Chickie’s & Pete’s CrabFries.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Lincoln Financial Field's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Will Call always opens at least 3 hours prior to kickoff. Will Call – The Will Call windows are located at the northwest side of the HeadHouse Plaza, next to the XFINITY Gate (formerly Comcast Gate).
