Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC totes up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches and nonclear clutches up to 6 × 9 inches are allowed.

Allowed

  • Clear tote up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Clutch up to 6 × 9 inches

Not allowed

  • Other bags

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026
