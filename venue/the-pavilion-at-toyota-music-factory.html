    Bag policy

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Bags must be clear and smaller than 12” x 6” x 12”. Small clutch bags, wristlets, or fanny packs no bigger than 6” x 9” are allowed and do not need to be clear.

    Allowed

    • CLEAR plastic/vinyl tote bags no larger than 12” x 6” x 12”
    • Small clutch bags, wristlets, or fanny packs no bigger than 6” x 9”

    Not allowed

    • Weapons of any kind
    • Large Bags / Backpacks / Luggage / Computer Bags / Camera Bags
    • Chairs of ANY kind including Lawn Chairs
    • Coolers of any kind
    • Outside food or drinks – except factory sealed water
    • FROZEN water bottles
    • Alcohol
    • iPads (tablets), selfie-sticks, GoPros
    • Cameras with detachable lenses
    • Video or audio recording devices
    • Illegal drugs/substances
    • Glass containers or cans
    • Laser pointers
    • Selfie Sticks
    • Signage larger than 8.5"x11"
    • Animals (except service animals)
    • Aerosol Sunscreen
    • Fireworks
    • Chains / Spike jewelry
    • Any other item that may pose a threat
