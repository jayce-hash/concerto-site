Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Citizens Bank Park enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Only specific types and sizes of bags are allowed, and all bags are subject to search.

Parking

Parking

Citizens Bank Park is in the South Philadelphia Sports Complex with shared stadium lots (also serving Lincoln Financial Field and the Wells Fargo Center) plus the Citizens Bank Park garages. Prepaid parking is cheaper than drive-up, and the SEPTA Broad Street Line's NRG station is a short walk away.

Rideshare

Rideshare

On event days, this is the only location to drop off and receive an Uber and other rideshare pickups. Lot T is designated for Uber RideShare for all Citizens Bank Park and Wells Fargo Center events.

Concessions

Concessions

Citizens Bank Park offers a variety of food and beverage options including local and popular beers at the Beer Garden, classic comfort foods with a modern twist at Bridges Grille, and elevated ballpark classics at Cadillac Diner. Special offerings include a Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry.

Accessibility

Accessibility

All guests enter Citizens Bank Park on the Main Concourse at street level and there are wheelchair-accessible gates at every bank of turnstiles. Companion Seating is available alongside the spaces for guests using wheelchairs at a maximum 3:1 ratio (3 companions per wheelchair).
