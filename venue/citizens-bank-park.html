Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Citizens Bank Park enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Only specific types and sizes of bags are allowed, and all bags are subject to search.

Allowed

  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) no larger than 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag (1 Gallon)
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”
  • Seat cushions without pockets, zippers or concealable areas no larger than 18” wide

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags and wallets exceeding 6.5" x 4.5"
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage, computer bags, camera bags, briefcases, fanny packs, cinch bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Citizens Bank Park. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Citizens Bank Park is in the South Philadelphia Sports Complex with shared stadium lots (also serving Lincoln Financial Field and the Wells Fargo Center) plus the Citizens Bank Park garages. Prepaid parking is cheaper than drive-up, and the SEPTA Broad Street Line's NRG station is a short walk away.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

On event days, this is the only location to drop off and receive an Uber and other rideshare pickups. Lot T is designated for Uber RideShare for all Citizens Bank Park and Wells Fargo Center events.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All guests enter Citizens Bank Park on the Main Concourse at street level and there are wheelchair-accessible gates at every bank of turnstiles. Companion Seating is available alongside the spaces for guests using wheelchairs at a maximum 3:1 ratio (3 companions per wheelchair).

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Citizens Bank Park offers a variety of food and beverage options including local and popular beers at the Beer Garden, classic comfort foods with a modern twist at Bridges Grille, and elevated ballpark classics at Cadillac Diner. Special offerings include a Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Citizens Bank Park's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Citizens Bank Park. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
