Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Daikin Park enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Parking

Parking

Daikin Park is in downtown Houston with roughly 25,000 parking spaces in nearby garages and lots within walking distance. The Astros operate Lots A, B, C and H near the gates; Lot C passes (about $15) can be pre-purchased via Ticketmaster, while city garages a short walk away are often cheaper. The METRORail Red Line's Convention District stop is about five minutes away.

Concessions

Concessions

Daikin Park offers a variety of food and drink options, including classic comfort foods with modern twists and indulgent ballpark classics. Highlights include specialty items like the Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog, Pastrami Fry, and a selection of local and popular beers. Sweet treats like Soft Serve and Milkshakes are also available.

Accessibility

Accessibility

With the combination of private lots and garages, approximately 25,000 parking spaces are available within a half-mile radius of Daikin Park, including many ADA spaces. An elevator on the first base side near Section 128 is designated for use by guests with mobility limitations.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

On game days beginning four (4) hours before first pitch, box office windows at each entrance will open for ticket resolutions. The main Daikin Park Box Office is located on the southwest side of the ballpark, near the intersection of Texas Avenue and Avenida de las Americas.
