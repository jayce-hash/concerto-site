Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags 10x6x2 inches and smaller can use expedited entry, while bags up to 14x14x6 inches must be screened by x-ray machines. Bag check is available at the P3 Lobby of the Garden Garage.

Verified 2026-08-20

Parking

Parking

The Rose Quarter offers over 2500 parking spaces across four garages and two lots, with prices ranging from $10 to $40 depending on the event. Pre-paid parking is available for most events, and motorcycle parking costs the same as regular vehicles.

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Moda Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Accessibility

Accessibility

The Rose Quarter campus has four parking garages each with ADA accessible parking on most levels. Moda Center's dedicated ADA entrance, is located on P3 in the Garden Garage on the north end of the Moda Center.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Moda Center offers a variety of food and drink options including local favorites like Baes fried chicken and Fifty Licks ice cream. There are multiple bars serving beer, wine, and cocktails, as well as vegan and vegetarian options such as Cha Cha Cha's Mexican food and Crafter's veggie burgers.

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Moda Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Rose Quarter Box Office at Moda Center and the Veterans Memorial Coliseum Box Office opens 2 hours prior to the event start time and is closed on non-event days.
