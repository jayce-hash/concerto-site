Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Governors Ball does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Governors Ball. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The festival recommends public transportation. Use the 7 train or LIRR to Mets–Willets Point; the official rideshare point is at the New York Hall of Science when published for the current edition.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There is a designated pick-up and drop-off area on Shea Road.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Governors Ball, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The festival features New York City restaurants and Queens vendors. Outside food and beverages are prohibited except approved items required for medical dietary needs.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Governors Ball's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Please visit the Box Office to pick up Will Call orders, for wristband assistance, or to upgrade tickets (if available), or to receive support for tickets purchased through the Ticketmaster Verified Exchange.

Official source
