Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Backpacks, hard-sided bags, luggage, coolers and bags over 16 × 16 inches are prohibited. Every admitted bag is manually checked; some concerts impose stricter bag limits.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 16 × 16 inches when the concert allows

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Hard-sided bags
  • Bags over 16 × 16 inches

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors typically open one hour before the event; use the exact opening time in the event-day email. Bags are inspected by security.

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Nearby parking facilities are operated separately from the theater; consult the theater parking map and allow additional time on show days.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

An accessible ramp reaches the front entrance; the theater publishes details about accessible seating and support.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concessions are cash-free and accept payment cards and mobile wallets. Guests with valid ID may buy no more than two alcoholic beverages per transaction.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

For the permitted outdoor smoking break, scan your ticket out in the Ticket Lobby and then back in and repeat the security check. Smoking access closes 30 minutes before the scheduled end of a show.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office is just inside the main Market Street entrance; the operator lists updated hours on its box-office page.
