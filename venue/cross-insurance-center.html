Know before you go

What matters at Cross Insurance Center.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

The Cross Insurance Center enforces a bag policy limiting bags to a size of 14" x 14" x 6" with some exceptions. Prohibited items include weapons, explosives, and outside food and beverages.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

The Cross Insurance Center offers three parking lots on site, labeled as Lot J, Lot O, and Lot E. Parking can be reserved in advance through Ticketmaster to ensure availability. On-site parking is accessible from Buck Street.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Cross Insurance Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

The Cross Insurance Center offers a variety of concession stand options, including classic arena favorites and locally-inspired treats, with gluten-free and dairy-free options available. They also provide comprehensive catering services for events with customizable menus.

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Cross Insurance Center, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Will Call is at the Maine Credit Unions Box Office [located at the Renewal by Andersen Entrance] available 90 minutes before – on the day of show*. Will Call allows individuals to pick up pre-purchased tickets for an event at the Box Office.
