Center Stage Theater

Bag policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Backpacks are not permitted. Small bags under 12 inches are allowed and will be searched.

Allowed

  • small bags under 12 inches

Not allowed

  • backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Doors are generally scheduled to open one hour before the time printed on the ticket. Lines often form near 17th Street and West Peachtree Street.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at the Center Stage Garage for $10-$25 and the Midtown Plaza Deck for $17.50. Both accept credit, debit, and ParkMobile, but no cash. Limited complimentary parking is available for box office visits.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For your safety, please use 17th Street (b/w West Peachtree St & Spring St) for Uber, Lyft, or Taxi.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Accessible seating can be arranged by calling the venue at (404) 885-1365 or emailing info@rivalentertainment.com.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Center Stage, The Loft, and Vinyl have bars. Snacks are available at the bar, and certain shows also offer pizza.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Center Stage Theater's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Will Call is located at the entrance to each room. Bring a valid photo ID. The box office also sells tickets in person during posted hours and, when available, at show entry.

Official source
