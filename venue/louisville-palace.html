Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches are allowed after search; opaque bags receive extra inspection.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Larger bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors generally open one hour before showtime. Guests with the Marquee Lounge upgrade may use its private entrance 30 minutes earlier, but that entrance has stairs.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Nearby parking choices include Clay Commons, Brown Hotel and Fifth Street garages, as well as metered street spaces.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Palace visitor guide describes garages and street parking but does not designate a rideshare pickup area.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

ADA seating is on the main floor; the loge and balcony require stairs. Guests who need step-free access should use the main entrance and its elevator.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concession stands sell popcorn, soft pretzels, soda and snacks; bars sell alcohol to guests aged 21 or older with valid ID. Main concession stands accept payment cards.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Louisville Palace has a strict no-re-entry policy.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Collect physical Will Call or guest-list tickets on the show day from two hours before showtime; bring picture ID and preferably the confirmation number.

Official source
