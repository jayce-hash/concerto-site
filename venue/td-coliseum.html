Bag Policy

Bag Policy

TD Coliseum enforces a restricted bag policy with no bag check available.

Parking

Parking

There is no on-site parking at TD Coliseum, but upgraded parking can be purchased for the York Boulevard Parkade through the TD Coliseum Mobile App. Several nearby lots and garages are within walking distance, and arriving early is recommended.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

Guests using Uber, Lyft, or other rideshare services can be dropped off and picked up in the Vine Street lot off of James Street North.

Concessions

Concessions

TD Coliseum offers a variety of food and drink options ranging from quick-service concessions to luxury dining experiences. Highlights include local favorites and arena classics, premium dining with bespoke bars, and themed lounges like the TD Lounge and Vinyl Room. Special offerings feature Ontario cheese boards, Hamilton Brewery drafts, and Niagara wines.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by TD Coliseum, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.
