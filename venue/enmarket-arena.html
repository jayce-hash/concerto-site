Enmarket Arena

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Opaque clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 × 2 inches or clear bags up to 14 × 14 × 6 inches with at most two pockets are allowed. Inspected medical and diaper bags are exceptions.

Allowed

  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 × 2 inches
  • Clear bag up to 14 × 14 × 6 inches with two pockets maximum
  • Medical or diaper bag

Not allowed

  • Large opaque bags
  • Backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors usually open 60 or 90 minutes before showtime according to the specific listing. Most events use mobile tickets displayed with a QR code on a phone.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Advance parking reservations are strongly advised because paid parking near the arena is limited. Downtown shuttles for concerts cost $10 per person, boarding at Liberty and Barnard Streets.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The dedicated rideshare pickup and drop-off area is Lot B; drivers enter from West Gwinnett Street.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

All building entrances have wheelchair-accessible ramps; contact an arena attendant for assistance.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Outside food, drinks and containers are prohibited, except items for infants such as baby bottles and formula. Food and drinks are sold inside.

Allowed

  • Infant bottles and formula

Not allowed

  • Other outside food and drink
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena prohibits re-entry after leaving, except for any explicitly announced event exception.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The ticket office opens three hours before showtime on event days. VIP will-call tickets follow the tour’s pickup instructions and are not distributed by the arena ticket office.

Official source
