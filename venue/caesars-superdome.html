Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Caesars Superdome does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Superdome uses OpenGate technology and walk-through metal detectors at all entry gates to enhance safety and security at public ticketed events.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Caesars Superdome offers parking in seven garages and two surface lots, with pre-purchase options available through JustPark EventPass. Long-term monthly parking is available in Garage 5 and Champions Garage for $125 via EFT.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The designated rideshare drop-off and pick-up zone is located on Poydras Street, between Clara Street and Loyola Avenue.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The ADA Facilitator can be contacted at (504) 587-3826 or ada@caesarssuperdome.com. Wheelchairs may be checked out at the Guest Relations Center located at Gate F on the Plaza Level.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food and beverage availability at Caesars Superdome varies by event and operating setup. Review the linked official guest information before arrival.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Caesars Superdome's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Prior to event day, Will Call is located at the Smoothie King Center Box Office. On event day, Will Call is located at the Superdome Box Office at Gate A Ground.

Official source
