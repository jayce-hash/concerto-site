What matters at Essence Festival of Culture.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

ESSENCE Festival spans multiple venues, so there is not one bag rule for the entire weekend. The Caesars Superdome evening concert series requires stadium-compliant clear bags; daytime Convention Center rules can differ.

Parking
