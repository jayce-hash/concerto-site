Know before you go

What matters at 3Arena.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags no bigger than 40cm x 40cm x 20cm are allowed. Large rucksacks, luggage, or oversized bags are not permitted.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

There is limited car parking available in The Point Village carpark, which is not operated by 3Arena. Due to potential traffic congestion on show nights, public transport is recommended.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in 3Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Bars and retail units throughout 3Arena serve alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks plus confectionery and snacks. Outside food and drinks are not permitted, and the venue says it does not have sit-down food areas.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Accessible tickets should be booked in advance through Ticketmaster. Guests with mobility needs are directed to the accessible area, flat seating, or lower-tier Blocks A–G; wheelchairs are not permitted on the ground floor. Contact enquiry@3arena.ie or 01 866 3400 for special-access questions.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Re-entry is not permitted. Guests who leave during a show will not be readmitted.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Ticket Collection

Collect tickets at the box office at the front of the building on the Liffey side. It opens at 6:30 p.m. on show nights.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Entrances & Doors

Use the entrance shown in event communications and follow on-site signage. The box-office entrance is at the front of the building on the Liffey side; public door times vary by show.
