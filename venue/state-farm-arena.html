Bag Policy

Bag Policy

State Farm Arena allows bags smaller than 14" x 14" x 6" and does not enforce a clear bag policy. All bags are subject to screening.

Parking

Parking

State Farm Arena offers multiple parking options with varying proximity to the venue. Pre-purchasing parking through ParkMobile guarantees a spot and the lowest prices. Options include the Sapphire Deck, Ruby Lot, State Farm Drive, and Diamond Deck.

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in State Farm Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

State Farm Arena offers a diverse range of food and beverage options, featuring local Atlanta favorites like Chick-fil-A, Dolo's Pizza Co., and J.R. Crickets. New offerings include a BBQ Smashburger and a Black Bean Burger, with a focus on quality, speed, and sustainability.
