Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Guests attending public ticketed events at UW – Panther Arena are allowed to bring a clear stadium bag up to 12″ x 6″ x 12″, a gallon-sized clear zip bag, a wristlet up to 4″x6″x1″, or a diaper or medical bag. Bags will be searched upon entry.
