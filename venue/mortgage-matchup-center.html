Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For quick entry, fans are encouraged to leave bags at home. Small clutches, crossbody bags, and clear bags are allowed, while standard backpacks are not permitted.

Allowed

  • Small clutches
  • Crossbody bags
  • Clear bags
  • Purses
  • Medical bags
  • Diaper bags

Not allowed

  • Standard backpacks

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Mortgage Matchup Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Valet parking is available starting 2 hours prior to events, and the attached five-story, 700-space parking structure offers convenient parking for suite holders. Several other parking garages and lots are within walking distance.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lyft Drop Off Locations: 1st Street/Jefferson, S 2nd Street, S 3rd Street, E Jefferson Street. Lyft Pick Up Location: S 3rd Street & E Jefferson Street.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

If you have difficulty navigating stairs but do not need a wheelchair accessible location, row 20 in the 100 level and row 2 in the 200 level do not require taking stairs. Accessible parking spaces are located on floors 1-5 near elevators, available as first-come, first-served.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Mortgage Matchup Center offers a variety of food and beverage options catering to different dietary preferences, including vegetarian and gluten-free choices. However, specific details about the offerings are not provided.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Mortgage Matchup Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Mortgage Matchup Center ticket office will be open at 3pm on weekday events and three (3) hours prior to event time for weekends.
