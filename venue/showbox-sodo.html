Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Backpacks and bags larger than 16 × 16 × 8 inches are prohibited. The FAQ does not require clear bags.

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags larger than 16 × 16 × 8 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office opens one hour before scheduled doors. Guests age 21 or older purchasing preshow food or drinks in Showbox SoDo Lounge may be admitted first when doors open; the lounge opens one hour before doors.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue has no public parking; nearby paid lots and some street parking are available.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The shared venue FAQ does not specify a rideshare pickup or drop-off site for Showbox SoDo.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

All entries are accessible. Guests may purchase an accessible ticket through AXS, or if sold out, buy general admission and notify security of accessibility needs; dedicated accessible seating is not guaranteed.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue and its preshow lounge sell food or drinks and take major cards, not cash. Outside food, beverages and containers, including reusable water bottles, are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food
  • Outside beverages
  • Reusable water bottles
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The shared Showbox venue FAQ does not publish a rule for re-entry after leaving.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call tickets may be collected only on show night at the Showbox SoDo box office; bring the named buyer’s photo ID. The box office opens one hour before scheduled doors.

Official source
