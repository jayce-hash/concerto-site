What matters at Schottenstein Center.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Schottenstein Center does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Parking

Parking

Parking is available immediately around the arena with over 10,000 surface and garage spaces within walking distance. Parking lots are cashless, accepting credit/debit cards and smartphone payments only. Lots typically open 2 hours prior to event doors.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

Guests can be dropped off before an event via personal vehicle, Uber, Lyft, or Rideshare services along Borror Drive. Post-event, use the rideshare app to select Schottenstein Center for the closest pick-up area.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Doors

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Schottenstein Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Schottenstein Center, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Concessions

The Schottenstein Center offers a variety of food and drink options, with a focus on responsible alcohol consumption. Alcohol sales are managed according to specific guidelines and may be cut off at designated times during events.

Re-Entry Policy

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Ticket Office (venue's Northeast corner) lobby is open from 9AM to 4PM Monday thru Friday with extended hours on event days for assistance or the purchase of available tickets.
