What matters at Anfiteatro degli Scavi.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags, backpacks, luggage, or cases with dimensions exceeding 30x30x15cm are not allowed on the sites. This is to protect the frescoes and ensure visitor safety.

Parking

Parking

There is no dedicated parking area for visitors at the Archaeological Park. However, there are many municipal parking spaces available at a cost, marked by blue lines, as well as several private car parks nearby.

Concessions
