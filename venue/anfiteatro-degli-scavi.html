Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags, backpacks, luggage, or cases with dimensions exceeding 30x30x15cm are not allowed on the sites. This is to protect the frescoes and ensure visitor safety.

Not allowed

  • Bags, backpacks, luggage, or cases exceeding 30x30x15cm
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Anfiteatro degli Scavi. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There is no dedicated parking area for visitors at the Archaeological Park. However, there are many municipal parking spaces available at a cost, marked by blue lines, as well as several private car parks nearby.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Anfiteatro degli Scavi's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Anfiteatro degli Scavi, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Visitors can enjoy a variety of local and seasonal products at the 'Pizza, Pasta & More' self-service restaurant managed by CIRFOOD. The venue offers a Café & Wine Bar with charcuterie, wines, cheeses, and traditional pastries, enhancing the visitor experience with quality and local flavors.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Anfiteatro degli Scavi's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Anfiteatro degli Scavi. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
