Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches may enter after search; opaque bags receive extra inspection. The venue also lists backpacks and large purses as prohibited.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Large purses
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors generally open one hour before showtime. Use a mobile ticket on a phone; if it cannot be displayed, ask for help at the box office with photo ID.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Metered street parking and nearby Clay Commons, Brown Hotel and Fifth Street garages are available.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Mercury Ballroom visitor guide does not specify a dedicated rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue publishes an accessibility guide and asks guests to contact staff before visiting with questions or accommodation requests.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The bar offers beer, wine, spirits and nonalcoholic drinks; snacks include popcorn, pretzels and nachos. Outside food and drinks are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue does not permit guests to leave and re-enter.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue lists box office hours before Mercury shows and mobile ticket entry, but does not publish a general Will Call pickup procedure.

Official source
