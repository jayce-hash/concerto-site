Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Bags may not be larger than 14 × 14 inches; the venue’s prohibited list flags backpacks and oversized luggage.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 14 × 14 inches

Not allowed

  • Bags larger than 14 × 14 inches

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue publishes door times on individual show listings rather than a fixed time for all events.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Hollywood & Gower lot next to the theatre is entered from Gower Street; prices vary by show and the lot is independently operated. Other lots and metered street parking are nearby.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The theatre’s FAQ and venue guide do not designate a rideshare pickup or drop-off point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The main floor is standing room only, and the balcony is reached by stairs; guests needing accommodations should buy an ADA ticket or contact the venue before the event.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The theatre’s concessions accept cards and digital payment, not cash. Outside food and drinks are prohibited.
