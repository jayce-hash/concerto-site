Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Only handbags or very small bags up to 35 × 40 × 19 cm are allowed, with no on-site bag storage. Arrange a medical-bag exception in advance.

Allowed

  • Small handbag up to 35 × 40 × 19 cm

Not allowed

  • Larger bags
