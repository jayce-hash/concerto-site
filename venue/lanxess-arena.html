Bag Policy

Bag Policy

LANXESS Arena prohibits weapons, drugs, laser pointers, and large or hazardous items. Exceptions are made for medical needs and baby supplies.

Parking

Parking

Parking at LANXESS Arena's P5 lot is available 24/7 with a fee of 1.00 € per 30 minutes. Payment can be made with cash or NFC-enabled cards. The lot is suitable for vehicles over 1.90 m, including buses and RVs.

Concessions
