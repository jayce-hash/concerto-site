Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The SEC clear-bag policy applies at Stegeman Coliseum. Guests may use a clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bag up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches, a one-gallon clear freezer bag, or a small clutch up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear freezer bags
  • Clutches up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Non-approved bags

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Accessible parking is first come, first served in the Hoke Smith Annex lot for vehicles displaying a state-issued disability permit; overflow is available by the Rankin Smith Student-Athlete Academic Center.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

All Stegeman Coliseum concourse entrances and exits are accessible. An elevator at the Smith Street entrance connects arena areas, and accessible seating is available for ticketed events.

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Georgia uses mobile ticketing, and guests should save tickets to their phone wallet before arrival. Printed digital tickets are not accepted at the gate.
