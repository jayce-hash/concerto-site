What matters at Utilita Arena Newcastle.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Only essential items are encouraged, with no large bags or backpacks allowed. All bags must be smaller than 40cm x 35cm x 19cm.

Parking

Parking

There are over 600 official car park spaces available on site with CCTV and security patrols. When full, the NCP Car Park next to the Arena is recommended. Pre-booking is not available and parking for cars with wheelchair patrons is on a first-come, first-served basis.

Verified 2026-08-20

Rideshare

Rideshare
