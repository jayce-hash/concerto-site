Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Only essential items are encouraged, with no large bags or backpacks allowed. All bags must be smaller than 40cm x 35cm x 19cm.

Allowed

  • Bags smaller than 40cm x 35cm x 19cm

Not allowed

  • Backpacks including holdalls, luggage, computer bags and cushions
  • Glass, cans, aluminium bottles or thermoses
  • Flammable liquids
  • Laser pens/pointers
  • Video cameras, professional cameras and recording devices
  • Laptop computers, iPads and Go Pro’s
  • Large/golf umbrellas
  • Knives, firearms or weapons
  • Illegal drugs or substances
  • Alcoholic beverages
  • Signs, banners or flags
  • Animals (except service animals)
  • Air horns, whistles, cowbells
  • Projectiles
  • Aerosol cans
  • Fireworks, confetti or glitter bombs
  • Glow sticks
  • Balloons
  • Wheeled footwear, skateboards
  • Food
  • Helmets and full face masks
  • Large belt buckles, studs, chains and spiked collars
  • Glass bottles of perfume
  • Any refreshments or refreshment containers
  • Cameras & recording equipment

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

1 hour before doors open until the main act is on stage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There are over 600 official car park spaces available on site with CCTV and security patrols. When full, the NCP Car Park next to the Arena is recommended. Pre-booking is not available and parking for cars with wheelchair patrons is on a first-come, first-served basis.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Dropping off can be made along Railway Street. Collecting people is advised to be done away from the Arena due to heavy traffic.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Utilita Arena Newcastle, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Utilita Arena Newcastle's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Utilita Arena Newcastle. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
