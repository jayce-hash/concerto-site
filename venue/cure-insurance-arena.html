Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags that do not meet the specified requirements must be returned to the patron's vehicle or stored in a locker. Parental and medical bags are allowed inside but are subject to search and size restrictions.

Parking

Parking

The CURE Insurance Arena offers 3,500 parking spaces across four main lots, with costs varying by event. Parking is first come, first serve, and ADA accommodations are available in Lot #1. Lots open two hours before events, and alcohol consumption is prohibited.

Concessions
