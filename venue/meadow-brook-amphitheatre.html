Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Single-compartment bags, wallets, clutches and fanny packs smaller than 4 × 6 × 1.5 inches may enter. Medical and diaper bags smaller than 16 × 16 × 8 inches are exceptions.

Allowed

  • Bag smaller than 4 × 6 × 1.5 inches
  • Medical or diaper bag smaller than 16 × 16 × 8 inches

Not allowed

  • Larger ordinary bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Use the main entrance or the VIP entrance indicated for your ticket; the show determines gate time. The box office at the main entrance opens at noon on event days.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

On-site general parking is included with the concert ticket; the operator offers an optional paid closer parking upgrade. Use the Meadow Brook parking directions for your event.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Meadow Brook A–Z guide does not designate a universal rideshare pickup area.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible restrooms are by the VIP entrance, behind the main entrance box office and near the main concession stand. The amphitheater publishes separate accessible ticket and parking guidance.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concession stands serve drinks; guests aged 21 or older may buy up to two alcoholic beverages per transaction with valid ID. Outside alcohol is prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside alcohol
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

There is no re-entry into Meadow Brook Amphitheatre.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office at the main entrance opens at noon on event days and closes 60 minutes after the event starts. Guests with walking difficulty can drive to the box office to collect tickets before parking.

Official source
