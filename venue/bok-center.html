Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fans can bring one clear bag per person not exceeding 12″ x 6″ x 12″ or a one-gallon clear plastic bag. Small purses or clutches no larger than 5″ x 8″ are also allowed.

Allowed

  • clear bag up to 12″ x 6″ x 12″
  • one-gallon clear plastic bag
  • small purses or clutches up to 5″ x 8″

Not allowed

  • bags larger than the specified sizes

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at BOK Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available for purchase prior to events through ParkWhiz. Consider arriving early and using sustainable transportation options like the city bus or carpooling.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All rideshare pick-ups and drop-offs take place at the designated Rideshare Zone on 1st Street, between Denver and Cheyenne.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by BOK Center, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

BOK Center offers a variety of food and drink options, including fast and casual chicken from Big Chicken, traditional arena fare, and unique offerings like boozy slushies from Slush Rush. The venue is cashless, accepting only debit/credit cards for payment.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in BOK Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The QuikTrip Box Office at BOK Center is located at the corner of 3rd St & Frisco Ave. On event days that fall on the weekend, the box office will open 2 hours prior to door time.

Official source
