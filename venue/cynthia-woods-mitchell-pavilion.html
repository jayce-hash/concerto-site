Cynthia Woods Mitchell Pavilion

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Clear bags up to 12 × 12 inches and nonclear handheld clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches are allowed. Medical equipment, diaper and nursing bags are screened; tinted or patterned bags are refused.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 12 inches
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches
  • Screened medical, diaper or nursing bag

Not allowed

  • Tinted or patterned bags
  • Oversized bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Gate times vary by show and are posted on the event page. Entrances use walk-through metal detectors; the Pavilion Lounge can open an hour before the main gates.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Free parking is available in monitored lots entered from 2203 Timberloch Place. Limited paid Gold Lot parking at 9809 Six Pines Drive is typically $45 for contemporary concerts and $15 for performing arts shows; lots open 30 minutes before gates.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue designates 2005 Lake Robbins Drive for passenger drop-off and pickup; follow the event traffic signs.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible parking is free with valid credentials in the Gold Lot, first come first served, with overflow at 9669 Grogans Mill Road. Accessible seating is offered and sign language interpretation may be arranged through the box office.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concession stands sell food and drinks. For most contemporary concerts, each guest may bring personal food in one one-gallon resealable bag; outside liquids are prohibited and empty reusable bottles are generally allowed.

Allowed

  • Food in one one-gallon resealable bag at contemporary shows
  • Empty reusable bottle at most shows

Not allowed

  • Outside liquids
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests cannot re-enter after leaving the Pavilion gates.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call tickets may be picked up by the named Ticketmaster or Live Nation account holder, or a designated alternate where transfer is available. Bring government-issued ID; the account number is also preferred.

Official source
