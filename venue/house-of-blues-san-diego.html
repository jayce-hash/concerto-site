Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The FAQ permits clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches and nonclear clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches; noncompliant bags must be returned to a vehicle. A second security section allows other same-sized bags after extra search, so ask the venue about any opaque bag larger than a clutch.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Larger bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Music Hall and box office enter at 1056 Sixth Avenue. Guests may be screened by wand, detector or pat-down; select preshow Salvation Bar events start one hour before doors.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue recommends the garage on Fifth Avenue between Broadway and C Street, across from its entrance; advance parking is available.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ describes garages and the entrance but does not designate a rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Contact the box office for ADA arrangements; the venue publishes a separate accessibility page.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food is available from bartenders during the show and at select preshow events in the Salvation Bar. In-venue food and drink sales accept cards or mobile pay, and outside food and drinks are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The House of Blues does not permit re-entry to shows.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Pick up Will Call or guest-list tickets at the box office on the show day, starting two hours before doors; the original named buyer needs physical government photo ID.

Official source
