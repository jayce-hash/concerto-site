Bag policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches are permitted and will be searched before entry. Non-clear bags may receive additional inspection.

Allowed

  • bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches

Not allowed

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Doors generally open about one hour before the scheduled show time. Confirm the exact door time on the event listing or ticket.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There is limited metered street parking near the venue. Commercial lots are available on 5th and Bainbridge Streets, but they accept cash only. The venue has partnered with ParkWhiz for additional parking options.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in The Theatre of Living Arts's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Theatre of Living Arts offers an eclectic mix of beers, wines, spirits, and non-alcoholic beverages. Snack options include popcorn, soft pretzels, chips, and candy, available at the bar.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 4, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in The Theatre of Living Arts's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at The Theatre of Living Arts. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
