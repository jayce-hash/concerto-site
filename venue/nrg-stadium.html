Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

NRG Stadium does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Elevator lobbies are located at the main entrances on the NE, SE, SW and NW corners of Reliant Stadium.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

NRG Stadium event parking is managed through NRG Park. Entry gates, lot availability, pricing, and prepaid options vary by event; the venue commonly directs stadium traffic through Gate 9 at Kirby Drive and Westridge Street.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Enter Yellow Lot and look for tent where rideshares can pick up.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accessible Seating, designated for guests with disabilities who use wheelchairs, other mobility devices, or otherwise require the use of the features of accessible seating (e.g., a guest with a service animal that requires the additional space offered by accessible seating), is available on all levels for all events held at Reliant Stadium and Reliant Arena.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food and beverage availability at NRG Stadium varies by event and operating setup. Review the linked official guest information before arrival.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in NRG Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

If a customer’s delivery option is will call, there will be a dedicated window for will call pickup at the Reliant Stadium Box Office. Guests are not permitted to leave tickets at will call for anyone.

Official source
