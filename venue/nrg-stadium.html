    Bag policy

    Checked Oct 11, 2026

    Backpacks and bags larger than 11" x 17" are not allowed. When the clear bag policy is in effect, only clear bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" are allowed.

    Allowed

    • Bags up to 11" x 17" (events without the clear bag policy)
    • Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bag up to 12" x 6" x 12" (clear bag events)
    • One-gallon clear plastic bag (clear bag events)
    • Small clutch up to 4.5" x 6.5" (clear bag events)

    Not allowed

    • Backpacks
    • Bags larger than 11" x 17"
    • Purses and diaper bags at clear bag events
    • Coolers
    • Outside food or beverage
    • Professional camera equipment
    • Audio and video recorders
    • Bottles and cans
    • Laser pointers
    • Drones
    • Noisemakers and air horns
    • Binocular and camera cases
    Official source

    Entrances and doors

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Elevator lobbies are located at the main entrances on the NE, SE, SW and NW corners of Reliant Stadium.

    Official source

    Parking

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Reliant Stadium event parking is managed through Reliant Park. Entry gates, lot availability, pricing, and prepaid options vary by event; the venue commonly directs stadium traffic through Gate 9 at Kirby Drive and Westridge Street.

    Official source

    Rideshare

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Enter Yellow Lot and look for tent where rideshares can pick up.

    Official source

    Accessibility

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Accessible Seating, designated for guests with disabilities who use wheelchairs, other mobility devices, or otherwise require the use of the features of accessible seating (e.g., a guest with a service animal that requires the additional space offered by accessible seating), is available on all levels for all events held at Reliant Stadium and Reliant Arena.

    Official source

    Food and drink

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Food and beverage availability at Reliant Stadium varies by event and operating setup. Review the linked official guest information before arrival.

    Official source

    Re-entry

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Reliant Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

    Official source

    Tickets and will call

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    If a customer’s delivery option is will call, there will be a dedicated window for will call pickup at the Reliant Stadium Box Office. Guests are not permitted to leave tickets at will call for anyone.

    Official source
