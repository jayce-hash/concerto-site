Bag Policy

Bag Policy

The venue allows only clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC tote bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutch bags up to 6” x 9”. All bags will be searched upon entry.

Concessions

Credit Union 1 Amphitheatre offers a variety of food and drink options, including Mosh Burger's hormone-free beef burgers, Rebel Hen's crispy chicken tenders, and Ziggy's Magic Pizza Shop's NY-inspired pizzas.
