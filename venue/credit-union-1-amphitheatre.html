Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The venue allows only clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC tote bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutch bags up to 6” x 9”. All bags will be searched upon entry.

Allowed

  • CLEAR plastic, vinyl or PVC tote bags no larger than 12” x 6” x 12”
  • small clutch bags no larger than 6” x 9”

Not allowed

  • Alcohol
  • Animals (except service animals)
  • Bags that do not meet the bag policy
  • Cans
  • Chairs of ANY kind
  • Fireworks
  • Glass or metal containers
  • Hard sided/large coolers
  • Illegal drugs/substances
  • Laser pointers
  • Strollers
  • Umbrellas
  • Weapons of any kind

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accessible Parking is located just outside of Gate 4 on the southwest side of the venue, in front of the Premier Parking lot. All venue entrances at Credit Union 1 Amphitheatre are wheelchair accessible.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Credit Union 1 Amphitheatre offers a variety of food and drink options, including Mosh Burger's hormone-free beef burgers, Rebel Hen's crispy chicken tenders, and Ziggy's Magic Pizza Shop's NY-inspired pizzas. Koko's Bavarian provides German-inspired sausages and pretzels, while general concessions offer popcorn, nachos, and a selection of drinks.
