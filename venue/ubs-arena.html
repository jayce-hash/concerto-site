Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags no larger than 12″ x 12″ x 6″ are allowed and will be screened. Backpacks of all sizes are prohibited, but medical and baby bags are permitted with screening.

Allowed

  • Bags no larger than 12″ x 12″ x 6″
  • Medical bags
  • Baby bags

Not allowed

  • Backpacks of all sizes
  • Weapons of any kind
  • Professional camera equipment
  • Laptops and personal computers
  • Audio/video recording devices
  • Laser pointers or flashlights
  • Illegal drugs/substances
  • Outside alcohol
  • Fireworks and projectiles
  • Large signs, flags, and banners
  • Balloons and inflatables
  • Unapproved pamphlets
  • Firestarters and lighters
  • Cans, bottles, and insulated containers
  • Pets, other than service animals
  • Selfie sticks and poles
  • Coolers of any size
  • Offensive signs or garments
  • Noisemakers
  • Optical illusions
  • Drones
  • Patrons without shoes, shirts, or pants/shorts
  • Flowers, unless allowed by tour production
  • Skateboards and rollerblades

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at UBS Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available for purchase prior to events via ParkWhiz and Ticketmaster, with prices increasing by $5-$10 at the arena. General paid parking is available at Emerald Parking, with additional lots accessible via the Cross Island Parkway.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in UBS Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Wheelchair seating is located in each of the following sections: 103, 104, 105, 109, 112, 114, 115, 117, 205, 210, 214, 217, 222. Also, if ASL interpreter services are needed, we recommend sitting at an accessible platform, located at the following Sections: 103, 104, 105, 109, 112, 114, 115, 117.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

UBS Arena offers a diverse range of dining options with 12 market-style outlets featuring hot, freshly prepared foods, ready-to-go meals, and self-serve snacks and beverages. Highlights include vegetarian, gluten-free, and kosher options, as well as unique offerings like the 'Fear the Schmear' Porkbelly and Habanero Sandwich and Benihana Hibachi Bowls.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in UBS Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at UBS Arena. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
