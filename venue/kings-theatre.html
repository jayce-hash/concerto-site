Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Bags must measure 18 × 14 × 9 inches or smaller and are searched at entry. Hiking backpacks, suitcases and other oversized bags are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 18 × 14 × 9 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
  • Hiking backpacks
  • Suitcases
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Everyone passes security screening that can include metal detectors, visual inspection and bag searches. The box office opens two hours before advertised showtime, but the venue does not publish a universal door time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Kings Theatre recommends public transit, taxi or rideshare because nearby street parking is limited.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Kings Theatre recommends taxi, Uber or Lyft for arrival but does not designate a pickup or drop-off curb.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Kings Theatre has an official accessibility assistance page and contact for accommodations; check available seating and facilities before booking.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The theatre prohibits outside food and drinks except medical exemptions; contact the venue before the event about an exemption.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Kings Theatre enforces a no re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Collect will-call tickets at the theatre box office beginning 60 minutes before advertised showtime. The box office does not accept third-party will call.

Official source
