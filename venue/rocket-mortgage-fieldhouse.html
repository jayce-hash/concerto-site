Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Rocket Arena allows bags that are 14” x 14” x 6” or smaller and fit under the seat without obstructing aisles. Larger purses, diaper bags, and medical bags must use the designated X-ray lane.

Parking

Parking

The Gateway East Garage and JACK Cleveland Casino Garage offer on-site parking for 3,300 cars with varying event prices. Metropolis Technologies manages the Gateway East Garage, using license plate readers for automatic billing. Additional parking options are available nearby.

Concessions

Rocket Mortgage FieldHouse offers a variety of food and beverage options across several bars and lounges.
