Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Small clutches, wristlets, or fanny packs no bigger than 6”x 9” and clear plastic bags no bigger than 12"x12"x6” are allowed. All bags are subject to search upon entry.

Parking

Parking

Parking is available at lots surrounding the waterfront with varying prices. For easier entry and exit, consider Premier Parking or VIP Parking. Accessible parking is available, with Lot 1 being the closest to the venue.

Concessions

Concessions

Freedom Mortgage Pavilion offers a variety of food and drink options including Mosh Burger's hormone-free beef burgers, Rebel Hen's crispy chicken tenders, and Ziggy's Magic Pizza Shop's NY-inspired pizzas. Koko's Bavarian provides German-inspired sausages and pretzels, while CVT offers premium soft serve ice cream. General concessions include popcorn, nachos, and a selection of alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages.
