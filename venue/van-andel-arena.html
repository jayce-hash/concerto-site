Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Van Andel Arena enforces a No Bag policy, allowing only small clutches and bags for medical or parenting needs. This policy aims to reduce contact between guests and security staff.

Allowed

  • Small clutches no larger than 4.5" x 6.5" x 2"
  • Bags required for medical or parenting needs

Not allowed

  • Bags
  • Backpacks
  • Purses

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Van Andel Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking facilities near Van Andel Arena are managed by the City of Grand Rapids. Metered parking is available on surrounding streets, and accessible parking is available in city-owned ramps and lots on a first-come, first-served basis.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Van Andel Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Wheelchair accessible seating and semi-ambulatory seating are located in various sections and levels throughout Van Andel Arena.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Van Andel Arena offers a variety of food and beverage options through its exclusive partner, Legends Global. The venue emphasizes local flavors and cashless transactions, with gift cards available for purchase. Alcohol purchases require ID validation.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

As re-entry is not permitted, patrons who exit the arena for any reason, including to smoke, will not be allowed to re-enter the facility.

Official source
