Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches are permitted and searched; nonclear bags may receive additional screening. Backpacks and drawstring bags are barred.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Drawstring bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office opens at doors on show days, and security screening takes place at the main entrance. Check the show listing for its exact door time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue advises using street parking where available and following posted signs. AutoZone, Club Carnaval and Taco More lots tow or boot unauthorized vehicles.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue recommends rideshare and directs drop-offs to the front of its security screening lanes.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The main entrance is accessible. Accessible viewing is first come first served, accessible stalls are in all restrooms, and accessible parking is in front of the main entrance by security.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Beer, wine, spirits and nonalcoholic beverages are sold throughout the venue. Transactions are cashless and accept major cards and Apple Pay.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue does not allow guests to leave and return during a show.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Emo’s box office opens when doors open on show days, but the venue guide does not set out a separate will-call ticket pickup process.

Official source
