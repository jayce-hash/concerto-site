Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Texas Athletics clear-bag rule allows clear plastic bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches, one-gallon resealable bags and clutches smaller than 4.5 × 6.5 inches. The football guide does not establish whether an individual concert has a different bag rule.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear bag
  • Clutch smaller than 4.5 × 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized clear bags
  • Nonclear bags larger than 4.5 × 6.5 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For football, gates open two hours before kickoff and guests must use the gate on their mobile ticket. Concert opening times and assigned gates are event-specific.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The official 2026 football parking page lists garages and lots including Brazos, Manor and LBJ, but it does not publish concert parking assignments or rates. Check the concert event page.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For football, Texas Athletics designates the south curb of Dean Keeton Street between Robert Dedman Drive and Red River Street for rideshare pickup and drop-off. Concert routing may differ.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For football, accessible guest entry is designated at Gates 7, 14, 16 and 27; Fan Services golf carts can transport guests with mobility, hearing or vision impairments toward the gates. Concert arrangements may differ.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The stadium’s football guide says concession and merchandise purchases are cashless and that cash-to-card kiosks are in the Hall of Fame and at Gate 7. Outside food and drink are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drink
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The football guide prohibits re-entry at football games but does not state the policy for concerts at this stadium.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The football guide identifies mobile ticket support at ShopBack Gate and LLH Gate but does not publish a concert will-call procedure.

Official source
