Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Oriole Park at Camden Yards has implemented a clear bag policy to enhance safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Allowed

  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) with no obscured interior pockets up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag (Re-sealable, clear) 1 Gallon
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”
  • Seat cushions without pockets, zippers or concealable areas up to 18” wide

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags and wallets exceeding the size of a small clutch bag (6.5" W x 4.5" H)
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage of any kind
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags
  • Briefcases
  • Fanny packs
  • Cinch bags
  • Any bag larger than the permissible size (6.5" W x 4.5" H)
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Guest Experience Kiosks, located around the main concourse and on Eutaw Street, identified by light blue umbrellas, operate from the time the gates open until 15 minutes after the game ends. Gates open 2.5 hours before first pitch

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Oriole Park at Camden Yards uses the Camden Yards Sports Complex lots near the B&O Warehouse plus numerous downtown Baltimore garages within walking distance. Prepaid parking is cheaper than drive-up, and the MARC/Light Rail Camden station is adjacent.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Oriole Park at Camden Yards's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Oriole Park at Camden Yards, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The ballpark offers a variety of food and drink options including local and popular beers at the Beer Garden, comfort foods with a modern twist at Bridges Grille, and elevated classics at Cadillac Diner. Special offerings include a Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Once in the ballpark, fans are not permitted to exit and re-enter ANY gate to receive multiple items. Fans requesting to leave the ballpark with re-entry permission should be directed to the Guest Services Center located behind home plate, where one of the above-mentioned individuals will be contacted for authorization.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lockers will be located at the north end of the Warehouse by Gate H near the Main Box Office and outside of Gate A & C. The Orioles Box Office is located at the north end of the Warehouse by Gate H.

Official source
