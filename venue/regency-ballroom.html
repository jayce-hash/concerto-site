Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ points to a separate prohibited-items list but does not state a general bag-size or clear-bag rule in its accessible FAQ text.

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors normally open one hour before the show, though concert times vary. The box office opens at the same time as doors.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue lists nearby public and private lots, including the public lot at 819 Ellis Street, plus limited street parking.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The FAQ notes that rides can be found on Van Ness Avenue but does not identify a venue-designated rideshare stop.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible entry is at 1270 Sutter Street; seating is on the main ballroom floor toward house left, and accessible stalls are on the main level and in the Social Hall and Lodge.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The ballroom serves alcoholic drinks, including featured $8 beer, wine and cocktail deals. Outside food and drinks are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

There is no re-entry after ticket scanning, except through the venue’s designated smoking area.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will Call opens when doors open. Bring photo ID, the card used for purchase and the AXS confirmation number; ordinary tickets are scanned from the AXS mobile app.

Official source
