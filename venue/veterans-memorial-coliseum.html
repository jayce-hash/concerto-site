What matters at Veterans Memorial Coliseum.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags 10x6x2 inches and smaller can go through expedited entry, while bags up to 14x14x6 inches must be screened by x-ray machines. Bag check is available at the P3 Lobby of the Garden Garage.

Parking

Parking

The Rose Quarter offers over 2500 parking spaces across four garages and two lots, with prices ranging from $10 to $40 depending on the event. Pre-paid parking is available for most events, and motorcycle parking costs the same as regular vehicles.

Concessions

Concessions

Veterans Memorial Coliseum offers a variety of food and drink options including local favorites like 19 Acres Cider and Cha Cha Cha. Visitors can enjoy a range of offerings from BBQ and Mexican food to vegan and vegetarian options. Special highlights include the woman-owned Fusion Shave Ice stand.

Accessibility

Accessibility

The Rose Quarter campus has four parking garages each with ADA accessible parking on most levels. Veterans Memorial Coliseum's dedicated ADA entrance, is located on P3 in the Garden Garage on the north end of the Moda Center.
