Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Olympiahalle does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Olympiahalle. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Visitors are advised to arrive early and approach the car park from the north via Georg-Brauchle-Ring due to potential traffic restrictions. Increased waiting times and traffic jams are expected due to ongoing construction work.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Olympiahalle's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Olympiahalle, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Olympic Park Munich offers a variety of dining options including Restaurant Coubertin, which serves snacks, pasta, and curry dishes, and DECK 21, a rooftop bar. The Olympia Parkcafe is open when the tennis courts are in use, and the Summer Garden beer garden is a popular spot when the weather is nice.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Olympiahalle's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Olympiahalle. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
