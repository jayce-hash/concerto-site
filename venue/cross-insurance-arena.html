Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Cross Insurance Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fans can pre-book parking through ParkWhiz for convenience. Additional street parking is available on Free Street, Center Street, and Spring Street, with the Spring Street Parking Garage charging $4/hr. Availability and rates may vary depending on the event.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Cross Insurance Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Cross Insurance Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.
