Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags larger than 8.5 × 11 inches, including backpacks, briefcases, luggage and duffel bags, are prohibited. Arrivals are screened before entering.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 8.5 × 11 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags larger than 8.5 × 11 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Fox is at 660 Peachtree Street NE, at Ponce de Leon Avenue. The ticket office opens three hours before doors on show days; the event page gives actual door time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theatre does not own its surrounding lots; parking may be prepaid with tickets up until two hours before the show. Accessible spaces are assigned first come first served within each lot.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theatre’s directions and ticket FAQ pages do not specify a dedicated rideshare pickup or drop-off area.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Closest accessible parking is at the Yellow Lot, Georgian Terrace deck and Orange Lot; the venue lists accessible seating and guest services for visitors needing assistance.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Mezzanine North bar sells cocktails, beer, wine, soft drinks, bottled water, popcorn, chips and candy. Items may vary by show.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Once inside the Fox Theatre, guests cannot leave and re-enter.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

For ticket-window and phone purchases made since April 1, 2026, the cardholder must collect tickets from will call on show day with the purchase card and matching photo ID. On show days the ticket office opens three hours before doors.

Official source
