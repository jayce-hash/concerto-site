Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Only small purses up to 13 inches wide, 4 inches deep and 9 inches high are admitted and searched. Other bags, including backpacks, are prohibited; there is no bag storage.

Allowed

  • Purse up to 13 × 4 × 9 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Other bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bars open when the event’s doors open. The show-day Riverside box office opens at noon; small purses are searched and other bags cannot be stored.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The operator provides options for paid advance parking near the Riverside Theater; the specific lot and rate depend on the show.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ and Riverside parking page do not designate a rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The operator says the theater offers accessible entrances and seating and a ramp or elevator; contact its box office for arrangements.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bars serve drinks starting when doors open and throughout the show; the theater does not serve food. Outside food, drink and water are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drink
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Riverside Theater operator FAQ does not state a general concert re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call tickets are held at the box office and require photo ID; pickup is during box office hours or beginning two hours before showtime. The Riverside box office opens at noon on event days.

Official source
