Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Approved bags are clear plastic up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches, one-gallon clear freezer bags, or clutches up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear freezer bags
  • Clutches up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Non-approved bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

All public entry points use walk-through metal detectors. Accessible drop-off is available at the north and south lobby entrances.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Paid basketball parking is available through Gates 2, 4, 12, 13, and 14. Accessible drop-off is available at the north and south lobbies, with designated accessible parking nearby.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Uber, Lyft, and taxi pickup and drop-off are at Bart Kaufman Field off Fee Lane.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue provides parking and accessibility accommodations through event staff; guests may contact the Indiana Athletics Ticket Office before arrival for assistance.
