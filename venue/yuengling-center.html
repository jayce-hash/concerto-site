Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Personal bags must be smaller than 12 × 12 × 12 inches and are searched. Backpacks are prohibited, with parenting, diaper, and medical bags allowed.

Allowed

  • Personal bags smaller than 12 × 12 × 12 inches
  • Parenting bags
  • Diaper bags
  • Medical bags

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The arena guide identifies Gates A, B and C and Guest Services at Gate A, but does not publish a general door-opening time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Parking is first come, first served, prices vary by event, and cash is not accepted. The venue lists Lot 22 for Gates A and B and Lot 6 for Gate C and the club; arrive early for concerts.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published a specific rideshare zone in its arena guide.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Accessible parking is available in every lot with an official placard; the venue asks for two weeks’ notice to arrange an ASL interpreter for a concert. The guest elevator is inside Gate D lobby.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Arena-managed concessions are cashless, with mobile ordering at select stands; the venue allows one empty plastic water bottle for refilling. Metal and glass bottles and outside beverages are prohibited.

Allowed

  • One empty plastic water bottle

Not allowed

  • Metal water bottles
  • Glass bottles
  • Outside beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Re-entry is generally prohibited; guests seeking an emergency exception should visit Guest Services in the Gate A lobby.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The ticket office is at Gate A on the southwest corner and opens three hours before ticketed concert showtime; tickets are mobile only. The page does not specify a distinct will-call process.

Official source
