Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Howard Theatre does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Howard Theatre. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The venue recommends reserving nearby parking in advance; additional parking is available at Progression Place, 1825 7th Street NW. The Shaw–Howard University Metro station is about one block away.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Ride share services such as Uber/Lyft are easily available from the metro stop.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Howard Theatre, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Howard Theatre offers a variety of food and drink options, including classic American fare and a selection of beverages. Patrons can enjoy a mix of casual dining and quick bites, with options to suit different tastes. Special offerings may include themed cocktails or seasonal menu items.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Howard Theatre's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Howard Theatre. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
