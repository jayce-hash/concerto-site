Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Baylor applies a clear-bag policy to ticketed athletics events: one clear bag up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches, a one-gallon clear freezer bag, or a clutch up to 4 x 6 inches.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear freezer bags
  • Clutches up to 4 x 6 inches

Not allowed

  • Non-approved bags
