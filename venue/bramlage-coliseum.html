Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

K-State permits clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches and approved small personal bags. Breast pumps may be carried in a dedicated inspected medical bag or compliant clear bag.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches
  • Approved small personal bags
  • Inspected breast-pump bags

Not allowed

  • Non-approved bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Gates open one hour before tipoff. Events expected to draw 5,000 or more guests use security screening.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Parking opens two hours before tipoff. Men’s basketball general parking costs $12 in advance or $15 at the gate; women’s basketball costs $10 in advance or $12 at the gate, and charter buses cost $30.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published specific rideshare guidance on its official venue or guest-information pages.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Bramlage Coliseum provides accessible seating, parking, and service-animal accommodations under its A–Z guide.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Concessions operate on the concourse when gates open, and ticket office, concession, parking, and retail points are cashless.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

A ticket logged at entry cannot be reused by another guest; unrestricted re-entry is not permitted.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

A photo ID is required for all will-call orders. The Athletics Ticket Office may reissue lost season tickets only to the ticket holder of record.

Official source
