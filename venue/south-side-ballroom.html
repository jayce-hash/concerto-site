Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Single-compartment clutches or wallets up to 6 × 9 inches, or clear plastic or vinyl bags up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches, are allowed. All bags are searched at entry.

Allowed

  • Single-compartment clutch up to 6 × 9 inches
  • Clear bag up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversize bags and backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors usually open 60 minutes before showtime, but the individual event page controls. Expect metal-detector screening or a physical pat-down and a bag search.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Use the Gilley’s Dallas parking area; an attendant directs guests to general or VIP parking. The lot accepts credit cards and does not offer prepaid event parking.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ does not publish a designated rideshare pickup or drop-off location.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible seats are available when booking; ask parking staff for the closest accessible lot or use the drop-off directly in front. Request an assistive listening device at least two days ahead or an interpreter at least four weeks ahead.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Beer, wine and spirits are available for purchase. Food service is not normally provided unless announced in advance; outside food and drinks are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Leaving the venue ends admission; a new ticket is needed to enter again.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ covers mobile ticket issues and box-office assistance but does not publish a routine will-call pickup procedure.
