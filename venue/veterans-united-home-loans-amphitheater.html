Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Veterans United Home Loans Amphitheater offers a variety of food and drink options, including Mosh Burger's hormone-free beef burgers, Rebel Hen's crispy chicken tenders, and Ziggy's Magic Pizza Shop's NY-inspired pizzas. Guests can also enjoy Koko's Bavarian sausages and pretzels, as well as CVT Premium Soft Serve Ice Cream.
