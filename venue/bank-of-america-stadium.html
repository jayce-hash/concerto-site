Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bank of America Stadium follows the NFL Clear Bag Policy, allowing only specific types of clear bags and small clutches. Prohibited items include non-transparent bags, weapons, and other potentially hazardous items.

Allowed

  • Any transparent plastic, vinyl or PVC bag no larger than 12" x 6" x 12"
  • Purses, bags, clutches, or fanny packs no larger than 4.5" x 6.5"
  • Containers for items of medical necessity
  • Flags without poles or sticks
  • Seat pads without covers, zippers, clasps, pockets or any other device that would allow concealment of any items
  • Tablets

Not allowed

  • Alcoholic beverages
  • All balls, including footballs of any size
  • All non-transparent bags or items, including backpacks, binocular/camera cases, cinch bags, computer bags, diaper bags, purses larger than 4.5" x 6.5", etc.
  • Baby seats
  • Balloons
  • Coolers or containers, including cans and bottles
  • Explosives
  • Fireworks
  • Folding chairs
  • Food and beverages, except two (2) sealed plastic non-flavored water bottles per person
  • Horns, bells, whistles and other noise makers
  • Illegal drugs
  • Laptops
  • Laser pointers
  • Pets, except service animals assisting those with disabilities
  • Seat cushions with covers, zippers, clasps, pockets or any other device that would allow concealment of any item
  • Selfie Sticks
  • Strollers
  • Umbrellas
  • Video equipment
  • Weapons of any kind and size, including guns, knives, scissors and those carried with a permit
  • Other items that in the judgment of stadium management pose a safety hazard or diminish the enjoyment of an event by other patrons

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Bank of America Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There are more than 30,000 parking spaces within a 10-15 minute walk of the stadium. Convenient and affordable parking can be reserved in advance through SpotHero.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The City of Charlotte advises fans to pick up and drop off using their preferred rideshare app at the corner of Third and Church Streets, next to Romare Bearden Park.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bank of America Stadium's stadium recognizes the needs of persons with disabilities, as defined by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) of 1990 and continues to make every effort to comply with both ADA and state accessibility mandates.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The stadium offers alcoholic beverages with a strict ID policy and limits on purchases. Alcohol sales end at the third quarter. No specific food options are detailed.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Bank of America Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For FC match days, the Ticket Office will open 4 hours prior to kickoff time. The Carolina Panthers / Charlotte FC Ticket Office is located between the South Gate and BCBS NC East Gate of Bank of America Stadium and due to construction is temporarily only open on event days.

Official source
