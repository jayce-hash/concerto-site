Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All guests and bags are screened before entry. Review the current official allowed/prohibited-items list before packing, because festival security may refuse or dispose of noncompliant items.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The 2026 main entrance and box office are near S. Sacramento Blvd. and W. Ogden Ave. Main gates are scheduled to open at 11:00 a.m. each festival day, subject to change.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Riot Fest strongly recommends CTA, bicycle, taxi, or rideshare instead of neighborhood parking. The main entrance is at W. Ogden Ave. and S. Sacramento Dr.; complimentary ADA parking is along S. Sacramento Dr. across from that entrance and requires a valid placard or plate.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Riot Fest recommends cab or rideshare service to Douglass Park. Use the main entrance area at W. Ogden Ave. and S. Sacramento Dr. and follow event-day traffic controls in the app.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The grounds include accessible routes linking parking, entrances, stages, viewing areas, vendors, and activity areas. Free first-come ADA parking is across from the main entrance on S. Sacramento Dr.; a verified placard or license plate is required.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food, beverage, and retail vendors operate throughout the festival; free water refill stations are provided. VIP, Deluxe, and Deluxe+ areas include dedicated food and bar options.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

GA tickets permit one entry per day and do not include same-day re-entry. VIP, Deluxe, and Deluxe+ wristbands permit re-entry.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

GA tickets are electronic and can be scanned at the gate. VIP, Deluxe, and Deluxe+ wristbands may be shipped or held for will call according to the order confirmation; the main box office is near S. Sacramento Blvd. and W. Ogden Ave.

Official source
