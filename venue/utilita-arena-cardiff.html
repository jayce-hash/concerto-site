Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Only one bag per person measuring at most 29 × 21 × 15 cm is allowed after search; there is no storage for larger bags. Coat check is £3.50 per item or £5 for a permitted filled bag.

Allowed

  • One bag up to 29 × 21 × 15 cm

Not allowed

  • Larger bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors normally open about one hour before the performance; the ticket or event page gives the show’s actual time. Have tickets and small bags ready for scanning and search.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

There is no public on-site event parking; use nearby multi-storey car parks or public transport.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Contact the arena’s access team before arrival with medical equipment, medicines or food needed for a medical condition; accessible booking information is on its dedicated page.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Arena foyers sell food and beverages, and free drinking water is available. Re:Fuel serves fries, hotdogs and pizza in the main foyer.
