Climate Pledge Arena

Seattle, WA · US

Know before you go

What matters at Climate Pledge Arena.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Climate Pledge Arena allows one bag no larger than 14″ x 14″ x 6″ or one small clutch/purse/wallet no larger than 4″ x 6″ x 1.5″. Bags larger than a clutch must go through x-ray screening.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Parking for events is available at Arena Garage, 1st Ave N Garage, and 5th Ave Garage, with rates starting at $7 per hour plus tax. Pre-purchase is available for event days via the Kraken + CPA Mobile App, while non-event day parking is walk-up only.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

All rideshare vehicles will be prohibited from dropping off riders on 1st Ave N. The best rideshare drop-off locations are not specified.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Climate Pledge Arena offers a diverse range of food and drink options, including gluten-free, vegan, and vegetarian choices. Highlights include nachos with various proteins, New York-style pizza, and the official sausage of the Seattle Kraken. The arena also participates in a pilot program allowing guests to move freely with mixed drinks.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

ADA &amp; Accessible Services

Fully ADA compliant. ADA Entry at Entry 3 (west side, near KEXP). Assistive listening devices at Guest Services near Section 24. All restrooms accessible.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Check with Guest Services before exiting. Service animal relief via Entry 8 with re-entry.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Will Call

Box Office at southwest corner (1st & Thomas). Opens 3 hrs before event. Photo ID required.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Gates &amp; Entrances

11 numbered entries. Entry 8 (southeast, Alaska Airlines Atrium) is the main public entrance. Entry 3 is the ADA entrance.
