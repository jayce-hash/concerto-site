Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Citi Field enforces a clear bag policy to ensure guest safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags larger than a small clutch
  • Luggage
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags
  • Briefcases
  • Fanny packs
  • Cinch bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests with accessibility needs may also enter through our designated accessible gates (Hodges, Seaver, or Stengel VIP entrances) even if their seating location does not grant them VIP entry.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Citi Field offers parking for regular season and special events with prices ranging from $40 to $120 depending on vehicle type and event. Fans are encouraged to use discounted offsite parking at Skyview Mall, a 10-minute walk from the stadium. All payments are cashless, with credit/debit cards and mobile payments accepted.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Citi Field's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The following sensory resources are available at no charge at the Lost and Found located in the Jackie Robinson Rotunda as well as Fan Assistance HQ at Section 106 for use during games and events: Empire Suites 201-239 are located on the Empire Suite Level, which is accessible from any of the three VIP entrances - Hodges, Seaver and Stengel - via elevator or staircase.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Citi Field offers a variety of food and drink options including a lively Beer Garden with local and popular beers, and several concession stands serving comfort foods with bold flavors. Highlights include specialty items like Bacon Wrapped Hot Dogs, Pastrami Fry, and indulgent desserts like Soft Serve and Milkshakes.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Citi Field's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For ticket sales, questions and concerns, fans may call the Mets Ticket Office at (718) 507-TIXX to speak to a Ticket Office representative. The main box office ticket windows at Citi Field are located on the exterior of the ballpark, on the third base side of home plate and the Jackie Robinson Rotunda, adjacent to the Ticket Services Office.
