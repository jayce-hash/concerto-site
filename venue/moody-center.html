Moody Center

Austin, TX · US

Know before you go

What matters at Moody Center.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Guests are allowed to bring one clear bag not exceeding 14″ x 14″ x 6″ or one non-clear small clutch/purse/wallet not exceeding 5″ x 9″ x 1.5″.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Parking is available at various garages near Moody Center, with pre-purchase options recommended. Manor Garage is pre-pay only and closest to ADA parking, while Trinity Garage is a short walk away. Expect traffic disruptions due to construction and plan accordingly.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A passenger drop-off/pick-up point is available on the northwest side of the venue on Robert Dedman Dr.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Moody Center offers a diverse range of food and beverage options, including market-style outlets with Austin favorites. Highlights include Shaq's hand-breaded chicken sandwich, build-your-own nachos, and New York style pizza. Various beverages and snacks are available throughout the venue.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Guests who require additional assistance because of a limitation in mobility can request a wheelchair transport from the entry doors of the arena to their seating location or you may text the code word “Moody” to [69050] upon arrival to our Accessibility Drop Off for assistance.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Moody Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Will Call is located at the Southwest corner of the facility at the intersection of Robert Dedman Drive and 20th Street. Will Call will open 2 hours prior to an event.

Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Moody Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.
