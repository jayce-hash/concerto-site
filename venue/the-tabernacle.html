Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” are allowed, but backpacks and multi-compartment bags are prohibited. All bags will be searched, and non-clear bags may undergo additional searches.

Allowed

  • Bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Multi-compartment bags
  • Laptop cases

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at The Tabernacle. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Premier Parking is available as an amenity on a show-by-show basis. It's recommended to check availability and consider arriving early to secure parking.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in The Tabernacle's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by The Tabernacle, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Tabernacle does not allow outside food or drink, and there is no specific information available about food and drink options inside the venue. Drinking fountains are available for water.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in The Tabernacle's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at The Tabernacle. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
