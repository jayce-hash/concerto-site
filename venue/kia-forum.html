Bag Policy

Bag Policy

The Kia Forum allows clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches and small clutches or wristlets up to 9 x 6 inches. All bags are subject to search.

Parking

Parking

Onsite parking is available at the Kia Forum with entrances via Prairie Ave. or Kareem Ct. gates. Cash and all major credit cards are accepted. Carpooling and public transportation are encouraged.

Rideshare

Rideshare

There is a dedicated ride-share pick up lot onsite at lot 'H' (cross streets are Pincay Dr. and Prairie Ave).

Concessions

Concessions

The Kia Forum offers a variety of food and drink options featuring popular Southern California brands like Carney’s, Fresh Brothers Pizza, and Papa Cantella’s Hot Dogs. Alcoholic beverages include Casamigos Tequila, Cîroc, Jack Daniels, and Stella. The venue also has checkout-free stores for quick access to snacks and drinks.
