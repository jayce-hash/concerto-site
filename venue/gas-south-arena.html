Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Gas South Arena enforces a clear bag policy to ensure effective and contactless screening. All bags will be checked, and guests must pass through a metal detector.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Gas South Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking rates vary by event, with non-event rates starting at $5 and event day parking at $15. Valet parking is available for $40, but may not be offered for all events. Prepaying for parking is recommended to save time and ensure a spot.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Pick-Up: Guests utilizing shuttle services, personal pickup or Rideshare such as UBER or Lyft will be allowed to pick up on Arena Drive near the surface lots in front of the Arena. Drop-Off: Guests using drop-off service transportation vehicles, including other ride-sharing services such as Uber and Lyft, may drop off near the front of Gas South District.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Assistive listening devices are available for select performances. The Arena has accessible seating located in all levels of the Arena at all price categories.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Gas South Arena offers a variety of food and drink options including pizza, hot dogs, cheesesteaks, and Mexican food. Beverages include Coca-Cola products and a selection of beers. The venue is cashless, accepting only card payments or specific gift cards.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Exiting and re-entering the building with the same ticket is not permitted.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For Arena events, the Ticket Office opens 1 hour prior to doors for ticket purchases for that specific event. For Theater events, the Ticket Office opens 1 hour prior to the event for ticket purchases for that specific event.

Official source
