Azura Amphitheater

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches and nonclear clutches or fanny packs up to 6 × 9 inches with at most one pocket are permitted.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-pocket clutch or fanny pack up to 6 × 9 inches

Not allowed

  • Larger nonclear bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Lots generally open two hours before showtime and gates one hour before; exact times appear on the event page one or two days before the concert.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Unless the show says otherwise, general and reserved parking cost $25 per vehicle; VIP parking is $35 per vehicle first come, first served. Lots normally open two hours before showtime.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

For rideshare pickup or drop-off, enter the VIP parking entrance off State Avenue and follow the signs; the same approach handles accessible drop-off.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible guests should use the VIP parking entrance off State Avenue for drop-off and pickup; the FAQ does not give detailed seating instructions.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drink vendors sell BBQ, hot dogs, pretzels and beverages. Concessions accept cards only; one factory-sealed plastic water bottle up to one liter is permitted per person.

Allowed

  • One sealed water bottle up to one liter

Not allowed

  • Outside food
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The amphitheater policy and FAQ page does not state a re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office off 130th Street opens Fridays from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.; the venue does not publish a general will-call procedure.

Official source
