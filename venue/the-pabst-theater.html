Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Only small purses up to 13 × 4 × 9 inches are allowed and subject to search. Other bags and backpacks are prohibited, with no bag storage.

Allowed

  • Purse up to 13 × 4 × 9 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Other bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The event sets its own door time, which is when bar service begins. Bring only a small purse and allow time for its security search.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The operator offers advance passes for nearby paid parking; the parking structure beneath the Pabst is operated separately from the theater.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Pabst FAQ and parking page do not specify a designated rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible entry and seating are offered; contact the theater box office for a suitable location.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The bar serves drinks from the posted door time through the performance. There is no food service; outside food, drinks and water are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drink
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Pabst operator FAQ does not publish a general re-entry policy.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will Call is at the box office with photo ID during normal hours or beginning two hours before showtime. The Pabst box office opens Tuesday–Friday noon–6 p.m., and two hours before weekend performances.

Official source
