Know before you go

What matters at Chase Center.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Chase Center has a strict bag policy to ensure the safety and enjoyment of all guests. Bags must be clear and no larger than 14" x 14" x 6".

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Chase Center offers multiple parking options, including official lots and nearby garages. Pricing varies depending on the event and location, and it is recommended to book in advance. Public transportation and rideshare services are also encouraged due to limited parking availability.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Chase Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Chase Center introduces eight new local eateries and concepts, emphasizing Bay Area flavors. Highlights include women-owned businesses from La Cocina, award-winning cheeses from Cowgirl Creamery, and unique offerings like the Hot Dog Bills Burger Dog.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

ADA &amp; Accessible Services

ADA drop-off: 16th St & Terry Francois Blvd, East Entrance. Wheelchairs from Thrive City or Main Lobby to seats. ADA parking in Mercedes-Benz & Warriors Way Garages, pre-purchase required. Contact: guestexperiences@warriors.com.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Re-entry policy varies by event, check your event page.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Will Call

West Entrance Box Office. Mon–Fri 10am–4pm; event days only on weekends. No printed tickets, mobile only.

Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Gates &amp; Entrances

Portal system, ticket shows your portal. Use any entrance to reach your portal. ADA entry at East Entrance.
