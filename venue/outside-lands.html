Bag policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags are allowed. Small non-clear bags, fanny packs, and purses may be up to 6 x 8 x 3 inches. Empty dedicated hydration packs under 2.5L with no more than one additional pocket are allowed; standard backpacks with hydration bladders are not.

Allowed

  • clear plastic/vinyl/PVC bags
  • small non-clear bags up to 6 x 8 x 3 inches
  • empty dedicated hydration packs under 2.5L with no more than one additional pocket

Not allowed

  • standard backpacks with hydration bladders
