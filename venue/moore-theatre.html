Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Large bags are admitted only after full inspection, and an artist may impose a no-bag policy for an individual show. The venue asks guests to avoid bringing large bags.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Theatres use walk-through metal detectors and bag searches; if the detector cannot be used, security conducts a pat-down. At the Moore, the line begins at the front door and may start forming after 6 a.m. on show day.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theatre has no onsite lot; its directions page lists nearby paid parking and transit. The Moore recommends the Securities Garage at 1922 3rd Avenue, with a presold event parking option.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

For a passenger with limited walking ability, the venue identifies a drop-off point at the loading zone in front of the theatre on 2nd Avenue. It does not identify a separate app-based rideshare zone.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theatre has an accessible restroom and its wheelchair seating is on the main floor. Ushers can assist and the accessibility team can discuss accommodation needs.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Full-service bars open for most performances about one hour before the show; food is offered on a show-by-show basis. Outside food and drinks are prohibited except when needed for disability or medical reasons.

Allowed

  • Food or drink needed for medical or disability reasons

Not allowed

  • Outside food or drinks otherwise
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theatre security guide says anyone entering or re-entering is screened, but it does not state when ticketed guests are allowed to leave and return.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The will-call window opens 90 minutes before the performance. Bring photo ID, the purchasing credit card and the Ticketmaster order number, as any of these may be requested.

Official source
