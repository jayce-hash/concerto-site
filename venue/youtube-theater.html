Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Bags must not exceed 11 × 14 inches and are searched; clear bags are not required. The theatre may change the rule for an event.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 11 × 14 inches

Not allowed

  • Bags larger than 11 × 14 inches

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Drinks and snacks are sold at East and West bars on the Orchestra, Plaza and Balcony levels; food trucks operate outside. One factory-sealed 20 oz water bottle or an empty refillable plastic bottle is allowed.

Allowed

  • One factory-sealed water bottle up to 20 oz
  • Empty refillable plastic bottle

Not allowed

  • Other outside food and drinks
