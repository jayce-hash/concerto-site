Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

American Family Field enforces a clear bag policy to ensure guest safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Allowed

  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag (1 Gallon)
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags and wallets exceeding 6.5" x 4.5"
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags
  • Briefcases
  • Fanny packs
  • Cinch bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at American Family Field. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

American Family Field has large on-site surface lots surrounding the ballpark in Milwaukee, with general and preferred options sold prepaid or at the gate. Tailgating is a tradition and permitted in the general lots. Lots open a couple hours before first pitch.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The taxi cab drop off and pick up uses the transit area located outside Helfaer Field. Rideshare companies, such as Uber and Lyft, have a Rideshare Lot located on the south end of the Molitor Lot.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by American Family Field, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

American Family Field offers a variety of food and drink options, including local and popular beers at the Beer Garden, comfort foods with a twist at Bridges Grille, and elevated ballpark classics at Cadillac Diner. Special offerings include a Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in American Family Field's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

An accessible ticket window (will call window #6) is located at the main American Family Field ticket office at the home plate entrance. Since tickets retrieved within the MLB Ballpark app will now be considered printed, the tickets will not be available for pickup at Will Call.

Official source
