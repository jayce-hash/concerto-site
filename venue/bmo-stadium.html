Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Each guest may bring one clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bag no larger than 12 × 6 × 12 inches, or a one-gallon clear bag. Nonclear bags and backpacks are prohibited; medical and child-care needs can be accommodated after screening.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear bag

Not allowed

  • Nonclear bags
  • Backpacks
  • Oversized bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The stadium has seven entrances, but concert entrances and opening times are set for each event. Consult that concert’s Know Before You Go instructions before arrival.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

General parking is in the Gold Lot and Expo Park lots. The stadium recommends buying a parking pass before arrival and entering via 39th Street and Figueroa Street or Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard and Hoover Street.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The published pickup and drop-off area is at Figueroa Street and Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard, entered from Figueroa Street. Event-specific traffic plans can change the location.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The stadium offers assisted listening devices, sensory kits and wheelchair escorts or storage. Request interpretation at least two weeks before the event.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drinks are available inside. Outside food and beverages are prohibited, with an exception for one factory-sealed plastic water bottle up to 20 ounces and approved infant or medical needs.

Allowed

  • One factory-sealed plastic water bottle up to 20 ounces

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
  • Coolers
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests cannot leave and re-enter BMO Stadium using the same ticket.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The stadium lists its northwest box office as open from 10 a.m. on event days, but does not publish a general concert will-call pickup procedure.

Official source
