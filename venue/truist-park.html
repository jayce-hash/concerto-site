Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Truist Park enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Only specific types and sizes of bags are allowed.

Allowed

  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag 1 Gallon
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets up to 6.5” x 4.5”
  • Seat cushions without pockets up to 18” wide

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags and wallets exceeding 6.5" x 4.5"
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags
  • Briefcases
  • Fanny packs
  • Cinch bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Truist Park. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Truist Park sits in The Battery Atlanta in Cobb County with color-coded decks and lots (Green, Gold, Orange, Red) around the complex, sold prepaid by event. Buying in advance is strongly recommended as drive-up is limited and pricey; there is no rail service, so plan for traffic.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The rideshare zone is located along Windy Ridge and allows easy pick-up and drop-off access.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All of Truist Park's Accessible Seating locations are as follows: Sections 1, 4, 9, 12, 22, 25, 30, 39, 107, 111, 113, 118, 122, 130, 133, 138, 141, 144, 145, 146, 156, 213, 215, 218, 220, 231, 233, 238, 243, 313, 315, 318, 323, 329, 333, 339, 344, 346, and all private suites.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Truist Park offers a variety of food and beverage options, including classic comfort foods with bold flavors and modern twists. Highlights include the Beer Garden for local brews, Bridges Grille's signature Garlic Fries, and Cadillac Diner's indulgent Chili Cheese Hot Dog. New offerings include a Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Truist Park's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

With all tickets being mobile, will call as a service is no longer offered through the Truist Park Ticket Office. Since tickets retrieved within the MLB Ballpark app will now be considered printed, the tickets will not be available for pickup at Will Call.

Official source
