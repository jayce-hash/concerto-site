Tickets And Will Call

Buy tickets online, by phone at 216-383-1124 weekdays 11am to 5pm, or in person at the venue's side door (ring the bell) weekdays 11am to 5pm, with no fees for cash. This Way Out, the shop in the building, also sells tickets and is open during most evening shows. Guests under 21 pay an extra fee at the door, and all purchases are non-refundable.
