Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Iron City’s FAQ covers prohibited weapons and security pat-downs but does not publish an explicit bag size or clear-bag rule.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concert attendees undergo a security pat-down at entry; weapons are prohibited and cannot be held by staff. Everyone, including children, needs a ticket and a valid photo ID.

Not allowed

  • Weapons
Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Iron City and Courtney’s lots are free; the Tool Solutions lot across 6th Avenue South is available after 5 p.m. The Backstage lot is closed on show days, and neighboring restaurant lots may tow.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Iron City’s FAQ and visit page do not designate a rideshare pickup point.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

If you need a seat due to a disability, inform the box office when you arrive.

Re-entry

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will Call tickets are available only after doors open for the event; the venue recommends print-at-home delivery for those needing a ticket earlier.
