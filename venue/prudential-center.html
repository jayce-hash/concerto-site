What matters at Prudential Center.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags up to 12"x14" are allowed inside Prudential Center, but backpacks of any size are not permitted. Larger bags are not allowed except for childcare or medical bags, which will be X-rayed.

Parking

Parking

Prudential Center offers several parking options including the Parking Deck presented by Hyundai and Green Street Garage. BMW owners can enjoy complimentary parking for select events, while Hyundai owners have access to reserved spaces. It's recommended to reserve parking in advance.

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Prudential Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

Prudential Center offers a diverse range of food and drink options, including burgers, hot dogs, chicken, Mexican, pizza, and desserts. Highlights include Smash by Pat LaFrieda, Mighty Quinn, and Melba's. Special offerings include a kosher section and Popcorn For The People.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Please visit our Guest Services Center located outside Section 9 on the Main Concourse and request an Assistive Listening Device. Sensory bags containing special KCVIP badges, fidget toys, noise cancelling headphones and other resources, are available for checkout (at no cost by leaving an ID) at our Guest Services Booths located behind Section 9 and 114.

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Will Call windows are located at the Ticketmaster Box Office at Prudential Center. (with the exception of an event on a Saturday or Sunday, then the box office opens at 11am)

Gates & Entrances
