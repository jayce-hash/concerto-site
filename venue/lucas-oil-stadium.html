Bag Policy

Bag Policy

All bags brought into Lucas Oil Stadium must comply with the NFL's clear bag policy. Bags must be clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC and not exceed 12"x 6"x 12".

Concessions

Concessions

Lucas Oil Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, ranging from classic football favorites to new culinary experiences. All concession stands are accessible and accept major credit cards. Sodexo Live! is the exclusive food and beverage provider.

Accessibility

Accessibility

The Sensory Room is located on Street Level in the southwest corner near Section 132. Fans who wish to utilize sensory bags can check them out from the Guest Services Kiosk located behind Section 121 on Street Level.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Lucas Oil Stadium Ticket Office and Will Call are located on the southeast corner of Street Level on Capitol Avenue, across from section 121. The interior Ticket Office is located in the southeast corner of Street Level, across from section 118.

Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

For Colts games, doors open times will be two (2) hours prior to kickoff. Individuals with special dietary/medical needs should contact a First Aid or Guest Services staff at their entry gate for the locations to store special-needs items such as prescription drugs, dietary foods or beverages.
