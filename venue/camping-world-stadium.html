Camping World Stadium

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The A–Z guide lists clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags up to 14 × 14 × 6 inches or a one-gallon clear bag for events applying the clear-bag policy, but also says bags over 4.5 × 6.5 inches are barred. Because the published limits conflict and concerts may differ, check the concert’s event guide before bringing a bag.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 14 × 14 × 6 inches where the event permits
  • One-gallon clear bag

Not allowed

  • Unapproved bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Six public gates A–F serve different seating areas; follow the ticket and concert event guide for the appropriate gate and opening time. Guests and their belongings are screened.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The stadium provides event-specific parking and entry instructions in its event guides; use the particular concert’s parking map and pass.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The general stadium A–Z guide does not publish a single rideshare pickup site applicable to every concert.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Listening devices are loaned from Guest Services near Section 148 against photo ID. A courtesy cart for mobility-impaired guests operates at select events; request an interpreter at least two weeks ahead.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Outside food and beverages are prohibited except when the stadium grants an individual medical exception. Food and drinks are sold within the stadium.

Allowed

  • Medical exception when approved

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages otherwise
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests who leave cannot return without a new unused or unscanned ticket.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The stadium box office opens on event days, but the A–Z guide does not give a universal will-call location for concerts; the North Rollup listed there is for bowl games only.

Official source
