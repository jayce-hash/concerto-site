Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags are allowed but searched on entry. Tipitina’s does not publish a numeric size limit and does not provide bag check.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Each show page gives its door time. All guests need a ticket and government photo ID; most shows are 18 and over, unless stated otherwise.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Patrons may use the back portion of the nearby Rouses lot closest to the venue; cars should not remain over 24 hours. Street parking is also available without blocking neighboring driveways.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue prohibits outside food and drinks, and bars accept cash or cards; the FAQ does not give a fixed food menu.

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call tickets need ID and can be collected at the box office Monday–Friday, 10 a.m.–4 p.m., or after doors open on show night. Digital tickets can be scanned on a phone.

Official source
