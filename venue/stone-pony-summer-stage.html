Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Average-size bags and small backpacks are allowed after inspection; oversized bags, luggage, duffels and large backpacks are prohibited. The FAQ gives no numeric maximum.

Allowed

  • Average-size bag
  • Small backpack

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
  • Luggage
  • Duffel bags
  • Large backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Ticket holders may be searched by wand or pat-down before entering; check the concert listing for the outdoor Summer Stage door time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Asbury Park has metered street parking and paid lots on Fourth, Second and Lake Avenues. The venue cautions that parking is challenging during summer.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Stone Pony FAQ recommends car service in summer but does not publish a designated rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The outdoor Summer Stage has an ADA viewing section; speak with security or event staff on arrival for help. A separate accessible ticket is not needed.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue has food service; outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue prohibits re-entry, with an explicit warning that re-entry is strictly forbidden for guests under 21.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Stone Pony FAQ lists summer box-office hours but does not publish a specific concert Will Call collection rule.
