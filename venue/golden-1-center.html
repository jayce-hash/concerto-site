Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For the Ariana Grande concert on May 3, 2019, Golden 1 Center requires all bags to be 100% clear and not exceed 12” x 6” x 12”. Cameras and recording devices are not permitted.

Allowed

  • clear plastic bags
  • clear vinyl bags
  • clear PVC bags
  • one-gallon resealable clear freezer bags

Not allowed

  • cameras
  • recording devices

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking options around Golden 1 Center include various nearby lots and garages. Pricing may vary depending on the event and location, so it's advisable to check in advance. Arriving early can help secure a convenient spot.

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Golden 1 Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Golden 1 Center is pleased to offer complimentary Assistive Listening Devices (ALDs) to guests with visual impairments & hearing difficulties.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Golden 1 Center offers diverse food options with a focus on local sourcing. Highlights include modern German bites, wood-fired pizza, and Mediterranean flavors, showcasing Sacramento's culinary scene.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Golden 1 Center has a “no re-entry” policy during events. Guests who wish to return a prohibited item to their vehicle, or need to exit and re-enter due to personal emergencies, must first make contact with a Security Supervisor or Manager via Guest Services Stations to make necessary arrangements.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Will Call: Will Call tickets may be picked up on the day of an event or Kings game at the Golden 1 Center Box Office only. Will Call tickets may be picked up on the day of the event at the Box Office only.
