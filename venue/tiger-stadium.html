Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

LSU’s concert event page links its Geaux Safe bag guidance; the stadium’s clear-bag policy applies to concerts. Check the linked policy for approved dimensions and any event exceptions.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For the March 2026 Zach Bryan concert, Tiger Stadium gates opened at 5 p.m. for a 7 p.m. show. Guests used Open Gate screening at entry; the timing was event-specific.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For the March 2026 Zach Bryan concert, free parking was in the Levee, Hayfield and other designated lots; prepaid reserved passes cost $40–$50 and opened at noon. These arrangements were show-specific.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published a specific rideshare pickup or drop-off point in its Zach Bryan concert guide.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For the Zach Bryan concert, free accessible parking and shuttle service were provided in Lot 406 off Skip Bertman Drive; paid accessible spaces were available in Lot 401. A lactation room was near Section 106.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Inside Tiger Stadium, transactions for the Zach Bryan concert were cashless; cash-to-card machines were listed near Gate 30 and Gates 6 and 31. Other concerts may set different menus and payment procedures.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The official Zach Bryan concert guide states that fans may not leave Tiger Stadium and be readmitted.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For the March 2026 concert, will-call windows opened at 2 p.m. at the LSU Athletics Ticket Office on the first floor of the Athletics Administration Building. Later concert pickup times may differ.

Official source
