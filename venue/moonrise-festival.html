Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags are subject to thorough TSA-style searches, and hydration packs with no more than two main compartments and one smaller compartment are allowed if empty upon entry. Other specific items are also permitted under certain conditions.

Allowed

  • Hydration packs with no more than two main compartments and one smaller compartment (must be empty upon entry)

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Moonrise Festival. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The organizer has not published a current edition's parking plan. For any future event, use the official Moonrise travel information rather than historical third-party directions.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Whether you’re ridesharing (Uber/Lyft), taking a taxi, or getting picked up/dropped off by a friend, all guest pick-up/drop-offs must go to the designated Rideshare area, located just a short walk away from the festival entrance.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Tickets and will call
