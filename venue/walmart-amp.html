Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Walmart AMP. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available on-site at Walmart AMP with options for both general and premium parking. Prices may vary based on the event and parking location. It's recommended to arrive early to secure a spot and avoid congestion.

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Walmart AMP's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Walmart AMP, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Walmart AMP's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Walmart AMP. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
