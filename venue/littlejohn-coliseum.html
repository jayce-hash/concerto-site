Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue rules allow clear plastic or vinyl bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches, one-gallon clear freezer bags, and clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches. Backpacks and oversized bags are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear freezer bag
  • Clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Oversized bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For basketball games, the student and club entrances open 90 minutes before tip-off and other doors open 60 minutes before tip-off. The page does not specify concert door times.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Accessible parking is on the north side of Littlejohn Coliseum along Ravenel Road and in the north circle lot. The venue rules do not list general concert lots.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published a specific rideshare pickup or drop-off location on its venue rules page.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Accessible parking is on Ravenel Road and at the north circle lot; four family restrooms are listed around the concourse. A wellness room for nursing mothers is near Sections 111/211.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Concession stands are on the Coliseum concourse and accessible from any portal.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue states that a patron who exits cannot re-enter except with an unscanned ticket.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For basketball games, will call is at the Burton Gallery Box Office near Perimeter Road and opens 90 minutes before tip-off. The page does not specify concert will-call times.
