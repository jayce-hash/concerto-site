Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Small bags and purses no larger than 12 × 12 × 6 inches are allowed; larger bags and backpacks are prohibited. Security may inspect bags at entry.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches

Not allowed

  • Bags and backpacks larger than 12 × 12 × 6 inches
  • Hard-sided briefcases
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The main gate and box office are near 1200 West Blackhawk Street. Security screens people and bags at entry; the show’s listing supplies its door time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

First-come, first-served street parking is available on Elston and Magnolia; nearby paid lots include one at 939 West North Avenue.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The designated Lyft pickup and drop-off zone is on Magnolia Avenue near the entrance; look for pink Lyft signs.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue is wheelchair accessible, and accessible grandstand seats are sold for indoor shows. Request an ASL interpreter at least three weeks ahead.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and beverages are sold at shows, with indoor and outdoor menus varying by performance. The venue is cashless and prohibits outside food and drinks.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Salt Shed does not permit re-entry.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office near 1200 West Blackhawk Street opens at doors on show nights and Fridays 9:30 a.m.–2 p.m.; the venue FAQ does not specify a will-call collection process.

Official source
