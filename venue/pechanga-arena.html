Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Pechanga Arena San Diego enforces a clear bag policy allowing clear tote bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches up to 4.5” x 6.5”. Oversized bags are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Clear tote bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • One-gallon clear plastic freezer or storage bags
  • Small clutch bags or wallets up to 4.5” x 6.5”

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
  • Backpacks
  • Suitcases
  • Beach bags
  • Laptop bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Pechanga Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Preferred Parking offers guaranteed closest to the door spaces, skip the line access, and priority exit. Parking fees vary by event and can be paid in advance or upon entry. Overnight parking is prohibited unless authorized.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Pechanga Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Pechanga Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Pechanga Arena San Diego offers a variety of local food and beverage options, including Pizza Port and Nicolosi’s Italian Restaurant. Beverage highlights include Cutwater Tequila and Just Enough Wines, providing a diverse selection of drinks throughout the concourse.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Pechanga Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Will Call is located at the Box Office on the North side of Pechanga Arena San Diego. In Person – Visit the Pechanga Arena San Diego Box Office located at 3500 Sports Arena Blvd, San Diego, CA 92110 and request accessible tickets.
