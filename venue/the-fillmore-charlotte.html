The Fillmore Charlotte

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches are allowed after search; opaque bags receive additional inspection.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Larger bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors generally open one hour before showtime. Mobile tickets are scanned at entry; pre-show patio guests enter before Fast Lane and general admission at the door time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The NC Music Factory lot across Hamilton Street past AvidXchange may charge an event-dependent rate; reserved VIP parking is adjacent to the deck and sold separately.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Rideshare drop-off and pickup are at the curb directly in front of the Fillmore box office.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible parking is on the first deck level and outside the deck, first come first served with a placard; request other arrangements through the accessibility guide.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The club sells food, alcoholic and nonalcoholic drinks; valid photo ID is needed to buy alcohol.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concerts have no re-entry policy; an outdoor smoking patio connected to the venue is available near the restrooms.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call pickup requires photo ID matching the name on the guest list. The show-day box office opens about two hours before doors.

Official source
