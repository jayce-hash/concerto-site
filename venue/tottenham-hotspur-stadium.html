Know before you go

What matters at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

Each section shows its source and the date it was last checked.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Tottenham Hotspur Stadium does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

There is no public parking available at or around Tottenham Hotspur Stadium. Visitors are advised not to drive to events due to parking restrictions and the operation of tow trucks. Cycling is encouraged with ample cycle parking available.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Tottenham Hotspur Stadium's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Doors

Gates & Entrances

Mobility aids can be stored at accessible entrances 1, 5 and 17 for these events.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Mobility aids can be stored at accessible entrances 1, 5 and 17 for these events. Changing Places provisions are located in the Tottenham Experience, at block 105 and in the East Atrium, all accessed with a radar key.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Tottenham Hotspur Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, including hot food, salads, barista coffee, and bakery goods at the M Café, which is open daily except on event days. Special event menus and exclusive items like loaded nachos and doughnuts are available during specific events.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Tottenham Hotspur Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
