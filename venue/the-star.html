Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Ford Center permits clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12 by 6 by 12 inches, one-gallon clear freezer bags, and small clutches up to 4.5 by 6.5 inches. Medical exceptions are inspected at a designated gate.

Concessions
