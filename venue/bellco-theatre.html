Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All guests at the Bellco Theatre are subject to a search, including metal detection and bag inspection. Prohibited items include outside food and beverages, weapons, and recording devices.

Not allowed

  • All outside food or beverages including alcoholic beverages
  • Illegal drugs
  • Laser pointers
  • All recording devices
  • Weapons or any items construed as weapons
  • Air horns, bullhorns or other distracting noisemakers
  • Animals (except service animals)
  • Personal property that is potentially hazardous

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Bellco Theatre. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking at the Bellco Theatre fills up quickly, and alternative parking options are available around the Convention Center. Parking rates are $13 for up to 8 hours, $16 for up to 12 hours, and $18 for up to 18 hours. SpotHero can be used to reserve parking in advance.

Accessibility
