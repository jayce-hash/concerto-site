Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The O2 Arena in Prague prohibits bringing excess luggage larger than 40 x 30 x 20 cm. Certain items like laptops and tablets are allowed only for VIP guests.

Not allowed

  • alcohol
  • food and beverages (except for health reasons)
  • bottles, containers of all kinds
  • pressurized objects
  • glass or glass objects
  • drugs, toxic and narcotic drugs
  • explosive and flammable substances
  • perfumes – over 100 ml
  • spray paints or deodorants – over 100 ml
  • excess luggage (bigger than 40 x 30 x 20 cm)
  • weapons
  • scissors
  • poles/rods
  • objects with a laser beam
  • drones
  • unworn clothing (excluding items purchased from official merchandising)
  • laptops and tablets (except for VIP guests)
  • professional cameras with detachable lens
  • binoculars (except up to 15 cm long)
  • umbrellas (except in the case of rain)
  • prams
  • bicycles, scooters
  • harmful marketing products
  • political and promotional materials

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at O2 Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at the dedicated parking garage accessible via Českomoravská Street and Lisabonská Street. Alternative parking options include the nearby STAGES HOTEL Prague and Galerie Harfa shopping center.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in O2 Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by O2 Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The O2 Arena offers a variety of food options, including fast food refreshments and exclusive catering for VIP areas. The Lokomotivka restaurant provides a unique dining experience with a bar accessible during certain events.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in O2 Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at O2 Arena. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
