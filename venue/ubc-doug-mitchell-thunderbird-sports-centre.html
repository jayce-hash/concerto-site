Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

UBC – Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at UBC – Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at Thunderbird Parkade, the closest to the venue, and Health Sciences Parkade, which offers quicker exit times. Event rates may vary, and payment can be made via the HonkMobile app on the day of the event.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in UBC – Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by UBC – Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre offers a variety of food choices, including healthy options, at its concession stands. Beverages are also available for every event, ensuring guests can quickly return to their seats.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests are not permitted to leave an event and then re-enter the same event.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at UBC – Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
