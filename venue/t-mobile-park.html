Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Clear plastic bags no larger than 12" x 6" x 12" and small clutch bags no larger than 4.5" x 6.5" are permitted. Backpacks, multi-compartment bags, and larger purses are not allowed.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic bags no larger than 12" x 6" x 12"
  • One gallon clear plastic zip-top bags
  • Small clutch bags no larger than 4.5" x 6.5"

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Multi-compartment bags
  • Duffle bags
  • Purses larger than 4.5" x 6.5"
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at T-Mobile Park. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking options include the T-Mobile Park Garage, Stadium Exhibition Center Garage, Union Station Garage, and the North Lot. Expect heavy traffic congestion for about 45 minutes post-game, especially on First Avenue at Royal Brougham Way.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in T-Mobile Park's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For Guests with sensory needs, our sensory room is located on our Main Level near Section 128 and is dedicated to guests who may feel overstimulated or overwhelmed at our events. Complimentary sensory bags containing fidget tools and noise-cancelling headphones are also available for checkout at our Guest Services Section 128 location.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

T-Mobile Park offers a diverse range of food and drink options, from local favorites like Moto Pizza and Ivar's to classic ballpark snacks. Highlights include the Nakagawa Poke Bowl, Ivar's Halibut and Chips, and Casamigos Cocktails available throughout the concourse. Special offerings include the Ichi Wings and Turn Two Teriyaki Chicken Bowl.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests are not permitted to re-enter the ballpark once they leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The T-Mobile Park Box office is located on Edgar Martinez Drive S. and First Avenue S. If available, you can purchase tickets at the T-Mobile Park box office, located near the Home Plate and Left Field Gates.

Official source
