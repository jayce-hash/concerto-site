What matters at SNHU Arena.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

SNHU Arena enforces a clear bag policy allowing clear totes up to 9" x 12" x 4" and wristlets up to 4" x 6" x 1". Purses and backpacks are not permitted.

Verified 2026-08-20

Parking

Parking

Parking for SNHU Arena events is available at all city meters, parking garages, and parking lots. SNHU Arena does not manage or have parking on site, and is not responsible for parking costs or location. Accessible parking is available on Harry Theo Lane.

Verified 2026-08-20

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in SNHU Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions
