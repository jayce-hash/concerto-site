SNHU Arena

Know before you go

What matters at SNHU Arena.

Each section shows its source and the date it was last checked.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

SNHU Arena enforces a clear bag policy allowing clear totes up to 9" x 12" x 4" and wristlets up to 4" x 6" x 1". Purses and backpacks are not permitted.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Parking for SNHU Arena events is available at all city meters, parking garages, and parking lots. SNHU Arena does not manage or have parking on site, and is not responsible for parking costs or location. Accessible parking is available on Harry Theo Lane.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in SNHU Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Doors

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at SNHU Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

For accessible seating options, please contact Ticketmaster at ticketmaster.com or 1-800-653-8000. The Accessible Parking lot is located on Harry Theo Lane.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

SNHU Arena offers a premium experience with options like the VIP Club and Sam Adams Lounge, which provide full bar and snack service. These areas offer comfortable seating and a modern atmosphere for VIP guests.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Exiting and re-entering the building with the same ticket is not permitted.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at SNHU Arena. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
