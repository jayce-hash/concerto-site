Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Full-size backpacks are prohibited; all other bags are subject to search. The venue does not publish a numeric bag-size limit.

Not allowed

  • Full-size backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The main entrance at 1099 Euclid Avenue is accessible; guests pass through metal detectors. Individual concert listings give door times.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

A paid lot is behind the venue and may fill on busy nights; nearby paid and street parking is first come first served.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue asks riders to set pickup and drop-off at 1099 Euclid Avenue NE, Atlanta, GA 30307.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The main entry is accessible and ADA viewing corrals are at the front left and right for general admission shows. Accessible stalls are on the first level; request an interpreter at least two weeks ahead.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Light snacks and drinks are sold at the bar. Purchases at the venue are cashless.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue’s FAQ and getting-here pages do not state a rule for leaving and re-entering a ticketed show.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office is open during shows and Monday–Friday from 2–6 p.m.; the venue FAQ does not specify a will-call collection process.

Official source
