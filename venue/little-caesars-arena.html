Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags larger than 4” x 6” x 1.5” are prohibited, except for certain exceptions. Single compartment bags, wallets, clutches, fanny packs, and clear bags smaller than the specified size are allowed.

Allowed

  • Single compartment bags
  • wallets
  • clutches
  • fanny packs
  • clear bags smaller than 4" x 6" x 1.5"

Not allowed

  • Bags larger than 4” x 6” x 1.5”
  • Air horns
  • fog horns
  • bullhorns
  • cowbells
  • bells
  • horns
  • kazoos
  • whistles
  • vuvuzelas
  • didgeridoos
  • Aerosol cans
  • Alcoholic beverages
  • Animals (except approved service animals)
  • Apparel with vulgar, obscene, profane, or discriminatory messages
  • Banners, signs, flags larger than 11” x 17”
  • Brooms
  • poles
  • bats
  • clubs
  • hockey sticks
  • selfie-sticks
  • frisbees
  • missile-like objects
  • balloons
  • beach balls
  • Bottles
  • Coolers
  • Chairs
  • Drones
  • Fireworks
  • Laser pointers
  • Marijuana and/or illegal narcotics
  • Masks
  • Outside food and beverage

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Little Caesars Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available around Little Caesars Arena with various lots and garages offering different pricing options. It is recommended to arrive early to secure a spot, especially during major events.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Little Caesars Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accessible restrooms are located on the main floor in the Columbia Street vestibule.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Little Caesars Arena offers a variety of food options including arena classics, chicken dishes, grilled items, and signature Little Caesars pizza. There are also pop-up stands and four in-venue restaurants, providing diverse choices for attendees.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests who exit the venue will not be permitted to re-enter.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Will Call - Located within the Box Office on Woodward Avenue. Will Call is located at the Fox Theatre Box Office.
