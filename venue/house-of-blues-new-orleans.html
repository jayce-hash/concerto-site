Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches may enter after search; nonclear bags are inspected more closely.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The show listing gives the door time, and the box office opens two hours beforehand. Bags are checked and guests are screened before admission.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue lists 716 Iberville Street as a premium parking location, priced at $15 for up to eight hours. It also links advance booking of other nearby garages.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The House of Blues FAQ does not designate a rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Call the box office at 504-310-4999 with accessible seating or entry requests.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Music Hall offers snacks, local beer and a full bar; concession sales are cashless, with a no-fee cash-to-card option. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Re-entry is not allowed for concerts.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call tickets are available on show day at the box office, which opens two hours before doors. Bring valid photo ID and the card used for purchase.

Official source
