What matters at FedExForum.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

FedExForum enforces a clear bag policy, allowing only specific clear bags and wallets inside the venue. Bags must be clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC and no larger than 6x8x2 inches.

Parking

Parking

FedExForum offers parking in the Gossett Motors Parking Garage with 1,500 spaces, available on a season basis for basketball games and first-come, first-served for other events. Rates vary by event type, and accessible parking is available with appropriate tags.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

Guest, limo, taxi, and express shuttle bus drop off is located at the east end of the arena on Fourth Street between Beale and Dr. M.L. King Jr. Avenue.

Concessions

Concessions

FedExForum offers a diverse range of food and beverage options including local favorites like Rendezvous BBQ and Rock'n Dough pizza. Visitors can enjoy a variety of drinks from specialty cocktails to local craft beers. Vegetarian options are available at select stands.

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by FedExForum, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.
