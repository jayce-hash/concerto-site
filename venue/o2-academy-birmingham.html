Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Only one small bag per person is allowed, not exceeding A4 size dimensions. Large bags, including rucksacks, are prohibited.

Parking

Parking

There is street parking available locally which can be reserved ahead of arrival using JustPark. The Mailbox car park is nearby, about 600 metres away, with standard tariffs and 16 accessible spaces.

Accessibility

Accessibility

If you're coming to a show in the main auditorium or O2 Academy2 Birmingham, there's an accessible toilet on the balcony level (first floor), it’s controlled by a RADAR key and our staff will also provide access to it if needed and also in stalls.
