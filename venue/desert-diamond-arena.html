Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Only clutches, wallets, wristlets, and crossbody bags that meet the dimensions of 4.5” x 6.5” are permitted inside Desert Diamond Arena. Diaper bags are allowed with a child and must enter through specific gates.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Parking is available in multiple lots including G, J, L, Yellow, Garage, and VIP lots. Pre-paid parking offers expedited entry and is available until 30 minutes after the event starts. Payment is by credit/debit card only, no cash accepted.

Verified 2026-08-20

Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Desert Diamond Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Entrances & Doors

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Desert Diamond Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Assistive listening devices are available to check out at the Guest Services Booth located near Section 115. Sensory bags containing KCVIP badges, fidget tools, noise canceling headphones and other resources, are available for checkout (at no cost by leaving an ID) at our Guest Services Booth located at Section 115.

Concessions

Concessions

Desert Diamond Arena offers gourmet food and beverage options, including chef-crafted cuisine and premium cocktails, as part of their premium experiences. Guests can enjoy an exceptional culinary experience with a selection of beer, wine, sodas, and water. First-class catering is available for an additional cost.

Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Desert Diamond Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Desert Diamond Arena Box Office is located on the Northeast corner of the venue between Gate 4 (main gate) and Gate 5. The Box Office is closed on non-event days and is open three hours prior to show time for all events.
