Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Acrisure Stadium enforces a clear bag policy to enhance security, allowing clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" and small non-clear bags up to 4.5" x 6.5". Each fan can carry one approved clear bag and a clutch purse.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags not exceeding 12" x 6" x 12"
  • One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags
  • Small non-clear bags not exceeding 4.5" x 6.5"
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Main entries are Gate A East, Gate A West, Ticketmaster Gate B, Heinz Gate C, and the FedEx Great Hall gates. For Steelers and Pitt football games, gates generally open two hours before kickoff; other events publish their own schedules.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Acrisure Stadium offers various parking options including the new Champions Garage with over 400 spaces. Parking passes are mobile delivery only, and no tailgating is allowed in the Champions Garage. Event day parking is card only.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Acrisure Stadium's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accessible seating is available on every stadium level, and all entry gates are accessible. Guests needing additional screening assistance can use the dedicated accessibility lane; contact Guest Services at 412-697-7150, option 4.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Acrisure Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, including mobile ordering for beer and food to minimize wait times. Local favorites like Franco's Pizzeria and Franco's Italian Army Gridiron Sausages highlight the stadium's focus on Pittsburgh's culinary scene.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Exit and re-entry are not permitted. For a medical emergency, request authorization at the Guest Services kiosk at Ticketmaster Gate B before leaving.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Steelers Ticket Office and ticket will-call location are on the east side of the stadium along Art Rooney Avenue. Mobile tickets are standard and screenshots are not accepted.

Official source
