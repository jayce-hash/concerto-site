Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Reed Arena uses Texas A&M’s 12-1-1 bag policy. Bags, purses, and backpacks that do not comply are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Policy-compliant clear bag
  • One-gallon clear bag
  • Small clutch

Not allowed

  • Non-compliant purses, bags, and backpacks
  • Ice chests
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Public entries open 60 minutes before Texas A&M games. For men’s basketball, the north and south entries open 90 minutes before tipoff exclusively for Texas A&M students.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Men’s basketball parking is $5 and accepts cash or credit; listed Texas A&M permits are accepted in designated Lots 100, 74, 95, and 97. Lot 102 is the designated ADA lot.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published specific rideshare guidance on its official venue or guest-information pages.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Lot 102 is the designated ADA parking lot, and Reed Arena provides accessible seating and arena access accommodations.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Levy Restaurants is the official concessionaire for Reed Arena. Outside food and beverages are prohibited except one sealed clear plastic water bottle.

Allowed

  • One sealed clear plastic water bottle

Not allowed

  • Outside food
  • Outside beverages
  • Alcoholic beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Re-entry is prohibited and the arena does not issue pass-outs.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Reed Arena does not operate a will-call location.

Official source
