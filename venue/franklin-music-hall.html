Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Franklin Music Hall does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Franklin Music Hall. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking information is not explicitly detailed on the website. Visitors are advised to check nearby parking facilities or street parking options. Pricing and availability may vary.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Franklin Music Hall's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Franklin Music Hall, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Small bites and nonalcoholic drinks are sold in the lobby between merchandise and coat check. Alcohol is available in designated 21-and-over areas with valid ID; outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Franklin Music Hall's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

On show days the box office will open 2 hours before the scheduled door time. You may also purchase tickets for all Franklin Music Hall shows with NO SERVICE FEES directly from our box office (421 N 7th Street) on Fridays 12pm-6pm.

Official source
