White River Amphitheatre

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Small clutches, wristlets or fanny packs up to 6 × 9 inches, or clear plastic bags up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches, are permitted. All bags are searched; oversized bags and backpacks are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Clutch, wristlet or fanny pack up to 6 × 9 inches
  • Clear plastic bag up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
  • Large backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Gates usually open 90 minutes before showtime, but event-specific times can differ. Entry involves metal detectors, possible pat-downs and bag checks; use the event page for the actual gate time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

General parking is included with the ticket; Premier, Ultra Premier and Star parking are paid upgrades. Lots open at noon on show days according to the current venue guide.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Taxis and rideshare vehicles follow staff directions to enter through Gate C; pickup is also at Gate C.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue publishes a separate accessibility guide for arrival, parking and services; contact staff for needs at the show.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue sells food and drinks and is cashless; a cash-to-card exchange is offered near the main gates. Guests may bring food in one clear one-gallon resealable bag and an empty plastic bottle or up to one gallon of factory-sealed plain water in plastic.

Allowed

  • Food in a clear one-gallon bag
  • Empty plastic water bottle
  • Up to one gallon of sealed plain water in plastic

Not allowed

  • Outside alcohol
  • Metal water bottles
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Re-entry is prohibited; after leaving, a new ticket would be required to return.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue uses mobile entry: download the ticket to a phone in the Live Nation app. The box office opens two hours before doors on show days.

Official source
