Concessions

Concessions

Great American Ball Park offers a diverse range of food options, including traditional ballpark fare, local Cincinnati favorites, and new creations by Chef Gary Davis. Highlights include the Redlegs BBQ Walking Taco and the 50 West Soft Pretzel Double Cheeseburger, available for a limited time. Fans can also enjoy specialty cocktails, a premium selection of bourbons, and a variety of ice cream treats.

Accessibility

Accessibility

All Suites are ADA accessible and have 2 removable seats at the top row of seating for wheelchairs/scooters. Accessible parking is available in the East section of the Central Riverfront Garage (formerly called the Great American Ball Park Parking Garage) for $27 and in the East Garage for $16.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Limited re-entry is permitted at Great American Ball Park. Guests are subject to metal detection screening upon re-entry and there will be no carry-in items permitted through the re-entry gate.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Box Office at the main Crosley Terrace entrance (corner of Second Street and Joe Nuxhall Way) will be open for purchasing tickets and Will Call, along with any digital ticketing questions you may have Will Call: Reds tickets left for will call and drop-offs may be picked up at the “paid” will call windows located at the Main Ticket Office at Crosley Terrace.

Verified 2026-08-20

Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Guests inside of the ballpark can use the re-entry gate at the Gapper’s Alley gates to gain access to the smoking area. Guests are subject to metal detection screening upon re-entry and there will be no carry-in items permitted through the re-entry gate.
