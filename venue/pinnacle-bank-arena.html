Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Pinnacle Bank Arena allows clear bags up to 12” x 12” x 6”, one clear gallon plastic freezer bag, and small clutch purses or wallets up to 4.5” x 6.5”. Bags will be inspected prior to entry.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bag that does not exceed 12” x 12” x 6”
  • One clear gallon plastic freezer bag
  • Small clutch purse or wallet 4.5” x 6.5”

Not allowed

  • Bags, backpacks, mesh bags, partially translucent bags, or large purses
  • Aerosol cans
  • Alcohol, drugs, illegal substances or any paraphernalia associated with drug use
  • Animals (except service animals)
  • Apparel with explicit wording or graphics
  • Athletic balls
  • Audio/Video recorders, professional cameras, or tripods
  • Balloons
  • Cans, glass bottles, bottles, coolers & picnic baskets
  • Chains
  • Fireworks
  • Flags or signs with poles
  • Frisbees
  • Glow Items
  • Laser pens/pointers
  • Masks (non-medical)
  • Musical instruments
  • Noisemakers
  • Outside food and beverage
  • Roller skates, skateboards, scooters, bicycles
  • Selfie sticks
  • Signs larger than 8.5″ x 11″
  • Spiked jewelry
  • Strollers, car seats or booster seats
  • Tablets, iPads, or computers
  • Tactical vests
  • Umbrellas
  • Water bottles
  • Weapons including pocket knives, tasers, firearms, spiked jewelry, mace/pepper spray, and personal defense items

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Pinnacle Bank Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available in the Festival Parking Lot north of the arena with fees ranging from $3 to $10 per event. The parking garage attached to the arena is reserved for premium seating ticket holders. Additional public parking garages are available nearby.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Pinnacle Bank Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Pinnacle Bank Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Pinnacle Bank Arena offers a variety of food and beverage options through Legends Global, which provides tailored services for different events. The venue features 105 concession points of sale and emphasizes sustainability with initiatives like banning polystyrene foam and sourcing local products.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Pinnacle Bank Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Pinnacle Bank Arena. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
