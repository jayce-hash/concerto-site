Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags no larger than 12 × 6 × 12 inches and one-gallon clear bags are permitted. Small nonclear clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches are also permitted; medically necessary items receive inspection.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear bag
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
  • Backpacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The four main entrances are at the southwest corner by Legends Plaza, northwest by Lot 10, northeast by Lot 13 and Driveway D, and southeast by Lot 6. Door times depend on the event.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Several on-site lots offer general event parking, payable by credit card only. Prices depend on the event; accessible parking holders should enter the complex through vehicle Gates A, B or D.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The designated rideshare pickup and drop-off point is at the top of vehicle Gate A, off Avalon Boulevard and 184th Street.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible parking is available through vehicle Gates A, B or D with a valid placard or plate. Guest Services can assist with wheelchair access, and selected concerts offer signed performances.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue operates cashless concession stands and has reverse ATMs for converting cash. Vegan options are listed at Melissa’s, Stand 9 on the east concourse.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue prohibits re-entry during events; an exception is made for guests accompanying a service animal to the animal relief area.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

All tickets are delivered digitally through the AXS app; paper tickets are not issued. The main box office near Legends Plaza opens four hours before event start on event days.

Official source
