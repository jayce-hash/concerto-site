Know before you go

What matters at The Gorge Amphitheatre.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Only small clutches, wristlets, or fanny packs no bigger than 6" x 9" and clear plastic bags no bigger than 12" x 12" x 6" are allowed. All bags are subject to search upon entry.

Parking

Parking

General admission parking is included with your ticket. For easier entry and exit, consider upgrading to Reserved Parking, Star Parking, or Star Parking Plus. Arrive early to avoid heavy traffic and long wait times.

Rideshare
