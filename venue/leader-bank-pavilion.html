Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

One clear plastic or vinyl tote up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches, or a nonclear clutch up to 6 × 9 inches, is permitted; all bags are searched.

Allowed

  • Clear tote up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Clutch up to 6 × 9 inches

Not allowed

  • Other bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Gates usually open 60–90 minutes before showtime; exact times vary. Guests pass metal detectors and bag checks, and the show-day box office opens at 1 p.m.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

There is no onsite parking. The venue suggests the Seaport Hotel Garage, entered from Seaport Boulevard at B Street, and other nearby paid garages.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue visit and transportation guide does not designate a rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue publishes a separate accessibility guide; request an interpreter at least two weeks in advance after purchasing a ticket. The pavilion does not control guest parking.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and beverages are sold inside; selections and prices vary by event. The venue is cashless.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The pavilion visit guide does not publish a general re-entry policy.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call tickets can be collected once doors open with photo ID and the purchasing credit card; artist guest-list tickets are also generally released at door time.

Official source
