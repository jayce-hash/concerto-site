Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags brought into the venue will be searched, and large or oversized bags/backpacks are not permitted. Express entry lanes are available for guests without bags.

Not allowed

  • Weapons of any kind
  • alcohol
  • illegal drugs/substances
  • glass containers
  • cans
  • hard sided/large coolers
  • large or oversized bags/back-packs
  • laser pointers
  • animals (except service animals)
  • fireworks

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Synovus Bank Amphitheater at Chastain Park. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There is limited metered street parking near the venue. Commercial lots are available on 5th and Bainbridge Streets, but they accept cash only. ParkWhiz offers additional parking options.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Synovus Bank Amphitheater at Chastain Park's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Synovus Bank Amphitheater at Chastain Park, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Synovus Bank Amphitheater at Chastain Park's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Will Call is located outside the gates at the bottom of the hill on Elliott Galloway Way, next to Gate #1. The box office is open on event days, two hours prior to show time.
