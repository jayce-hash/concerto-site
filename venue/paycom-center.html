Bag Policy

Concessions

Paycom Center offers a variety of food and drink options including hot dogs, nachos, pizza, and specialty items like Big O's BBQ and Freddy's burgers. Beverage options include bottomless souvenir sodas, draft beers, and bottled water. Some stands may not be open during concerts and special events.
