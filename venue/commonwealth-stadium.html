Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags must be clear plastic and no larger than 12x12x6 inches. Non-compliant bags are not allowed.

Allowed

  • clear plastic bags no larger than 12x12x6 inches

Not allowed

  • Weapons of any kind
  • Glass bottles
  • Cans
  • Containers with a capacity larger than 1 liter
  • Alcoholic beverages
  • Outside food
  • Drugs/narcotics
  • Cannabis
  • Umbrellas larger than 16 inches or with a point
  • Illegal substances
  • Explosives
  • Fireworks
  • Incendiary devices
  • Smoke bombs
  • Aerosol cans
  • Paints
  • Flammable liquids
  • Video cameras
  • Recording devices
  • Professional grade cameras
  • Tripods
  • Selfie sticks
  • Pets
  • Air horns
  • Megaphones
  • Whistles
  • Mechanical noise makers
  • Peanuts in the shell
  • Laser pointers/pens
  • Beach balls
  • Coolers
  • Bags that are not clear plastic and are larger than 12x12x6 inches
  • Unmanned aircraft systems - Drones
  • Pipes
  • Rods
  • Dowels
  • Hockey sticks
  • Signs on poles or sticks or constructed of wood or metal material
  • Any other items deemed to be dangerous or inappropriate

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Commonwealth Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking in Edmonton includes on-street and off-street options with hourly rates ranging from $1 to $4.50. Event parking rates apply during major events at Rogers Place. The first 15 minutes of on-street parking are free, but registration is required.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The curb lane on 112 Avenue eastbound from 95 Street to Stadium Road is a dedicated drop-off and pick-up location for the public and rideshare. A taxi stand is located outside the stadium, on 89 Street and 112 Avenue.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Commonwealth Stadium, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Commonwealth Stadium offers a variety of food and beverage options including premium offerings at market areas on the east and west sides. Highlights include the Teriyaki Chicken Burger, Gourmet Bacon Burger, and Ukrainian Dog. The Legends Club and Jackie Parker Room provide exclusive experiences for Elks Season Seat Holders and ticket holders respectively.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Commonwealth Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Commonwealth Stadium. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
