Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches are admitted after search; nonclear bags receive additional inspection.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors usually open one hour before showtime, subject to the specific listing. Bags are searched before entry.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theatre has no private lot and prohibits parking on Hamilton Place. Boston Common Garage and Center Plaza Garage are nearby.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue directions do not designate a rideshare stop; Hamilton Place cannot be used for parking.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible tickets are offered through the box office or online ticket request; the downstairs restroom has accessible stalls. Ask the venue at least two weeks before a show for sign language interpretation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Beer and wine are sold, with limited food depending on the event. Food and beverage sales accept credit and debit cards.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Re-entry is not allowed.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will Call is available on show night only; bring the physical card used to order and a photo ID.

Official source
