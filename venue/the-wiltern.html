What matters at The Wiltern.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” are allowed in the venue. All bags will be searched prior to entry, and non-clear bags will undergo additional search.

Verified 2026-08-20

Parking

Parking

The Wiltern offers premium underground parking and upgrades for select shows. The most convenient parking option is the Ralphs parking structure adjacent to the venue, accessible from Oxford Street through the north entrance.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in The Wiltern's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Entrances & Doors

Gates & Entrances

The accessible restroom is located on the main level to the right of the main entrance as you enter.

Accessibility

Concessions

Concessions

The Wiltern offers a variety of concession items including popcorn, chips, pizza, churros, and nachos, along with alcoholic and nonalcoholic beverages. The venue is cashless, accepting major credit cards and ApplePay.

Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in The Wiltern's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at The Wiltern. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
