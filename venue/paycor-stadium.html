Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Paycor Stadium enforces a clear bag policy, allowing clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags not exceeding 12" x 6" x 12". Exceptions are made for medically necessary items after inspection.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags up to 12" x 6" x 12"
  • Clear backpacks
  • Clear fanny packs
  • Clear cinch bags
  • One-gallon reusable clear plastic bags
  • Small clutch purse no larger than 4.5" x 6.5"

Not allowed

  • Non-clear bags exceeding size restrictions
  • Backpacks
  • Camera bags
  • Binocular cases
  • Large tote bags
  • Mesh bags
  • Purses
  • Clinch bags
  • Printed pattern/tinted bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Paycor Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking lots open 4-6 hours before kickoff, with accessible parking available in Lot 1. Additional parking options include nearby garages and lots. Queen City Riverboats offer transport from Newport, KY for $10 per person.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

2nd Street between Vine and Walnut

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Approximately 30 ADA parking spaces are available for purchase on the day of Bengals home games in Lot 1 It is strongly recommended that fans purchase parking in advance of gameday. Accessible (ADA) seating is available throughout Paycor Stadium, on all three levels.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Paycor Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options including premium chicken tenders, fries, beer, hard seltzer, soda, and water. These items are available at the TLC sections 142, 112, 152, and 122.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

To relieve the animal, the handler should contact a stadium gate manager to obtain permission prior to exiting the stadium to ensure they will be granted re-entry.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

On game days, the Ticket Office/Will Call location is on the North side of the stadium, located between the altafiber Northwest Gate and Gate C (next to the Bengals Pro Shop). Will Call and sales windows are open three hours prior to kickoff until the end of the second quarter.
