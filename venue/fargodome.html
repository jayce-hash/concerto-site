Fargodome

Know before you go

What matters at Fargodome.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

FARGODOME encourages guests not to bring bags to events. Bags larger than 13”x13” are not permitted unless medically necessary.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Parking lots at Fargodome open approximately 2 hours before events, with general parking costing $5.00 cash. Bus parking is available in lot E for $15.00 cash per bus. All lots close no more than one hour after the event.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

Buses, limos, Uber, Lyft and other transportation services must drop off and pick up guests on the west side on Albrecht Blvd.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

The Fargodome offers alcoholic beverages with a limit of two per purchase, and sales end at the start of the 4th quarter during NDSU Football games. Alcohol service policies may vary for special events.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Fargodome, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Fargodome's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Generally, will call is located at the main box office, located in the lobby of the east, main entrance of FARGODOME Box office opens a minimum of 2 hours prior to scheduled event time.

Gates & Entrances
