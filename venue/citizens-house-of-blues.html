Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" are allowed in the venue and will be searched prior to entry. Non-clear bags will undergo additional searches.

Allowed

  • Bags up to 12" x 6" x 12"

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

It is encouraged that guests who require accessible accommodations arrive 30 minutes prior to doors opening.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking options and pricing details are not provided on the venue's website. Visitors are encouraged to check nearby parking facilities or use public transportation. Consider arriving early to secure parking.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Citizens House of Blues's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Citizens House of Blues, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Citizens House of Blues in Boston offers a variety of food and drink options, including shareable bites like jambalaya arancini and chicken wings, handhelds such as a double cheeseburger and po'boy, and desserts like bananas foster bread pudding. The drink menu features signature cocktails, draft and craft beers, and a selection of wines and sangria.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Citizens House of Blues's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The box office opens day of show ONLY starting one hour prior to doors.
