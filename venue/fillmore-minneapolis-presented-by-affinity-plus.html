Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” are allowed in the venue and will be searched prior to entry. Non-clear bags will undergo additional searches.

Allowed

  • Bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”

Not allowed

  • Outside Food or Drink
  • guns
  • knives
  • weapons
  • pepper spray
  • projectiles
  • weapon replicas
  • vapes
  • drugs
  • drug paraphernalia
  • personal video cameras
  • Go-Pros
  • selfie sticks
  • drones
  • laser pointers
  • professional photo, video, or audio recording equipment
  • unsealed liquids or gels

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Fillmore Minneapolis presented by Affinity Plus. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Fillmore Minneapolis does not have its own parking lot but is conveniently located near the Target Field station light rail stop. Metered parking is available on surrounding streets, and several parking ramps are a few blocks away. Parking can be booked through ParkWhiz for convenience.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Fillmore Minneapolis presented by Affinity Plus's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Fillmore Minneapolis offers a preshow dining experience at Trax Burgers & Bar, featuring craft burgers, appetizers, salads, cocktails, a wide beer selection, and desserts. The venue is fully cashless, accepting major credit cards for all purchases.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Fillmore Minneapolis presented by Affinity Plus's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026
