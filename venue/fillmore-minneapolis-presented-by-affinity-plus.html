Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” are allowed in the venue and will be searched prior to entry. Non-clear bags will undergo additional searches.

Parking

Parking

The Fillmore Minneapolis does not have its own parking lot but is conveniently located near the Target Field station light rail stop. Metered parking is available on surrounding streets, and several parking ramps are a few blocks away. Parking can be booked through ParkWhiz for convenience.
