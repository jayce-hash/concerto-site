Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

AT&T Stadium has implemented a bag policy to enhance public safety by restricting the size and style of bags allowed. Only clear totes, small clutch purses, and clear plastic storage bags are permitted.

Allowed

  • Clear Tote – Plastic, vinyl or PVC and do not exceed 12″x6″x12″
  • Plastic Storage Bag – Clear, one(1) gallon, re-sealable
  • Small Clutch Purse – Approximately the size of a hand, no larger than 4.5″ x 6.5″

Not allowed

  • All purses, bags or containers larger than a small clutch bag
  • Backpacks
  • Binocular case
  • Briefcases
  • Camera bags
  • Cinch bags
  • Computer bags
  • Coolers
  • Fanny packs
  • Luggage of any kind
  • Seat Cushions

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at AT&T Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

AT&T Stadium offers approximately 12,000 parking spaces in 15 numbered lots, with additional spaces available in lettered lots around the Texas Rangers Ballpark. Parking areas open five hours before Cowboys games, and accessible parking is available on a first come, first served basis.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

AT&T Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, with a focus on responsible alcohol consumption. There is a two beer limit per transaction at concession stands, and alcohol sales end at the third quarter. The venue is committed to providing an exceptional experience for all guests.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in AT&T Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at AT&T Stadium. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
