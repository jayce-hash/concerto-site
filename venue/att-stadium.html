Bag Policy

Bag Policy

AT&T Stadium has implemented a bag policy to enhance public safety by restricting the size and style of bags allowed. Only clear totes, small clutch purses, and clear plastic storage bags are permitted.

Parking

Parking

AT&T Stadium offers approximately 12,000 parking spaces in 15 numbered lots, with additional spaces available in lettered lots around the Texas Rangers Ballpark. Parking areas open five hours before Cowboys games, and accessible parking is available on a first come, first served basis.

Concessions

Concessions

AT&T Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, with a focus on responsible alcohol consumption. There is a two beer limit per transaction at concession stands, and alcohol sales end at the third quarter. The venue is committed to providing an exceptional experience for all guests.
