Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

One A4-size bag or standard handbag per person may enter; backpacks, laptop bags, travel cases and large totes are prohibited. Bags are checked at entry.

Allowed

  • One A4-size bag or standard handbag

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Laptop bags
  • Travel cases
  • Large totes
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Follow the entrance on the ticket and have it ready for scanning; current security guidance asks guests to keep bags and coats on their person during screening.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

On-site concert parking is reserved for approved Blue Badge applications; both the event parking pass and Blue Badge must be displayed.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The current stadium security and concert access pages do not designate a rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible platform and ambulant seating use step-free entrances; standing tickets require about 55 steps and do not have accessible entry. Pre-approved Blue Badge parking needs an event pass.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Stadium food and drink stands take cashless card and contactless payment; outside food and drink must be finished before entry.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drink
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue’s published concert guide says tickets scan only once, so leaving prevents re-entry; check the current show’s guide for changes.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Hampden does not sell concert tickets and directs ticket issues to the SEC or the original seller; no universal Will Call pickup process is published.

Official source
