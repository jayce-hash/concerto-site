Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All bags over 12″x12″x6″ are prohibited and subject to search upon entry. Guests can refuse a bag search, but the venue may deny entry.

Allowed

  • Bags up to 12″x12″x6″

Not allowed

  • Bags larger than 12”x6”x12”
  • Weapons of Any Kind
  • Outside Alcohol
  • Illegal Drugs/Substances
  • Glass Containers
  • Cans
  • Food or drinks (except items on allowed list)
  • Hard sided/large coolers
  • Large or oversized bags/back packs
  • Laser pointers
  • Animals (except service animals)
  • Fireworks
  • Selfie Sticks
  • Signs larger than 8.5×11 inches
  • Professional Cameras
  • Wallet Chains
  • Laptops/Computers/iPads
  • Ski Masks

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at The Met Presented by Highmark. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Limited parking spaces are available on a first-come, first-served basis in designated lots. Lot A opens 1 hour before doors, Lot C and Lot D open at 6:00 PM on weeknights and 4:30 PM on weekends. Valet parking is also available and opens one hour before doors.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Drop off / pick up is located on Broad Street, next to the venue, immediately south of Poplar Street.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by The Met Presented by Highmark, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The venue prohibits outside food or drinks, except for water in a factory sealed or empty bottle. Concession stands are available on all accessible seating levels, offering a variety of food and drink options.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in The Met Presented by Highmark's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at The Met Presented by Highmark. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
