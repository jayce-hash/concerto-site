Know before you go

What matters at Hollywood Bowl.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags do not need to be clear but must fit under your seat and measure no more than 15 inches wide, 15 inches high, or 22 inches long. All bags are subject to security screening.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Parking is available directly beneath Walt Disney Concert Hall with regular parking costing $9 and valet parking at $23. Accessible parking is available near elevators on each level.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Hollywood Bowl's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Lucques at the Circle offers a seasonal made-to-order pre-fixe menu and an exceptional wine list, curated by Suzanne Goin and Caroline Styne. This fine-dining experience is exclusive to Pool Circle ticket holders and features artisan cheese by Rodolphe Le Meunier and passion fruit pavlova with yuzu.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →

ADA &amp; Accessible Services
