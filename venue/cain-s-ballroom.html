Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Backpacks are prohibited; the venue FAQ does not specify a numeric limit for other bags.

  • Backpacks

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Check the specific ticket for its door time; there is no indoor waiting area before doors open.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

A parking lot is 0.1 mile south at Cameron and Main; street spaces in the Tulsa Arts District are free after 5 p.m. and on weekends.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ describes parking but does not specify a rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

There is no designated accessible seating area; contact the venue at least 72 hours in advance to discuss accommodation. Mezzanine access requires stairs.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Full bars serve beer and spirits, concessions have soft drinks and Mac’s BBQ serves food during shows. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

No re-entry is allowed, except for an emergency.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Pick up Will Call tickets while the box office is open; bring photo ID, confirmation number and the purchasing card. It opens Wednesdays and Fridays 10 a.m.–5 p.m. and roughly 30 minutes before doors on show nights.

Official source
