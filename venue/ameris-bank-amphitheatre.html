Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Nonclear bags up to 6 × 9 inches are allowed. Larger bags must be clear and no bigger than 12 × 12 × 6 inches; mesh does not count as clear, and medical or diaper bags are searched.

Allowed

  • Nonclear bag up to 6 × 9 inches
  • Clear bag up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches
  • Screened medical or diaper bag

Not allowed

  • Oversized or mesh bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guest Services booths operate at the East and West gates. Concert-specific gate opening times are published close to the event on its Know Before You Go page.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concert parking usually opens two to three hours before showtime; arrival and pass instructions depend on the ticket and lot. Parking spaces are first come first served within each lot.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

All rideshare and other passenger pickups and drop-offs use 11450 Maxwell Road, Alpharetta, GA 30009.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guest Services is at the East and West gates and can provide wheelchair rides to seats. Medical device bags are permitted after inspection.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and beverages are sold onsite; most outside food is prohibited. Each guest may bring one empty refillable bottle or a sealed water bottle up to one gallon, and guests with medical or dietary needs may bring food in a clear one-gallon bag.

Allowed

  • One empty refillable bottle or sealed bottle up to one gallon
  • Dietary food in a clear one-gallon bag

Not allowed

  • Other outside food
  • Lawn chairs
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

There is no re-entry after leaving; returning requires a new ticket.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office opens Fridays 10 a.m.–4 p.m., show days Monday–Saturday at 10 a.m., and Sunday show days at noon. The venue FAQ does not state a general will-call collection process.

Official source
