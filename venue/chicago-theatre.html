Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags must fit under a seat; the theatre prohibits bags larger than 22 × 14 × 9 inches.

Allowed

  • Bag fitting under a seat

Not allowed

  • Bags larger than 22 × 14 × 9 inches
