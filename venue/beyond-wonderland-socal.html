Bag policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches are allowed. Small non-clear or clear clutch bags up to 6 x 9 inches are also allowed, along with empty hydration packs within the festival compartment rules.

Allowed

  • clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches
  • small clutch bags up to 6 x 9 inches
  • empty hydration packs within policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Parking

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Use the festival's current Parking & Drop-Offs page for official parking directions and restrictions. Vehicles must leave the parking lot after each festival day.

Rideshare

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Rideshare, taxi, and guest drop-offs use the designated Guest Drop-Off and Pickup location. The current guide directs rideshare drivers toward the Big Lots parking lot off Orange Show Road and Arrowhead Boulevard.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 4, 2026

The festival provides accessible routes connecting parking, entrances, stages, viewing areas, vendors, and other activity areas. Limited accessible parking is available first come, first served.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Food, drinks, and other festival amenities are available on site. Exact vendors and locations vary by edition; use the current festival map and Amenities & Services guide.

Re-entry

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Re-entry is not allowed; the festival does not permit ins-and-outs.
