Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The updated 2026 policy allows bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches and clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches; guests and bags may be searched. Backpacks, fanny packs and larger bags are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Fanny packs
  • Oversized bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests and bags are subject to search or wanding at entry. General admission permits standing-room access to the showroom on concert nights; reserved tickets include an assigned seat.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The nearly 20-acre lot is across Stockyards Boulevard east of Cooper’s BBQ. Published rates are $7 Monday–Wednesday, $10 Thursday and $15 Friday–Sunday, plus fees; Friday and Saturday valet is $20 plus fees.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ does not publish a specific rideshare pickup or drop-off area.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The main lot has 22 accessible parking spaces and charges the usual parking fee. Contact the box office about a particular show’s accessible arrangements.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The onsite restaurant sells barbecue, burgers, catfish, appetizers, desserts and drinks. Entry to the club is required even if you only plan to eat.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The current venue FAQ does not publish a general rule about re-entering after leaving a concert.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concert tickets may be held at will call for pickup or accessed with the AXS mobile app. The FAQ does not publish a fixed pickup time for will-call orders.

Official source
