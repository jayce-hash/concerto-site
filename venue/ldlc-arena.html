Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Large backpacks and luggage are not accepted. Small bags and pouches are allowed; self-service cloakrooms are available inside, and exterior storage is available for prohibited items.

Parking

LDLC Arena has car, motorcycle, and bicycle parking. Paid car/motorcycle parking must be reserved online through the event page; bicycle parking is available near the arena.

Concessions

Concessions

The LDLC Arena offers a variety of food options with 75% of the menu made in-house using fresh ingredients. Visitors can enjoy burgers, tenders, hot-dogs, fries, sandwiches, and more. Additionally, there are special carts for crêpes and Haribo treats in the main hall.
