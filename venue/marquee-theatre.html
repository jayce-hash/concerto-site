Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

A purse, clutch or bag up to 5 × 7 inches may be opaque; larger bags must be clear and are searched. The venue does not give a maximum dimension for clear bags.

Allowed

  • Purse or clutch up to 5 × 7 inches
  • Clear bag larger than 5 × 7 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

A physical government photo ID is required for age-restricted shows; digital and paper IDs are rejected. All bags are searched at entry; the concert page gives that show’s door time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Limited on-site parking costs $15 per vehicle. Larger shows may use the paid garage north of the theater; its price is not set on the venue FAQ. Accessible spaces require a placard or plate.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ mentions light rail and occasional pedal taxis but gives no designated rideshare stop.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The building is ADA compliant and has limited parking for guests with a valid disability placard or plate. Email the venue for specific accommodations.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Full bars operate in the venue, and pizza is served at most events along with a snack machine. Outside food and drinks are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Re-entry is generally prohibited; the venue says staff can make exceptions for special circumstances.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Show the mobile ticket barcode or a printed ticket to enter. If tickets were lost, the box office can resend them on presentation of the purchasing credit card and ID.

Official source
