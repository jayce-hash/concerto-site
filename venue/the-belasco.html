Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Bags must be smaller than 12 × 12 inches. Backpacks and large purses are prohibited; the venue cannot check oversized items.

Allowed

  • Bag smaller than 12 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Large purses
  • Bags 12 × 12 inches or larger
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Door times are listed by event; the box office is open only on show days and will call begins at doors or meet-and-greet check-in.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue is at 1050 S. Hill Street and links to reservable nearby parking through ParkWhiz; it does not list a fixed event parking price.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue visit page does not publish a designated rideshare pickup or drop-off area.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The main floor has an ADA area reached by ramp, with accessible restrooms; there is no elevator. Request assistive listening at least two days ahead or an interpreter at least two weeks ahead.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Belasco offers food and beverage packages and has accessible concession stands; outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue does not permit guests to leave and come back in.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Ticketmaster will call opens at door time or the meet-and-greet check-in time. Bring photo ID and the payment card used for the order.

Official source
