Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

UVM recommends arriving without a bag. Approved clear bags are permitted for general guests and are subject to search; UVM students are not permitted to bring bags into Patrick Gym.

Allowed

  • One approved clear bag for general guests
  • Medical essentials in an approved clear bag

Not allowed

  • Bags carried by UVM students
  • Diaper bags
  • Unapproved bags
