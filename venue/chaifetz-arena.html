Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Chaifetz Arena allows one clear bag up to 12" x 6" x 12" and one small clutch up to 7.5" x 5.0" per guest. Bags are subject to search for a safer and quicker entry.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12" x 6" x 12"
  • One-gallon clear plastic Ziploc-style bag
  • Clutch bags up to 7.5" x 5.0"
  • Diaper bags with child after inspection

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Chaifetz Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A variety of covered and uncovered parking areas are available, including the Olive/Compton Garage, Laclede Garage, and Fred Weber VIP Lot. All parking is cashless, accepting only major credit/debit cards. Plan ahead and arrive early to avoid traffic.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Rideshare Drop-Off/Pick-Up Location is at 3402 Laclede Ave, St. Louis, MO 63013.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Chaifetz Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Chaifetz Arena offers a diverse range of food and beverage options through Delaware North Catering. Highlights include Mexican cuisine at Blue Agave, Italian favorites at Midtown Italiano, and award-winning BBQ from Blue Flame BBQ. Alcoholic beverages are available for guests over 21, and all sales are cashless.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Chaifetz Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Chaifetz Arena. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
