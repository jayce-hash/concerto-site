Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags larger than 10" x 6" x 2" are not permitted inside Barclays Center. Guests without bags can use Express Lanes for faster entry.

Allowed

  • Bags within 10" x 6" x 2"
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Atlantic Ave entrance is the main entry. All entrances are ADA accessible. Access-A-Ride bus stop on Atlantic Ave.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking options and pricing are not specifically detailed on the page. Visitors are encouraged to use public transportation due to the venue's proximity to major transit hubs.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Barclays Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All entrances ADA accessible. Elevators on Main Concourse, Suite Levels A & B, and Upper Concourse. Assistive listening devices at Guest Services (Sections 112 & 212). Sensory room near Section 17.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Barclays Center offers a diverse culinary experience through its Brooklyn Taste program, featuring local neighborhood staples. The venue provides a variety of wine and beer options, including exclusive selections in certain clubs. Craft beer is available at Brooklyn Craft, located outside Section 26.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Re-entry is not permitted.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guest Services outside Section 112 (Main Concourse) or Section 212 (Upper Level).
