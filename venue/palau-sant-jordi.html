Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Palau Sant Jordi does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Palau Sant Jordi. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking availability, access routes, and pricing at Palau Sant Jordi can vary by event. Review the linked official information and reserve in advance when that option is offered.

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Palau Sant Jordi's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Palau Sant Jordi, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The bars at Palau Sant Jordi offer a wide variety of food and drinks including hot and cold sandwiches, snacks, and options for vegetarians and gluten-free products. The venue encourages recycling with designated bins for different types of waste.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Palau Sant Jordi's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The box office only opens on the day of the event, usually 1 hour before the doors open.
