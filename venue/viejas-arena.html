What matters at Viejas Arena.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Viejas Arena is a clear bag venue to expedite guest entry and enhance security. Bags must be clear plastic or vinyl and not exceed 12" x 6" x 12".

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Event parking is available at Parking Structures 3 and 6/6B for $40 per vehicle, payable via credit/debit cards or mobile payment options. Parking areas open 90 minutes before event doors open, and overnight parking and tailgating are prohibited.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Viejas Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

Viejas Arena offers a variety of food and drink options including bratwurst, hot dogs, nachos, and specialty mac & cheese. Beverage options feature a wide selection of beers, wines, and cocktails, with several third-party vendors providing diverse offerings like pizza, cheesesteaks, and ice cream.

Accessibility

Accessibility

For any inquiries about accessibility, disabled seating or any other ADA assistance, please contact June Barreras at (619) 594-5085. ADA parking is available in Parking Structure 7, Parking Lot 8, and Parking Structure 12.

Re-Entry Policy

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The box office is located on the 55th Street, in front of Viejas Arena. The Viejas Arena box office is located next to our main entrance at Gate 2, and can be accessed off of 55th Street.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

The VIP/Customer Service Gate and Gate 2, which are located at our main entrance off 55th Street, remain open for the entire duration of the event. Bicycle racks are located outside our main entrance at Gate 2.
