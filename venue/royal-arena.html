Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 40 × 40 × 20 cm are generally allowed, although individual shows may have tighter rules. Event lockers cost DKK 50 and measure 45 × 30 × 30 cm.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 40 × 40 × 20 cm

Not allowed

  • Larger bags

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena garage has four accessible parking spaces on the ground level and two on each upper level.

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Arena bars and stands sell pizza, hot dogs, snacks and drinks. Outside food or drinks cannot enter.

Not allowed

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

If a show is not sold out, the show-day box office to the right of main entrance A may sell tickets; tickets can be shown on a phone.
