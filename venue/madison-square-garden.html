Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Madison Square Garden has implemented a strict bag policy to ensure the safety and security of all guests. Only small bags are permitted, and all bags are subject to search upon entry.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Main entrance on 7th Avenue. Multiple accessible entrances on all sides. Gates open 1–2 hrs before showtime.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Madison Square Garden offers several parking options nearby, with varying prices depending on the event and location. It's advisable to book parking in advance to secure a spot and potentially save on costs. Street parking is limited, so using a nearby garage is recommended.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Madison Square Garden's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Wheelchair accessible entrances on all sides. Assistive listening devices available. Wheelchair escorts on request. Disabled Services: 212-465-6034.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Madison Square Garden offers a variety of food and drink options, including classic stadium fare and unique culinary experiences. Highlights include gourmet hot dogs, artisanal pizzas, and a selection of craft beers. Special offerings may include local New York favorites and seasonal menu items.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

No re-entry. Exiting MSG voids your ticket.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Box Office at 7th Ave & 31st St. Opens 2 hrs before event. Photo ID required.

Official source
