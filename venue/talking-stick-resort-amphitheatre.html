Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Only small clutches, wristlets, or fanny packs no bigger than 6”x 9” and clear plastic bags no bigger than 12"x12"x6” are allowed. All bags are subject to search upon entry.

Allowed

  • small clutches
  • wristlets
  • fanny packs no bigger than 6”x 9”
  • clear plastic bags no bigger than 12"x12"x6”

Not allowed

  • backpacks
  • luggage
  • camera bags
  • metal hydroflasks
  • weapons
  • lawn chairs
  • outside food or drink
  • alcohol
  • illegal drugs/substances
  • glass containers
  • cans
  • coolers
  • laser pointers
  • selfie sticks
  • animals (except service animals)
  • fireworks
  • chain wallets
  • aerosol sunscreen
  • audio or video equipment
  • strollers
  • blankets at certain shows
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Talking Stick Resort Amphitheatre. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking lots open 30 minutes before gate time. Premier Parking and Easy Out Parking are available for easier entry and exit. Arrive early to secure your spot.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For rideshares, head to Palm Lane on the southwest side of the venue.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accessible Parking is located inside Gate 3 & Gate 6 off of Encanto Blvd.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Talking Stick Resort Amphitheatre offers a variety of food and drink options, including Mosh Burger's hormone-free beef burgers, Rebel Hen's crispy chicken tenders, and Ziggy's Magic Pizza Party's NY-inspired pizza. Guests can also enjoy Koko's Bavarian sausages and pretzels, as well as CVT Premium Soft Serve Ice Cream.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Re-entry is not permitted at Talking Stick Resort Amphitheatre.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The West Box Office opens at noon, the East Box Office opens about an hour prior to doors.
