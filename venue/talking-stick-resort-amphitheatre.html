Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Only small clutches, wristlets, or fanny packs no bigger than 6”x 9” and clear plastic bags no bigger than 12"x12"x6” are allowed. All bags are subject to search upon entry.

Concessions

Concessions

Talking Stick Resort Amphitheatre offers a variety of food and drink options, including Mosh Burger's hormone-free beef burgers, Rebel Hen's crispy chicken tenders, and Ziggy's Magic Pizza Party's NY-inspired pizza. Guests can also enjoy Koko's Bavarian sausages and pretzels, as well as CVT Premium Soft Serve Ice Cream.
