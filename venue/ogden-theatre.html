Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Large backpacks, suitcases, beach bags and equipment bags are prohibited. The venue does not publish a smaller bag size limit.

Not allowed

  • Large backpacks
  • Suitcases
  • Beach bags
  • Equipment bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

All guests pass through walk-through metal detectors. Quick Pass has a separate general-admission entry line at the regular door time and also requires an event ticket.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The preferred lot behind the theatre is sold first come first served as a ticket add-on; remaining spaces may be sold by the lot attendant. The lot is staffed from one hour before doors until 30 minutes after the show; street parking is also available.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ and directions do not designate a rideshare pickup or drop-off point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

There is no need to purchase a separate accessible-seat ticket; tell security on arrival if a chair in the ADA area is needed. The venue provides alternate security screening when needed and admits service animals.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concessions are cashless, with assistance available through Guest Services. Outside food and beverages are not allowed.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ and policies do not publish a re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Most tickets are delivered through the AXS mobile app using the purchasing email address. The show-day box office opens one hour before doors, and in-person tickets carry a $2.50 fee.

Official source
