Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bring at most one small essentials bag no larger than A4; a clear bag is preferred. Medical and changing bags for babies or small children are exceptions, and there is no left-luggage storage.

Allowed

  • One essentials bag up to A4
  • Medical bag
  • Changing bag for baby or young child

Not allowed

  • Larger bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Use the Cityside, Riverside or Floor entry shown on your ticket. The opening time is printed on your ticket and in the pre-event email.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The on-site Kings Dock Car Park has 1,450 spaces, accessible bays and electric chargers on every level.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena’s visitor and travel pages do not designate an app-based rideshare pickup location.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Kings Dock Car Park has 79 accessible spaces; Blue Badge parking is charged and the riverside has a passenger drop-off point.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food, drinks and merchandise are sold at the venue; concession payments are contactless only. An event email describes the available food and drink.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena’s at-the-event FAQ and security information do not state a universal concert exit-and-re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Ticket Quarter box-office collection starts at noon on show day; tickets from other official agents are available from when doors open, if collection was chosen at checkout.

Official source
