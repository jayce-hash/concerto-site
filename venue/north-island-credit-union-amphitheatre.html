    Bag policy

    Checked Oct 11, 2026

    Clear bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" and small clutches up to 6" x 9" are allowed. Any bag larger than a clutch must be clear, and all bags are searched.

    Allowed

    • Clear bags up to 12" x 6" x 12"
    • Small clutches up to 6" x 9" (do not need to be clear)

    Not allowed

    • Bags larger than 12" x 12" x 6"
    • Non-clear bags larger than a clutch
    • Luggage and duffel bags
    • Large or camping-size backpacks
    • Weapons and sharp objects
    • Outside food
    • Glass containers
    • Coolers
    • Umbrellas
    • Tripods
