Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches are allowed after search; nonclear bags receive extra inspection. Backpacks, Camelbaks and bota bags are not allowed.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Camelbaks
  • Bota bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Security searches bags before entry. The box office opens at 2 p.m. on concert nights, and event listings give the door time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Self-parking is complimentary in Disney Springs’ Watermelon surface lot and Lime and Orange garages. Arrive at least 60 minutes before the scheduled show because capacity and entry can be restricted.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ gives its Disney Springs location but does not designate a House of Blues-specific rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Contact the box office to request an ADA accommodation; the venue publishes an additional accessibility guide.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The restaurant, bar, Smokehouse and Music Hall offer food and drinks and accept cards or mobile pay; free cash-to-card conversion is available. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests cannot re-enter a concert after leaving.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office opens at 2 p.m. on concert nights and noon–7 p.m. on Wednesdays. Check the purchase confirmation for any show-specific will-call arrangement.

Official source
