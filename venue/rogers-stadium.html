Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For safety and security, only small clutches, belt bags, fanny-packs, or clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” are allowed. Bags larger than 6” x 9” must be clear and single compartment.

Allowed

  • small clutches
  • belt bags
  • fanny-packs
  • clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • bags smaller than 6” x 9”

Not allowed

  • backpacks

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Rogers Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking onsite and in the surrounding area is extremely limited. Fans are encouraged to use satellite parking lots at nearby TTC subway stations or York University for convenient access to the venue.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The fan plaza and accessible seating sections at Rogers Stadium are all at ground level and are fully accessible via wheelchairs and/or mobility devices. All entrance gates, accessible seating sections, bathrooms, and the fan plaza, are ground level and accessible via wheelchair and/or mobility device.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Rogers Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, including alcohol at many concession stands and bars. Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options are available, with guidance provided by signage or team members.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Rogers Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Rogers Stadium. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
