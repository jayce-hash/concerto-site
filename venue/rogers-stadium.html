Bag Policy

Bag Policy

For safety and security, only small clutches, belt bags, fanny-packs, or clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” are allowed. Bags larger than 6” x 9” must be clear and single compartment.

Accessibility

The fan plaza and accessible seating sections at Rogers Stadium are all at ground level and are fully accessible via wheelchairs and/or mobility devices. All entrance gates, accessible seating sections, bathrooms, and the fan plaza, are ground level and accessible via wheelchair and/or mobility device.
