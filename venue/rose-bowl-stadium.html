Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The stadium event guidance permits clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches, one-gallon clear freezer bags and clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches; diaper bags may be permitted with a child. Medical items outside these dimensions are screened at the Public Safety Building between Gates A and B.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear freezer bag
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches
  • Diaper bag with a child

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Fanny packs
  • Camera bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The stadium event page identifies item-check tents outside Gates A and D and the medical-item check between Gates A and B. It does not publish a universal concert door-opening time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Rose Bowl event guide describes stadium parking areas and an express shuttle from the Parsons Engineering Complex in Old Pasadena to Lot B/Gate B. Parking prices and opening times depend on the event.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The stadium event page directs rideshare and taxi pickups and drop-offs to the Old Town area of Pasadena; confirm this with the concert’s event page.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Medically necessary items that do not fit the bag policy are screened at the Public Safety Building between Gates A and B. The cited event page does not state broader concert accessibility arrangements.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The official stadium event page lists prohibited outside alcoholic beverages, cans, glass bottles and coolers, but does not give a general concert concessions menu.

Not allowed

  • Outside alcoholic beverages
  • Cans
  • Glass bottles
  • Coolers
