Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The stadium guide permits clear bags up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches; medical bags receive additional inspection at Gates 6 and 10. The university’s published clear-bag rule covers sporting events, so confirm requirements for a concert.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches
  • Medical bag at Gates 6 or 10

Not allowed

  • Backpacks

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The stadium A-Z guide says there is no designated rideshare pickup area.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The stadium guide lists wheelchair seating in the upper West Endzone plaza and Sections 114, 126, 136 and the 600 Level. Medical bag screening is at Gates 6 and 10.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Alcohol is sold at various stadium concession stands for university events. Concert menus and hours may differ.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The stadium A-Z guide states that re-entry is not allowed.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The football A-Z guide lists will call at the East End ticket window on East Campus Road; concert pickup instructions should be checked for the specific show.
