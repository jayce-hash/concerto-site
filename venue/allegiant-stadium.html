Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Allegiant Stadium enforces a clear bag policy to enhance public safety and expedite entry. Bags must be clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC and not exceed 12” x 6” x 12”.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags not exceeding 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Small clutch bags, purses, or wallets 4.5" x 6.5" x 2" or smaller

Not allowed

  • Purses larger than 4.5” x 6.5” x 2”
  • Coolers
  • Briefcases
  • Diaper bags
  • Backpacks
  • Fanny packs
  • Cinch bags
  • Seat cushions
  • Luggage of any kind
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Multiple gates, parking gates open 4 hrs before Raiders/UNLV games. Check event details for other events.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking at Allegiant Stadium can be reserved in advance through SpotHero. Parking is operated by MGM Resorts International and must be purchased ahead of time. The stadium is cashless, so payments must be made via debit/credit card or mobile payment.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Rideshare pick-up and drop-off is located in Lot N, just north of the stadium.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All gates ADA accessible. Accessible seating throughout. ADA parking in all zones with valid placard + pass. ADA drop-off in Lot B near Gate 2. Contact: guestexperience@raiders.com or 725-780-2100.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Allegiant Stadium offers a diverse range of food and beverage options, including BBQ, Mexican, and Hawaiian cuisine, as well as a variety of drinks like specialty cocktails and draft beers. Notable highlights include Bloody Marys, Micheladas, and checkout-free stores for quick service.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

No re-entry permitted.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Ticketmaster Box Office opens 4 hrs before event. Government-issued photo ID required. Will call drop-off not permitted.

Official source
