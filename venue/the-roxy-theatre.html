Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Small bags are searched; bags larger than 14 × 14 inches, backpacks, briefcases, luggage and duffle bags are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Small bag up to 14 × 14 inches

Not allowed

  • Bags larger than 14 × 14 inches
  • Backpacks
  • Briefcases
  • Luggage
  • Duffle bags
