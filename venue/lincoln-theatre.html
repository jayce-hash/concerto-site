Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Backpacks, purses and small bags are permitted after inspection; luggage is not. Touring shows may set additional bag restrictions.

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue homepage lists door time and the event page gives showtime. Bags are inspected on entry; weekend-show box office service starts two hours before doors.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Parking near the theatre is limited. The venue recommends transit or booking a nearby parking space ahead of time.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Call the box office to arrange accessible seating or an ASL interpreter for an event.

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theatre sells snacks, light fare, alcoholic and nonalcoholic drinks; a $0.75 sustainability fee is added per transaction. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Re-entry

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office opens Monday–Friday 11:30 a.m.–6 p.m. and on weekend show days beginning two hours before doors. Ticket delivery or pickup for an individual show follows its confirmation.
