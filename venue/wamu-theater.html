Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

WAMU Theater enforces a clear bag policy to enhance public safety. Only clear bags, small clutches, and one-gallon clear plastic bags are allowed.

Allowed

  • Clear bags that do not exceed 12″ x 6″ x 12″
  • One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags
  • Clear backpacks within dimension guidelines with no more than 2 pockets
  • Small bags not exceeding 4.5″ x 6.5″

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags that cannot fit into clear bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at WAMU Theater. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Paid parking is available at the Lumen Field Event Center Garage, which opens at 6 a.m. on event days and closes 2 hours after the event. Pre-purchase parking options are available for most events. Electric vehicle charging is available, but parking is not guaranteed without pre-purchase.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in WAMU Theater's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by WAMU Theater, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

WAMU Theater offers a variety of food and beverage options including fan favorites like chicken strips with fries, Bavarian pretzels with cheese, and handcrafted cocktails. The venue also features a Starbucks menu with espresso drinks and pastries. Outside food is allowed under specific conditions, but outside beverages are generally not permitted.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in WAMU Theater's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Typically the WAMU Theater Box Office opens 2 hours prior to an event. Starting July 16th the Lumen Field NW Stadium box office (located on the corner of the Olympic neighborhood of the stadium along Occidental Ave) will be open Thursday, Friday, and Saturday from 10:00 a.m. - 2:30 p.m.
