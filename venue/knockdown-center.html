    Bag policy

    Checked Oct 11, 2026

    The venue does not set a bag size limit. All bags, purses, coats and hats are subject to search, and bags can be checked for $5 cash.

    Allowed

    • Bags and purses (subject to search)
    • One factory sealed water bottle

    Not allowed

    • CamelBaks
    • Parcels
    • Packages whose contents are not displayed
    • Umbrellas
    • Outside food and beverages
    • Video or audio recorders
    • Professional camera equipment
    • Strollers and baby seats
    • Folding chairs
    • Signs, balloons, beach balls
    • Lasers of any kind
    • Weapons of any kind
    Official source

    Entrances and doors

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    Door times are listed on individual event pages; guests must show valid government-issued photo ID and may be searched on arrival.

    Official source

    Parking

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    The venue-specific FAQ says accessible vehicle parking can be arranged in advance by emailing the venue; it does not list a general concert parking lot or rate.

    Official source

    Rideshare

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    The venue-specific event FAQ does not designate a rideshare pickup or drop-off point.

    Official source

    Accessibility

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    Email mail@knockdowncenter.com with the event and accommodation needs before the show, arrive early, and alert box-office staff or security. Accessible vehicle parking may be arranged in advance.

    Official source

    Food and drink

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    Outside food is prohibited except for certain medical needs. Guests may bring one factory-sealed water bottle; other outside beverages are prohibited.

    Allowed

    • One factory-sealed water bottle

    Not allowed

    • Outside food except for medical needs
    • Other outside beverages
    Official source

    Re-entry

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    The venue-specific FAQ says no re-entry for ticketed events unless the individual event calendar says otherwise.

    Official source

    Tickets and will call

    Checked Sep 23, 2026

    The DICE ticketing FAQ says a mobile QR code can be activated starting two hours before the event; it does not give a physical will-call pickup procedure.

    Official source
