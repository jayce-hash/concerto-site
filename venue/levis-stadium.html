What matters at Levi’s Stadium.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Levi’s Stadium enforces a strict bag policy prohibiting most bags, with exceptions for clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutch bags. All bags are subject to inspection.

Parking

Parking

Levi's Stadium offers a variety of parking options including Premium Red, Pink, Blue, Green, and Yellow lots. Tailgating is permitted in some lots with restrictions on open flames and glass containers. It's recommended to follow game day traffic patterns for the best routes.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Levi’s Stadium's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

Levi's Stadium offers a wide variety of food and beverage options, including hot dogs, nachos, sandwiches, and specialty cocktails. Highlights include the Faithful Frank Hot Dog, Micheladas, and a selection of premium canned and draft beers. Concession options may vary by event.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Once accessible parking is full, guests with a valid and current DMV-issued Disabled Person Placard or License Plate and a parking pass to a Levi’s® Stadium parking lot will be directed to a mobility-friendly parking lot that provides a courtesy shuttle. (i.e., Blue Lot 1, Pink Lots 4/5/8).

Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Re-entry is not permitted at any Levi’s® Stadium event.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Gameday Will Call opens three (3) hours before kickoff. Will call requests received before gameday will be forwarded in advance of the game to the intended recipient via the San Francisco 49ers App or by email.

Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Levi’s Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.
