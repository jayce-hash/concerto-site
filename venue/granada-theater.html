Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Backpacks and oversized bags are prohibited; small purses and bags may be brought in and are searched. The theatre does not publish a size limit for permitted small bags.

Allowed

  • Small purses and bags subject to search

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Oversized bags
