Bag Policy

Bag Policy

The Arizona Diamondbacks have implemented a clear bag policy to enhance fan safety and expedite entry. Only clear bags of specific sizes are allowed, with all bags subject to search.

Parking

Parking

Dodger Stadium parking is in the lots around Chavez Ravine, with general admission around $27-35 in advance (cashless, more at the gate) and preferred lots higher. Off-site Lots 13 and 14 on Stadium Way are about $5 with an uphill walk. The free Dodger Stadium Express bus from Union Station is included with a game ticket; entry gate determines which lots you can reach.

Rideshare

Rideshare

Uber, the official rideshare partner of the Dodgers, has moved its pickup and drop-off location from Lot 11 to Lot 1. Ubers will now enter through Gate B in an effort to decrease Gate A congestion and increase the speed of arrival and drop off for fans.

Concessions

Concessions

Dodger Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, including classic comfort foods with a modern twist at Bridges Grille, elevated ballpark classics at Cadillac Diner, and crispy, golden goodness at Coop & Kennel Pub. Special offerings include a Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry. The Beer Garden provides a selection of local and popular beers.

ADA &amp; Accessible Services
