    Bag policy

    Checked Oct 11, 2026

    Clear bags up to 12" x 12" x 6" and small clutches up to 4.5" x 6.5" are allowed. Any bag larger than a clutch must be clear.

    Allowed

    • Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags up to 12" H x 12" W x 6" D
    • Small clutches up to 4.5" x 6.5" (do not need to be clear)
    • Prior approved food in a clear 1-gallon zip lock bag

    Not allowed

    • Non-clear bags larger than 4.5" x 6.5"
    • Luggage, duffel bags, large camping backpacks
    • CamelBaks and water backpacks
    • Coolers and ice chests
    • Aerosols, including sunscreen and hairspray
    • Professional cameras with detachable lenses
    • Chairs of any kind
    • Umbrellas
    • Strollers and wagons
    • Weapons, including pepper spray

    Entrances and doors

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at FivePoint Amphitheatre. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

    Official source

    Parking

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Parking at FivePoint Amphitheatre offers General Admission and Preferred options. Advance online purchase costs $10 for GA and $20 for Preferred on Thursdays, while onsite and online purchases on the day of the event are $15 for GA and $25 for Preferred. Additional fees may apply.

    Official source
