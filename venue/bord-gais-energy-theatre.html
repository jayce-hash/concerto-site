Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bring only one small bag: large bags are not permitted in the auditorium, the cloakroom is closed, and there is no bag storage. Belongings may be searched at entry.

Allowed

  • One small bag

Not allowed

  • Large bags
