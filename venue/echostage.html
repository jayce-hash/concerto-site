Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Small bags are permitted after search; backpacks, large totes, large purses and duffel bags are prohibited. The venue does not give a numeric bag-size limit.

Allowed

  • Small searched bag

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Large totes or purses
  • Duffel bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests must show current physical government photo ID and submit to a thorough search and pat-down. Show-specific door times are listed with the concert.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

There are limited paid lots around the venue; the operator encourages carpooling or rideshare.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue recommends rideshare for arrival but does not publish a designated loading zone.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Email the venue about accessibility or medical needs up to 24 hours before arriving so staff can discuss accommodations.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue is cashless for purchases, with cash accepted for tips and seasonal coat check. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Re-entry is not permitted unless an event specifically notes an exception.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office sells tickets on show nights until capacity is reached. Confirm the individual ticket delivery method for any pickup.
