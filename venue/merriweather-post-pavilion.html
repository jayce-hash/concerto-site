Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Backpacks and backpack-style purses are prohibited unless they are clear. Permitted bags and clear backpacks may not exceed 13 by 15 inches, and all bags are searched.

Allowed

  • Permitted bags no larger than 13 by 15 inches
  • Clear backpacks no larger than 13 by 15 inches

Not allowed

  • Non-clear backpacks
  • Backpack-style purses
  • Bags larger than 13 by 15 inches
  • Coolers and baskets

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

ADA accessible parking is available in Lots 2 and 4. Wheelchair-accessible and companion seats are located throughout Section 100, in Row Z of Sections 200-204, and in Row JJ of Sections 300-302.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Merriweather Post Pavilion offers a variety of food options including sandwiches, wraps, pizzas, and salads. Highlights include the Merriweather Deluxe Cheeseburger, Steak Frites, and a selection of vegetarian options like the Impossible Cheeseburger and Falafel Pita. Guests can also enjoy snacks like seasoned curly fries and nachos.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Merriweather Post Pavilion's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.
