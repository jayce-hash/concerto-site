Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Only handbags up to DIN A4 size (21 × 29.7 cm) may enter; limited bag storage outside the arena costs €6.

Allowed

  • Handbag up to A4

Not allowed

  • Larger bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Entry requires a valid ticket and an event-specific arrival time; allow time for security screening.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Plaza underground garage and the arena parking garage have paid parking; arrive early or use public transport.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The official venue guidance does not specify a general rideshare pickup location for concerts; consult the event instructions.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible parking is available for a fee in both the Plaza underground and arena parking garages.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drinks are sold inside; guests cannot bring their own food or drinks.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

A ticket loses its validity when the guest leaves the arena, so re-entry is not permitted.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The official venue guidance does not specify a general will-call pickup procedure for concerts; consult the event instructions.
