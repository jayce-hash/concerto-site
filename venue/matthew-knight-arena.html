Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Fans may bring one clear bag up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches or one one-gallon plastic storage bag, plus a small clutch or purse for privacy.

Allowed

  • One clear bag up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches
  • One-gallon plastic storage bag
  • Small clutch or purse

Not allowed

  • Bags that do not meet the clear-bag policy
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Doors open one hour before tipoff. Student-athlete guest admission begins 60 minutes before tipoff at the West Gate.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Parking at the arena and surrounding lots serves basketball, volleyball, acrobatics and tumbling, concerts, and events. Guests should use the event parking map because inventory and pricing vary.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published specific rideshare guidance on its official venue or guest-information pages.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Accessible seating is available through the arena ticket office. Limited accessible parking is in the arena garage, with additional spaces on Columbia Street west of the arena.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Athletics concessions are cashless and accept Visa, Mastercard, and Discover. Outside cans, bottles, coolers, and food or beverage containers are prohibited inside Matthew Knight Arena.

Not allowed

  • Outside cans or bottles
  • Coolers
  • Outside food or beverage containers
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Re-entry is not permitted at Matthew Knight Arena.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Men’s basketball will call is at the West Box Office, opening 90 minutes before tipoff and closing at halftime. Photo ID is required.

Official source
