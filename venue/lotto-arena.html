What matters at Lotto Arena.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags may not be larger than 21 cm x 30 cm x 10 cm (A4 format). Large handbags, backpacks, and luggage are not allowed.

Parking

Parking

Visitors to Lotto Arena can park for free at P+R Linkeroever, P+R Luchtbal, and P+R Merksem, which all have direct tram connections to the venue. Your event ticket also serves as a valid ticket for De Lijn public transport.

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Lotto Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

Lotto Arena offers a variety of food and drink options with bars and vending machines available on multiple levels. Drinks are served in reusable cups to reduce waste. Nearby restaurants include Pizzalijn Merksem and Pampas Rodizio.

Accessibility
