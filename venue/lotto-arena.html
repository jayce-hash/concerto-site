Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags may not be larger than 21 cm x 30 cm x 10 cm (A4 format). Large handbags, backpacks, and luggage are not allowed.

Allowed

  • Bags up to 21 cm x 30 cm x 10 cm

Not allowed

  • Luggage
  • Large handbags
  • Backpacks
  • Bottles
  • Cans
  • Drinks
  • Food
  • Alcohol
  • Cutlery
  • Glass bottles
  • Aerosol sprays
  • Flagpoles
  • Selfie sticks
  • Fireworks
  • Sharp objects
  • Audio recording equipment
  • Film cameras
  • Photo cameras

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The main entrance to the Lotto Arena is accessible via the street.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Visitors to Lotto Arena can park for free at P+R Linkeroever, P+R Luchtbal, and P+R Merksem, which all have direct tram connections to the venue. Your event ticket also serves as a valid ticket for De Lijn public transport.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Lotto Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Lotto Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lotto Arena offers a variety of food and drink options with bars and vending machines available on multiple levels. Drinks are served in reusable cups to reduce waste. Nearby restaurants include Pizzalijn Merksem and Pampas Rodizio.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Lotto Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Lotto Arena. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
