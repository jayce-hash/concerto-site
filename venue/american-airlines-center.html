Know before you go

What matters at American Airlines Center.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Wristlets, clutches, or wallets are allowed at all entrances, while purses up to 14''x14''x6'' are permitted at designated X-ray entry points. Backpacks and large totes are not allowed.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Parking options include routes for Platinum pass holders via Oaklawn Exit to Harry Hines Blvd or Tollway to Payne St. General parking access is available via Continental Exit to Victory Ave or Woodall Rodgers Frwy to Field St.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in American Airlines Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

The American Airlines Center offers a variety of food and drink options including Slurpees, artisan pretzels, and a range of tacos and nachos. Highlights include signature smashburgers, made-from-scratch pizzas, and a selection of non-alcoholic beverages, beer, and wine. Special offerings include frozen margaritas and cold brew coffee.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

ADA &amp; Accessible Services

Fully ADA compliant. Wheelchair seating on all levels. Accessible parking. Wheelchair escorts free from any entry. Contact: 214-665-4299 or guestservices@americanairlinescenter.com.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

No re-entry once you exit the arena.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Will Call

Mobile tickets via Ticketmaster are standard. Will call also available. Doors open 90 mins before events.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Gates &amp; Entrances

North Entrance is the main entry. Accessible entrance with ramps available. Located at 2500 Victory Ave, Dallas.
