Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Comerica Park allows single-compartment bags, wallets, and clutches smaller than 4" x 6" x 1.5". Larger bags are prohibited, except for medical and diaper bags up to 16" x 16" x 8".

Parking

Parking

With 32 parking locations available within The District Detroit, parking is less than a 10-minute walk from Comerica Park. Event parking opens 3 hours before and closes 2 hours after events. Parking is credit card only, with no in/out privileges.

Concessions

Concessions

Comerica Park offers a diverse range of food and beverage options, including local favorites and unique twists on traditional ballpark fare. Highlights include Smashburger, health-conscious wraps, and BBQ sandwiches from Slows Bar BQ. Fans can also enjoy rotating local food trucks on weekends and themed concessions on Heritage Nights.

Accessibility

Accessibility

The preferred accessible drop-off and pick up locations are at the intersections Adams Ave and Witherell Street. Accessible Ticket Windows are available at Gate A - A1, A2, A14, A15; Tiger Club Lobby A17 (in-house future game purchase); Gate Gallagher Entry - C25.
