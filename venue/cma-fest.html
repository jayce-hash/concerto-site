Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at CMA Fest. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Festival organizers encourage the use of rideshare, SpotHero, or public parking options. Reserve parking in advance through SpotHero for convenience and affordability.

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in CMA Fest's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by CMA Fest, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The CMA Fest in Nashville offers a variety of food and drink options, featuring local and regional cuisine. Attendees can enjoy a range of culinary delights from numerous vendors, with special offerings that highlight the vibrant food scene of Nashville.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in CMA Fest's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.
