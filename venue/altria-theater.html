    Bag policy

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    Bags up to 12 × 8 × 6 inches, diaper bags and medical bags may enter after search. Backpacks, boxes, laptop bags and briefcases are prohibited.

    Allowed

    • Bag up to 12 × 8 × 6 inches
    • Diaper bag
    • Medical bag

    Not allowed

    • Backpacks
    • Boxes
    • Laptop bags
    • Briefcases
    Official source

    Entrances and doors

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    The lobbies generally open one hour before the event and seating-area doors may open only 30 minutes before it. Entry can include metal detection and a bag search.

    Official source

    Parking

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    The theater does not manage parking; public paid parking is available in VCU decks within two blocks, which open three hours before showtime and operate cashlessly.

    Official source

    Rideshare

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    The Altria Theater visitor FAQ and directions page do not specify a dedicated rideshare pickup location.

    Official source

    Accessibility

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    Accessible entrances are on North Laurel Street and West Main Street; elevators serve all levels and accessible restrooms are in the main lobby. Assisted-listening devices are available with a photo ID deposit.

    Official source

    Food and drink

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    Cashless concessions sell popcorn, candy, snacks, soft drinks, beer, wine and mixed drinks on all levels except the fourth floor. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

    Not allowed

    • Outside food and beverages
    Official source

    Re-entry

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    The theater FAQ mentions a designated outdoor smoking area but does not publish a general re-entry rule for concerts.

    Official source

    Tickets and will call

    Checked Sep 24, 2026

    Will Call tickets may be picked up at the venue box office starting two hours before the advertised showtime; bring valid photo ID.

    Official source
