Bag Policy

Bag Policy

PNC Arena does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Parking

Parking

Parking is accessible via Edwards Mill Road from I-40 or Wade Avenue. Follow signs to the main entrance for parking options. For more details, refer to the Parking Guide.

Rideshare

Rideshare

Taxi services may be available in the drop off and pick up area located in gate C (near the TowneBank Center). Lenovo Center Rideshare offers convenient, one-stop drop-off and pick-up location across the main roadway from VIP Parking, next to Townebank Center at Carter-Finley Stadium.

Concessions

Concessions

PNC Arena offers a diverse range of food and drink options including gourmet hot dogs, Peruvian chicken, and signature mac & cheese bowls. Special offerings include Willy's Hot Chicken with multiple spice levels and Sup Dogs' branded vodka drinks.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Sensory Bags, Weighted Lap Pads, and Photosensitivity Glasses: Available for guests to check-out at Guest Services (Section 127) at no cost. Accessible Seating is available throughout all levels of the facility.
