Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Coors Field enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Only specific types and sizes of bags are permitted.

Allowed

  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag (1 Gallon)
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets up to 6.5” x 4.5”
  • Seat cushions without pockets, zippers or concealable areas up to 18” wide

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags and wallets exceeding 6.5" x 4.5"
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage, computer bags, camera bags, briefcases, fanny packs, cinch bags

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Coors Field is in Denver's LoDo district with Rockies-operated lots near the ballpark plus numerous downtown garages and surface lots within walking distance. Prepaid parking is available through the team and SpotHero; rates rise for marquee games. Light rail and the Free MallRide are convenient alternatives.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Coors Field's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Coors Field, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Coors Field offers a variety of food and beverage options, including classic comfort foods with bold flavors and modern twists. Highlights include specialty items like Bacon Wrapped Hot Dogs, Pastrami Fries, and indulgent Milkshakes. Fans can enjoy local and popular beers at the Beer Garden.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Coors Field's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call
