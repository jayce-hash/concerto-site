Know before you go

What matters at Mohegan Sun Arena.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

For the health and safety of guests, Mohegan Sun Arena prohibits several types of bags including backpacks and diaper bags. Increased screening procedures may delay entry, so early arrival is encouraged.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Mohegan Sun offers over 13,000 parking spaces with options for reserved parking through Riverview Reserved Parking. Valet service is available 24/7, and electric vehicle charging stations are located in multiple garages.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

Guests utilizing ride share services (Uber/Lyft/Taxi) should utilize the Highland Park Blvd. entrance and inform the parking attendants upon arrival. Parking attendants will direct you to the drop off/pick up location

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Doors

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Mohegan Sun Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Mohegan Sun Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Mohegan Sun Arena offers a variety of food and drink options, with numerous lounges and bars available throughout the venue. Special offerings include the VISTA Lounge at Wombi Rock and the Avalon nightclub, providing a vibrant nightlife experience.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Mohegan Sun Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Mohegan Sun Arena. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
