Frost Amphitheater

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

One bag per person: clear bags, backpacks or fanny packs up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches, a one-gallon clear bag, or a nonclear clutch or fanny pack up to 6 × 9 inches. A nonclear hydration pack up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches with two pockets and an empty bladder up to 2 liters is also allowed.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches
  • One-gallon clear bag
  • Small clutch up to 6 × 9 inches
  • Qualifying two-pocket hydration pack

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
  • Nonclear bags larger than 6 × 9 inches otherwise
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Security searches guests and belongings at the entrance. Lines may start forming at 10 a.m.; event pages give show-specific door times.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Event parking is in designated lots near Lasuen Street and Campus Drive; buy a pass through the event page or AXS when offered. Parking closes one hour after the event and price varies by show.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The official accessible passenger drop-off is near Roth Way and Lasuen Street, around one to two blocks from Lasuen Gate. The university does not list a separate rideshare-only zone.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible seating can be purchased through the standard event ticket path. The main entrance has accessible approaches and a ramp; accessible parking is on Roth Way and in Galvez Lot with a valid plate or placard.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drinks are sold inside and the venue is cashless. Guests may bring personal snacks in a one-gallon clear bag, one sealed plastic water bottle up to 32 ounces or one liter, or an empty reusable bottle of that size.

Allowed

  • Snacks in a one-gallon clear bag
  • One sealed plastic water bottle up to 32 ounces or one liter
  • Empty reusable bottle up to 32 ounces or one liter

Not allowed

  • Outside alcohol
  • Glass containers
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The university Frost FAQ and what-to-bring page do not state a general re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The university FAQ says Goldenvoice-presented shows sell online through AXS, but does not publish a general will-call pickup procedure for Frost concerts.
