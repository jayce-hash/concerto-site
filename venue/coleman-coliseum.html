Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The athletics A–Z guide permits clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches, one-gallon clear freezer bags and clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches. Medical exceptions are inspected; check concert-specific rules.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear freezer bag
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Nonclear bags over 4.5 × 6.5 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For basketball, all gates open 90 minutes before tip-off, with public entry at the north, east, south and west doors. Concert door times are not specified in this guide.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Alabama Athletics provides a Coleman Coliseum basketball parking map, but its A–Z guide does not specify concert parking lots or rates.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published a concert rideshare pickup or drop-off zone in its Coliseum A–Z guide.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

An elevator for guests needing access to higher rows is in the North Lobby. The first-aid room behind Sections N and O provides a private nursing area.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Levy operates the athletics concessions, which accept Mastercard, Visa, American Express and Discover. The venue prohibits outside food and drink except approved medically necessary snacks.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drink
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Coliseum A–Z guide says guests cannot leave and re-enter on a scanned ticket; every entry requires an unscanned ticket.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For athletics games, public will call is at the North Doors and requires a photo ID; the ticket office opens two hours before weekend or evening tip-off. Concert will-call arrangements are not listed.

Official source
