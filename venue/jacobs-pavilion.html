Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Large bags and backpacks are prohibited; the maximum permitted bag measures 12 × 12 × 6 inches.

Allowed

Not allowed

  • Large backpacks

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The main gate and box office are accessible; consult the concert event page for opening time. The venue prohibits oversized bags and outside food.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The 1,200-space Nautica Entertainment Complex surface lot is at 1200 Elm Street. It is separately managed, first come first served and opens several hours before events.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue information mentions taxis and Uber or Lyft but does not specify a dedicated pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible parking is available in the main lot next to the venue, and the main entry gate and box office are accessible. Ask Guest Services at the main entrance about the ramp-access viewing platform.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Beverage and snack stands accept cards and mobile wallets only. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
