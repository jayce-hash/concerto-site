Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Large backpacks, suitcases, beach bags and equipment bags cannot be brought in. The FAQ gives no numeric limit for smaller bags.

Not allowed

  • Large backpacks
  • Suitcases
  • Beach bags
  • Equipment bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue uses walk-through metal detectors and asks guests to allow time for screening. A Quick Pass uses a dedicated general-admission line at normal door time and requires an event ticket.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Street parking is available near the theatre; check local parking signs.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theatre FAQ does not designate a rideshare location.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

A separate accessible seating ticket is not required. Ask security at arrival for a chair in the ADA section and plan to arrive near door time.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Venue concessions are cashless; outside food and drinks are not permitted.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The published FAQ and policies do not give a re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Most tickets use AXS Mobile. The Bluebird box office opens 30 minutes before doors on show days, and in-person tickets carry a $2.50 fee.

Official source
