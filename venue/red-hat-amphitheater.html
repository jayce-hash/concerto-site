Concessions

The Red Hat Amphitheater does not allow outside food and beverages, except for one factory-sealed bottle of water per person. The venue has gone cashless, accepting only credit, debit, or mobile payments. Cash can be converted to a prepaid debit card at select shows.

Accessibility
