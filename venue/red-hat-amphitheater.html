Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Red Hat Amphitheater. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Parking availability, access routes, and pricing at Red Hat Amphitheater can vary by event. Review the linked official information and reserve in advance when that option is offered.

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Red Hat Amphitheater's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accessible parking spaces are located on the first level of parking garages. Accessible drop-off and pick-up areas are located by the Red Hat Amphitheater Box Office near the intersection of Cabarrus and McDowell Streets.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Red Hat Amphitheater does not allow outside food and beverages, except for one factory-sealed bottle of water per person. The venue has gone cashless, accepting only credit, debit, or mobile payments. Cash can be converted to a prepaid debit card at select shows.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Red Hat Amphitheater's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Red Hat Amphitheater. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
