Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Backpacks, large bags and large purses are prohibited; small purses and fanny packs may measure up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches.

Allowed

  • Small purse or fanny pack up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Large bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The ticket’s door time is when guests can enter; shows usually begin 60–90 minutes later. The theater asks guests not to line up more than one hour before doors.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Masonic has no venue parking or valet; use paid District Detroit lots or garages. Parking at city meters can be risky because the theater does not permit re-entry.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ gives no designated rideshare drop-off or pickup area.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The accessible entrance is the ramp at Second Avenue and Temple Street. The Temple Theatre has an elevated accessible platform and elevators; contact the box office in advance for help.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Most public shows offer light snacks such as popcorn and chips, and the venue has full-service bars. No outside food or drinks, including gum and candy, are allowed.

Not allowed

  • Outside food or drinks
  • Gum and candy
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

No re-entry is permitted once a ticket has been scanned.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Masonic box office is open Tuesday–Friday from noon to 6 p.m. Guest-list and contest winners need photo ID matching the name given to the box office.

Official source
