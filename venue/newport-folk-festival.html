Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags under 20”x20”x10” are allowed at the Newport Folk Festival. Larger bags are prohibited.

Parking

Parking

On site parking is limited at Fort Adams State Park, and purchasing a parking pass in advance is strongly encouraged. On site parking sales are credit card only, and once full, attendees will be directed to an offsite satellite lot. Parking for patrons with disabilities is available with a designated lot and shuttle service.

Accessibility

Accessibility

There will be a shuttle bus fitted with a wheelchair accessible lift to bring patrons from the parking lot directly to the event entrance, running from 9am until the end of the day. Animals whose sole function is to provide comfort or emotional support do not qualify as service animals under the ADA and are not permitted at the festivals.
