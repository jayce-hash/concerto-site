Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Fertitta Center enforces a clear-bag policy. Approved bags are subject to inspection at entry.

Allowed

  • Approved clear bags
  • Medically necessary bags after inspection

Not allowed

  • Bags that do not meet the clear-bag policy
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

For basketball, gates and game-day ticket windows open 60 minutes before tipoff. Visiting-team will call is at the Holman Street ticket office.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Parking arrangements vary by event. Accessible entry to the garage requires a valid ADA-compliant license plate or hangtag.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The arena has accessible parking, restrooms, concessions, seating, and public entrances. Guests may contact the Houston Athletics Ticket Office for assistance.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Chartwells operates concession stands and portable carts throughout the concourse. Major credit cards are accepted at concessions.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Guests receiving player tickets must enter when the ticket is issued and follow the arena’s no-re-entry policy.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

General will call is available in advance at the Houston Athletics Ticket Office and at both arena ticket booths on game day. Game-day windows open 60 minutes before tipoff, and photo ID is required.
