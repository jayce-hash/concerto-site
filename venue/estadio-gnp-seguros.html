Know before you go

What matters at Estadio GNP Seguros.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags or backpacks may not exceed 10 x 15 x 30 cm and are preferably clear. Small handbags and fanny packs may not exceed 11 x 16 cm; medium clear storage bags are also allowed.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Parking

Parking

The Estadio GNP Seguros offers parking with access through gates 7, 8, 9, and 15 of the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez and Ciudad Deportiva Magdalena Mixhuca. Access points may vary depending on the event configuration and location.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Estadio GNP Seguros's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

The venue offers a premium gastronomic experience with comfort food and beverages available in a lounge area. These offerings are part of the hospitality benefits for Boxes Diamante and Boxes Oro ticket holders.

Verified 2026-08-20

Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Estadio GNP Seguros, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.
