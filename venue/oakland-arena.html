Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags smaller than 14"x14"x6" are allowed, while larger bags and backpacks of any size are prohibited. All items are subject to search.

Allowed

  • Bags Smaller Than 14"x14"x6"

Not allowed

  • Bags Larger Than 14"x14"x6"
  • Backpacks Of Any Size

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Oakland Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at the Oakland Arena with various options for attendees. It's recommended to arrive early to secure a spot and check the website for any specific event-related parking details.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests taking rideshare at Oakland Arena events will need to proceed to the rideshare pick-up/drop-off point at Baldwin Gate. Guests taking rideshare at Oakland-Alameda County Coliseum events will need to proceed to the rideshare pick-up/drop-off point at Baldwin Gate.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Oakland Arena also offers accessible parking, which is located in the first two rows of the parking lot, closest to Oakland Arena. Wheelchair accessible restrooms are located throughout Oakland Arena.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Oakland Arena offers a variety of food and drink options including elevated arena classics and specialty items. Guests can enjoy draft beer, craft cocktails, and a range of food from nachos and hot dogs to international delicacies. The venue is 100% cashless.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

No re-entry is permitted after you have exited Oakland Arena. Exiting and re-entering the stadium with the same ticket is NOT permitted.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The “Will Call” window is located on the Plaza Level at the Oakland Arena Box Office. Will Call tickets are available for pickup on the scheduled event day.

Official source
