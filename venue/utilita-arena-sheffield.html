Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags larger than 30cm x 40cm x 20cm are prohibited. Rucksacks and backpacks are not allowed, and there is no storage facility for prohibited items.

Allowed

  • Bags up to 30cm wide x 20cm long x 20cm deep

Not allowed

  • Rucksacks/Backpacks
  • Bags larger than 30cm x 40cm x 20cm

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

On-site parking at Utilita Arena Sheffield often sells out in advance, so it's recommended to purchase parking tickets online beforehand for guaranteed reserved parking. Parking is for one admission only, and re-entry is not allowed.

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Utilita Arena Sheffield, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Utilita Arena Sheffield offers a variety of food and drink options with a focus on fresh, tasty, and locally sourced products. Gluten-free and vegan options are available, and all outlets are cashless, accepting only card payments.
