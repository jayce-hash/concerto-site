Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

PNC Park enforces a clear bag policy to ensure guest safety and expedite entry. Only specific types and sizes of bags are permitted.

Allowed

  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) no larger than 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag (1 Gallon)
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”
  • Seat cushions without pockets or zippers

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags larger than a small clutch
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage, computer bags, camera bags, briefcases, fanny packs, cinch bags

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at PNC Park. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

PNC Park is on Pittsburgh's North Shore with Pirates-operated lots (Gold, Red, General Robinson Garage) right by the ballpark plus downtown garages reachable via a walk over the Roberto Clemente Bridge. Prepaid parking is recommended, and Light Rail (the 'T') is free to the North Shore station.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in PNC Park's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accessible parking is available in all four (4) parking garages and two (2) surface parking lots (East Lot 2 & East Lot 3) on a first-come, first-served basis. Wheelchair accessible and semi-ambulatory seats with operable armrests are located in various sections on all ballpark levels.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

PNC Park offers a variety of food and beverage options including classic comfort foods with modern twists, such as Chicken Tenders & Fries, Cheeseburgers, and specialty hot dogs. Highlights include the Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry, with a range of local and popular beers available at the Beer Garden.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in PNC Park's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026
