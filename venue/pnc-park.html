Parking

Parking

PNC Park is on Pittsburgh's North Shore with Pirates-operated lots (Gold, Red, General Robinson Garage) right by the ballpark plus downtown garages reachable via a walk over the Roberto Clemente Bridge. Prepaid parking is recommended, and Light Rail (the 'T') is free to the North Shore station.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Accessible parking is available in all four (4) parking garages and two (2) surface parking lots (East Lot 2 & East Lot 3) on a first-come, first-served basis. Wheelchair accessible and semi-ambulatory seats with operable armrests are located in various sections on all ballpark levels.

Concessions

Concessions

PNC Park offers a variety of food and beverage options including classic comfort foods with modern twists, such as Chicken Tenders & Fries, Cheeseburgers, and specialty hot dogs. Highlights include the Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry, with a range of local and popular beers available at the Beer Garden.
