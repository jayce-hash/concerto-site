Bag Policy

Bag Policy

The Rogers Centre bag policy allows soft-sided bags up to 16"x16"x8" for Blue Jays games, while concerts have stricter rules with only small purses and clutches typically under 6"x9" allowed. Hard-sided coolers and large bags are prohibited at all events.

Parking

Parking

Rogers Centre offers both surface and underground parking options with rates varying by time of day. Early bird rates are available, and amenities include EV charging stations and valet service. Rates are subject to change, and public access may vary per event.

Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Rogers Centre's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

Rogers Centre offers a variety of food and beverage options, including gluten-friendly, halal, vegan, and vegetarian choices. Specialty hot dogs and Loonie Dogs are featured on Tuesdays. Alcohol is available until the 9th inning at specific locations.
