Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Co-op Live allows small handbags no larger than A4 size and enforces a one bag per person policy. Large bags, backpacks, and similar items are not permitted but can be stored on site for a fee.

Allowed

  • small handbags no larger than A4 size

Not allowed

  • large bags
  • backpacks
  • travel cases
  • laptop bags
  • large umbrellas
  • professional cameras
  • large tote bags
