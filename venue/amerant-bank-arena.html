What matters at Amerant Bank Arena.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Amerant Bank Arena discourages guests from bringing bags to events. If necessary, only small bags, clutches, crossbodies, and wristlets smaller than 4in x 6in x 1.5in are allowed.

Parking

Parking

Parking is available at various gates with options for general, club, garage, and VIP parking. Pre-purchasing parking for events can save time and money. Pedestrians should use Gate 6 for access.

Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Amerant Bank Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

Amerant Bank Arena offers a diverse menu of premier concessions available during Florida Panthers hockey games, concerts, and events. Highlights include the Funky Buddha Brewery Tap Room, Jameson Crossbar, and Patrón Patio, providing a range of drink options and social spaces.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Amerant Bank Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

In an emergency situation, guests should make contact with an Event Supervisor or go to Guest Services located near the North Box Office to make arrangements for re-entry.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Ticket information/Will Call is located on the Plaza Level at the South Box Office. The SeatGeek Box Office is open Monday-Friday from 9am to 5pm, with extended hours on event days.

Gates & Entrances

Escalators are located at the main entrance of the building and lead to all levels of the arena.
