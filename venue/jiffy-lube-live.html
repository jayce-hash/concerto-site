Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Jiffy Lube Live allows clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" and small clutch bags up to 6" x 9". All bags are subject to search upon entry.

Allowed

  • clear plastic bags (max size 12" x 6" x 12")
  • vinyl bags (max size 12" x 6" x 12")
  • PVC bags (max size 12" x 6" x 12")
  • small clutch bags (max size 6" x 9")

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Jiffy Lube Live. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

General admission parking is included with your ticket. For easier entry and exit, consider upgrading to Premier or VIP Parking. Early Parking is also available for those who want to arrive before the rush.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Jiffy Lube Live's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Jiffy Lube Live, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Jiffy Lube Live offers a variety of food and drink options, including Mosh Burger's hormone-free beef burgers, Rebel Hen's crispy chicken tenders, and Ziggy's Magic Pizza Shop's NY-inspired pizzas. Unique offerings include Koko's Bavarian sausages and ColdRush's shaved ice with bold flavors. Concessions also feature classic concert bites like popcorn and nachos, alongside a selection of alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Jiffy Lube Live's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Jiffy Lube Live. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
