Bag policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Small personal bags and backpacks are allowed. Large backpacks are not permitted.

Allowed

  • small personal bags
  • small backpacks

Not allowed

  • large backpacks
  • strollers
  • signs larger than 8.5″ x 11″
  • alcohol
  • cans
  • bottles
  • weapons
  • pets
  • professional recording devices

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Fox Theater. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at 1800 SAN PABLO LOT for a $20 flat rate and at FRANKLIN PLAZA GARAGE for a $25 flat rate during event hours. Public transportation via the 19th Street BART station is highly encouraged.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lyft is the official rideshare partner of Fox Theater.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Fox Theater, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Den at the Fox Theater offers a gourmet menu and specialty cocktails for ticket holders. It is a sophisticated bar open an hour before showtime, providing a convenient entry to the theater.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Fox Theater's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The will call window opens 30 minutes prior to doors at the Fox Theater box office. The Will Call window opens approximately 30 minutes prior to doors for each show at the Fox Theater box office.

Official source
