Know before you go

What matters at The Van Buren.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” are allowed in the venue. All bags will be searched prior to entry, and non-clear bags will undergo additional search.

Parking

Parking

There is no on-site parking at The Van Buren. They have partnered with ParkWhiz for parking options nearby. Limited metered street parking and nearby paid lots are available, but fill up quickly.

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in The Van Buren's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

Bars sell beverages inside The Van Buren. Outside food and drinks are prohibited; guests may generally bring up to two factory-sealed water bottles of no more than 32 ounces each.
