Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

GIANT Center does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at GIANT Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking availability, access routes, and pricing at GIANT Center can vary by event. Review the linked official information and reserve in advance when that option is offered.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in GIANT Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

If you have specific accessibility questions or concerns, please contact Box Office Customer Service directly at 717-534-3300, TTY 717-534-8955. Accessible parking is located in the Preferred Parking Lot and in the General Parking areas.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The GIANT Center offers a wide variety of food and beverage options, including traditional concessions like hot dogs and nachos, as well as unique offerings such as Cuban sandwiches and smoked barbecue. Beverage options include a selection of beers, cocktails, and fresh squeezed lemonades.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in GIANT Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Will Call is located at Hersheypark Stadium Box Office and opens at 9 a.m. on the day of the concert. GIANT Center's team will be happy to assist you with your specific requests during regular box office hours.

Official source
