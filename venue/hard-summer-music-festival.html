HARD Summer Music Festival

Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12" x 6" x 12", one-gallon clear freezer bags, non-clear bags up to 6" x 9", and qualifying empty hydration packs are allowed. Other bags are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12" x 6" x 12"
  • One-gallon clear freezer bags
  • Non-clear bags up to 6" x 9"
  • Empty hydration packs with no more than two main compartments and one smaller compartment

Not allowed

  • Bags outside the published size and compartment limits
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

HARD Summer takes place at Hollywood Park adjacent to SoFi Stadium, 1011 Stadium Dr. Event hours are 2:00–10:00 p.m. Saturday and Sunday; arrive early for TSA-style screening.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The 2026 festival is at Hollywood Park adjacent to SoFi Stadium, 1011 Stadium Dr., Inglewood. Use the current official travel page for paid parking, shuttle, and arrival options before departure.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Use the current HARD Summer travel instructions and the signed Hollywood Park event rideshare areas; pickup traffic controls may change during egress.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Limited paid ADA parking is available first-come in all festival lots for vehicles with a valid state-issued placard, permit, or plate; the credential holder must be present.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

HARD Summer is cashless and sells food and beverages inside the festival. Outside food, beverages, alcohol, candy, glass, cans, cups, and coolers are prohibited.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

No re-entry or ins-and-outs are permitted on the same festival day.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For 2026, the box office is open 9:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m. Saturday and 1:00 p.m.–9:00 p.m. Sunday at Hollywood Park.

Official source
