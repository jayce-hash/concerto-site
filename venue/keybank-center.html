Bag Policy

Concessions

Concessions

KeyBank Center offers a wide range of traditional and specialty food options. In-seat food and beverage service is available to Club seat patrons on the 200 level during Sabres games. All concession stands are accessible, and assistants are available for guests with visual impairments.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Drinking fountains are located on all Levels and are accessible. The Security Office is located on the Ground Level of the Arena and is accessible from the outside of the building.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

For your convenience, KeyBank Center's Box Office has twelve ticket windows available in the east end of the Pavilion located to the left of the Account Services Office. To unlock the suite, patrons may sign-out the key at Account Services located in the Pavilion next to the Box Office.
