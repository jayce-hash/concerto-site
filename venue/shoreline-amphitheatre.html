Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags must be clear and no larger than 12" x 12" x 6". Small clutches up to 6" x 9" are allowed.

Allowed

  • Small clutch (6" x 9")
  • Clear, plastic, or PVC tote bag no larger than 12" x 12" x 6"

Not allowed

  • Large or oversized bags
  • Any bag larger than 12" x 12" x 6"
  • Chairs / Lawn Chairs
  • Aerosol products/cans
  • Alcohol or beverages other than a sealed bottle of water
  • Animals (except service animals)
  • Balloons
  • Balls or Frisbees
  • Banners
  • Blankets larger than 6’x 6’
  • Bota bags
  • Coolers
  • Costumes resembling public safety uniforms
  • Drones
  • Eye drops
  • Fireworks
  • Gang activity
  • Gas masks
  • Glass containers
  • Glow sticks
  • Illegal drugs/substances
  • Large chains or spiked jewelry
  • Laptops/tablets
  • Laser pointers and air horns
  • LED gloves or LED microlights
  • Liquid makeup
  • Markers, pens or spray paint
  • Massagers
  • Musical instruments
  • Offensive clothing
  • Over-the-counter medication not in original packaging
  • Pacifiers
  • Professional photo, video, or audio recording equipment
  • Selfie sticks
  • Signs larger than 8.5”x11”
  • Stickers, flyers
  • Strollers, baby carriers or wagons
  • Stuffed animals or dolls
  • Tents
  • Umbrellas
  • Unsealed tampons or pads
  • Weapons, sharp objects or weapon accessories
  • Wind chairs, inflatable furniture

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Shoreline Amphitheatre. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

General admission parking is included with your ticket. For a shorter walk, easier exit, or oversized parking, consider upgrading to Premier Parking options.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Shoreline Amphitheatre's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Accessibility Shuttle pick-up and drop-off zone is located in the northwest corner of Lot A, near the Shoreline Blvd & Bill Graham Pkwy/North Road intersection. Limited storage is available for wheelchairs, canes, walkers, and other mobility devices.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Shoreline Amphitheatre offers a variety of food and drink options, including Mosh Burger's hormone-free beef burgers, Rebel Hen's crispy chicken tenders, and Ziggy's Magic Pizza Shop's NY-inspired pizzas. Koko's Bavarian provides German-inspired sausages and pretzels, while general concessions offer popcorn, nachos, and a selection of beverages. CVT Premium Soft Serve Ice Cream is also available in convenient pouches.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Shoreline Amphitheatre's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Will call tickets are available for pickup at the box office, between both main entry gates. On show days, Box Office opens at 12pm.
