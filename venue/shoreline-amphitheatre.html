Bag Policy

Concessions

Concessions

Shoreline Amphitheatre offers a variety of food and drink options, including Mosh Burger's hormone-free beef burgers, Rebel Hen's crispy chicken tenders, and Ziggy's Magic Pizza Shop's NY-inspired pizzas. Koko's Bavarian provides German-inspired sausages and pretzels, while general concessions offer popcorn, nachos, and a selection of beverages. CVT Premium Soft Serve Ice Cream is also available in convenient pouches.

Accessibility

Accessibility

The Accessibility Shuttle pick-up and drop-off zone is located in the northwest corner of Lot A, near the Shoreline Blvd & Bill Graham Pkwy/North Road intersection. Limited storage is available for wheelchairs, canes, walkers, and other mobility devices.
