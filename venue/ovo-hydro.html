Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Small bags such as handbags and small shopping bags are allowed at OVO Hydro. Large bags, including suitcases and backpacks, are not permitted but can be stored in the SEC Centre cloakroom for a fee.

Allowed

  • handbags
  • small carrier/shopping bags

Not allowed

  • suitcases
  • large handbags (A3 size/30cm x 42cm and above)
  • backpacks/rucksacks
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Available from 1 hour before doors open. The doors open time is usually listed on the event page of our websites and social media channels a few days before the event at ovohydro.com and sec.co.uk, or connect with us on Facebook, Twitter and Instagram.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The OVO Hydro offers a free Drop-off/Pick-up zone located at the west of the SEC Centre, accessible via Stobcross Road. Long-term parking is available in the multi-storey car park on Finnieston Street. The DOPU zone allows a flexible 30-minute stay and provides access to various facilities.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026
