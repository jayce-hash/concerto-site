Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Small bags such as handbags and small shopping bags are allowed at OVO Hydro. Large bags, including suitcases and backpacks, are not permitted but can be stored in the SEC Centre cloakroom for a fee.

Parking

Parking

The OVO Hydro offers a free Drop-off/Pick-up zone located at the west of the SEC Centre, accessible via Stobcross Road. Long-term parking is available in the multi-storey car park on Finnieston Street. The DOPU zone allows a flexible 30-minute stay and provides access to various facilities.
