Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

No bags are permitted in PPG Paints Arena, with exceptions for medical bags, diaper bags, and small wristlets or wallets. All exceptions are subject to inspection.

Allowed

  • Medical Bags
  • Diaper Bags
  • Wristlets or Wallets (no larger than 4" x 6" x 1.5")

Not allowed

  • All other bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Highmark Gate is located next to the Epiphany Catholic Church on Centre Avenue and is the main entryway to PPG Paints Arena and home to the famous Mario Lemieux Statue.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available in the PPG Paints Arena Lots and the Parking Garage at PPG Paints Arena for non-hockey events. All lots are cashless, and pre-purchasing parking in advance is encouraged through ParkWhiz. Event parking rates vary.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

As the Official Rideshare Partner of PPG Paints Arena, Lyft offers a discount code PPGPAINTS for 50% off your first 2 rides (up to $10/ride).

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by PPG Paints Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

PPG Paints Arena offers a variety of food and beverage options to enhance the event experience. While specific offerings may vary by event, guests can expect a range of traditional and specialty concessions. Highlights often include local favorites and unique culinary experiences.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Exit and re-entry is not permitted at PPG Paints Arena. Exit and re-entry into the building is not permitted.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Tickets ordered or prepaid can be picked up at the DICK'S Sporting Goods Box Office at PPG Paints Arena, located in the lobby.

Official source
