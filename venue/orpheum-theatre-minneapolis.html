Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Backpacks and bags larger than 14 × 15 inches are prohibited. Smaller bags must fit under the seat or on a lap and are searched on arrival.

Allowed

  • Small bag that fits under seat or on lap

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags larger than 14 × 15 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Plan to arrive at least 30 minutes early for bag search and walk-through metal detector screening. The Orpheum show-day box office opens two hours before showtime; each concert sets its door time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible spaces are available in the Hawthorne Municipal Ramp behind the Orpheum; its third-level skyway reaches the theater balcony. Other nearby downtown ramps may also be open.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Orpheum operator FAQ gives accessible drop-off areas but does not designate a rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible seating accommodates the guest plus up to three ticketed companions. Request sign language interpretation at least two weeks ahead; listening devices are free from ushers with photo ID held until return.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Wine, beer, soft drinks and light snacks are sold inside. Outside food and drinks are prohibited except items needed for a disability or medical reason.

Allowed

  • Medically necessary food or drink

Not allowed

  • Other outside food and drink
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Orpheum operator FAQ does not state a general concert re-entry policy.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

On show days, the Orpheum box office opens two hours before showtime. In the final hour before the show, it may serve only ticket needs for that performance.

Official source
