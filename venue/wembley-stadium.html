Know before you go

What matters at Wembley Stadium.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Wembley Stadium allows each person to bring one small bag no larger than A4 size to enhance safety and speed up security checks. Bags will be inspected, and exceptions are made for medical equipment with prior permission.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Wembley Stadium is primarily a public transport destination with easy access via three railway stations. Visitors driving to the stadium should be aware of the Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) charges applicable to non-compliant vehicles.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Wembley Stadium's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Wembley Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options including hearty and healthy breakfast menus, buffet and bowl food menus, and a la carte dining. Special offerings include customizable day delegate packages and dinner packages with room hire and drinks. The venue also provides options for canape receptions and drinks receptions.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Wembley Stadium, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Re-Entry Policy
