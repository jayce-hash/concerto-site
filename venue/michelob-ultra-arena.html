Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags and backpacks are prohibited. Small wallets or clutches no larger than 9 × 5 × 2 inches may enter; there is no bag check.

Allowed

  • Wallet or clutch up to 9 × 5 × 2 inches

Not allowed

  • Bags
  • Backpacks
  • Larger clutches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena entrance is past Starbucks down the long hallway from the resort walkway. The venue lists restricted items and opens its box office four hours before event time; event doors vary.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Mandalay Bay self-parking is $20 Monday–Thursday or $25 Friday–Sunday for up to 24 hours; special-event rates may be higher. Valet is $40 daily before special-event surcharges.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena and Mandalay Bay transport guides do not specify an arena-specific rideshare pickup point.

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Outside food, beverages and alcohol are prohibited, as are all bottles and flasks, including empty bottles.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
  • Bottles and flasks

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena box office opens four hours before the scheduled event on show days; follow the purchase confirmation for ticket delivery or will-call eligibility.
