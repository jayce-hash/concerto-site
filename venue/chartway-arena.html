Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Large purses, backpacks and other large bags are prohibited. Medically necessary items and diaper bags may enter after a search; the guide gives no numeric bag size.

Allowed

  • Medically necessary bag
  • Diaper bag

Not allowed

  • Large purses
  • Backpacks
  • Other large bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors open about one hour before most events; the VIP and accessible entrances are on the north side at 45th Street and Hampton Boulevard.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Event parking is available in the Constant Center 43rd Street and 45th Street garages; advance garage passes are encouraged and cost less than any passes still available onsite.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena A–Z and parking pages do not publish a designated rideshare pickup or drop-off point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The accessible entrance is on 45th Street, where the north-side VIP entrance is also located. All restrooms have accessible facilities.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food service is on the second-floor concourse; selections vary by event and all concession payments are cashless. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests who leave the arena cannot re-enter.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The A–Z guide states the box office is at 43rd Street and Hampton Boulevard, open weekdays 10 a.m.–5 p.m., but does not publish a specific will-call pickup procedure.

Official source
