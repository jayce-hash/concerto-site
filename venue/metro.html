Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Backpacks and bags larger than 9 × 11 inches must be checked at the $5 credit-card-only coat and bag check. Security may inspect bags.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 9 × 11 inches

Not allowed

  • Unchecked backpacks
  • Unchecked bags larger than 9 × 11 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Metro is at 3730 North Clark Street, with its box office next door at 3728 North Clark. Guests should carry valid physical ID as proof of age at entry.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Metro has no dedicated patron parking. Metered spaces on Clark Street and nearby pay lots are options.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Metro’s directions and policy page does not designate a rideshare pickup or drop-off location.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The stage-viewing room is up two flights of stairs and the balcony is up a total of 41 stairs; Metro says it is not wheelchair accessible. Request limited balcony seating at least two weeks ahead or hearing assistance at least three weeks ahead.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Alcohol and a range of nonalcoholic drinks are available. To drink alcohol, guests aged 21 or older must present current physical ID and wear a wristband.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Metro usually does not allow re-entry; its door staff may allow it for smokers aged 21 or older at selected shows. Ask staff about that night’s rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Pick up will-call admission by checking in at the guest list with a physical ID matching the ticket buyer’s name; no physical ticket is issued.

Official source
