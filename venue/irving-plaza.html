Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Irving Plaza, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Irving Plaza offers a selection of beers from local breweries and national brands, along with handcrafted cocktails available at every bar. The Jocco's Ransom VIP Lounge provides an exclusive beverage menu in a comfortable atmosphere.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Checked Aug 20, 2026
