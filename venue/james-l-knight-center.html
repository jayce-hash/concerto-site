Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue operates a no-bag policy with an exception for bags smaller than 10 × 7 × 2 inches, which are inspected. Backpacks are prohibited; paid lockers remain available until 45 minutes after the event.

Allowed

  • Bag smaller than 10 × 7 × 2 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Larger bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Show the live ticket barcode on a phone and use the posted general admission or reserved seating entrance. Bags and guests are subject to inspection.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Miami Tower Garage at 100 SE 2nd Street has a $20 special-event parking rate set by the Miami Parking Authority; the venue does not manage the garage.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue Know Before You Go page covers transit and parking but does not specify an app-based rideshare point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Outside food and drinks are prohibited. The venue’s FAQ does not publish a fixed concession menu.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

No re-entry is allowed after leaving the venue.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Tickets are displayed from a Ticketmaster account or added to a digital wallet; screenshots and printouts are not accepted.
