Bag Policy

Small personal bags and backpacks are allowed at the UC Berkeley Greek Theatre. The policy encourages bringing reusable water bottles.

Parking

Parking

Parking is very limited around the Greek Theatre, and it's recommended to use public transportation, biking, or carpooling. UC Berkeley lots near the venue open at 5:00pm, accept credit cards only, and have limited spots. Disabled parking is available at Upper Hearst parking garage.
