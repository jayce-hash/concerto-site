Lambeau Field

Know before you go

What matters at Lambeau Field.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Lambeau Field allows clear bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" and small non-clear bags up to 4.5" x 6.5". All other bags are prohibited. Security measures include walk-through metal detectors.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

All parking spaces in the lots surrounding Lambeau Field are sold out to holders of season parking passes. Individual game parking is available through PMI in nearby lots, and free parking can be found on residential streets. Accessible parking is available for $50 on a first-come, first-served basis.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Lambeau Field's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

The Green Bay Packers and Delaware North Sportservice have introduced new concession items for the 2022 season at Lambeau Field. These offerings aim to enhance the game-day experience with diverse food and beverage options.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Those who utilize a wheelchair to maneuver the concourse but are able to view the game from a standard stadium seat can store their wheelchair at one of the two Guest Services booths located underneath Section 109 in the main concourse and underneath Section 353 in the upper concourse.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Lambeau Field's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Lambeau Field. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Lambeau Field. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.
