Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches are permitted and searched; nonclear bags get additional inspection.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors generally open an hour before showtime, and bags are searched at entry. Check the event listing for the actual door time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Fillmore has no lot. Street parking and public garages are nearby, including accessible spaces at the Japantown garages entered at 1650 Fillmore Street and 1610 Geary Boulevard.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue visit guide does not identify a designated rideshare pickup or drop-off zone.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The second-floor performance space is reached by an elevator on the building’s west side, and bathrooms are accessible. Buy general admission and email the venue for accommodation; limited seated space is first come first served.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drink options are sold in the venue; payment is cashless at most points of sale. The guide lists outside food among prohibited items.

Not allowed

  • Outside food
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

A guest who leaves during the show cannot return without a new ticket.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Events use mobile entry. If a ticket is held at will call, pick it up at the show-day box office with photo ID matching the purchaser; the office opens 30 minutes before published doors and closes at 9 p.m.

Official source
