Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags up to 12"x6"x12" are allowed in the venue and will be searched prior to entry. Non-clear bags may undergo additional searches.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

House of Blues Chicago offers a Southern-inspired menu with made-from-scratch dishes. Highlights include BBQ nachos, smoked buffalo wings, jambalaya, and a variety of salads and mains like blackened salmon and baby back ribs. The venue also features a selection of burgers and sandwiches.
