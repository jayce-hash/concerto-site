Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Each guest may bring one clear bag no larger than 12 by 12 by 6 inches or one small clutch no larger than 4.5 by 6.5 inches. Medical, family, and childcare bags are permitted after inspection.

Allowed

  • Bags within 13” x 15” x 6”

Not allowed

  • Over-sized bags/backpacks/duffel bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For most events, doors open 60 minutes prior to the start time of the event.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Iowa Events Center parking is available from entrances on 3rd Street and 5th Avenue. The facility is cashless; rates and advance-reservation availability can change by event.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Casey's Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Casey's Center, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Permanent and portable concession stands operate on the main and upper concourses. Casey's Center is cashless, with the limited cash-to-gift-card option described in the official venue policies.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Casey's Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For other events that fall on a Saturday, Sunday, or Monday, the box office will open at 12pm (or 2 hours before the event starts for morning shows). The Casey's Center Ticket Office located in the Southwest corner of Casey's Center.

Official source
