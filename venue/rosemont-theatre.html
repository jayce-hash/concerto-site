Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Fans attending events at Rosemont Theatre cannot bring backpacks, totes, mid/large sized bags, and coolers. Diaper bags with a child and medically necessary bags are allowed after a security search.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Wheelchair area is located behind sections 113 and 116 (depending on event set-up behind sections 105 and 108 may also be available). Please note that the number of seats available in the accessible seating area meets the requirements of the Americans With Disabilities Act (ADA) and other laws relating to accessibility, however, the number of seats in the accessible seating areas is limited.
