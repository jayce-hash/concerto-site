Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fans attending events at Rosemont Theatre cannot bring backpacks, totes, mid/large sized bags, and coolers. Diaper bags with a child and medically necessary bags are allowed after a security search.

Allowed

  • Diaper bags (with child)
  • Bags approved for medical needs

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Totes
  • Mid/large sized bags
  • Coolers

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Wheelchair area is located behind sections 113 and 116 (depending on event set-up behind sections 105 and 108 may also be available). Please note that the number of seats available in the accessible seating area meets the requirements of the Americans With Disabilities Act (ADA) and other laws relating to accessibility, however, the number of seats in the accessible seating areas is limited.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food and beverage service may be available inside Rosemont Theatre and varies by event. Outside food and beverages are prohibited unless required for a documented medical need.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Rosemont Theatre. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
