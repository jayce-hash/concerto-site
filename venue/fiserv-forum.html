Fiserv Forum

Know before you go

What matters at Fiserv Forum.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Fiserv Forum does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

The 5th Street Parking Structure is open 24/7 and offers ADA parking spaces and EV charging stations. Oversized vehicles have limited parking options, and monthly rates are available for long-term parking.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Fiserv Forum's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Fiserv Forum introduces new food and beverage options for the 2024-25 season, including an upgraded Jameson bar and unique menu items like the MKE Burger and Greek Burger. Fans can also enjoy new dessert offerings and a variety of tacos and nachos in the suites.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Fiserv Forum, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Guests who leave Fiserv Forum during an event after their ticket has been scanned are not allowed to re-enter without an unused/unscanned ticket.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Phillips Avenue Milwaukee, Wisconsin 53203 Ticketmaster Box Office #: (414) 227-0511 Hours: The Ticketmaster Box Office is open on event days. Fans may redeem gift cards for Milwaukee Bucks tickets and tickets to most concerts and shows at the Ticketmaster Box Office during regular business hours, Fiserv Forum concessions stands, Bucks Pro Shop at Fiserv Forum, retail locations during Milwaukee Bucks games, and online at shop.bucks.com.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Fiserv Forum. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.
