Paradise Rock Club

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

All bags are searched by security before entry; the venue does not publish a general numerical bag size limit.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Club doors usually open an hour before showtime, with the lounge often opening an hour earlier. Most shows are 18 and over unless the listing says otherwise.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Metered street parking is available on Commonwealth Avenue and nearby streets; the venue also recommends booking paid parking in advance.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The club directions do not identify a designated rideshare pickup site.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The club asks guests who need accommodations to call or email in advance. It is a standing-room venue with floor and wraparound balcony.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Paradise Lounge sells food and has a bar. On most concert nights it opens one hour before club doors.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Re-entry is allowed when the ticket taker has given the guest the appropriate entry stamp.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will Call is available only on show night beginning at door time. The box office operates Monday–Friday noon–6 p.m. and on weekend show nights.

Official source
