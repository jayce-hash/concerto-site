Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The FSU Athletics policy permits one clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches or a one-gallon clear resealable bag, plus a clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches. The published scope is FSU Athletics events; concert rules should be checked with the organizer.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear resealable bag
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Nonconforming bags
