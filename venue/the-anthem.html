Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Anthem prohibits most bags, including backpacks and large purses, allowing only small clutches or wallets smaller than 5” x 7” x 1”. Medical and diaper bags are permitted but subject to search.

Allowed

  • Small clutches and wallets smaller than 5” x 7” x 1”
  • Medical bags
  • Diaper bags

Not allowed

  • Bags
  • Backpacks
  • Large purses
  • Outside food and beverages
  • Weapons
  • Professional photographic gear
  • Laser pointers
  • Sound amplifiers

Parking information is available by clicking the provided link. The Anthem is accessible by various transportation methods including Metro, bike, and car. There is bike parking and a Capital Bikeshare station nearby.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For guidance on which seats may be most accessible (aisle seats, close to restrooms, etc.), please contact us before purchase. phone: 202-888-0020, or email ada@theanthemdc.com Accessible Entrances: Step-free access is available at our 900 Maine Ave entrance and our ADA entrance which is located between the venue’s main entrance and Whitlows Bar and Grill on Wharf St.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Anthem offers a variety of handhelds such as Argentine beef empanadas, chicken masala wraps, and vegan falafel wraps. Shareables include sidewinder fries and cheesy potato pupusas. Beverage options feature a selection of bottled and draft beers, cocktails, and non-alcoholic drinks. A sustainability fee is applied to each transaction.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in The Anthem's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.
