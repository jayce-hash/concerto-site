Bag Policy

Bag Policy

The Anthem prohibits most bags, including backpacks and large purses, allowing only small clutches or wallets smaller than 5” x 7” x 1”. Medical and diaper bags are permitted but subject to search.

Concessions

Concessions

The Anthem offers a variety of handhelds such as Argentine beef empanadas, chicken masala wraps, and vegan falafel wraps. Shareables include sidewinder fries and cheesy potato pupusas. Beverage options feature a selection of bottled and draft beers, cocktails, and non-alcoholic drinks. A sustainability fee is applied to each transaction.

Accessibility
