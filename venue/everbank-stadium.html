Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

EverBank Stadium enforces a Clear Bag Policy to ensure safety and expedite entry. Only specific clear bags and small clutches are allowed.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags not exceeding 12" x 6" x 12"
  • One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags
  • Small clutch bags not exceeding 4.5" x 6.5"

Not allowed

  • Bags larger than 12" x 6" x 12"
  • Cameras with detachable lenses
  • Coolers
  • Outside beverages
  • Drones
  • Weapons
  • Laser pointers
  • Laptops
  • Noise makers
  • Pets
  • Seat cushions
  • Large signs
  • Sticks
  • Strollers
  • Tobacco products
  • Umbrellas
  • Video equipment

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at EverBank Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking passes for EverBank Stadium are sold out for the 2025 season, but cash lots are available nearby. The GEICO Game Day Shuttle Program offers complimentary shuttle service for ticketed fans from select lots.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The rideshare pick-up location is on Beaver Street near Lot Z, with signage throughout the Sports Complex for guidance.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Please note that if you need a wheelchair for all movements throughout the stadium, it is advised that you bring a wheelchair for your personal use throughout the entire day and contact the Jaguars ticket office at (904) 633-2000 for accessible seating availability.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food and beverage availability at EverBank Stadium varies by event and operating setup. Review the linked official guest information before arrival.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests choosing to leave the perimeter gates after entering on game day will not be granted re-entry.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For children 0-23 months old, guests can contact the Jaguars Ticket Office at (904) 633-2000 regarding the steps to obtain a lap pass ticket in advance or visit the Game Day Ticket Office located between Gates 2 and 3.
