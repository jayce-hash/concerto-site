Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags must be smaller than 12 × 12 inches; they need not be clear and are searched. Backpacks and oversize bags are barred, and will call cannot store them.

Allowed

  • Bag smaller than 12 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags 12 × 12 inches or larger

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office opens at 2 p.m. on show days and is otherwise closed. The venue uses AXS Mobile ID for entry and does not require a printed ticket.
