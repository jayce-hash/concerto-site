Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Small bags and handbags up to 35 × 40 × 19 cm are permitted after search; backpacks and larger bags are barred. No cloakroom or bag storage is available.

Allowed

  • Small bag up to 35 × 40 × 19 cm

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Larger bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Entry uses screening arches and bag searches; check the event page for show-specific entry time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena has no dedicated public parking; Q-Park offers event discounts at several nearby city-centre garages.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena’s current travel and security pages do not designate a rideshare pickup location.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The arena publishes an accessible travel and seating guide; Woodhouse Lane car park has 14 Blue Badge spaces, with listed evening event parking at £7.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concessions and hospitality are available inside. Outside food and drinks are prohibited; request advance approval for medically necessary drinks.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

No readmission is allowed after leaving the arena; designated smoking areas are upstairs on either side of the concourse.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The safety and visitor FAQ pages explain AXS ticketing but do not publish a general concert Will Call collection procedure.

Official source
