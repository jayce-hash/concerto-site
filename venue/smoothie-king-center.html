Bag Policy

Bag Policy

The venue's general maximum bag size is 14 by 14 by 6 inches, though individual events may require clear bags or prohibit most bags. Backpacks and oversized bags are prohibited.

Parking

The Smoothie King Center offers parking in seven garages and two surface lots, accommodating approximately 7,000 vehicles. Parking garages accept credit or debit cards only.

Concessions

Concessions

Smoothie King Center offers a variety of food and beverage options including smoothies, Cajun dishes, ice cream, and classic concession stand items like hot dogs and popcorn. Beverage options feature a selection of cocktails, wines, and beers. Notably, only card transactions are accepted.

Accessibility

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Walk-up ticket sales for events at Caesars Superdome, Smoothie King Center, and Champions Square are available at the Caesars Superdome Box Office (Gate A Ground) on Fridays from 9 AM to 4:30 PM. The Smoothie King Center Box Office is located at Northeast Ground on the corner of Le Rouge Street and Dave Dixon Drive.
