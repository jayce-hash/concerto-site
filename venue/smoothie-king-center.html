Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The venue's general maximum bag size is 14 by 14 by 6 inches, though individual events may require clear bags or prohibit most bags. Backpacks and oversized bags are prohibited.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Smoothie King Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Smoothie King Center offers parking in seven garages and two surface lots, accommodating approximately 7,000 vehicles. Parking garages accept credit or debit cards only.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Smoothie King Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Sensory Bags are available at Guest Services booth located at Section 108. ADA Parking for Pelicans games is in Lot 3 of the Smoothie King Center.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Smoothie King Center offers a variety of food and beverage options including smoothies, Cajun dishes, ice cream, and classic concession stand items like hot dogs and popcorn. Beverage options feature a selection of cocktails, wines, and beers. Notably, only card transactions are accepted.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Smoothie King Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Walk-up ticket sales for events at Caesars Superdome, Smoothie King Center, and Champions Square are available at the Caesars Superdome Box Office (Gate A Ground) on Fridays from 9 AM to 4:30 PM. The Smoothie King Center Box Office is located at Northeast Ground on the corner of Le Rouge Street and Dave Dixon Drive.

Official source
