Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Fans attending events at Allstate Arena cannot enter with backpacks, totes, mid/large sized bags, and coolers. Diaper bags with a child and medical bags are allowed after a security search.

Parking

Parking

Parking is available close to all entrances of the Allstate Arena for a fee of $25, payable by cash or credit card. Parking opens two hours prior to event start time, and tailgating is not permitted.
