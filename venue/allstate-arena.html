Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fans attending events at Allstate Arena cannot enter with backpacks, totes, mid/large sized bags, and coolers. Diaper bags with a child and medical bags are allowed after a security search.

Allowed

  • Diaper bags (with child)
  • Medical bags (approved, no bigger than 12" x 12")

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Totes
  • Mid/large sized bags
  • Coolers

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Allstate Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available close to all entrances of the Allstate Arena for a fee of $25, payable by cash or credit card. Parking opens two hours prior to event start time, and tailgating is not permitted.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Allstate Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Allstate Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.
