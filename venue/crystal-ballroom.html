Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue lists nearby metered street spaces, the Brewery Blocks garage with entrances on 12th and 13th Avenues, and public lots near SW 13th Avenue and Washington Street.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue directions and parking page do not specify a rideshare pickup or drop-off area.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue’s official event information says an elevator is in the lobby; guests needing an accommodation may call the ballroom in advance to arrange early entry.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The ballroom has bar service; its venue event page describes available beverages and the attached McMenamins dining options. The site does not state a general outside-food admission policy for shows.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The ballroom’s official venue and event pages do not state a general re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Crystal Ballroom box office operates daily 11:30 a.m.–6 p.m. and later on performance nights. Tickets purchased directly at that box office incur a facility charge according to the venue event page.
