Ascend Amphitheater

Bag policy, entry, parking, rideshare, and accessibility at Ascend Amphitheater. From official sources, dated.

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC totes up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches and nonclear clutches up to 6 × 9 inches may enter after inspection. Medical and diaper bags are screened and tagged.

Allowed

  • Clear tote up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Clutch up to 6 × 9 inches
  • Screened medical or diaper bag

Not allowed

  • Other bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Gate 1 is beside the box office on the northwest corner, with another entrance at First Avenue South and Korean Veterans Boulevard. Gates generally open 60–90 minutes before the show, depending on event.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

This downtown amphitheater does not have its own parking lot. Use paid public garages or lots nearby.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

For taxi and rideshare pickup after the concert, go to Gate 2; regular passenger pickup and drop-off may use Gate 1 or Gate 2.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue has family and accessible restrooms throughout, offers an alternative to metal-detector screening for medical needs and permits screened medical bags.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drinks are sold on both sides of the seating bowl and behind the lawn. Outside food is prohibited; each guest may bring up to one gallon of water in a soft squeezable plastic bottle.

Allowed

  • Up to one gallon of water in a soft plastic bottle

Not allowed

  • Outside food
  • Outside alcohol
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests cannot re-enter after leaving without a new ticket.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office opens two hours before concert gates and admission uses AXS Mobile ID. Its published FAQ does not describe a separate will-call pickup procedure.

Official source
