Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches or nonclear clutches up to 6 × 9 inches are allowed after search; other bags are prohibited.

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Check the specific show listing for times. The main entrance is accessible and Fast Lane is available as a separately purchased upgrade.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

A garage is directly across from the venue on 29th Street; eligible event tickets may include parking validation until the garage fills.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue arrival guide and accessibility page do not designate a rideshare point; the accessibility page directs passenger drop-offs to the main entrance on Granada Avenue.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The main entrance on Granada Avenue and lobby restrooms are accessible; accessible floor seating is to the right of the stage. Request sign-language interpretation at least two weeks ahead.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The hall has a full bar and West Coast Tavern serves food. Outside food, drinks and alcohol are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Leaving and re-entering the venue is prohibited.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Pick up Will Call tickets at the box office beginning two hours before doors on show nights; bring photo ID and the purchasing card.

Official source
