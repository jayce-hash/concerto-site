Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Angel Stadium enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Parking

Parking

Angel Stadium offers thousands of on-site surface parking spaces in lots surrounding the ballpark, with entrances off State College Blvd, Orangewood Ave, and Douglass Rd. Prepaid parking is available through the Angels/Ticketmaster and is cheaper than paying at the gate; lots are cashless. Arrive early as lots fill before first pitch.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

The rideshare pick-up zone is located in the parking lot, directly in front of Gate 1, near the City National Grove of Anaheim building. Rideshare drop-offs should enter through the Orangewood entrance.

Concessions

Concessions

Angel Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, including a lively Beer Garden with local and popular beers. Highlights include Bridges Grille's Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and signature Garlic Fries, Cadillac Diner's Chili Cheese Hot Dog and Milkshakes, and Coop & Kennel Pub's Pastrami Fry and Crispy Fried Chicken Sub. The Golden Spike Chophouse serves hearty classics like Chicken Tenders & Fries.
