    Bag policy

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Angel Stadium enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

    Allowed

    • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) with no obscured interior pockets up to 12” x 6” x 12”
    • Plastic Storage Bag (Re-sealable, clear) 1 Gallon
    • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”
    • Seat cushions without pockets, zippers or concealable areas up to 18” wide

    Not allowed

    • Backpacks
    • Bags and wallets exceeding the size of a small clutch bag (6.5" W x 4.5" H)
    • Non-approved seat cushions
    • Luggage of any kind
    • Computer bags
    • Camera bags
    • Briefcases
    • Fanny packs
    • Cinch bags
    • Any bag larger than the permissible size (6.5" W x 4.5" H)
    Official source

    Entrances and doors

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Angel Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

    Official source

    Parking

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Angel Stadium offers thousands of on-site surface parking spaces in lots surrounding the ballpark, with entrances off State College Blvd, Orangewood Ave, and Douglass Rd. Prepaid parking is available through the Angels/Ticketmaster and is cheaper than paying at the gate; lots are cashless. Arrive early as lots fill before first pitch.

    Official source

    Rideshare

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    The rideshare pick-up zone is located in the parking lot, directly in front of Gate 1, near the City National Grove of Anaheim building. Rideshare drop-offs should enter through the Orangewood entrance.

    Official source

    Accessibility

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Angel Stadium, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

    Official source

    Food and drink

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Angel Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, including a lively Beer Garden with local and popular beers. Highlights include Bridges Grille's Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and signature Garlic Fries, Cadillac Diner's Chili Cheese Hot Dog and Milkshakes, and Coop & Kennel Pub's Pastrami Fry and Crispy Fried Chicken Sub. The Golden Spike Chophouse serves hearty classics like Chicken Tenders & Fries.

    Official source

    Re-entry

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Angel Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

    Official source

    Tickets and will call

    Checked Aug 20, 2026

    Since tickets retrieved within the MLB Ballpark app will now be considered printed, the tickets will not be available for pickup at Will Call.
