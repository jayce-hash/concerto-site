Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Backpacks are allowed but must be checked in to the cloakroom. Suitcases and holdalls are not allowed.

Allowed

  • Backpacks

Not allowed

  • Suitcases
  • Holdalls
  • Professional camera equipment
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Accor Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking at the Accor Arena requires booking in advance online, with a car park located at 85 Rue de Bercy for regular vehicles and another at 8 Boulevard de Bercy for reduced mobility. Vehicle height must be less than 1.90m.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Accor Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests with disabilities or reduced mobility can use Accor Arena's dedicated booking service with a disability card or medical certificate. Accessible parking is at 8 Boulevard de Bercy and should be arranged in advance through the venue.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accor Arena offers a variety of food and drink options, including juicy burgers, New York-style hot dogs, and Italian pizza. Vegetarian and vegan options are available at all stands, and visitors can enjoy drinks at the Moët & Chandon, Heineken, and Coca-Cola bars.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Accor Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Ticket collection at the box office of the venue is not possible.

Official source
