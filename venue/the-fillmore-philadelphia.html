Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches are allowed after a search. Check the show page for any event-specific changes.

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Guests pass security and bag screening; the event listing gives the door time. The box office operates on show days only.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Several venue lots serve concert guests; prepaid parking guarantees a space, while other parking is first come first served. Lots open two hours before doors; use 1025 N. Front Street or 98 Richmond Street for navigation.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concert tickets use mobile entry rather than emailed or printable tickets. The show-day box office opens two hours before doors to assist guests with ticket problems.
