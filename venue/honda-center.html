Know before you go

What matters at Honda Center.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

At Honda Center, bags for Ducks games must be no larger than 4 in. x 6 in. x 1.5 in., while for non-Ducks events, bags can be up to 5 in. x 9 in. x 2 in. Backpacks of any size are not allowed.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

General parking at onsite lots and garages is included with event tickets, with no separate parking pass required. Accessible parking is available on a first-come, first-parked basis. Guests are encouraged to arrive early and use alternative transportation options.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

Rideshare and taxi drop off and pick up are available at ARTIC at 2626 East Katella Avenue, Anaheim 92806.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Doors

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Honda Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Please call the Honda Center box office at 714-704-2500 to request relocation or if you have specific questions regarding wheelchair-accessible and companion seating. Honda Center provides wheelchair-accessible and companion seating on each level of the arena, including the floor when the event offers floor seats.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Honda Center offers a variety of food and drink options across different levels, including personal pizzas, wings, burgers, tacos, and specialty desserts. Unique offerings include a dedicated wine cellar with over 270 bottles and self-pour beer selections. Concession availability may vary due to ongoing renovations.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

In an effort to continue the highest level of safety and security, Honda Center enforces a “no re-entry” policy for all ticketed events.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Will Call is located at the box office and opens one and a half hours prior to the event start time.
