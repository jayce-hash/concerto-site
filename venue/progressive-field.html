Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Progressive Field enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Parking

Parking

Progressive Field is in downtown Cleveland's Gateway District, served by the Gateway East Garage and many nearby lots and garages within walking distance. Prepaid parking through the team or SpotHero is cheaper than drive-up. RTA Rapid Transit stops at Tower City a short walk away.
