Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Progressive Field enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Allowed

  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) with no obscured interior pockets up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag (Re-sealable, clear) 1 Gallon
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”
  • Seat cushions without pockets, zippers or concealable areas up to 18” wide

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags and wallets exceeding the size of a small clutch bag (6.5" W x 4.5" H)
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage of any kind
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags
  • Briefcases
  • Fanny packs
  • Cinch bags
  • Any bag larger than the permissible size (6.5" W x 4.5" H)

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Progressive Field is in downtown Cleveland's Gateway District, served by the Gateway East Garage and many nearby lots and garages within walking distance. Prepaid parking through the team or SpotHero is cheaper than drive-up. RTA Rapid Transit stops at Tower City a short walk away.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Progressive Field's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fans can also rent out sensory bags and headphones at the Fan Services booths located behind section 153 and 550.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Progressive Field offers a variety of food and beverage options, including classic comfort foods with modern twists and indulgent ballpark classics. Highlights include specialty items like Bacon Wrapped Hot Dogs, Pastrami Fries, and a selection of local and popular beers.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Progressive Field's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Progressive Field. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
