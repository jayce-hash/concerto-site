Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Mercedes-Benz Stadium allows clear bags made of plastic, vinyl, or PVC up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small non-clear bags up to 4.5” x 6.5”. All other bags are prohibited.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Small non-clear bags up to 4.5” x 6.5”

Not allowed

  • Bags that do not meet the specified guidelines

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Mercedes-Benz Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available in the Red Deck and Silver Deck of the Georgia World Congress Center, subject to availability. MARTA offers a convenient and affordable option at $2.50 each way, with stops near the stadium.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Mercedes-Benz Stadium's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Mercedes-Benz Stadium, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Mercedes-Benz Stadium offers a variety of food and drink options, including vegetarian and gluten-avoiding choices. Highlights include a Vegan Smokehouse Sausage and Quinoa Bowl. Beverage options range from sodas to premium draft beers.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Mercedes-Benz Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Mercedes-Benz Stadium. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
