Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Delta Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking at the Delta Center offers a variety of options, including both covered and uncovered stalls. Prices and availability vary, with some lots offering game-by-game passes and others providing free parking for a limited time. TRAX is also available for free within the downtown zone, but stops running at midnight.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Delta Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Sensory Bags can be checked out at Guest Services located on the Main Concourse across from Portal Y/Door 4 free of charge.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Delta Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Delta Center. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
