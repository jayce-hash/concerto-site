Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

All bags are searched. Small bags are permitted, but bags over 18 × 18 inches are prohibited and cannot be stored.

Allowed

  • Small bag up to 18 × 18 inches

Not allowed

  • Bags larger than 18 × 18 inches
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Each event page gives a separate door and show start time. The box office at 1026 Spring Garden Street opens at 5 p.m. on show nights, and all bags are searched at entry.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The staffed lot behind the building between 10th and 11th Streets and Nectarine and Buttonwood Streets opens at 6 p.m. and costs $20. Parking purchases are card only.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ does not specify a rideshare pickup or drop-off zone.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The main floor, lobby bars and restrooms are accessible. Email the venue before the show to arrange special seating, as there is no fixed disabled seating section.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Lobby bars accept cash and card; outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and beverages
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Once admitted, guests cannot leave and return. A designated outdoor smoking area is available for guests age 21 or older.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call tickets can be picked up on show day beginning two hours before doors, typically 5 p.m., by the named purchaser with photo ID; will-call orders cannot be transferred.

Official source
