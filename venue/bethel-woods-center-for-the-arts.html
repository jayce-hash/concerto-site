Bethel Woods Center for the Arts

Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bethel Woods Center for the Arts does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Member Gate opens approximately 30 minutes before General Gates. The main concert General Gates open approximately 90 minutes prior to scheduled show time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at Bethel Woods Center for the Arts with alternative exits to help manage traffic flow. Follow signs for Lots D and C for specific exit routes.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Bethel Woods Center for the Arts's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Bethel Woods Center for the Arts, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bethel Woods Center for the Arts offers a diverse range of food and drink options, including vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free choices. Highlights include Yasgur's Farm Café for salads and wraps, Market Sheds for Italian fun food, and various stands offering everything from BBQ to tacos and cupcakes.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Bethel Woods Center for the Arts's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Tickets can be picked up at the Will Call Table outside the Box Office on Hurd Road. These chairs are available for rental at the Box Office or for purchase on our website in advance, or directly at the Lawn Chair Tent located at the top of the lawn in Festival Plaza (ask any staff member to guide you).

Official source
