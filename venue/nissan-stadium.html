Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Nissan Stadium enforces a Clear Bag Policy, allowing only specific sizes and types of bags. Binocular cases must adhere to size restrictions.

Allowed

  • Clear bags
  • Binocular cases no larger than 4.5” x 6.5”
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Nissan Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Nissan Stadium offers several parking options with lots labeled from A to T. It's recommended to use the Waze app for real-time directions and traffic updates to your designated parking lot. Pricing and availability may vary, so plan to arrive early on event days.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Nissan Stadium's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Scroll down to “Accessibility” and select “Wheelchair” to see available Accessible Seating Locations. 100 Level Accessible Seating Sections: 102, 103, 104, 106, 108, 109,110, 114, 115, 118, 120, 121, 125, 127, 132, 133, 134, 138

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Nissan Stadium offers a wide variety of food and beverage options including burgers, chicken tenders, hot dogs, and local specialties like Hattie B’s Hot Chicken. Beverage options include Coke products, alcoholic beverages, and specialty cocktails. Unique offerings include the Burger of the Game featuring local Nashville restaurants and desserts like Dippin' Dots and Ben & Jerry’s ice cream.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Nissan Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Nissan Stadium. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
