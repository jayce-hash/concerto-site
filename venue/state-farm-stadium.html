Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

State Farm Stadium enforces a Clear Bag Policy allowing only clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags not exceeding 12" x 6" x 12". Bags larger than 4.5" x 6.5" that are not completely clear are prohibited.

Allowed

  • clear plastic bags not exceeding 12" x 6" x 12"
  • clear backpacks
  • clear fanny packs
  • small non-clear bags within 4.5" x 6.5"

Not allowed

  • tinted bags
  • bags larger than 4.5" x 6.5" that are not completely clear
  • coolers
  • briefcases
  • non-clear bags
  • luggage
  • any bag exceeding permissible size

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at State Farm Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available through pre-sold permits and single-game options, with general and preferred parking areas. The venue is cashless, requiring credit card payments.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Preferred drop-off and pickup spot is the Black Lot located south of State Farm Stadium.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by State Farm Stadium, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

State Farm Stadium offers a variety of beer options including domestic and premium selections, as well as champagne and alcohol-free choices. Highlights include Veuve Clicquot champagne carts and a selection of draft beers from popular breweries. The venue features numerous bars and portable stands for convenient access to beverages.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Once ticketed guests exit the premises, re-entry with a previously scanned ticket is not permitted.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at State Farm Stadium. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
