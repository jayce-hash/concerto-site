Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Ford Field follows the NFL's bag policy to ensure safety and quick entry. Fans should avoid bringing bags, but clear bags and small clutches are allowed.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags not exceeding 12” x 6” x 12”
  • One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags
  • Small clutch bags approximately the size of a hand

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Ford Field, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Ford Field offers a diverse range of food and drink options, including local favorites like Detroit Spirits and Great Lakes Burger Bar. Special offerings include club-level exclusives such as Slows Bar BQ and the Detroit Bloody Mary Bar. Fans can enjoy a variety of cuisines from burgers and pretzels to Greek salads and cheesecake.
