Bag policy

Checked Oct 11, 2026

At UNC athletics events, clear plastic or vinyl bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" and small clutches up to 4.5" x 6.5" are allowed.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic or vinyl bags up to 12" x 6" x 12"
  • Gallon clear freezer bags
  • Small clutch purses up to 4.5" x 6.5"

Not allowed

  • Purses larger than a clutch
  • Backpacks
  • Diaper bags
  • Fanny packs
  • Briefcases
  • Coolers
  • Luggage of any kind
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags
