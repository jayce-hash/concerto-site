Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

No backpacks or large bags over 11”x17” are allowed. Diaper and medical bags are permitted.

Allowed

  • Diaper bags
  • Medical bags

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Large bags over 11”x17”
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Enter from the 2nd Street side of the venue and allow at least 30 extra minutes for wanding and bag checks. Door times are listed for each event.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There is no official parking garage affiliated with ACL Live, but nearby options are available. Limited valet parking is offered at the W Hotel for ticketholders on a first-come, first-served basis. Metered parking starts at $2 per hour with varying hours.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Let Lyft be your ride to the action! Use code ACLLIVELYFT for 50% off your first 2 rides (up to $10/ride).

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accessible seating and guest accommodations are available. Contact ACL Live at 512-404-1300 or info@acllive.com before the event for seating, mobility, hearing, or other access needs.

Official source

Food and drink

The FAQ page does not provide specific details about food and drink options or any special offerings at ACL Live at The Moody Theater.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Re-entry is generally allowed when guests present their ticket again and repeat security screening. Events that prohibit re-entry are identified at the entrance.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The event-day box office is on 2nd Street between Lavaca and Guadalupe, next to the ACL Live retail store. It opens one hour before the scheduled door time; photo ID is required for will-call pickup.
