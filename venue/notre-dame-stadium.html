Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Luke Combs concert guidance allowed clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches, one-gallon clear zip bags and clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches. Medical equipment was subject to extra screening; all bags were inspected.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear zip bag
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches
  • Approved medical equipment

Not allowed

  • Bags outside clear-bag policy

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Luke Combs concert page states that bags are inspected on entry but does not provide a universal stadium concert gate-opening time.

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The university’s Luke Combs concert page advises buying a parking pass before arrival and provides a shuttle map from the concert lots. Individual lots and prices are event-specific.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Notre Dame Stadium concert guidance does not specify a rideshare pickup or drop-off point for the Luke Combs show.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Luke Combs concert page permits medical bags and equipment after additional screening and pumps for nursing mothers. The stadium football guide lists upper-level elevators near Sections 10/11 and 29/30.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Notre Dame Stadium is cashless, according to its Luke Combs concert page. The venue’s guide says stadium concessions accept cards and mobile payment, and outside food and drinks are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drink
