Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Oversized purses and backpacks over 14 × 14 × 6 inches are prohibited, along with luggage and duffle bags.

Not allowed

  • Bags over 14 × 14 × 6 inches
  • Luggage
  • Duffle bags

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Will-call tickets can be picked up at the box-office window starting two hours before the show; bring the purchasing card and valid photo ID. The venue will not accept third-party will-call drop-offs.
