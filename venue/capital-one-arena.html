Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Capital One Arena enforces a strict no bag policy. Only small clutches, medical bags, and parenting bags are allowed.

Allowed

  • Wallet-size clutches no larger than 5” x 7”
  • Medical bags no larger than 14” x 14” x 6”
  • Parenting bags no larger than 14” x 14” x 6”

Not allowed

  • Bags
  • Backpacks
  • Purses

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Capital One Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at the Arena Garage and through SpotHero, which offers guaranteed parking at nearby lots and garages. Parking passes can be printed at home or redeemed by mobile.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Capital One Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Capital One Arena, in partnership with MedStar Health, has recently installed two Mamava Lactation pods which are located at the F-Street elevator landing across from sections 200 and 400, with our ADA accessible pod stationed on the 200 level.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Capital One Arena offers a variety of food and drink options with a focus on providing a family-friendly environment. Alcohol sales are regulated with specific cut-off times during events, and the venue is cashless with reverse ATMs available for convenience.

Official source
