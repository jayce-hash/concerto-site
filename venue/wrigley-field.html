Know before you go

What matters at Wrigley Field.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Wrigley Field enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Only specific types and sizes of bags are permitted.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Wrigley Field sits in a dense residential neighborhood with very limited parking; most streets are permit-only. The Cubs offer a prepaid remote lot at Gallagher Way/DeVry with a shuttle, plus scattered private lots and garages that fill fast. The CTA Red Line Addison stop is the easiest way in.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

Rideshare services will have a geo-fence for pick-up and drop-off spots around Wrigley Field for game and event days. The shuttle drop-off and pickup point is on Irving Park Road between Clark Street and Seminary Avenue.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Wrigley Field offers a variety of food and beverage options including classic comfort foods with modern twists, such as Chicken Tenders & Fries, Cheeseburgers, and specialty hot dogs. Highlights include the Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry. Fans can also enjoy a selection of local and popular beers at the Beer Garden.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Accessible seating and parking for guests with mobility disabilities can be purchased at the Wrigley Field Ticket Office, by calling 800-THE-CUBS, by emailing fanservices@cubs.com or by visiting Cubs.com/Tickets.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

During games, guests may exit the ballpark to access Gallagher Way only through the Gallagher Way Gate after the conclusion of the first inning with re-entry closing at the bottom of the eighth inning.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Wrigley Field Ticket Office located on Clark Street, just north of the Wrigley Field Marquee, will be open Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Saturday and Sunday from 9 a.m. until 4 p.m. with extended hours for evening home games.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Wrigley Field. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.
