Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags will be searched upon entry, and large or oversized bags/backpacks are not permitted. Express entry lanes are available for those without bags.

Not allowed

  • large or oversized bags/back-packs

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Broadview Stage at SPAC. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

There is limited metered street parking near the venue. Commercial lots are available on 5th and Bainbridge Streets, which accept cash only. ParkWhiz offers additional parking options.

Official source

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Broadview Stage at SPAC, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The “Will Call” window is located off the Route 50 parking lot at the Main Box Office ONLY. Broadview Stage at SPAC suggest you arrive at Will Call 1-2 hours prior to the show to avoid long lines.
