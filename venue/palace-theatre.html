Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Small bags may be brought into the theater; large bags and backpacks must be checked for $5 per item. Bag check is mandatory for larger bags.

Allowed

  • Small bags

Not allowed

  • Large bags on the concert floor
  • Backpacks on the concert floor
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

A valid photo ID is required for entry; door time is specific to the concert. Large bags and backpacks must be checked for $5 per item.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Paid street meters, lots and ramps surround the Palace Theatre in St. Paul. The nearby Victory and Lawson Commons ramps typically cost $8–$15 depending on parking time.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue FAQ does not designate a Palace Theatre rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Buy ADA tickets on the accessible-seating tile; one guest requiring accessible seating can purchase up to three companion tickets. The Palace floor is generally standing room only, and the first balcony level has elevator access.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Palace Pub next door sells food and drinks and opens at 3 p.m. Tuesday–Saturday, plus event days on Sunday and Monday.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Palace Theatre section of the operator FAQ does not state a general re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bring valid photo ID to claim will-call tickets. The adjacent Palace Pub also sells event tickets at its front desk.

Official source
