Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The NFL Clear Bag Policy is enforced at M&T Bank Stadium to enhance safety and expedite entry. Only specific clear bags and small clutches are allowed.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags not exceeding 12" x 6" x 12"
  • One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags
  • Small clutch bags/belt bags not exceeding 4.5" x 6.5"

Not allowed

  • Purses larger than a clutch bag
  • Coolers
  • Briefcases
  • Backpacks
  • Cinch bags
  • Luggage
  • Seat cushions with a back, pocket, or zipper
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking for Ravens games is sold out to permit holders, with non-disabled parking typically sold in advance. Disabled parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis in Lots B, C & R. Consider using public transportation or downtown parking options.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

M&T Bank Stadium Box Office Hours of Operation: Mon-Fri 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. (available for phone calls on Saturday prior to Sunday home games from 10:00 a.m. - 2:00 p.m.) On Gamedays when the team is at home, the main ticket office is located by SeatGeek Gate F.
