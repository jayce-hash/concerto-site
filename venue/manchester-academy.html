    Bag policy

    Checked Oct 11, 2026

    Bags larger than 40cm x 40cm x 20cm are not allowed into Manchester Academy. All bags are searched at entry.

    Allowed

    • Bags up to 40cm x 40cm x 20cm

    Not allowed

    • Bags and containers larger than 40cm x 40cm x 20cm
    • Food and drink (unless medically required)
    • Liquids, gels and perfumes over 100ml
    • Alcohol, glass and metal cans
    • Laser pointers
    • Professional cameras
    • Laptops and tablets
    • Large umbrellas
    • Chairs, stools and strollers
