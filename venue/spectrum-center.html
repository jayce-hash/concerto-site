Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Spectrum Center has introduced a new bag policy allowing only small clutches, wallets, and wristlets not exceeding 4in x 6in x 1.5in for all arena events and Hornets games.

Allowed

  • small clutches
  • wallets
  • wristlets not exceeding 4in x 6in x 1.5in
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For ticket holders who enter through the Bank of America 5^{th} Street Executive Entrance, there are elevators to the Event Level, Founders Level and Suite Level. Media members should enter through the Rick Bonnell Media & Employee Entrance, located on the 5^{th} Street side of Spectrum Center.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Nearly 30,000 off-street parking spaces are available within a 15-minute walk of Spectrum Center. Guests can use SpotHero to find and reserve parking at various lots around the venue, with options for tailgating and oversized vehicles.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Spectrum Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Sensory kits are available for check out at the Guest Experience booths located outside sections 102 and 208. To purchase wheelchair-accessible and companion seating, please call (704) 688-8128.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Hugo’s Hive Menu offers fan-favorite concessions at $2 each, including hot dogs, pretzel bites, popcorn, bottled water, and soda. This initiative aims to provide affordable food options during Hornets games.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Re-entry to Spectrum Center is not permitted during any event.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For select non-Hornets events, an artist will call may be available at the Box Office. On non-event days, the Box Office is open every Friday from 10 a.m. – 2 p.m. for walk up sales.

Official source
