Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Only nonclear clutches or belt bags up to 6 × 9 inches and clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches are allowed; backpacks and totes are barred.

Allowed

  • Clutch up to 6 × 9 inches
  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Tote bags
  • Large purses
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Present a mobile ticket and allow time for security screening and bag searches at the main gates.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

There is no general public parking on site on concert nights; advance-purchased accessible parking is the on-site exception.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Use the designated pickup, drop-off, rideshare and taxi area a short walk east of the main gates.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible seating is available at the back of listed 300 and 400 sections, with an accessible entrance near the box office. Arrange wheelchair escort or parking with the accessibility coordinator.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concert entry is mobile only: save the mobile ticket to your wallet before reaching the gates; print-at-home tickets are unavailable.
