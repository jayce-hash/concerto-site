Bag Policy

Bag Policy

GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium follows the NFL's clear bag policy to ensure safety and speed up entry. Fans can bring clear bags up to 12x6x12 inches or small clutches no larger than 4.5x6.5 inches.

Parking

Parking

Parking at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium is managed with a counter-clockwise traffic flow. The parking lot is determined by the tollgate entered and the type of parking pass held. Parking staff will direct traffic based on availability.

Rideshare

Accessibility
