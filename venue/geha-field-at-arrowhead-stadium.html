Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium follows the NFL's clear bag policy to ensure safety and speed up entry. Fans can bring clear bags up to 12x6x12 inches or small clutches no larger than 4.5x6.5 inches.

Allowed

  • 12x6x12 clear plastic bag
  • 1-Gallon Plastic Freezer Bag
  • Clear Backpacks no larger than 12x6x12
  • Non-clear bags no larger than 4.5" x 6.5"

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Binoculars Case
  • Camera Case
  • Printed Pattern Plastic Bag
  • Fanny Packs or Belt Bags larger than 4.5" x 6.5"
  • Tinted Plastic Bag
  • Seat Cushion
  • Oversized Tote Bag
  • Diaper Bag
  • Mesh Bag
  • Purse

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Chiefs Games: GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium gates open 2 hours prior to kickoff.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium is managed with a counter-clockwise traffic flow. The parking lot is determined by the tollgate entered and the type of parking pass held. Parking staff will direct traffic based on availability.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests and ride-sharing services such as zTrip, Uber, and Lyft may drop-off and pick-up at the Missouri Welcome Center located between Gates 1 and 2 on Blue Ridge Cutoff.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accessible/wheelchair seating is located on the Field, Community America Club and Ford Fan Zone Levels. There are men's and women's restrooms located on each level of GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium; each is an ADA accessible facility.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium offers a variety of premium seating options with all-inclusive food and beverage services. Highlights include the Kingdom Club and Bud Light Locker Room Club, which provide specialty drinks and access to exclusive areas. Tailgate Suites and Champions Tailgate offer catered buffets and tailgate fare.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
