Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Olympiastadion does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Olympiastadion. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is accessible via Flatowallee for those coming from different directions. Specific parking options and pricing details are not provided on the website.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Olympiastadion's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Olympiastadion, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Aramark Restaurations GmbH provides catering services at the Olympiastadion in Berlin, offering a unique and creative culinary experience for various events. They cater to concerts, sports events, and private occasions, ensuring a diverse range of food and drink options for up to 75,000 spectators.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Olympiastadion's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Olympiastadion. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.

Official source
