Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Alamodome enforces a clear bag policy to enhance public safety, allowing only specific sizes and types of bags. Exceptions are made for diaper bags and medically necessary bags.

Allowed

  • Small bags and/or clutches - 5.5”x 8.5” or smaller
  • Clear Fanny Pack - 12"x 6"x12"
  • Clear Backpack 12"x 6"x12"
  • Clear Plastic Bags - 12”x6”x12”
  • Clutch Purse - 3"x4"
  • 1-Gallon Clear Plastic Storage Bag

Not allowed

  • Backpack (opaque)
  • Purse
  • Camera Bag/Case
  • Fanny Pack
  • Seat Cushion
  • Printed pattern Bag
  • Binoculars Case
  • Tinted or Colored Plastic Bag
  • Plastic Shopping Bag
  • Over-sized Tote Bag
  • Folding Lawn Chair Bag
  • Any non-clear bag larger than 5.5” x 8.5”

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Doors open one (1) hour prior to the start time of an event. Wheelchair escorts are provided through Guest Services located at the main entrances for every event.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Alamodome offers several parking lots for event attendees. Pricing may vary depending on the event, and it's recommended to arrive early to secure a spot. Consider using public transportation or rideshare services to avoid parking congestion.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Alamodome's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Alamodome provides accessible seating, elevators, restrooms, and parking accommodations. Guests needing event-specific assistance should contact the facility at 210-207-3663 before arrival.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Alamodome offers a dining experience inspired by the authenticity of San Antonio's people and foods, with a focus on quality and sustainability. Legends Global provides food services, emphasizing fresh ingredients from local businesses and environmentally friendly practices.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Alamodome does not permit re-entry on the same ticket after a guest exits.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Day-of-event will call is at the northeast box-office windows. The southwest box office is normally open Monday–Friday from 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.; online purchases are handled through Ticketmaster.

Official source
