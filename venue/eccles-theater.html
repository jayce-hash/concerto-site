Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Eccles Theater prohibits bags and backpacks over 12 × 12 × 6 inches; there are no lockers or bag check for oversize bags. Medical equipment and diaper bags are exceptions.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 12 × 6 inches
  • Medical-equipment exception
  • Diaper bag

Not allowed

  • Larger bags and backpacks

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Paid downtown garages and metered street spaces serve the theater; the City Center TRAX station is nearby. Weekday street meters operate 8 a.m.–8 p.m. with a two-hour limit.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The county visitor guide covers transit and parking but does not designate a rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The theater offers wheelchair seating, curbside assistance and free assisted-listening devices at the lower-lobby coat check; contact ArtTix for accommodation requests.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Concessions open before shows and during intermission, offering soft drinks, candy, baked goods and beer. Eccles allows concession-purchased food in the auditorium, and water is allowed.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The county venue and visitor pages do not state a general re-entry rule for Eccles Theater performances.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Salt Lake County visitor page names ArtTix for tickets but does not specify a Will Call pickup method.
