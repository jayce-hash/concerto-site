Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Fenway Park has a clear bag policy to ensure guest safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutches no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Parking

Parking

Fenway Park has no large stadium lot; parking is in private garages and surface lots around the Fenway and Kenmore area, typically $30-50 and pricier for big games. Prepaying through SpotHero or similar is strongly recommended. Many fans take the MBTA Green Line to Kenmore or Fenway instead.

Concessions

Concessions

Fenway Park offers a variety of food and drink options including classic comfort foods with bold flavors and modern twists. Highlights include crispy Chicken Tenders & Fries, footlong hot dogs, and specialty items like Bacon Wrapped Hot Dogs and Pastrami Fry. Fans can also enjoy refreshing brews at the Beer Garden.
