Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags may not exceed 12 × 6 × 12 inches and are searched. Medical-device bags may exceed that size; request other medical exceptions at least 48 hours before the event.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Screened medical-device bag

Not allowed

  • Oversized nonmedical bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The box office is outside the Box Office Gate next to west-side Lot J. Check the event Know Before You Go instructions for current gate access during construction.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Venue parking is for ticket holders; accessible parking is in Lot J on the west side. Construction may alter lots, so check the show’s Know Before You Go instructions.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The designated passenger drop-off and pickup area is south of the Box Office Gate via Lot J on the west side; tell the parking attendant when dropping off.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible parking is in Lot J; wheelchair escorts are available at all gates. Call the box office about accessible seating and request a sign language interpreter at least three weeks in advance.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bars and concessions operate north of the bowl and on the concourse. Guests may bring unwrapped food in a clear one-gallon bag up to 11 × 11 inches and one sealed plastic water bottle of 16.9 ounces or less.

Allowed

  • Unwrapped food in one clear one-gallon bag
  • Sealed plastic water bottle up to 16.9 ounces

Not allowed

  • Other outside drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue has a strict no-re-entry policy.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The west-side Dream Finders Homes Box Office opens three hours before scheduled start; named will-call patrons need government-issued photo ID. It closes an hour after scheduled start.

Official source
