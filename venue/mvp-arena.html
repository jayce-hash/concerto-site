Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

MVP Arena allows clear bags no larger than 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutch bags no larger than 4.5” x 6.5”. Larger bags are prohibited and there is no storage for them.

Allowed

  • CLEAR plastic, vinyl or PVC bags no larger than 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Small clutch bags no larger than 4.5” x 6.5”

Not allowed

  • Professional cameras
  • Video/Audio recorders
  • Weapons of any kind
  • Backpacks
  • Luggage
  • Coolers
  • Parcels
  • Briefcases
  • Outside food and beverages
  • Glass items
  • Lasers and glow sticks
  • Fireworks
  • Laser pens
  • Banners on poles
  • Umbrellas
  • Selfie sticks
  • Tablets/laptops

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at MVP Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

MVP Arena offers a parking garage located behind and attached to the venue, accessible via Market St., with rates starting at $8 daily. Parking is first come, first served, and availability is not guaranteed. Street parking is typically available on nights and weekends.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in MVP Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Accessible seating is available by request through the MVP Arena Box Office during normal business hours (M-F 10am-4pm) by calling (866) 308-3394.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

MVP Arena is a cashless facility where concessions accept cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in MVP Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source
