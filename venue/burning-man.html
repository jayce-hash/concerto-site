Know before you go

What matters at Burning Man.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Burning Man is not a conventional gated concert venue and does not publish a standard concert-style bag policy. Participants must follow Black Rock City prohibited-item, vehicle, and survival guidance.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

Black Rock City vehicle access requires event credentials and adherence to the current arrival, departure, Gate, and vehicle-pass rules. Do not navigate to the event using an ordinary map pin.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Burning Man's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Black Rock City does not operate normal public concession stands. Participants are expected to bring sufficient food, water, and supplies and follow the current survival guide; Center Camp offerings, if any, are separately announced.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Burning Man, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Burning Man's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Box Office at the front gate opens at 9am Pacific Time Monday, August 24, 2026, and is open 24/7 through 12pm (noon) Pacific Time Saturday, September 5, 2026. Will Call means your tickets are held for pick up at the Box Office of the event.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

The Gate opens in early August for pre-event traffic (only people with early entry passes are allowed in prior to the first day of the event), then opens for all participants at 12:01 am on the Sunday prior to the Man Burn, and is open 24 hours a day throughout the event.
