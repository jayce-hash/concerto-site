Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fishers Event Center enforces a clear bag policy where bags must be clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC and not exceed 12” x 6” x 12”. Non-clear bags smaller than 4.5” x 6.5” are permitted.

Allowed

  • Clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Non-clear bags smaller than 4.5” x 6.5”
  • Clear diaper bags with a child

Not allowed

  • Tinted bags
  • Bags with oversized content preventing visibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking information is not explicitly detailed on the page. Visitors are encouraged to use the Waze app for real-time traffic updates and directions. Consider arriving early to find parking and comply with the clear bag policy for quicker entry.

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Fishers Event Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Fishers Event Center’s Sensory Room, located in the Guest Services Center near Section 109, is available for guests with sensory sensitivities. For assistance or directions to the Sensory Room on event day, please check in at the Guest Services Center at Section 109.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fishers Event Center offers a diverse range of food and beverage options, including gluten-free, vegetarian, and vegan choices. Highlights include locally sourced items from Indiana vendors and eco-friendly initiatives. Outside food is generally not allowed, with some exceptions.
