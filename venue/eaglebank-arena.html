Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

EagleBank Arena has a clear bag policy to enhance the contactless experience. Bags must be clear and not exceed 12”x12”x6”, with exceptions for small clutches and medical or parenting bags.

Allowed

  • Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12”x12”x6”
  • 1 gallon plastic zip lock style bags
  • Non-clear clutch style bags or purses up to 5”x7”

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at EagleBank Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking areas surrounding EagleBank Arena are available for event parking three hours prior to the event start time. A parking fee is included in the ticket price, and there is ample parking on the campus. Accessible parking is available for patrons with disabilities adjacent to the South Entry.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in EagleBank Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by EagleBank Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

EagleBank Arena offers a variety of food options including specialty burgers, chicken tenders, BBQ, and plant-based items like the BEYOND Burger and Sausage. Concessions are staffed by volunteers, and payments can be made with cash, credit cards, or Mason Money Loyalty Cards.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in EagleBank Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For George Mason Basketball games, the pass gate or press entrance for the media is located at the West entrance, to the left of the box office when you are facing the building. Check the Box Office page for current box office hours.

Official source
