Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue operator says bags are searched at entry and lists bag restrictions in its New York club FAQ. The page mixes answers for several regions, so confirm the specific show’s allowed size before bringing a bag.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The operator publishes door times on individual event pages rather than a fixed opening time for all shows.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The operator says its New York clubs have no designated parking and suggests neighborhood street parking or public transportation.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue operator does not designate a rideshare pickup point in its guest FAQ.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue operator asks guests seeking accessible accommodation to alert security on arrival; event pages provide individual door times.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The operator says its clubs have a full bar serving water, soft drinks, beer, wine and spirits; outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The operator says there is no re-entry to its clubs; designated smoking areas are an exception.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The operator directs will-call questions for its clubs to AXS and says its box offices sell available tickets on show nights.
