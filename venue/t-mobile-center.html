Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Backpacks are not allowed in the facility. All bags will be checked upon entry.

Not allowed

  • backpacks
  • pocket knives
  • weapons of any kind
  • laser pointers
  • outside food or beverage
  • coolers
  • tablet devices larger than 7 inches
  • spurs
  • artificial noisemakers
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at T-Mobile Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The T-Mobile Center offers convenient on-site parking in the Magenta Lot, available for advance purchase on a first-come, first-served basis. Additional parking is available at the Red Lot Parking Garage and the Yellow Lot Parking Garage. Parking links for concerts can be found on individual event pages.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Oak Street is open for passenger drop-off, including taxi or ride-share drop-off. It is also the place for ADA drop-off.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by T-Mobile Center, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

T-Mobile Center sells a range of food and beverages across arena concessions. Outside food and beverages are prohibited, and the venue operates cash-free points of sale.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in T-Mobile Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Box Office at T-Mobile Center opens 3 hours prior to show time. *Please note on some events that begin prior to 12pm, the Box Office may have modified hours.

Official source
