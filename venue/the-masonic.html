Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches are allowed after inspection; nonclear bags may require extra screening. Oversized bags are refused.

Allowed

  • Bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Oversized bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Doors are normally an hour before showtime but may change. Security uses metal detectors or pat-downs and searches bags.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue’s garage entrance is next to the hall on California Street and operates 24 hours a day; advance purchase is recommended because spaces are limited.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue lists rideshare as an arrival option but does not publish a designated pickup or drop-off position.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue publishes a separate accessibility page covering arrival and on-site assistance; check its guidance or contact the venue for the specific concert.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The venue has a bar and food service; outside food and drinks are prohibited.

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Re-entry is prohibited; a guest who leaves needs a new ticket to return.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Will-call tickets are held at the lobby box office and require a photo ID matching the purchaser. The box office opens 30 minutes before event doors and also on Saturdays from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.

Official source
