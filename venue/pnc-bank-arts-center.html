PNC Bank Arts Center

Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches or small nonclear clutches, wristlets or fanny packs up to 6 × 9 inches are permitted after search; diaper bags are inspected at the south-side accessible entrance.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • Small clutch up to 6 × 9 inches
  • Inspected diaper bag

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Larger or opaque bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

All bags are subject to search at the ticket gates. Guest Services is in the middle of the ticket gates; consult the show listing for gate times.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

General parking is included with the ticket; Premier and VIP options are sold separately. Parking lots open about one hour before doors.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The operator’s visitor and arrival pages do not publish an official rideshare pickup point.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Accessible parking is first come, first served without a premium parking pass; accessible seating can be booked online. Guest Services is by the middle ticket gates.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Food and drinks are available inside; guests may bring food in a clear one-gallon bag and one empty bottle or factory-sealed water bottle up to one gallon.

Allowed

  • Food in clear one-gallon bag
  • Empty bottle
  • Factory-sealed water bottle up to one gallon
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The amphitheater’s official visitor guide does not state a general concert re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The visitor guide does not publish a general Will Call pickup procedure for concerts.
