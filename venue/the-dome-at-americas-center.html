Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Approved bags include clear plastic bags no larger than 12" x 6" x 12", standard 1-gallon disposable zip-top bags, or a small clutch no larger than 4.5" x 6.5". All bags are subject to search.

Allowed

  • clear plastic bags no larger than 12" x 6" x 12"
  • standard 1-gallon disposable zip-top bags
  • small clutch no larger than 4.5" x 6.5"

Not allowed

  • Food not dispensed by dome concessions
  • Beverages or Alcoholic Beverages
  • Chains
  • Bottles, cans, coolers, containers, thermoses, or glass containers
  • Recording Equipment, SOUND or VIDEO
  • Professional nor Semi Professional Cameras, STILL OR DIGITAL
  • Strollers
  • Glitter or confetti
  • Peanuts in the shell
  • Projectiles
  • Poles or sticks
  • Laser Pens or laser pointers
  • Go Pro Cameras, iPads or Tablets
  • Selfie Sticks
  • Laptop computers
  • Animals or pets (not including service animals)
  • Artificial noisemakers
  • Illegal drugs or substances
  • Fireworks, candles, flares, balloons
  • Firearms, knives, missiles and weapons
  • Mace and pepper gas
  • Any item that could obstruct a guest’s view
  • Any other item deemed dangerous or inappropriate by management
  • Posters, Banners, Signs

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at The Dome at America’s Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking options near The Dome at America’s Center include various garages and lots in downtown St. Louis. Prices and availability may vary, so it's advisable to check in advance or consider public transportation for convenience.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Passenger(s) drop-off and pickup will be permitted on the west lane of Broadway.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

ADA-accessible public elevators are located at the Broadway Central Entrance (on Broadway Avenue) and Courtyard Entrance (located at 7th Street & Convention Plaza, near Entry A). There are several ADA Accessible parking facilities located near The Dome at America’s Center (“The Dome”).

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

St. Louis offers a vibrant happy hour scene with diverse food and drink specials. Highlights include craft cocktails, seasonal fare, and discounted drinks at venues like Art Bar, Basso, and Big Sky Cafe. Broadway Oyster Bar is noted for its gumbo and live music.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests who exit The Dome outside the designated smoking areas will not be permitted to re-enter The Dome in accordance with The Dome re-entry policy.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

On show days, the box office opens three hours prior to the ticketed show time and remains open until one hour past the ticketed show time. The box office located at the Broadway Central entrance on the east side of the dome is usually open the week prior to shows and on show days only.

Official source
