What matters at Summerfest.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

All bags are subject to inspection at Summerfest, with no backpacks or bags larger than 9x10x12 inches allowed. Special considerations are made for parents with infants and those with medical needs.

Parking

Parking

Patrons are encouraged to park in downtown Milwaukee parking structures and use Summerfest Shuttles for direct access to the festival. Shuttles run every 15 minutes from designated Park and Ride lots with a round-trip fare of $12 for adults and $6 for reduced fare riders.

Concessions

Summerfest 2025 introduces a diverse array of new food and beverage options, including unique items like Lumpia City Mac 'n Cheese Rolls and Funnel Cake Pork Sandwiches. The festival also highlights local vendors through the BMO Small Business Spotlight program.
