Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Stanford Athletics has implemented a Clear Bag Policy to enhance safety and expedite security screenings. Approved bags include clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12"x6"x12" and small clutches no larger than 4.5"x6.5".

Allowed

  • clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12"x6"x12"
  • small clutch bags or purses no larger than 4.5"x6.5"

Rideshare

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Stanford Stadium, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Fixed and portable concession locations sell food and beverages throughout Stanford Stadium. Concessions are cashless, and selection varies by event.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Stanford Stadium's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Stanford Stadium. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
