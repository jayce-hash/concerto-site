Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Stanford Athletics has implemented a Clear Bag Policy to enhance safety and expedite security screenings. Approved bags include clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12"x6"x12" and small clutches no larger than 4.5"x6.5".
