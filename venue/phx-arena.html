Bag Policy

Bag Policy

For quick entry, fans are encouraged to leave bags at home. Small clutches, crossbody bags, and clear bags are allowed, while standard backpacks are not permitted.

Parking

Valet parking starts 2 hours before events, and the garage opens a few hours prior. The venue has a five-story, 700-space parking structure for convenient parking.

Concessions

Accessibility

Accessibility

If you have difficulty navigating stairs but do not need a wheelchair accessible location, row 20 in the 100 level and row 2 in the 200 level do not require taking stairs. Accessible parking spaces are located on floors 1-5 near elevators, available as first-come, first-served.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Mortgage Matchup Center Ticket Office is located in the Pavilion on the Northwest side of the facility at 201 East Jefferson, Phoenix, AZ 85004 (corner of 1st Street & Jefferson). The Mortgage Matchup Center ticket office will be open at 3pm on weekday events and three (3) hours prior to event time for weekends.
