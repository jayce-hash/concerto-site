Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For quick entry, fans are encouraged to leave bags at home. Small clutches, crossbody bags, and clear bags are allowed, while standard backpacks are not permitted.

Allowed

  • small clutches
  • crossbody bags
  • clear bags

Not allowed

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at PHX Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Valet parking starts 2 hours before events, and the garage opens a few hours prior. The venue has a five-story, 700-space parking structure for convenient parking.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Lyft Drop Off Locations: 1st Street/Jefferson, S 2nd Street, S 3rd Street, E Jefferson Street. Lyft Pick Up Location: S 3rd Street & E Jefferson Street.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

If you have difficulty navigating stairs but do not need a wheelchair accessible location, row 20 in the 100 level and row 2 in the 200 level do not require taking stairs. Accessible parking spaces are located on floors 1-5 near elevators, available as first-come, first-served.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Mortgage Matchup Center offers a variety of food and beverage options to cater to different dietary preferences, including vegetarian and gluten-free choices. While specific vendors are not listed, the venue promises a diverse selection to enhance the event experience.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in PHX Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Mortgage Matchup Center Ticket Office is located in the Pavilion on the Northwest side of the facility at 201 East Jefferson, Phoenix, AZ 85004 (corner of 1st Street & Jefferson). The Mortgage Matchup Center ticket office will be open at 3pm on weekday events and three (3) hours prior to event time for weekends.

Official source
