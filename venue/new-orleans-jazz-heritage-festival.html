New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival

New Orleans, LA · USA

Verified venue informationOfficial sources first

Know before you go

What matters at New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Jazz Fest publishes festival-specific allowed and prohibited items rather than a simple universal bag-size rule. Check the current official FAQ and prohibited-items guidance shortly before attending.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Parking

Parking

On-site parking is very limited and is reserved for eligible VIP packages and a limited number of accessible spaces. Parking in the surrounding neighborhood is restricted to residents.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

The City restricts rideshare pickup and drop-off within a perimeter around the Fair Grounds. Use the current official Jazz Fest transportation map for the designated pickup zone.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Jazz Fest has two major food areas with a wide range of Louisiana cuisine and vegan/vegetarian-friendly options. Food, beverage, craft, and merchandise booths are cashless.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Jazz Fest has a dedicated Access Program and Access Center, accessible routes, viewing accommodations, accessible parking/loading guidance, and services for guests with mobility, vision, hearing, and sensory needs.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

General admission does not include re-entry. Certain qualifying VIP credentials include re-entry privileges at the festival's main gates.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Ticket delivery and event-day support depend on the ticket or package purchased. Follow the instructions from the official Jazz Fest ticket provider and current festival communications.

Verified 2026-09-04
Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Public pedestrian access is available at the Gentilly Boulevard, Trafalgar Street, and Sauvage Street gates. Festival gates open at 11 a.m. daily for the currently published schedule; confirm the current edition before arrival.

Verified 2026-09-04
