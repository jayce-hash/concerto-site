Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags up to 12 by 6 by 12 inches are permitted and searched before entry. Non-clear bags receive additional screening.

Allowed

  • Bags no larger than 12 by 6 by 12 inches

Not allowed

  • Bags larger than 12 by 6 by 12 inches
  • Hydration packs
  • Coolers

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Brooklyn Paramount. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

No specific parking options or pricing details are provided on the venue's website. Visitors are encouraged to plan their visit considering the venue's downtown location and proximity to public transportation.

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Brooklyn Paramount's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Limited storage is available for wheelchairs, canes, walkers, and other mobility devices. Nearby accessible subway stations include Jay St-MetroTech for A/C/F/R trains (0.4 miles), and Hoyt St for 2/3 trains, New Lots Av/Flatbush Av-bound only (0.2 miles).

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Brooklyn Paramount offers a sophisticated selection of beer, wine, spirits, and non-alcoholic beverages, along with light bites and snacks. There are seven bars throughout the venue to minimize wait times.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Brooklyn Paramount's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Brooklyn Paramount. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
