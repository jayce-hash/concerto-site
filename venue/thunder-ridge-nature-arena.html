Thunder Ridge Nature Arena

Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Thunder Ridge Nature Arena enforces a clear bag policy. Small purses and handbags are allowed but will undergo a security check.

Allowed

  • Clear Bag/Totes - no larger than 12 x 6 x 12
  • Small Clutch Purse - no bigger than 4.5 x 6.5

Not allowed

  • backpacks
  • duffle bags
  • large sacks
  • cases
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Thunder Ridge Nature Arena. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

General admission parking is free with a valid concert ticket, while Diamond and Premium parking are available for purchase. ADA parking is also free with a valid ticket. Tailgating is allowed with restrictions, and RV parking is available without hookups.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Rideshare is labeled 'Rideshare & Guest Pick up/Drop off' with tram services offered before and after an event.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Thunder Ridge Nature Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Thunder Ridge Nature Arena offers a full-service bar in the exclusive arena lounge, providing a cool-off area with A/C, private restrooms, lounge seating, and an outdoor patio. The venue operates as a cashless facility, accepting only debit and credit card transactions.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Thunder Ridge Nature Arena's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Will Call tickets will be available at the Thunder Ridge Nature Arena Box Office one hour before doors open for each event. The Box Office opens when the fan zone and parking lots open.

Official source
