Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Chase Field enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Approved bags include clear bags up to 12” x 6” x 12” and small clutch bags no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”.

Allowed

  • Clear Bags (Plastic, vinyl or PVC) up to 12” x 6” x 12”
  • Plastic Storage Bag (1 Gallon)
  • Small Clutch Bags/Wallets no larger than 6.5” x 4.5”
  • Seat cushions without pockets, zippers or concealable areas up to 18” wide

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Bags and wallets exceeding 6.5" x 4.5"
  • Non-approved seat cushions
  • Luggage
  • Computer bags
  • Camera bags
  • Briefcases
  • Fanny packs
  • Cinch bags

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests may leave Chase Field at any time; however, specific Gates (A, F, J, and K) will be set up as re-entry gates.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Chase Field sits in downtown Phoenix surrounded by public and private garages and surface lots within a short walk, including the Chase Field Garage on 4th Street. Prepaying through SpotHero or the team site is recommended, and the Valley Metro light rail stops nearby. Rates vary by event.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Chase Field's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests wishing to check in their own walkers, wheelchairs, scooters or other mobility aids during the game may do so at the Guest Relations Centers located across from Sections 128 on the main concourse and 322 on the upper concourse.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Chase Field offers a variety of food and beverage options including local and popular beers at the Beer Garden, comfort foods with a modern twist at Bridges Grille, and elevated classics at Cadillac Diner. Special offerings include a Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Chase Field's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Main Will Call is located outside Gate E on the west side of the ballpark where the main ticket windows are located.
