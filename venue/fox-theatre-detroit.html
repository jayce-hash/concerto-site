Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags larger than 4” x 6” x 1.5” are prohibited. Single compartment bags, wallets, clutches, fanny packs, and clear bags smaller than the specified size are allowed.

Allowed

  • Single compartment bags
  • wallets
  • clutches
  • fanny packs
  • clear bags smaller than 4" x 6" x 1.5"

Not allowed

  • Bags larger than 4” x 6” x 1.5”
  • Bags associated with laptops, tablets, iPads, cameras, and binoculars

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The accessible drop-off and pick-up area is on Woodward Ave. between Montcalm St. and Columbia St.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The sensory room is designed to be fully accessible, accommodating individuals of all abilities and ages, with features such as wheelchair accessibility. The sensory room is located on the upper concourse, across from PORTAL 66, next to First Aid.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Little Caesars Arena offers a variety of food options including arena classics, chicken dishes, grilled items, and signature Little Caesars pizza. There are pop-up stands and four main restaurants: Mike's Pizza Bar, PointsBet Sports Bar, and The UWM District Market.
