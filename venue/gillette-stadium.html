Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Gillette Stadium allows only specific types of clear bags and small wristlets on event days. All items are subject to inspection.

Allowed

  • One-gallon clear, plastic freezer bag (does not exceed 11" x 11")
  • Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bag (does not exceed 12" x 12" x 6")
  • Small wristlet or handheld wallet (does not exceed 6.5" x 4.5")

Not allowed

  • Animals (other than service animals)
  • Unmanned aerial vehicles including drones
  • GoPro cameras and video cameras
  • Tripods or monopods
  • Portable folding and/or standalone chairs
  • Seat cushions with pockets or zippers
  • Strollers or baby seats
  • Coolers or containers, including cans, bottles, and kegs
  • Food or beverages
  • Illegal drugs and substances
  • Weapons of any kind
  • Umbrellas
  • Clothing or signs with wires, batteries or electronic components
  • Selfie sticks or any stick or pole of any size
  • Noisemakers, bullhorns, air horns, vuvuzelas
  • Balloons
  • Beach balls, lacrosse balls, inflated soccer balls, inflated footballs
  • Fireworks, pyrotechnics, flares
  • Laser pointers
  • Hover boards and Segways
  • Any item deemed inappropriate or dangerous by stadium management

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Gillette Stadium. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Gillette Stadium offers two parking options: prepaid stadium-side parking for $50 and general parking included with your ticket on the opposite side of Route 1. Prepaid parking guarantees a spot, while general parking does not require a pass.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Gillette Stadium has a designated rideshare drop off and pick up location in Lot 15 for Uber and Lyft users. Taxi pickup is in Lot 6A.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Please visit our Accessible Services page to learn about the stadium’s Assistive Listening System or call our Ticket Office at (800) 543-1776. Please visit our Accessible Services page to learn about elevators that can be used for guests with mobility concerns or call our Ticket Office at (800) 543-1776.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Gillette Stadium offers a variety of food and beverage options including personal pizzas, BBQ sandwiches, nachos, and a range of beverages from domestic and craft beers to cocktails. Special dietary options include nut-free, dairy-free, and gluten-free items. All concession stands are cashless, with cash-to-card kiosks available.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

For all stadium events, there is no parking lot re-entry nor are refunds granted.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Please visit our Tickets page for information regarding the Will Call and the Gillette Stadium Ticket Office. The official Patriots and Revolution team store, the ProShop, is located in the Patriot Place Plaza adjacent to the Gillette Stadium Ticket Office in the northeast corner of the stadium.
