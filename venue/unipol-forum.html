Bag policy

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Unipol Forum does not publish a universal bag-size limit in its current access rules. Motorcycle helmets and potentially dangerous items are prohibited, and event promoters may impose additional restrictions.

Not allowed

  • motorcycle helmets
  • potentially dangerous items

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Unipol Forum. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Unipol Forum offers ample parking near the venue, with Park B being a notable option. Electric car charging is available at a hub in front of the Repower Theatre. Parking fees during events start from €10.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Unipol Forum's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by Unipol Forum, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Inside the Unipol Forum, visitors can enjoy a variety of food and drink options, including several bars and a large food court. Specific details about the offerings are not provided.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 4, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Unipol Forum's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 4, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Unipol Forum. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
