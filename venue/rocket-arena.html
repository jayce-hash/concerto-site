What matters at Rocket Arena.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Only clear bags no larger than 12" x 12" x 6" are permitted at Rocket Arena. All bags are subject to inspection upon entry.

Verified 2026-08-20

Parking

Parking

Garage parking is available across 8th Street and 3rd Avenue from the arena, with additional parking at Pullman Square a block further east. Street parking may also be available.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Rocket Arena's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Doors

Gates & Entrances

Door times for all Cleveland Monsters games will be 60 minutes prior to the puck dropping with Atrium doors opening 90 minutes before the game. Door times for Cavaliers games Monday through Thursday are 75 minutes prior to tipoff with Atrium doors opening 90 minutes before the game.

Concessions

Concessions

Marshall Health Network Arena offers a variety of food and drink options including large Cokes, popcorn, Miller Lite, and hot dogs. Gluten-free options are available, and all concessions are cashless, accepting major credit/debit cards and mobile payments.

Verified 2026-08-20

Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Rocket Arena has a no re-entry policy: once you leave the building after entering, you cannot re-enter, including to smoke or vape. Please Note: Rocket Arena has a no re-entry policy; once you leave the building during an event, you will not be permitted to return.

Verified 2026-08-20

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Rocket Arena. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
