Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Only clear bags no larger than 12" x 12" x 6" are permitted at Rocket Arena. All bags are subject to inspection upon entry.

Re-Entry Policy

Rocket Arena has a no re-entry policy: once you leave the building after entering, you cannot re-enter, including to smoke or vape. Please Note: Rocket Arena has a no re-entry policy; once you leave the building during an event, you will not be permitted to return.
