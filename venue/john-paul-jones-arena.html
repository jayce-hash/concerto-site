Bag Policy

Bag Policy

John Paul Jones Arena does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Parking

Parking

Parking is available in several lots surrounding the arena, including JPJ Garage and JPJ West, South, and East Lots. Parking passes can be purchased online or at the venue, and the arena is cashless, so plan to use credit/debit cards.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Current official guidance confirms that accessibility arrangements are handled by John Paul Jones Arena, but event-specific services may require advance coordination. Contact the venue before purchasing if you need accessible seating, mobility assistance, interpreting, assistive-listening support, or another accommodation.

Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

Will Call is located at the John Paul Jones Arena Box Office. For ticket-related problems encountered inside the Arena, patrons should visit the back window of the box office located outside Section 308.

Gates & Entrances
