Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Hall permits one bag per ticket holder in the auditorium; additional or oversized bags must go to the cloakroom for £2 per item. Its safety guidance asks guests to avoid bringing large bags.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Allow extra time for security searches; the Hall asks guests not to bring large bags. Use the entrance indicated on the concert ticket.

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026
