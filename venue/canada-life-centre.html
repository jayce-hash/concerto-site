Know before you go

What matters at Canada Life Centre.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

For safety and expedited entry, bags at Canada Life Centre must be 12”x 9”x 5” or smaller and will undergo contactless security screening. On-site bag check is not available.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Parking

Parking

True North Sports + Entertainment has partnered with Platinum Parking to offer over 1,700 parking stalls for events. Guests are encouraged to reserve parking in advance via ParkMobile or ParkWhiz.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Canada Life Centre's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Concessions

Concessions

Canada Life Centre offers a variety of food and beverage options through permanent concession stands and portable kiosks, featuring well-known items like Tim Hortons and Pizza Pizza. All transactions are cashless for an expedited payment experience.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Accessibility

Accessibility

Accessible drop-off and pick-up is located on the west lane of Donald Street beside Canada Life Centre.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Re-Entry

Re-Entry Policy

Guests attending any event at Canada Life Centre will not be allowed to re-enter the arena after exiting.

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Ticket Pickup

Box Office & Ticket Pickup

The Canada Life Centre Box Office/Will Call is located at street level at the corner of Portage Avenue and Donald Street in the main atrium (Gate 1).

Verified 2026-08-20
Official source →
Entrances & Gates

Gates & Entrances

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Canada Life Centre. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.
