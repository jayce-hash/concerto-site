Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Nationals Park enforces a clear bag policy to ensure fan safety and expedite entry. Only specific types and sizes of bags are allowed.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Nationals Park. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Nationals Park is in DC's Navy Yard with team-affiliated Garages A, B and C plus many private lots and garages nearby; prepaid parking is recommended. The Metro Green Line's Navy Yard-Ballpark station is steps from the gates and is the easiest way in.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Nationals Park's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

To further accommodate season plan holders who purchased an accessible season ticket parking permit package in one of the designated lots, the team will make, based on availability, select additional accessible parking spaces available in on a first come, first served basis in the GEICO Garage and Garage C.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Nationals Park offers a variety of food and beverage options, including classic comfort foods with bold flavors and modern twists. Highlights include crispy chicken tenders, footlong hot dogs, and specialty items like the Bacon Wrapped Hot Dog and Pastrami Fry. Fans can also enjoy refreshing brews at the Beer Garden.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests who leave Nationals Park at any time (without the authorization of a gate supervisor) will not be permitted to re-enter.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Main Box Office facing N street near the corner of N & Half Streets is open beginning 4 hours prior to first pitch and closes at the end of the 6th inning. The Nationals Park Box Office is located outside and adjacent to the Center Field Gate.

Official source
