Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Kyle Field A–Z guide requires the 12-1-1 clear-bag policy and says bags are searched on entry. This guide is written for athletics; check concert-specific restrictions.

Not allowed

  • Bags outside the 12-1-1 policy

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue directs guests to Texas A&M Transportation Services for parking; its A–Z guide does not publish concert lots or rates.

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published a concert-specific rideshare location in its Kyle Field A–Z guide.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Elevators are on every side of Kyle Field; some north-side elevators require tickets for particular seating sections. Lactation stations are in first-aid rooms near Sections 128 and 338.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Levy concession stands are located throughout Kyle Field and operate cashlessly. The stadium prohibits outside food and drink, with an exception for one clear, unopened plastic water bottle in the athletics guide.

Allowed

  • One clear, unopened plastic water bottle

Not allowed

  • Outside food and drink
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The official Kyle Field guide prohibits pass-outs and re-entry under SEC rules. Concert promoters may publish different rules for their events.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The Kyle Field A–Z guide states that there is no will-call location and tickets are digital; it describes athletics ticketing.

Official source
