Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All bags are subject to search, and backpacks of any size are prohibited. Bags over 22″ by 14″ by 9″ are not allowed, with see-through/transparent bags recommended.

Allowed

  • See through/transparent bags

Not allowed

  • Backpacks of any size
  • Bags over 22″ by 14″ by 9″
  • Outside food and beverages
  • Weapons
  • Professional photography equipment
  • Noisemaking devices
  • Drones
  • Skateboards
  • Aerosol cans
  • Tools
  • Toys and/or projectiles
  • Cans, bottles, glass or any other beverage containers
  • Chains, studded bracelets or oversized studded rings
  • Large and non-folding umbrellas
  • Illegal drugs or paraphernalia

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Doors open one hour before the scheduled show time (e.g., if the show is at 8 pm, doors open at 7 pm).

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Meyer's Parking Garage is conveniently located next door to the venue at 323-331 W 34th St., New York, NY 10001. No specific pricing information is provided.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Manhattan Center Hammerstein Ballroom's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Manhattan Center is conveniently located one block away from Penn Station and is easily accessible by taking the 1, 2, 3, A, C, or E trains to 34th Street/Penn Station. ADA restrooms are located on the 1st balcony.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food and drink options are not detailed on the venue's FAQ page. Outside food and beverages are prohibited.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Manhattan Center Hammerstein Ballroom's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Located on the far west side of the main lobby, will call opens at door time.
