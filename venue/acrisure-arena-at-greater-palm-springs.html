Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Acrisure Arena at Greater Palm Springs does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All public entrances are accessible. Guests who need prompt elevator access should use the marked Accessible Entrance beside the Ticketmaster Ticket Office; event-specific door times appear on the event page.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking at Acrisure Arena offers convenience for various events, from hockey games to concerts. It's advisable to arrive early to secure a spot and avoid last-minute stress.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Acrisure Arena at Greater Palm Springs's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

All public entrance doors are accessible. A dedicated Accessible Entrance beside the Ticketmaster Ticket Office provides immediate elevator access; accessible seating and guest assistance are available through Guest Services.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food and beverage availability at Acrisure Arena at Greater Palm Springs varies by event and operating setup. Review the linked official guest information before arrival.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Guests who leave during an event may not re-enter with a used ticket. Limited exceptions may be approved by arena management or a gate supervisor.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Ticketmaster Ticket Office is at the northeast corner of the Main Concourse beside the front plaza. It opens at 10:00 a.m. on event days and generally remains open until about one hour after the event begins; tickets are delivered digitally.

Official source
