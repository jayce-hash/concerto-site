Bag policy

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Backpacks and excessively large bags cannot be brought in. Bags are inspected at entry; the FAQ does not give a numeric size limit.

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Excessively large bags
Official source

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Entry includes a metal detector and thorough bag check. Door times are set per event.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Only street parking is available; Neumos has no official parking partners.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Neumos FAQ does not give a designated rideshare location.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 24, 2026

For an accessible seat, purchase a ticket and submit the venue’s request form at least 36 hours in advance on weekdays. Limited regular mezzanine seating is first come first served.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Outside food and drinks are not permitted at a show.

Not allowed

  • Outside food
  • Outside drinks
Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 24, 2026

The Neumos FAQ does not publish a re-entry rule.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 24, 2026

Ticket delivery options include AXS Mobile ID, will call and mail. Paper tickets may be bought in person through the venue’s Runaway box office during its operating hours.

Official source
