Bag policy

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Snapdragon Stadium permits clear bags up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches, one-gallon clear freezer bags and small clutches up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches. Paid bag lockers are available outside on the east side.

Allowed

  • Clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 inches
  • One-gallon clear freezer bag
  • Clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 inches

Not allowed

  • Backpacks

Entrances and doors

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The A-Z guide describes entry screening and bag inspection but does not publish a universal concert door-opening time.

Official source

Parking

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The stadium lists Orange and Yellow general parking, accessible parking in the Green Lot and overflow in SDSU structures P3 and P4. Overflow parking is $3 per hour; event parking prices can differ.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The designated rideshare pickup and drop-off area is on Mission Village Drive north of Jacaranda Street.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Assistive listening devices are available at Guest Services behind Sections 101, 114, 135 and 328; accessible parking is in the Green Lot.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Stadium food and beverage stands operate without cash; reverse ATMs are available near the southeast and northeast entrances. Offerings vary by event.

Official source

Re-entry

Checked Sep 23, 2026

Snapdragon Stadium does not allow re-entry after a guest leaves.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Sep 23, 2026

The venue has not published a general will-call pickup procedure in its A-Z visitor guide.

Official source
