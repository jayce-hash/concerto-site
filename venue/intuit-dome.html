Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Intuit Dome does not publish one universal bag rule for every event. Review the official event information before arrival because size and type restrictions may vary.

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Intuit Dome. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking is available at the VIP West Garage, Standard East Garage, and Players' Garage. Parking fees vary by event, and garages open approximately four hours before showtime. Purchasing a parking pass in advance is recommended.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Intuit Dome's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Additionally, Sensory Bags are available at the Answers Portal located by Section Main 6 on the main level concourse. Please visit the Intuit Dome Mobility Hub located on the first level of our Standard East Garage (3700 W Century Blvd, Inglewood, CA) for rideshare drop off and pickup.

Official source

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Food and beverage availability at Intuit Dome varies by event and operating setup. Review the linked official guest information before arrival.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Intuit Dome's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Official source

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Intuit Dome. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
