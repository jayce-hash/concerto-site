Bag policy

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Bags larger than 4.5” x 6.5” and smaller than 14”x14”x6” are allowed with X-Ray screening. Small clutches and purses under 4.5”x6.5” are permitted without X-Ray but are subject to inspection.

Allowed

  • Bags larger than 4.5” x 6.5” and smaller than 14”x14”x6”
  • Hand clutches
  • Wristlets
  • Small purses under 4.5”x6.5”

Not allowed

  • Backpacks
  • Suitcases
  • Large multicompartment bags
  • Vape pens

Entrances and doors

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Entrance assignments and door times are event-specific at Wells Fargo Center. Use the entrance shown on the official event page or ticket, arrive early for screening, and follow on-site signage.

Official source

Parking

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Parking at Xfinity Mobile Arena can be reserved in advance through SpotHero for up to 50% off drive-up rates. Accessible parking is available near entrances, and daily subway parking is offered in Lot A on weekdays.

Official source

Rideshare

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Wells Fargo Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Official source

Accessibility

Checked Aug 20, 2026

The Kidology Sensory Room, located on the Suite Level, is open to any fan that needs sensory relief.

Food and drink

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Wells Fargo Center offers a wide variety of food and drink options, including popular chains like Shake Shack and Chickie's & Pete's, as well as local favorites such as Federal Donuts and 9th Street Italian Sandwiches. Special offerings include gluten-free options and kosher meals, ensuring there's something for everyone.

Re-entry

Checked Aug 20, 2026

A venue-wide re-entry rule was not confirmed in Wells Fargo Center's current published guidance. Treat exit as final unless event staff explicitly confirms re-entry before you leave.

Tickets and will call

Checked Aug 20, 2026

Current official guidance does not publish one universal will-call schedule for every event at Wells Fargo Center. Use the delivery instructions in the official ticket order and confirm event-day box-office details before traveling to the venue.
