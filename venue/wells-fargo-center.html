Know before you go

What matters at Wells Fargo Center.

Bag Policy

Bag Policy

Bags larger than 4.5” x 6.5” and smaller than 14”x14”x6” are allowed with X-Ray screening. Small clutches and purses under 4.5”x6.5” are permitted without X-Ray but are subject to inspection.

Verified 2026-08-20

Parking

Parking

Parking at Xfinity Mobile Arena can be reserved in advance through SpotHero for up to 50% off drive-up rates. Accessible parking is available near entrances, and daily subway parking is offered in Lot A on weekdays.

Verified 2026-08-20

Rideshare

Rideshare

A dedicated rideshare zone was not confirmed in Wells Fargo Center's current published guidance. Follow the event page and your rideshare app on show day, and use only signed pickup or drop-off areas.

Concessions

Concessions

Wells Fargo Center offers a wide variety of food and drink options, including popular chains like Shake Shack and Chickie's & Pete's, as well as local favorites such as Federal Donuts and 9th Street Italian Sandwiches. Special offerings include gluten-free options and kosher meals, ensuring there's something for everyone.
