# Concerto Partner Agreement (Founding): restaurants, hotels, artists

Between Concerto LLC ("Concerto") and the business named below ("Partner").

1. What Concerto provides. Partner's Perk inside Your Night for fans who saved a show at the venues Partner selects, on the venue's page in the app and on concertocity.com, and in Concerto Perks under Account. Every placement is labeled Concerto Partner. A monthly report of impressions and taps.
2. What Partner provides. A real fan benefit (an offer, rate, item, or access), honored as written for the dates published; a link or redemption method; a named contact.
3. Review. Concerto reviews each Perk before it goes live and may decline or ask for changes. Concerto never sells unlabeled recommendations.
4. Term and price. Founding partners pay nothing through December 31, 2026. From January 1, 2027, $149 per month per business, cancel any time, confirmed in writing 30 days before billing begins.
5. Data. Reports are aggregate. No fan personal data is shared.
6. Ending. Either side may end this with 30 days' notice. Perks are removed within 24 hours.
