# Concerto Venue Partner Agreement (Founding)

Between Concerto LLC ("Concerto") and the venue named below ("Venue").

1. What Concerto provides. A claimable venue page in the Concerto app and on concertocity.com; a console where Venue verifies its own bag policy, entry, parking, rideshare, concessions, accessibility, re-entry, and ticket pickup information; stage times per event that appear to fans who saved a show at Venue; a monthly report of fan activity on Venue's page.
2. What Venue provides. Accurate information, updated when it changes; a named contact; permission for Concerto to mark the page "Verified by the venue".
3. Term and price. Founding partners pay nothing through December 31, 2026. From January 1, 2027, $299 per month, cancel any time. Concerto will confirm pricing in writing 30 days before billing begins.
4. Information. Venue controls what it publishes. Concerto may keep its own researched baseline visible where Venue has not published a section, marked with its source and date.
5. Data. Reports are aggregate. Concerto does not share fan personal data with Venue.
6. Ending. Either side may end this with 30 days' notice. Venue's published content is removed from the page within 5 days.
