Key Features

Everything for
Your Show Day.

Six features, one seamless experience — from the moment you buy your ticket to the last song of the night.

No. 01

Featured Tours

Explore current tours through beautifully organized tour pages. Each stop includes a venue-based City Guide, direct ticket access, bag policies, rideshare, concessions, and parking — all in one tap.

No. 02

Setlists & Tour Info

Instantly access accurate setlists, local start times, and official tour websites. Tap any song to reveal Apple Music and Spotify links — so fans know exactly what to expect before the lights go down.

No. 03

City Guides

The best restaurants, hotels, and shops near your venue — curated and ranked. Top Picks surface the standout spots first, using smart search and accurate distance tracking tied to your specific event night.

No. 04

Events Near Me

Discover concerts, sports, comedy, festivals, theater, and nightlife happening around you — searchable by city, date, and category. Perfect for extending a concert weekend or planning a spontaneous night out.

No. 05

At The Venue

Everything fans need once they arrive. Rideshare zones, bag policies, concessions overviews, parking guidance, and real-time concert day weather — centralized so you can focus on the experience.

No. 06

Festivals

Plan multi-day festival experiences with ease. Discover lineups, nearby stays, dining, and attractions — organized into a personalized itinerary designed around your schedule and style.
