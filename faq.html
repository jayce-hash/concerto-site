FAQ

Answers without the scavenger hunt.

How Concerto works, what stays free, what Your Night is, and what Concerto+ adds. For step-by-step help, use the Help Center.

Help CenterContact
What exactly is Concerto?

Concerto helps concert fans prepare for the night around their ticket. Save a show to keep venue guidance, available setlists, nearby places, and transportation details together.

Is Concerto free to use?

Yes. Venue guides, nearby discovery, available setlists, saved shows, countdowns, and weather are free. Concerto+ adds personalized planning, AI Bag Check, and show-day alerts.

What happens when I save a show?

Your next saved show moves to Home with a countdown. Tap it to open Your Night, where venue essentials, weather, setlist, restaurants, hotels, things to do, getting-there guidance, and getting-home guidance live together.

What is Your Night?

Your Night brings together the details for a concert you saved. Open it from Home or My Shows for venue essentials, available timing and setlists, nearby places, and getting-there and getting-home guidance.

How does Concerto know when the headliner goes on?

When available, Concerto shows venue-supplied timing or an estimate based on previous shows, with its source and basis. Historical estimates are not confirmed times for your event. When timing is unavailable, check the official event page.

Can Concerto find shows I already have tickets for?

With your permission, Concerto checks your calendar for events that name an artist and a venue in its catalogue and offers to save them with one tap. It reads only; it never writes to your calendar or stores your events.

What are Concerto Perks?

Perks are specific benefits from partner restaurants, hotels, venues, and artists. Available offers explain who qualifies, the valid dates, and how to redeem them. Check Perks or Your Night for current availability; a nearby listing by itself is not a Perk.

Does Concerto sell tickets?

No. Concerto is not a ticketing platform. Where ticket links appear, they point users toward the ticketing destination for the event.

How much venue and tour coverage is there?

Browse Venues and Tours for the current catalogue. Setlists and other details appear where information is available; coverage varies by show.

What does a verified date mean?

It shows when Concerto last confirmed a critical venue fact against an official source. The date gives you a way to judge freshness instead of being asked to trust an undated rule.

Do I need an account?

You can browse without one. Sign in to connect supported saved-show and membership features across devices. Saved plans and private concert notes in this release stay on the device where you create them.

What is Concerto+?

Concerto+ adds a suggested plan around your concert and preferences, plus AI Bag Check and show-day alerts. You can adjust a generated plan and share its text. Plans do not make reservations; confirm timing and availability before you go.

How does AI Bag Check work?

AI Bag Check compares the bag you show the app with the venue policy Concerto has on file. It is guidance, not a guarantee of entry; venue security always has the final say.

How do I cancel Concerto+?

Manage or cancel the subscription through your Apple subscription settings. Access continues through the paid period after cancellation.

I paid but the app does not show membership. What do I do?

Use Restore Purchases in Account. That asks Apple to re-check the subscription and restore the entitlement on the signed-in account.
