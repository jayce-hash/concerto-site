FAQ

Answers without the scavenger hunt.

What Concerto is, what stays free, what Concerto+ adds, and how the saved-show experience works.

What exactly is Concerto?+

Concerto is a concert-night companion. It brings discovery, saved shows, venue intelligence, setlists, weather, nearby places, arrival, and getting-home guidance into one connected product.

Is Concerto free to use?+

Yes. Core venue information, nearby discovery, setlists, saved shows, countdowns, weather, and show-night context stay free. Concerto+ is the personalized concierge layer that turns those pieces into a plan around you.

Does Concerto sell tickets?+

No. Concerto is not a ticketing platform. Where ticket links appear, they point users toward official or verified ticketing destinations.

How much venue and tour coverage is there?+

The current 2.5 data set includes 346 venue guides and 158 tour guides, with setlist coverage attached where available.

What does a verified date mean?+

It shows when Concerto last confirmed a critical venue fact against an official source. The date is there so you can judge freshness instead of being asked to trust an undated rule.

What happens when I save a show?+

A countdown appears on Home. Tap it to open Your Night, where venue essentials, weather, setlist, nearby restaurants, hotels, things to do, getting there, and getting home live together.

Do I need an account?+

You can browse without one. An account keeps saved shows, favorites, membership, and other personal product state connected when you sign in across devices.

What is Concerto+?+

Concerto+ is the personalized concierge layer. Free Concerto tells you what you need to know; Concerto+ helps decide what to do with it by using your saved show, venue, timing, nearby places, weather, and preferences.

How does AI Bag Check work?+

AI Bag Check compares the bag you show the app with the venue policy Concerto has on file. It is guidance, not a guarantee of entry; venue security always has the final say.

How do I cancel Concerto+?+

Manage or cancel the subscription through your Apple subscription settings. Access continues through the paid period after cancellation.

I paid but the app does not show membership. What do I do?+

Use Restore Purchases in Account. That asks Apple to re-check the subscription and restore the entitlement on the signed-in account.
