Concerto is the app you open after you buy the ticket. Instead of juggling maps, reviews, and ticketing websites, Concerto gives you one hub for your entire night out, curated city guides, venue logistics (parking, bag policies, concessions), official setlists, and tour guides for 300+ venues worldwide.
Yes. The core of Concerto is completely free, Events Near Me, City Guides, Setlists, Bag Policies, Parking, and 1-Tap Rideshare routing at no cost. Concerto Premium ($7.99/month or $69.99/year) unlocks AI Bag Check, Live Mode, and Concerto+ AI itineraries.
As part of Concerto Premium, hold your bag up to your phone’s camera. Our AI estimates your bag’s dimensions and structure, cross-references it with your specific venue’s official policy, and gives you an instant Pass, Warn, or Fail verdict, before you leave the house.
Concerto+ is our AI-powered itinerary builder. Tell it your show, your city, and your vibe, it generates a full evening including dinner reservation timing, ideal arrival window, and post-show plans mapped precisely around your venue.
Live Mode is your in-show companion. Once you arrive at the venue, the app shifts into a real-time experience. When the show ends, you receive a beautifully designed, shareable Story Card documenting your whole night, like a Strava post for your concert.
