FAQ

Answers without the scavenger hunt.

How Concerto works, what stays free, how Your Night and Perks fit in, and what Concerto+ adds. For step-by-step help, use the Help Center.

Help CenterContact
What exactly is Concerto?

Concerto is a concert-night companion. It connects discovery, saved shows, venue intelligence, setlists, weather, nearby places, getting there, and getting home in one product built around the show you are actually attending.

Is Concerto free to use?

Yes. The factual layer stays free: venue guides, nearby discovery, setlists, saved shows, countdowns, weather, and show-night context. Concerto+ is the personalized concierge layer that helps turn those pieces into a plan around you.

What happens when I save a show?

Your next saved show moves to Home with a countdown. Tap it to open Your Night, where venue essentials, weather, setlist, restaurants, hotels, things to do, getting-there guidance, and getting-home guidance live together.

What is Your Night?

Your Night is the show-specific home for a concert you saved. Instead of making you jump between separate tools, it brings the most useful information around that exact venue and date into one place.

What are Concerto Perks?

Concerto Perks are fan benefits created with restaurants, hotels, venues, artists, and tours. They are meant to be useful in the context of a real concert night, so a Perk can be tied to a venue, a tour, a specific show, or a useful moment before or after it.

Where do Perks appear?

In the app, open Account and tap Concerto Perks for the full view. When a Perk is relevant to a show you saved, Concerto can also surface it inside Your Night and related venue or tour experiences so it appears where the decision is happening.

Are Concerto Perks paid recommendations?

Perks come from partner relationships and are labeled. Concerto Picks are separate editorial recommendations and cannot be purchased. A partner can create a Perk or receive a clearly labeled placement, but cannot buy Concerto's editorial opinion.

Do I need Concerto+ to use Perks?

Perks are separate from Concerto+. Eligibility belongs to the individual offer, so any membership or other requirement will be stated on that Perk.

Does Concerto sell tickets?

No. Concerto is not a ticketing platform. Where ticket links appear, they point users toward the ticketing destination for the event.

How much venue and tour coverage is there?

The current 2.5 data set includes 346 venue guides and 158 tour guides, with setlist coverage attached where available.

What does a verified date mean?

It shows when Concerto last confirmed a critical venue fact against an official source. The date gives you a way to judge freshness instead of being asked to trust an undated rule.

Do I need an account?

You can browse without one. An account keeps saved shows, favorites, membership, and other personal product state connected when you sign in across devices.

What is Concerto+?

Concerto+ is the personalized concierge layer. Free Concerto tells you what you need to know; Concerto+ helps decide what to do with it using your saved show, venue, timing, nearby places, weather, and preferences.

How does AI Bag Check work?

AI Bag Check compares the bag you show the app with the venue policy Concerto has on file. It is guidance, not a guarantee of entry; venue security always has the final say.

How do I cancel Concerto+?

Manage or cancel the subscription through your Apple subscription settings. Access continues through the paid period after cancellation.

I paid but the app does not show membership. What do I do?

Use Restore Purchases in Account. That asks Apple to re-check the subscription and restore the entitlement on the signed-in account.
