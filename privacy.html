Privacy Policy.

What we collect, why, and the controls you have over it.

Effective October 21, 2026

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

This policy covers Concerto. Reprise and Medley, from the creator of Concerto, use the same account. What each of them keeps is set out on its own page: Reprise Privacy and Medley Privacy.

What the app can use, and why

Calendar. With your permission, Concerto reads your calendar to find concerts you already have planned and offers to save them with one tap. It never writes to your calendar and never stores your events; the match happens on your phone.

Location. Used to show shows and venues near you. Concerto does not track or store your location history.

Reports. When you tap "Report wrong info," Concerto stores which fact you flagged and why, with no account required, so we can correct it.

Partner content. Venues that verify their own page and restaurants and hotels that partner with Concerto see aggregate counts only: how many times their card was shown, tapped, or had its Perk opened each month. No personal data is shared with any partner.

At Concerto, we are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a transparent explanation of how we collect, use, and safeguard your information. We built this app to enhance your real-world experiences, and we only collect data that helps us do exactly that.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information & App Usage

  • Account Details: Email address and profile information, if you sign up for premium updates or in-app features.
  • Location Data: GPS and location data to provide tailored recommendations, Events Near Me, 1-Tap Rideshare routing, and localized venue intel.
  • Preferences & Inputs: Your answers when planning your night (how you’re getting there, food, bag, and the way home), used to build your plan.
  • Payment Information: Handled securely through Apple/third-party providers for Concerto+ subscriptions. We do not store your full credit card details.

b. Camera & Photo Access (Concerto+)

  • To use the Bag Check feature, Concerto requires temporary access to your device’s camera or photo library. Images captured or uploaded are processed by our computer vision AI solely to estimate bag dimensions and structure against official venue policies.

c. Non-Personal Information

  • Device type, operating system, and anonymous usage statistics.
  • Search queries, setlist views, and app interactions used to improve functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to power the Concerto experience:

  • To personalize your experience based on your current concert location and selected events.
  • To recommend hotels, restaurants, shops, and transport options anchored to your venue.
  • To process Bag Check verdicts and build your planned night.
  • To communicate important updates, new features, or exclusive drops, only if you opt in.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party services as strictly necessary for:

  • AI Processing: Anonymized image data and itinerary prompts may be processed by secure third-party AI partners to generate Bag Check verdicts.
  • Payment Processing: Managed securely by the App Store.
  • App Infrastructure: Analytics, crash reporting, and location-based mapping providers. All partners comply with strict data protection standards.

4. Your Controls & Choices

You have ultimate control over your data within the Concerto ecosystem:

  • Location & Camera: You can revoke access at any time via your device’s system settings. Note that revoking these will disable features like Bag Check and localized City Guides.
  • Communications: You can opt out of promotional emails or notifications at any time.
  • Data Deletion: You may request to permanently delete your account and associated data by contacting us at support@concertocity.com.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Children’s Privacy

Concerto is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as we introduce new features. If we make significant changes, we will notify users via the app or email. Continued use of the app after changes implies acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Service Providers and Cookies

Concerto uses a small number of service providers to run the app and website. They process information to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase: account sign-in and your saved shows, favorites, and preferences. It also stores Concerto’s anonymous usage statistics: which screens and features are used, and when a Concerto Partner’s card is shown or tapped. These are tied to a random ID made on your device or in your browser, never to your name or location, and the app lets you turn them off in Settings.
  • Apple and RevenueCat: Concerto+ subscriptions and purchase status. Concerto does not receive your card details.
  • Anthropic: processes the questions you ask Concerto AI and the information you submit to Bag Check to produce that result. Concerto does not send your name or account with them. Concerto also keeps the text of questions asked of Concerto AI, with the venue or tour it was about, to see what fans need and improve the guides. They are stored without your name, account, IP address, or device ID.
  • Google Places and Ticketmaster: venue photos and details, and event and artist information. These requests describe the venue or artist being looked up, not your account.
  • Netlify: hosts the website and handles its requests, including an approximate location from your IP address to show shows near you.
  • Google Analytics (website only): measures how the website is used, using cookies. You can block or delete these cookies in your browser settings.
