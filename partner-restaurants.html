The opportunity

Useful for fans. Relevant to you.

  1. 01

    What fans receive

    A concert menu, a reservation window, or a clearly defined dining benefit.

  2. 02

    What you can explore

    Introduce your business while fans are deciding where to eat. Agree on the venue, dates, placement, and action before launch.

  3. 03

    What success can mean

    Reservations, offer uses, or tracked visits to your booking page. Confirm measurement capabilities before launch; clicks are not confirmed purchases.

Working together

A clear path to launch.

  1. Step one

    Tell us the fit.

    Share your business, audience, location or tour, and an initial idea. An inquiry is not a commitment.

  2. Step two

    Agree on the details.

    We review the fan benefit, dates, placements, responsibilities, pricing if applicable, and what can be measured.

  3. Step three

    Prepare and review.

    Provide the approved copy, links, eligibility, and redemption instructions. The Partner Console lets you prepare an offer; publication follows review.

  4. Step four

    Launch and learn.

    Activate the agreed placement, keep the offer current, and review available results. Reach, bookings, and sales are not guaranteed.
