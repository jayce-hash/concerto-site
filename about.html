Why Concerto

A concerto is written for a soloist and an orchestra.
The artist is the soloist. The venue, the city, and the ride home are the orchestra.

We build for the orchestra.

Our story

It started with
one night in Toronto.

Our founder, Jayce Wells, had tickets to the Eras Tour in Toronto. Planning the night meant bouncing between Google, Apple Maps and OpenTable just to figure out what was around the stadium.

So he designed one app for the exact show you are going to. It started with every NFL, NBA and MLB stadium, entered by hand, and now covers more than 600 venues worldwide.
