About Concerto

The concert is one part of the night.

Concerto connects the show, the venue, and the city around it. Save a show and the scattered questions around the night become one experience: what the venue allows, how to get there, what the artist has been playing, where to eat, what the weather looks like, and how to get home.

The problem

Most concert apps stop once you know the show exists.

Buying the ticket is the easy part. Then come the questions: Can I bring this bag? Where should I park? Where are we eating? How early do I need to leave? What songs should I know? The answers exist, but they live across venue sites, maps, review apps, ticketing pages, weather apps, and group chats.

The Concerto standard

Verified beats comprehensive.

Critical venue information is researched from official sources and dated so you can see when Concerto last confirmed it. If a venue has not published something, Concerto says so instead of filling the gap with a guess. The product is not just information. It is confidence about what is actually true.

Free and Concerto+

Free tells you what to know. Concerto+ helps decide what to do with it.

Free Concerto is the trustworthy layer: venue guides, tours, setlists, Near Me, saved shows, and nearby places. Concerto+ is the personalized layer: Your Night planned around the show you saved, Plan My Night timing, AI Bag Check, and alerts that react when conditions change.

Founder

Built from the fan problem outward.

Concerto was founded by Jayce Wells in Dallas-Fort Worth after seeing how fragmented concert-night planning remained even after tickets were purchased. It started as a set of venue guides and grew into the iPhone app and this site, both running on the same data. The goal has not changed: make the night around the concert feel as considered as the concert itself.
