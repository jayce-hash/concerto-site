01 · The problem

Most apps stop at discovery.

Buying the ticket is the easy part. Then come the questions nobody has a good answer for. Will this bag get me turned away? Where do I actually park? Is there somewhere to eat that will not make us late? What have they been playing?

Every app can tell you the show exists. After that you are on your own, bouncing between maps, review sites, and a venue page that was last updated who knows when.

The answers exist. They are just scattered across a dozen places, and half of them are out of date.

02 · The difference

A date on every fact, or it does not ship.

How the data works

Bag policies and parking are researched from the venue’s own official sources, then stamped with the date they were last confirmed.

That date is the whole point. Anyone can publish a bag policy. Almost nobody tells you when they last checked it, which means you find out it changed while you are standing at the gate. In Concerto the date sits right next to the rule, so you can decide how much to trust it before you leave the house.

03 · Beyond concerts

Same arenas. Same questions.

The venues do not change between a concert and a playoff game. Neither do the bag rules, the parking lots, or the rideshare zone that is somehow three blocks from where you were dropped off.

So Concerto covers the building, not just the show. If you are heading to a game, a match, a comedy taping, or a festival at one of these arenas and stadiums, you get the same guide, the same verified dates, and the same answer to the same questions.
