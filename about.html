Behind the App

Our Story.

Whether it’s your favorite artist taking the stage, or your hometown team hitting the court, the experience starts long before the event.

It's the dinner before the show, the hotel you stay in, the rush of grabbing merch, and figuring out how to get home when the encore ends. Most apps stop at discovery. They tell you the show exists, and then you're on your own—bouncing between maps, review sites, and venue pages just to piece the night together.

At Concerto, we believe every detail of the night matters. That’s why we built the app you open after you buy the ticket. A single hub for the entire night out.

We actively cover over 300 venues globally, providing verified bag policies, parking maps, official setlists, and 1-tap rideshare routing. We skip the generic Yelp reviews to bring you curated City Guides physically anchored to your venue.

And Concerto isn’t just for concerts. If you’re heading to a game, a match, or any live event at these same arenas and stadiums, you get the exact same benefits. From grabbing dinner before tip-off to finding the right rideshare zone after the final whistle, we built Concerto to make the entire experience effortless.

Concerto Premium

For fans who want to remove all friction from the live event experience, Premium unlocks our most powerful tools:

  • AI Bag Check: Scan your bag with your camera for an instant verdict against the venue's official policy. No surprises at the door.
  • Live Mode & Recaps: Switch to an in-show experience and receive a beautifully designed, shareable Story Card documenting your night—a Strava post for your concert.
  • Concerto+ Itineraries: A personalized AI builder that mathematically maps out your dinner timing, arrival windows, and post-show plans.
  • The Essentials Kit: Annual Premium subscribers receive an exclusive physical kit (featuring a stadium-approved clear crossbody bag, portable charger, and more).

With Concerto, planning your night is as easy as enjoying it.

From the pre-show buzz to the final encore—or from the opening tip to the last play—we’re here to make every moment smoother.
