Our story

It started with
one night in Toronto.

Our founder, Jayce Wells, had tickets to the Eras Tour in Toronto. Planning the night meant bouncing between Google, Apple Maps and OpenTable just to figure out what was around the stadium.

So he designed one app for the exact show you are going to. It started with every NFL, NBA and MLB stadium, entered by hand, and now covers more than 1,000 venues worldwide.

Today you save a show and Concerto builds Your Night around it, with what you need to know about the venue and the tour, and what’s around you in the city. You can ask Concerto AI anything about your show. Afterward, the show is kept in Reprise with a card of your photos and the setlist.

The family

One Account,
Three Apps.

Concerto is the night itself. Two more apps from the same founder cover what comes around it, and one account works in all three.

Reprise turns the photos from a show or a trip into an album and a card to share. Medley is the quick way to see what an artist is playing on tour, with setlists added by fans and fans streaming shows.

What fans ask

The same questions,
before every show.

What bag can I bring? Where do I park? When do doors open? What will they play? Where should we eat?

Fans ask these before every concert, and the answers are usually spread across a venue’s website, a map, a setlist site and a few tabs of restaurants. Concerto answers all of them for the exact venue and tour, on one page, and Concerto AI answers them in a sentence.

Fans are already searching for these answers, and Google is sending them to Concerto. Once a fan knows what they can bring, the next thing they decide is where to eat and where to stay, so the same page answers that too.
