About Concerto

The concert is one part of the night.

Concerto connects the show, the venue, and the city around it. Save a show and the scattered questions around the night become one experience: what the venue allows, how to get there, what the artist has been playing, where to eat, what the weather looks like, and how to get home.

The Concerto standard

Useful only if you can trust it.

  • Venue facts researched from official sources
  • Verification dates shown beside critical information
  • Setlists and tour context tied to the show you saved
  • Nearby places presented when they are actually relevant

The problem

Most concert apps stop once you know the show exists.

Buying the ticket is the easy part. Then come the questions: Can I bring this bag? Where should I park? Where are we eating? How early do I need to leave? What songs should I know? The answers exist, but they live across venue sites, maps, review apps, ticketing pages, weather apps, and group chats.

Trust

Verified beats comprehensive.

Critical venue information is researched from official sources and dated so you can see when Concerto last confirmed it. If a venue has not published something, Concerto should say that instead of filling the gap with a guess.

The product is not just information. It is confidence about what is actually true.
