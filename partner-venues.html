The three-front standard

Every partnership has to work for the fan, for you, and for Concerto.

What fans get

A reason to care.

  • Venue information that is official, current, and dated
  • Parking, concessions, and premium offers before they arrive
  • Fewer surprises at the door

What you get

A useful commercial outcome.

  • Fewer bad-information calls and fewer bag-policy incidents at the gate
  • Parking, premium, and F&B actions from fans planning ahead
  • A direct channel to fans with a saved show at your venue

What Concerto looks for

A real contribution.

  • A named official contact for verification and updates
  • Official parking, premium, or concession offers, or paid sponsorship
  • Permission to label the guide Official Partner

How it appears in Concerto

Fans meet the offer where the decision happens.

This is the Home card a fan sees when a Concerto Partner has a real benefit near their saved show. The same offer carries into Your Night, the venue page, and Concerto Perks. Paid placements are always labeled Partner.
