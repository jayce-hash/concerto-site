Where it lives

The collaboration can live inside the fan journey.

Content is built around a specific tour, venue, city, or concert weekend, not dropped into a feed as a generic sponsorship.

Before

Help plan the night.

The restaurant you would actually pick, the neighborhood worth arriving early for, the venue tip you wish somebody told you.

Around the show

Meet fans in context.

Setlist reactions, venue walkthroughs, city guides, and show-week content tied to a real date.

After

Make it memorable.

Year-in-review, fan recaps, creator picks, and Concerto Rewind concepts that extend past one post.

Ideas we like

Useful beats generic.

  • Creator-curated pre-show picks for a venue or city
  • City and venue recommendations from someone who has been
  • Concert-night guides and account takeovers
  • Giveaways and fan perks around a tour date
  • Tour or artist-specific setlist content
  • Concerto Rewind campaigns

Creator picks stay clearly labeled as the creator's. Concerto Picks remain Concerto's editorial view, and neither is for sale.

Who should reach out

You do not need millions of followers to have a good idea.

Music creators, local city creators, food and travel voices, concert photographers, fan communities, and artists are all welcome. We care about fit and creativity, not a vanity number.
